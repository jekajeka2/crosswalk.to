# Ask, specifically and boldly, then believe you'll get it. Asking sets things in motion, receiving takes care of itself, and there's no shortage, so the only real failure is not asking or asking too small. Define exactly what you want (write it down, with details), and hold it with childlike faith. Plan like an adult, believe like a child, and try it for 90 days.

Distilled from Jim Rohn's talk "How to Get Whatever You Want."

Three principles:
1. Asking starts the process. It sets mental and emotional machinery in motion; you don't need to know how it works to use it.
2. Receiving is automatic. So the real problem is failure to ask. Working hard without written goals makes you a good worker but a poor asker.
3. There's plenty. Success isn't rationed; it's an ocean. Don't show up with a teaspoon. Bring at least a bucket.

Two ways to ask:
- With intelligence: be clear and specific (how much, how big, what color, by when). Well-defined goals act like a magnet, and the better you describe them, the harder they pull.
- With faith: believe you'll get it the way a child does, without adult skepticism.

Formula: make plans like an adult, believe in them like a child. Try it for 90 days; you can always go back to the old way.
