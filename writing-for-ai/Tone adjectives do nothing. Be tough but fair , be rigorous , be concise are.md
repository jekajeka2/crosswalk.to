# Tone adjectives do nothing. "Be tough but fair", "be rigorous", "be concise" are unfalsifiable at runtime and the model resolves them toward agreeable. What works is structural: name the moment and the prohibited action ("ask the question, then stop talking"), fix the order ("verdict before encouragement", or every criticism reads as a caveat), and anchor the scale ("3 is the hire bar; a 2 is normal").
