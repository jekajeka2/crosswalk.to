# Acta Pharmaceuticals raised $24M seed (total seed financing), lead not named. The Boston biotech is developing GSM-779690T, a once-daily oral gamma-secretase modulator for early intervention in and potential prevention of Alzheimer's disease, now in a Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers.

Shareholders include the Alzheimer's Disease Research Foundation, Dolby Family Ventures, Gamma Bio Investors and family offices
HQ: Boston, MA
Scientific founder: Dr. Rudolph E. Tanzi (MGH, Harvard Medical School); co-founder and CEO George Alex; CSO Kevin Rynearson
Lead program: GSM-779690T, from ~25 years of GSM research by Tanzi and the late Dr. Steven L. Wagner at MGH and UC San Diego, backed by ~$70M of preclinical development funding; lowers Aβ42 while raising Aβ37 and Aβ38
Pipeline: combination therapies from MGH's Alzheimer's-in-a-Dish platform, with 30+ validated combinations; IP licensed from MGH
Use of funds: move GSM-779690T through clinical development and expand the early-intervention pipeline
Reported by: Pulse 2.0
