# Wrapped Health raised $2.1M pre-seed co-led by Lightbank and Arteria Capital. The Washington, DC startup plugs into electronic health records so the over-the-counter products clinicians recommend become one-tap orders, fulfilled by retailers and payable with FSA/HSA benefits.

HQ: Washington, DC
Founder: Max Russell-Harris (CEO)
Arteria Capital is the healthcare AI fund of former Headspace CEO Russell Glass
Use of funds: product and engineering, more EHR integrations, partnerships with provider groups, health systems and digital health platforms
Traction: available through the athenahealth Marketplace to 180,000+ clinicians on athenaOne
Reported by: PR Newswire
