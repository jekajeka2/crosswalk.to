# Warp Sciences raised $215M undisclosed-stage round led by Lightspeed, emerging from stealth. The San Francisco company, led by ex-OpenAI CPO Kevin Weil, is building a US-based chemistry CRO that pairs medicinal chemists with lab robotics and AI models trained on its own experimental data, starting with lead-optimization analog synthesis for pharma and biotech.

Other investors: Premji Invest, Mayfield, IVP, Lux, Conviction, Menlo Ventures, 01 Advisors, Standard, Lowercarbon Capital, Gigascale Capital, Angular Ventures, Scribble Ventures, Uncork Capital, Innovation Endeavors, Matter VP, Zero Shot Fund, SignalFire, Moxxie Ventures
Angels: Brian Armstrong, Mike Krieger, Ion Stoica, Dylan Field, Mark Chen, Sarah Friar
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Kevin Weil (CEO; ex-OpenAI CPO and VP of Science), Sol Reisberg (CSO; ex-Terremoto Biosciences), Mano Paluri (CTO; ex-Meta Superintelligence Labs, helped start FAIR), DJ Patil (COO; first US Chief Data Scientist, Devoted Health founding exec)
Model: services only, no in-house drug pipeline; customers keep ownership of their programs; pitched as a domestic, IP-protective alternative to overseas (largely Chinese) chemistry CROs amid the BIOSECURE Act
Use of funds: build increasingly automated chemistry labs whose data improves its AI models
Team: robotics hires from Genesis, Skild AI and 1X; scientists from Genentech and Lawrence Livermore
Reported by: Pulse 2.0
