# Luxor Technology raised $16M strategic round led by CMT Digital. The Seattle compute company sells hardware, energy, software, compute trading and derivatives tools for Bitcoin mining and, since September's Luxor AI launch, for AI and high-performance computing.

Other investors: Canaan, MicroBT, Halo, Steelhead, angel investors
Round includes both primary and secondary capital
HQ: Seattle, WA
CEO and co-founder: Nick Hansen
Traction: hardware brokerage desk has traded $1B of ASIC hardware; Luxor Energy services 75+ MW of load across ERCOT and SPP; agentic cloud platform reached 1.8M workloads year to date as of September
Use of funds: scale its AI compute trading (OTC options, forwards and backstops on compute-rental indexes) and AI hardware business
Reported by: PR Newswire
