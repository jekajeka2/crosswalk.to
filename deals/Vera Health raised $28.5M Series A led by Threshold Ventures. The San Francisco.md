# Vera Health raised $28.5M Series A led by Threshold Ventures. The San Francisco company makes a clinical AI search engine that searches 60M+ peer-reviewed papers, guidelines and drug labels, grades the strength of the evidence behind each claim, and links every claim to its source.

Other investors: Mantis VC, SV Angel; existing investors Y Combinator and Gradient
Total raised: $32M (the press release headline uses the $32M total; the Series A itself is $28.5M, raised a few months before this announcement)
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Taieb Bennani and Maxime Allouch (co-founders)
Team: 12 employees
Traction: 800,000+ clinicians in 190 countries, about 1 in 4 physicians in Spain and Italy; most downloaded clinical app worldwide every month since June; September downloads exceeded OpenEvidence and Doximity combined; hundreds of thousands of questions a day
Partnership: integrates American College of Emergency Physicians (ACEP) clinical policies
Use of funds: close the gap between medical knowledge and patient care
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
