# Eka Robotics raised $150M Series A co-led by Andreessen Horowitz and F-Prime, at a $1.45B valuation. The Cambridge, Mass. startup builds a vision-force-action AI model for robotic manipulation, with force sensors in a custom sub-$1,000 gripper that let robots adjust their grip mid-task; it picks unseen objects at about 1,500 per hour versus a ~600 human baseline.

Other investors: Obvious Ventures, E14 Fund (seed co-leads)
Total raised: about $161M (seed of $11M in May 2025, per PitchBook)
Valuation: $1.45B (reported)
HQ: Cambridge, MA
Founders: Pulkit Agrawal (MIT professor, leads the Improbable AI Lab) and Tuomas Haarnoja (ex-Google DeepMind); founded 2025
Use of funds: extend the model to more tasks and speed up customer deployments
Traction: >95% pick success on objects up to 1 kg; unnamed industrial pilots in pick-and-place and wire handling; gripper rated for 5M duty cycles (target 20M)
Reported by: SiliconANGLE, Axios Pro Rata, TechCrunch
