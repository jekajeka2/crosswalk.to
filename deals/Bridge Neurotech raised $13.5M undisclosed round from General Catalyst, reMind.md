# Bridge Neurotech raised $13.5M undisclosed round from General Catalyst, reMind Capital, Sunflower Capital and Scott Sandell. The Silicon Valley startup is building a non-invasive brain-computer interface that decodes brain activity through the skull with functional ultrasound imaging, no surgery or implant.

Investors: General Catalyst, reMind Capital, Sunflower Capital, Scott Sandell, and others (no lead named)
HQ: Silicon Valley (city not stated)
Founder: Will Biederman, Ph.D., who developed the neural interface chip used in Neuralink's first implants, spent nearly a decade at Verily and Google, and co-founded Forest Neurotech
Technology: functional ultrasound imaging; the core challenge is the skull, which scatters and absorbs sound differently per person
Traction: first research study underway in Redwood City, CA, enrolling adults with PEEK or Longeviti cranioplasty implants and healthy adults 18+
Use of funds: move the technology into its next stage of research and development
Reported by: Pulse 2.0
