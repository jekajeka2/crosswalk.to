# Tenstorrent raised $1.5B Series E led by Trace Capital and Innovation X, at a $6.2B post-money valuation. Jim Keller's AI hardware company sells RISC-V-based AI processors, Galaxy servers, TT-QuietBox workstations and Blackhole cards with an open-source software stack, and licenses its CPU and AI IP to companies building their own chips.

Other investors: Agostinelli Group, Cadence Ventures, Morgan Stanley, SanDisk, Tether Investments, plus insiders ARK Invest, Brett Barna, Eclipse, Fidelity Management & Research, Maverick Silicon, Protagonist, T. Rowe Price, Troluce Capital, Virtu Financial and XTX Markets
Valuation: $6.2B post-money, more than double its December 2024 round (Tech Startups)
Total raised: $2.5B in equity (AlleyWatch); more than $2.19B across its latest two major rounds (Tech Startups)
HQ: Santa Clara, CA (Axios, Tech Startups, AlleyWatch); SiliconANGLE describes it as Toronto-based
Founders: Ivan Hamer, Ljubisa Bajic and Milos Trajkovic; founded 2016 (AlleyWatch)
CEO: Jim Keller, formerly a chip designer at Intel, AMD, Apple and Tesla
Products: Tensix Neo accelerators and Ascalon CPUs on RISC-V; open-source stack TT-Forge (compiler), TT-Metalium (SDK) and TT-LLK (low-level kernels)
Customers: Virtu Financial runs Galaxy servers for on-premises AI trading agents; Equinix offers them to enterprises
Use of funds: ramp volume manufacturing of Galaxy servers, RISC-V research, global hiring
Context: courted earlier this year by Intel and Qualcomm, but chose to stay independent (Axios)
Reported by: SiliconANGLE, Axios Pro Rata, Tech Startups, AlleyWatch
