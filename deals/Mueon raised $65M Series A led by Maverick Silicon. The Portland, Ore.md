# Mueon raised $65M Series A led by Maverick Silicon. The Portland, Ore. semiconductor systems company builds Cubelets, modular 3D compute-plus-memory blocks that integrate interconnect, power delivery and cooling, and targets up to 10x better power and cost efficiency in the same rack footprint for AI data centers.

Other investors: Intel Capital, Geodesic Alliances Fund, Oregon Venture Fund, M-Ventures, Wellington Capital, IQT, Defined
Total raised: over $80M, including a $15.5M seed in 2025
HQ: Portland, OR
Co-founder and CEO: Wilfred Gomes
Board: Manish Muthal (Maverick Silicon) and Srini Ananth (Intel Capital) join
Use of funds: grow engineering and operations, move Cubelets from platform development to system validation and production readiness, deepen customer engagements
Partners: d-Matrix and Ampere Computing are working with the company
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement), The Information

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mueon-raises-65m-in-series-a-funding-to-scale-its-cubelets-exponential-computing-platform-302909367.html
