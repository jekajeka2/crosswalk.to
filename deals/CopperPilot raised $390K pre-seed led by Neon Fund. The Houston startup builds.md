# CopperPilot raised $390K pre-seed led by Neon Fund. The Houston startup builds Epsilon, an AI agent that turns natural-language prompts into manufacturing-ready circuit board designs.

Other investors: US-based angel investors
HQ: Houston, TX (per FinSMEs; The SaaS News describes the company as based in the United States and India)
Founders: Aditya NG, Mohit Bagur, Anish Harsha; founded 2025
Use of funds: product research, improving the AI agent's reliability, growing the technical team
Reported by: FinSMEs, The SaaS News
