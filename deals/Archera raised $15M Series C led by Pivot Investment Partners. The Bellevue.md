# Archera raised $15M Series C led by Pivot Investment Partners. The Bellevue, Wash. cloud economics company sells insurance-backed "Guaranteed Commitments": cloud and AI discounts with terms as short as 30 days, where Archera covers the cost of unused commitments.

Other investors: existing investors including HighSage Ventures
Total raised: $38.3M
HQ: Bellevue, WA
Co-founder and CEO: Aran Khanna
Use of funds: expand Guaranteed Commitments (larger guarantees, upgraded insurance policies backing them) and its free cloud cost tools, including its FinOps Agent and MCP
Guarantee backed by a surplus lines policy from Crum & Forster Specialty Insurance Company
Traction: manages about $5.6B in annual cloud and AI spend; more than $700M in savings delivered to over 2,200 organizations, including PubNub, Art.com and Astronomer; operating profitably
Works with AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud billing data in read-only mode
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
