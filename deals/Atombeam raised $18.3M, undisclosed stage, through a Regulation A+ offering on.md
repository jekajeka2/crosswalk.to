# Atombeam raised $18.3M, undisclosed stage, through a Regulation A+ offering on StartEngine, bringing total funding to more than $55M. The Moraga, Calif. AI company sells Neurpac, software that shrinks and encrypts machine data in one pass (typically removing 50-90% of data to be moved), and is developing the Persistent Cognitive Machine, a continuously learning AI architecture.

Total raised: more than $55M
HQ: Moraga, CA
Founders: Charles Yeomans, co-founder, chairman and CEO
Use of funds: speed up Neurpac adoption and bring the Persistent Cognitive Machine through independent benchmarking (planned for November) and into customer pilots
Traction: Neurpac is in commercial production, with results measured on satellite, defense and commercial links, including independent validation on a major satellite operator's traffic; patent estate of 678 filings (308 issued or allowed)
Reported by: Atombeam (PR Newswire)
