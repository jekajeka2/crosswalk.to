# Sonilo raised $11M undisclosed-stage round led by B Capital. The San Francisco generative audio startup, founded by former TikTok AI and music executives, builds a multimodal 'Sound World Model' that analyzes video frame by frame to generate matching music and sound effects for creators, production teams and AI video developers.

Other investors: Redpoint
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: CEO Shawn Song, CTO Alex Yin, COO Keli Li, CMO Trista Taylor (former TikTok AI and music division executives)
Use of funds: compute to scale its Sound World Model; expansion across creator and developer platforms; growing its music licensing business
Funding date: October 8, 2026
Reported by: The SaaS News (citing Yahoo Finance)

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/sonilo-raises-11m-other
