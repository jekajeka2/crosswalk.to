# Doxx.net raised $38M Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz. Doxx.net (doxxnet) runs a parallel private network for people and their AI agents: encrypted device/agent meshes, peer-to-peer calls, messages and file transfer, its own backbone, IP space, naming system and certificate authority, with agents configuring firewall rules under user policies ("Agentic Defined Networking").

- Other investors: Animo Ventures, Focal.vc
- HQ: Miami, FL
- Founded: about 2025 (StrictlyVC: one year old)
- Founder: Barrett Lyon, previously founded Prolexic (acquired by Akamai), BitGravity (Tata Communications) and Defense.net (F5)
- Product: private browsing, direct file transfer, encrypted meshes of devices and agents, non-Internet domains resolving inside its network, DNS-level threat blocking; sign-up needs no email or phone number
- Reported by: StrictlyVC, Refresh Miami, a16z
