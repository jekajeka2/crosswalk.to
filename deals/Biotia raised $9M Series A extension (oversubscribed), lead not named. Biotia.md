# Biotia raised $9M Series A extension (oversubscribed), lead not named. Biotia is a clinical metagenomics company. Its BIOTIA-ID Urine Test finds the cause of recurrent and complicated UTIs with 97% sensitivity and 99% specificity, and is available in all 50 states.

- Investors: VillageMD co-founders Tim Barry and Dr. Clive Fields, Convergent Ventures, DigitalDx Ventures, Cloquet Capital Partners, Continuum Health Ventures, I-Lab Angels, EGB Capital, Leawood Venture Capital, Red Bear Angels, Red Bear Ventures
- Stage: extension of its Series A, per CEO Niamh O'Hara (AlleyWatch); the press release describes it as exceeding the previously announced Series A
- Total raised: $19.4M in reported equity funding (AlleyWatch)
- HQ: New York, NY; runs its own CLIA-certified, New York State–approved lab in Long Island City; spinout of the Jacobs Technion-Cornell Institute at Cornell Tech
- Founded in 2016 by Niamh O'Hara, PhD (CEO), Chris Mason, PhD (global director) and Rachid Ounit
- Product: the urine test detects 44 urogenital pathogens and 16 drug-resistance markers from one sample, with results in 2-3 business days; in a study of 200+ culture-negative samples it found a pathogen in 63%
- Distribution: at-home test-to-treat via telehealth partner Clinova Solutions, or ordered by patients' own clinicians
- Use of funds: scale the NYC lab, launch more women's health diagnostics (STI and vaginal health panel), expand into orthopedic diagnostics (a joint-fluid test with Hospital for Special Surgery), pursue reimbursement
- Partners: Mayo Clinic, Hospital for Special Surgery, NASA
- Board expanded alongside the round
- Reported by: PR Newswire, AlleyWatch
