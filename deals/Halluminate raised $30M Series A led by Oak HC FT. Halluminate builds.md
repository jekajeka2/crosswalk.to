# Halluminate raised $30M Series A led by Oak HC/FT. Halluminate builds benchmarking and reinforcement-learning environments that simulate financial work such as private equity and banking, so frontier AI developers can train models on complex finance tasks.

- Other investors: Y Combinator, Orange Collective, Heavybit (existing), plus individual researchers from Anthropic, OpenAI and Meta
- Total raised: $38.5M
- HQ: San Francisco, CA
- Founders: Jerry Wu (CEO) and Wyatt Marshall (CTO), founded 2024
- Use of funds: build out its verticalized data research labs and make its simulated training environments more complex, to keep up with frontier model developers
- Reported by: The SaaS News, Fortune
