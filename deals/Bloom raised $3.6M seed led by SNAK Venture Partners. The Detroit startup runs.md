# Bloom raised $3.6M seed led by SNAK Venture Partners. The Detroit startup runs an AI-driven marketplace that matches hardware companies (robotics, drones) with US-based manufacturers, engineers and logistics providers, handling quoting, booking and payments.

Other investors: Flyover Capital, Mana Ventures, Detroit Venture Partners, Invest Detroit Ventures, Michigan Outdoor Innovation Fund
HQ: Detroit, MI
Founded: 2023 by CEO Justin Kosmides
Use of funds: expand the marketplace and build out its supplier-matching capabilities for domestic US manufacturing
Reported by: The SaaS News, Analytics Insight

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/bloom-raises-3-6m-seed
