# Guardare raised $5.6M seed extension led by Album. The Clearwater, FL cybersecurity startup pulls data from a company's security tools and uses AI to rank vulnerabilities by business impact, for IT teams without dedicated security staff. Total equity raised is now $10.7M, ahead of a planned Series A.

Other investors: Ten Eleven Ventures, Tampa Bay Ventures, DeepWork Capital, Florida Opportunity Fund
Total raised: $10.7M in equity
HQ: Clearwater, FL
Founded: 2024; co-founder and CEO Lars Letonoff
Use of funds: scale sales and marketing, hire in sales and software development, more PR spending
Reported by: The SaaS News, Tampa Bay Business & Wealth

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/guardare-raises-5-6m-seed-extension
