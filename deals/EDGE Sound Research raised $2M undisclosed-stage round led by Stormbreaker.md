# EDGE Sound Research raised $2M undisclosed-stage round led by Stormbreaker Ventures. The Riverside, Calif. company builds perception infrastructure that isolates sounds from noisy environments and fuses them with video, tracking, timing and location data, deployed today in live sports and extending to physical AI and robotics.

Total raised: $9M
HQ: Riverside, CA
Founded 2020 out of doctoral research at UC Riverside by Valtteri Salomaki (CEO) and Dr. Ethan Castro
Use of funds: more deployments with live sports broadcasters, leagues and venues; expanding its multimodal sensor data; new applications in physical AI, robotics, autonomous systems and intelligent environments
Customers and partners: Shure, the NBA, Fox Sports, the United States Tennis Association
Product: Virtual Sound Engine, deployed commercially in live sports
Reported by: PR Newswire; SiliconANGLE
