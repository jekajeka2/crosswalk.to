# Sunlight AI raised $17.4M seed led by Basis Set Ventures. The New York and Miami startup launched a patient-to-payment operating layer for independent medical practices, combining practice-management infrastructure, human staff and AI so physicians can stay independent of hospital systems, and is starting by acquiring Bradford Medical Associates.

Other investors: Sofeon Ventures, Social Leverage, Actions Capital, Overlook Ventures, Early Light Ventures; angels including Chris Hubbell, Tilli Bannett, Dara Rahimzadeh, Frank Williams; other healthcare investors and family offices
HQ: New York, NY (also Miami)
Founded: 2026 by CEO Sina Chehrazi
Use of funds: grow the operating platform and acquire service providers that support independent physicians, starting with Bradford Medical Associates
Reported by: The SaaS News
