# Watchtower Labs raised $35M seed co-led by Shield Capital and Conviction. The Seattle defense startup, incubated at Shield Capital and emerging from stealth, builds AI-native decision tools that combine LLMs, simulation and optimization to help military planners model hundreds of possible futures and pick executable courses of action, starting with logistics and sustainment planning.

Other investors: Founders Fund; In-Q-Tel (IQT), per Upstarts (the press release names only Founders Fund)
HQ: Seattle, WA; team of nearly 30 across the Bay Area, Hawaii, Seattle and Washington, D.C.
Founders: CEO Akhil Iyer (former Marine special operations officer and Shield Capital investor), Souren Aghajanyan and John Morman (former Microsoft engineering leaders); Shield Capital founder Raj Shah is chairman
Origin: idea came from a 2025 meeting with U.S. Indo-Pacific Command's Adm. Samuel Paparo, who flagged logistics planning still run in Excel spreadsheets
Traction: selected in Jan 2026 by the Defense Innovation Unit under the Joint Sustainment Decision Tool program; partnered with the Naval Postgraduate School in 2025; several prototype contracts with major operational commands, per Upstarts
Use of funds: team growth, more applications on its core platform, and testing and deployment beyond sustainment into broader operational planning
Reported by: PR Newswire, Upstarts
