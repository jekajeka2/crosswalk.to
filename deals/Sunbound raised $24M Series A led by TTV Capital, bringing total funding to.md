# Sunbound raised $24M Series A led by TTV Capital, bringing total funding to $36M. The Phoenix company automates admissions, billing, payments and revenue-cycle management for senior living and post-acute care providers, and alongside the round acquired billing firm Richter Healthcare Consultants.

Other investors: Bling Capital, Fika Ventures, Live Oak Bank, Ziegler Linkage Fund
Total raised: $36M
HQ: Phoenix, AZ (per Axios Pro; StrictlyVC calls it a Washington startup)
Founded: about five years ago
Acquisition: bought billing firm Richter Healthcare Consultants
Reported by: citybiz, Axios Pro Rata (per Axios Pro), StrictlyVC
