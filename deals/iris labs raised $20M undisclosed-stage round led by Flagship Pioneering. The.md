# iris labs raised $20M undisclosed-stage round led by Flagship Pioneering. The Cambridge, Mass. Flagship-founded company launched iris chat, an AI "thought partner" on iMessage and RCS that learns a user's experiences and goals to help with relationships, sense of self and resilience.

Funding: an initial commitment of $20M from Flagship Pioneering at launch
HQ: Cambridge, MA
Founded: within Flagship Labs; conceived more than two years ago
Founders: CEO Armen Mkrtchyan, Ph.D. (Flagship Origination Partner); President Rockwell Shah (Flagship Partner); Noubar Afeyan, Ph.D. (Flagship founder and CEO); Ayşe Muñiz, Ph.D. (Flagship Principal)
Product: iris chat pairs expert-informed guidance with AI judges (agents that evaluate other agents' outputs), plus a parallel safety agent that assesses each conversation in real time
Traction: company cites strong early repeat engagement; no figures disclosed
Reported by: PR Newswire (company press release)

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/flagship-pioneering-launches-iris-labs-to-transform-human-wellbeing-through-ai-302898865.html
