# North.cloud raised $60M Series B led by PeakSpan Capital. The New York startup automates spend across cloud, AI and data for finance and engineering teams, covering AWS, Google Cloud and Azure plus OpenAI, Anthropic and Snowflake, with TokenFlow for AI model usage, budgets and controls.

Other investors: Litquidity Ventures, Companyon Ventures, Alpaca, Uncommon Denominator, Gaingels, Mana Ventures
HQ: New York, NY (StrictlyVC says Brooklyn); legal name North Cloud Holdings Inc.; about three years old per StrictlyVC
Co-founder and CEO: Matt Biringer
Use of funds: expand autonomous optimization across cloud providers, deepen integrations, accelerate AI spend management, scale engineering, product and go-to-market
Traction: nearly $2B in managed cloud spend; customers saved $400M+; 98.8% retention; customers include AssemblyAI, Atlan, Tines, SnapLogic, Emplifi and Imply
Product: North v3 added Azure, completing coverage of all three hyperscalers; Autobot automates commitment laddering
Reported by: PR Newswire, SiliconANGLE, StrictlyVC, Axios Pro Rata
