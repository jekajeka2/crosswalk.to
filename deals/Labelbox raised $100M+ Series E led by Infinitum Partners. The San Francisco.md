# Labelbox raised $100M+ Series E led by Infinitum Partners. The San Francisco company sells reinforcement-learning environments and evaluation infrastructure to frontier AI labs (Horizon) and a managed platform for enterprise agent fleets that improve with each run (Recursion Managed Agents).

Round includes equity and a credit facility (split not disclosed)
Other investors: Wipro Ventures, Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), B Capital, Tau Ventures, Snowpoint Ventures and others
HQ: San Francisco, CA
CEO and co-founder: Manu Sharma
Traction: 400+ customers, including more than 90% of leading US AI labs; profitable in 2026 while growing more than 5x year over year
Use of funds: research and engineering on Horizon and Recursion Managed Agents, and growing the teams that sell them
Reported by: PR Newswire

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/labelbox-raises-100-million-in-series-e-funding-to-advance-reinforcement-learning-and-enterprise-ai-agents-302909147.html
