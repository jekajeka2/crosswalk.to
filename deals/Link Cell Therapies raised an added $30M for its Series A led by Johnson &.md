# Link Cell Therapies raised an added $30M for its Series A led by Johnson & Johnson. The South San Francisco biotech develops CAR-T therapies that activate only when they detect specific combinations of markers on cancer cells, and has dosed its first patient.

- Other investors: Bristol Myers Squibb, Kyowa Kirin, Sherpa Healthcare Partners, Western Technology Investment, MD Anderson Cancer Focus Fund; existing investors Samsara BioCapital, Sheatree Capital, Wing Venture Capital
- The extension upsizes the Series A to $90M
- HQ: South San Francisco, Calif.; founded four years ago
- Reported by: Dealroom, StrictlyVC, PitchBook
