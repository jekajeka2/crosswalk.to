# BOB raised $5.5M seed led by Mischief. The Miami fintech builds an AI financial assistant for Main Street service businesses (roofers, restaurants, local services) that keeps books, sends bills, accepts payments and reports cash flow through WhatsApp, text, Slack, Teams, email and its own app, pitched as an employee that offers banking rather than a dashboard.

Other investors: Peter Thiel, Codie Sanchez, Mark Pincus, Karman Ventures, additional investors and operators
HQ: Miami, FL (Sunset Harbor office)
Founders: Adam Wininger (CEO), Jason Brill (CTO), Kody Greenbaum (CPO)
Use of funds: expand product capabilities, scale customer onboarding, hire in Miami
Traction: private beta launched last month; 500 businesses signed up, free to use for now; team of 10, all in Miami
Mischief is the venture firm founded by Plaid co-founder Zach Perret
Reported by: Refresh Miami
