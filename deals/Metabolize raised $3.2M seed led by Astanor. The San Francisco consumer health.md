# Metabolize raised $3.2M seed led by Astanor. The San Francisco consumer health company licenses Stanford Medicine research on naturally occurring metabolites, and its first product, NATLEAN (launching January 2027), aims to help people coming off GLP-1 drugs manage appetite and weight.

Other investors: Maia Ventures, LongGame Venture, SOSV
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founded 2024 by Dr. Morris Laster (CEO) and Gideon Meir; tech licensed exclusively from research led by Stanford's Jonathan Long
Use of funds: commercialize its Precision Metabolite Technology, launch NATLEAN, expand development, validation and manufacturing
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/metabolize-raises-3-2-million-seed-financing-to-accelerate-science-backed-solutions-for-metabolic-health-302909216.html
