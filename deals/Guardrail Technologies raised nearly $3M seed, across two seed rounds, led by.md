Other investors: strategic angels with senior experience at major tech companies
HQ: Park City, UT (FinSMEs); The SaaS News describes it as based in Australia and the US
CEO and founder: T.J. Marlin
Use of funds: expansion into Asia and Europe; hiring across all departments
Reported by: FinSMEs, The SaaS News
