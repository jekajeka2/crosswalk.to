# TriGemX Bio raised $94M undisclosed-stage round co-led by The Column Group, TCGX and Viking Global Investors. TriGemX (formerly Kallyope) is an 11-year-old New York biotech developing an oral drug that blocks a sensory-signaling protein to treat acute migraines.

- Leads: The Column Group, TCGX, Viking Global Investors (co-led, per StrictlyVC; Axios Pro Rata and FinSMEs list them as investors without naming a lead)
- Formerly known as Kallyope (FinSMEs)
- HQ: New York, NY
- Founded: about 2015 (StrictlyVC: 11 years old)
- Product: oral drug that blocks a sensory-signaling protein, for acute migraine treatment
- Reported by: Axios Pro Rata, FinSMEs, StrictlyVC
