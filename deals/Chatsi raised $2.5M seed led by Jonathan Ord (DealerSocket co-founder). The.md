# Chatsi raised $2.5M seed led by Jonathan Ord (DealerSocket co-founder). The Salt Lake City company sells AI sales agents that sit on merchants' websites to guide shoppers through complex catalogs, answer technical product questions and recommend products for B2C, D2C and B2B brands.

HQ: Salt Lake City, UT
Founded: 2024 by Ken Garff
Use of funds: product development and scaling operations to serve more merchants
Reported by: The SaaS News
