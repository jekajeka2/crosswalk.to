# ID Plans raised an undisclosed growth round led by Polaris Growth Fund. The Tampa company sells property intelligence for commercial real estate, using field staff, 3D cameras and drones to profile spaces, plus its ID Cloud property management OS.

Investor: Polaris Growth Fund, the Boston software-focused affiliate of Polaris Partners (strategic investment; amount not disclosed)
HQ: Tampa, FL
Founder: Jeff Landry (CEO); founded 1999
Use of funds: expand across the commercial real estate market and develop new capabilities; company keeps its name and leadership
Advisor: Software Equity Group (financial advisor to ID Plans)
Reported by: PR Newswire
