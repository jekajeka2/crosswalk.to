# Evos raised $9M pre-seed co-led by Uncork Capital and Field Ventures. The New York startup, out of stealth, builds autonomous AI "operators" for small and mid-sized manufacturing, transportation and logistics businesses: they learn how experienced employees make decisions, work inside existing software, and take over a function only after matching the team's decisions; it says it passed $1M ARR four weeks after going live.

Other investors: Crosslink Capital, Utopia Capital Management, Sandwith Ventures, angels
HQ: New York, NY; founded 2026
Product: Agentic Operations Platform, to formally launch soon as Workhorse; customers set autonomy from approval-at-every-step to fully independent; discovery to deployment in about 24 hours
Traction: passed $1M ARR four weeks after going live; at freight brokerage MyFreightWorld an operator audits every LTL carrier invoice against the TMS, making ~3,500 audit decisions at 97% accuracy in its first two months and closing $600K+ of invoice value in the latest five weeks
Use of funds: grow the team and bring operators to more businesses in manufacturing, transportation, logistics and the wider physical economy
Reported by: Pulse 2.0, SiliconANGLE, Axios Pro Rata
