# Collective X Health raised $3.2M pre-seed co-led by Council Capital and Anthemis Female Innovators Lab Fund. The San Francisco B2B platform licenses a cited, research-backed women's health intelligence layer via API to wearables, digital health and performance brands, built on female physiology data.

Other investors: SoGal Ventures, Emerson Collective, Muse Capital, Segal Ventures (round oversubscribed; company emerged from stealth)
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Lindsey Abramo (CEO; ex-POPSUGAR, Group Nine, World of Good Brands), Dr. Stacy Sims (Chief Research Officer; exercise physiologist, ex-Stanford sex-differences research), Angelica Marden (Chief Brand Officer)
Use of funds: deepen research across life stages and conditions, expand its intelligence, scale early enterprise demand, fund new women's health research
Customers: founding customers Human Powered Health and Altra Running; research/data partners include Open Medicine Foundation, WildAI and Eternal; 50+ prospective partners in pipeline
Research Lead Collective of 20+ MDs and PhDs vets the research base; $200K grant program launched with Momentous
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
