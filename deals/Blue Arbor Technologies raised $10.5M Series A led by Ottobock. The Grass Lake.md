# Blue Arbor Technologies raised $10.5M Series A led by Ottobock. The Grass Lake, Mich. company's RESTORE neuromuscular interface connects a patient's peripheral nerves to a robotic prosthetic arm for independent, simultaneous control of fingers, wrist and elbow.

Lead: Ottobock, a prosthetics and bionics maker, investing as a strategic partner
HQ: Grass Lake, MI
Use of funds: complete feasibility studies, pursue an FDA Investigational Device Exemption for U.S. clinical studies of the external sensing unit, and start developing a fully implantable internal sensing unit
Traction: two human implantations to date, including a second-in-human procedure at the Medical University of Vienna; FDA Breakthrough Device Designation; selected for an NIH UG3-funded University of Michigan study comparing surface and implanted electrodes
CEO: Tod Borton
Reported by: Pulse 2.0
