# Emerald Nuclear raised a $2.1M grant from The Research Council of Norway. The Seattle startup is developing 5 MW high-temperature gas microreactors using meltdown-resistant TRISO fuel to power offshore ships and floating power plants, leased rather than sold.

Funding: grant supporting Norway's Marine Nuclear Propulsion System Pilot project, led by Emerald Nuclear's Norwegian subsidiary
Prior funding: undisclosed amount from angel investors
HQ: Seattle, WA
Founders: Wesley Deason (CEO, ex-Ultra Safe Nuclear Corp.) and Hallgeir Juliebø (CCO); founded 2024
Team: 5 employees, most in Norway
Use of funds: validate integration with marine partners' power distribution and control systems; build a non-nuclear thermal prototype at its Norwegian engineering center
Partners: VARD Group, Vard Electro, Island Offshore, NTNU, Imperial College London, Maskindynamikk; Peregrine Turbine Technologies for power conversion
Milestones: first Gem Microreactor at NTNU targeted for 2030; first commercial deployment on a Norwegian offshore vessel targeted for 2031; still needs more financing, regulatory approvals and fuel supply
Reported by: GeekWire
