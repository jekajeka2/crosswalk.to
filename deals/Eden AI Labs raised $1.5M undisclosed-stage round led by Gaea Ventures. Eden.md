# Eden AI Labs raised $1.5M undisclosed-stage round led by Gaea Ventures. Eden builds a personal AI agent that works across apps and the web to book flights, reserve restaurants, order food and pay bills, free to users and earning a small fee on agent-made card purchases.

Other investors: Becker Ventures, GTM Ventures, family offices and angels
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Team: prior experience at Google, Goldman Sachs, Replit, Paxos and Aptos
Use of funds: expand the team, scale compute capacity, build partnerships with payment providers
Traction: more than 13,000 early-access signups; public beta opening by invitation
Reported by: GlobeNewswire (sponsored release)
