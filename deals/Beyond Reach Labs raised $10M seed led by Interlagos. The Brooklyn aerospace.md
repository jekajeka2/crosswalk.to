# Beyond Reach Labs raised $10M seed led by Interlagos. The Brooklyn aerospace startup builds Flarewing, a deployable space solar array that folds to dining-table size for launch and unfolds to football-field length in orbit, claiming up to 10x more power per launch than rigid panels.

Other investors: Y Combinator, Augur, TerraForge, Off Piste
Total raised: more than $11M
HQ: Brooklyn, NY (new 16,000 sq ft facility at Industry City)
Cofounder and CEO: Mitchell Fogelson; cofounder Pele previously developed parachute systems for SpaceX's Dragon capsule
Product: three configurations up to 200 kW; first unit to be ground-qualified by end of 2026; target of 10+ units a day by end of 2027
Market: satellites and orbital data centers; ~100,000 satellites expected by 2030
Reported by: AlleyWatch
