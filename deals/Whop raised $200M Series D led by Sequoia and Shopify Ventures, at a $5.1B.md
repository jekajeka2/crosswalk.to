# Whop raised $200M Series D led by Sequoia and Shopify Ventures, at a $5.1B valuation. The Palo Alto company gives business operators, from startup product heads to teenage first-time entrepreneurs, a gamified interface and API for distribution, business creation and payments; about 25,000 operators generate $550M a month on it.

Second raise of 2026, after a $200M strategic investment from Tether at a $1.6B valuation in February
Total raised: $478M in equity (AlleyWatch)
HQ: Palo Alto, CA, with offices in New York (AlleyWatch and Crunchbase News describe it as Brooklyn/New York-based)
Founders: Steven Schwartz (co-founder and CEO), Cameron Zoub and Jack Sharkey; founded 2021 (AlleyWatch)
Traction: ~25,000 business operators generate $550M on Whop each month; more than 50,000 other people earn income providing services to those businesses; $7B+ in on-platform transactions to people in 185 countries
Sequoia's Doug Leone: "about a half billion revenue"
Use of funds: global expansion and R&D to make payment and advertising markets more efficient; hiring across engineering, product, sales and operations
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement), AlleyWatch, Crunchbase News
