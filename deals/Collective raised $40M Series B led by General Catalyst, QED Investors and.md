# Collective raised $40M Series B led by General Catalyst, QED Investors and Gradient Ventures, plus a $60M growth facility from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund ($100M total). The San Francisco company is an AI-native financial operating system for solo businesses, combining bookkeeping, payroll, tax and financial services in one platform.

Other investors: Intuit Ventures, FT Partners, SemperVirens
Total package: $100M ($40M Series B equity + $60M growth facility from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund for customer acquisition)
Earlier backers: Expa, Sound Ventures, The General Partnership, Better Tomorrow Ventures; angels include Nikesh Arora, Ashton Kutcher, Elad Gil, Dylan Field, Garrett Camp, Aaron Levie, Gokul Rajaram
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Co-founder and CEO: Hooman Radfar
Use of funds: building the agentic financial OS, customer acquisition, product development in banking and insurance
Traction: ARR growing 120% year over year; estimated $350M in taxes saved for members, up to $40,000 a year for its highest earners
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
