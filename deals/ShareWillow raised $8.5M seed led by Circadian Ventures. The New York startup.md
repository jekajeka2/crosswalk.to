# ShareWillow raised $8.5M seed led by Circadian Ventures. The New York startup automates performance-pay calculations and shows home-service technicians what each job earned them, to help trades businesses keep their best techs.

Other investors: Lerer Hippeau, Twelve Below, Notation Capital, Charge Ventures, Expa, Scott Belsky
Lead: Circadian Ventures per Axios Pro; StrictlyVC lists investors without naming a lead
SEC Form D (filed 2026-10-30, as ShareWillow Compensation Inc.): $4.7M sold of a $6.2M offering in equity and SAFE conversions, 17 investors so far, first sale 2026-10-13, $1.5M remaining
Officers and directors per the filing: Ryan Shank (CEO, Secretary, CFO, Director); Taylor Greene (Director); Michael Dowdle (Director)
HQ: New York, NY
Founded: 2023 (incorporated per the Form D); Founder and CEO Ryan Shank
Reported by: Unite.AI, Axios Pro Rata, StrictlyVC, AlleyWatch, SEC Form D
