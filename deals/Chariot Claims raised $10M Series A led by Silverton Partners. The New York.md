# Chariot Claims raised $10M Series A led by Silverton Partners. The New York legal-tech startup runs mass-arbitration operations for partner law firms, finding cases over privacy violations, hidden fees and hard-to-cancel subscriptions, then signing up and vetting affected consumers.

Other investors: Indicator Ventures, Lightbank, Neon Fund, ZVC, Rosecliff Ventures, Alumni Ventures, FJ Labs, Graph Ventures, Vermilion Ventures
Total raised: $15.7M, including a $3.6M seed in April 2025
HQ: New York, NY
Founder and CEO: Zim Hang; started about three years ago
Business model: charges partner law firms flat service fees; it is not a law firm and files no claims itself
Traction: 200K+ claim submissions a month, about 80 cases since its model shift a year ago; ~15 employees, breaking even
Use of funds: hiring and product, including AI tools for case research and claimant eligibility review
Reported by: Crunchbase News
