# Ambiki raised an undisclosed growth round led by existing investor Green Hills Partners. The Nashville company sells integrated EMR and practice-management software (intake, scheduling, clinical documentation, billing) for pediatric speech, occupational and physical therapy practices.

Round: additional equity growth investment from existing investor Green Hills Partners; amount undisclosed
HQ: Nashville, TN
Founder: Kevin Dias
Use of funds: develop its EMR and practice-management platform, expand its Reconsil automated double-entry ledger, scale managed billing services, improve data migration and implementation support, and keep building Tenalog, its AI clinical documentation tool
Funding date: October 23, 2026
Reported by: The SaaS News, AccessNewswire

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/ambiki-secures-investment
