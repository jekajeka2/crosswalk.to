# Supabase raised $150M undisclosed-stage round led by GIC. Supabase is an open-source Postgres backend platform (database, auth, storage, edge functions, realtime, vector search) now positioned as infrastructure for AI agents; it is also acquiring Turso, which provisions low-cost per-agent databases.

- Other investors: CapitalG (Alphabet's independent growth fund), IronArc, SquarePeg
- Comes four months after Supabase's $500M Series F
- Round also provides liquidity for employees
- Acquisition: Turso; founders Glauber Costa (joins as Head of Agentic Services) and Pekka Enberg join with the team; Turso keeps operating. Turso customers include Superhuman, Sauna.ai, CTO.new, Mastra
- Traction: 13M+ developers; adding 1M+ users and 4M databases per month; 70% of new databases are created by agents or AI tools; 600% YoY database growth reported in June
- HQ: San Francisco, CA (release dateline)
- Founded 2020; co-founder and CEO Paul Copplestone
- Use of funds: product development around agent-driven databases
- Reported by: PR Newswire, Axios Pro Rata
