# Kodama Systems raised $7M undisclosed-stage round co-led by Konvoy Ventures and Breakwater Ventures. The Sonora, Calif. company retrofits forestry and logging machines (starting with skidders) for teleoperation plus supervised autonomy, integrated with OEMs, and runs on commercial plantation-timber sites in the southeastern US.

Other investors: Blue Forest Asset Management, NYA Ventures, Breakthrough Energy Ventures, Congruent Ventures, Dangerous Ventures, M1C Ventures, BTG Pactual
Total raised: $14M
HQ: Sonora, CA
Co-founder and CEO: Merritt Jenkins
Use of funds: commercial deployments, deeper OEM partnerships, supervised autonomy for complex off-road environments
Traction: machines with its system moved 100,000+ tons of timber across multiple states in the past six months; running 10-14 hours a day on work sites; integrated with two of the three largest forestry equipment makers; about a terabyte of training data per day of remote operation
Focus shifted from wildfire risk reduction in the western US to southeastern timber; next targets are cutters and loaders
Reported by: The Robot Report
