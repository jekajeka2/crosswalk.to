# Upwind raised $400M Series C led by Bessemer Venture Partners, at a $3.8B valuation. The San Francisco and Tel Aviv company's runtime platform monitors cloud workloads, APIs and AI agents to prioritize vulnerabilities and detect attacks, with built-in AI agents that investigate and fix alerts.

Other investors: Canapi Ventures (new); returning Cyberstarts, Greylock Partners, Craft Ventures, Leaders Fund, Salesforce Ventures, TCV, Alta Park Capital, Penny Jar Capital, Cerca Partners, Swish Ventures, Picture Capital
Valuation: $3.8B (Series B in January: $250M led by Bessemer at $1.5B)
Total raised: about $830M
Round grew from a reported $300M in early September
HQ: San Francisco, CA and Tel Aviv (Axios describes it as an Israeli company)
Founders: Amiram Shachar (co-founder and CEO) with former colleagues from Spot; founded 2022
Traction: revenue quadrupled so far in 2026; hundreds of customers including Siemens, Roku and Carvana; sold through AWS Security Hub and AWS billing
Use of funds: product development and a broader go-to-market push
Reportedly turned down a $4B takeover offer from Datadog days before the announcement
Reported by: TechCrunch, SiliconANGLE, StrictlyVC, Axios Pro Rata, Pulse 2.0
