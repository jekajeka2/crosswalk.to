# Daytona raised $60M Series B led by FirstMark, at a $1B valuation, bringing total funding to $91M. The three-year-old San Francisco company runs an agent cloud that spins up secure, elastic computers for AI agents to run code, use software and complete tasks.

Other investors: Qualcomm Ventures, Samsung Next, Okta Ventures (strategic), Alumni Ventures, Evergreen Fund, Torch Capital; angels Patrick Collison, Adam D'Angelo, Brendan Foody, Tobi Lütke, Olivier Pomel, Aravind Srinivas, Eric Yuan
Total raised: $91M
Valuation: $1B
HQ: San Francisco, CA (Axios Pro Rata and AlleyWatch describe it as NYC-based)
Founded: about three years ago
Reported by: FinSMEs, PitchBook News, StrictlyVC, Tech Startups, Axios Pro Rata
