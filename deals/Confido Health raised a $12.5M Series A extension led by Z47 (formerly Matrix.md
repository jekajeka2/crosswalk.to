# Confido Health raised a $12.5M Series A extension led by Z47 (formerly Matrix Partners India), bringing its Series A to $22.5M. The New York company sells CareOS, AI agents that run healthcare practice operations (intake, referrals, scheduling, refills, payments), mainly to PE-backed multi-site provider groups.

Total Series A: $22.5M
Total raised: $25.5M in equity (AlleyWatch)
Other investors: Justin Saks (healthcare PE veteran, joins as investor and board advisor); existing investors Together Fund and Blume Ventures
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Chetan Reddy (CEO) and Vichar Shroff; founded 2022
Use of funds: expand CareOS, its agentic AI operations platform, across multi-site healthcare operations
Traction: 15x growth since its last round; 95%+ AI handle rates on core office use cases; integrated with 50+ systems of record (EHRs, practice management, phone, billing, fax); specialties include ophthalmology/optometry, physical therapy, dental and primary/community care
Distribution: sells to private equity investors first, then expands into their portfolio companies
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement), StrictlyVC, AlleyWatch

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/confido-health-expands-agentic-operating-platform-to-move-healthcare-from-systems-of-record-to-systems-of-action-302908090.html
