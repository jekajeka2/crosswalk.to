# Firefly raised $20M Series A led by Definition Capital. The New York startup came out of stealth with Ember, a physical-AI safety platform that pulls signals from existing cameras, radios and sensors plus its own hardware (wearable panic buttons, gunshot-detecting AI beacons) to detect incidents and coordinate responses for schools, hospitals and enterprise campuses.

Other investors: BoxGroup (its third investment in Firefly), Susa Ventures (led the seed round earlier this year)
HQ: New York, NY (per Axios Pro Rata)
Founders: James Batt Buckley (CEO; previously scaled Verkada's Alarms business) and Alex Penn (MIT; nearly a decade leading engineering teams at Apple); founded 2024
Use of funds: build more physical AI products, grow engineering and machine learning teams, build out go-to-market, expand in education, healthcare and enterprise
Traction: deployed at Front Range Community College, Colorado's largest community college, across three campuses
Reported by: SiliconANGLE, Pulse 2.0, Axios Pro Rata
