# CarbonAI raised $12M undisclosed-stage round led by Cowboy Ventures. The New York startup sells AI agents for commercial real estate lenders that extract data from rent rolls, leases and financials, build underwriting, draft investment memos and monitor loans after close, with every output linked to its source document.

Other investors: Timber Grove Ventures, Bling Capital, Max Ventures
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Jeff Saul (CEO) and Josh Kaufman, ex-CRE lenders at Starwood Capital, Morgan Stanley and Guggenheim; they previously founded Nativ, a CRE deal management platform acquired by Built
Traction: first product launched early 2025; seven figures of contractual revenue within six months; customers include debt funds, mortgage REITs, agency multifamily lenders and public financial institutions, among them Greystone; says some underwriting tasks (lease review, financial statement analysis, loan onboarding) are up to 90% faster
Use of funds: expand its agentic AI platform for CRE lending
Reported by: Pulse 2.0, Axios Pro Rata
