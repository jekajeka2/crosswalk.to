# Homeward raised $120M Series D led by Saluda Grade, plus a separate $330M asset-backed debt facility. Austin-based Homeward works through real estate agents to offer cash offers and bridge financing (Buy Before You Sell, Sell Before You List, Buy With Cash) so homeowners can buy before selling.

- Other investors: Continental General Insurance Company, Citi Ventures, Magnetar, Harmony Partners, Norwest, Adams Street Partners, LiveOak Ventures, Parker89, Era Ventures, Javelin Venture Partners
- Total raised: $360M in equity since its 2018 founding (Crunchbase News)
- Valuation: undisclosed; the company told Crunchbase News it is similar to its 2021 Series C valuation, reported then as "just north of $800M"
- Debt: $330M asset-backed facility to fund more home transactions (separate from the equity round)
- HQ: Austin, TX
- Founder and CEO: Tim Heyl; founded 2018
- Use of funds: expand cash offer and bridge financing, invest in the platform; Buy Before You Sell to go nationwide by year-end (cash offers already across the 48 contiguous states)
- Traction: 25,000+ partner agents and $4B+ in transactions; revenue more than quadrupled since 2021 per the CEO, after a 2023 pivot to Sell Before You List
- Reported by: PR Newswire, Crunchbase News
