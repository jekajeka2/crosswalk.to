# Kalshi raised $1B Series G co-led by Sequoia Capital and Wellington Management, at a $40B post-money valuation (up from $22B in May). The CFTC-regulated prediction market lets retail and institutional traders buy event contracts on sports, crypto, commodities and other outcomes.

Other investors: Baillie Gifford, Andreessen Horowitz, Coatue Management, Morgan Stanley Investment Management
Stage: Series G per StrictlyVC
Valuation: $40B post-money, up from $22B in May; nearly double rival Polymarket's reported $21B
Use of funds: building out its product road map for retail and institutional customers (CEO Tarek Mansour)
Traction: revenue outside sports now over $1B per Mansour; sports is around 80% of volume; crypto growing faster than sports, commodities faster than crypto
HQ: New York, NY; founded about eight years ago
Founder and CEO: Tarek Mansour
IPO: widely expected to be its last private round; Mansour said "there's been discussions" about a public offering
Risk: more than a dozen states argue in court that prediction markets fall under state gambling laws
Reported by: The Information, DealBook (The New York Times), StrictlyVC

Source: https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/kalshi-raises-1-billion-40-billion-valuation
