# Coherence Neuro raised $45M seed led by Founders Fund and the OpenAI Startup Fund. The four-year-old San Francisco startup develops brain implants and AI models that detect electrical signals associated with brain tumors, and aims eventually to treat them with stimulation.

Stage: StrictlyVC calls it a seed round; Axios Pro Rata reports Series A
Leads: Axios names Founders Fund and the OpenAI Startup Fund as leads; StrictlyVC lists them as investors
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founded: about four years ago
Reported by: StrictlyVC, Axios Pro Rata, Dealroom
