# Longeye raised $20M Series A led by N47. The San Francisco startup sells an AI investigative platform that lets police and justice agencies search and analyze jail calls, phone extractions, warrant returns, video and financial records, with a citation engine tying every finding back to the source evidence; 50+ agencies use it.

Other investors: Andreessen Horowitz (American Dynamism)
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founded: 2025; co-founder and CEO Guillaume Delépine
Traction: 2,300+ investigations, including 230+ homicide and 160+ sexual violence cases; 15M+ document pages, 10M+ images, 32,000+ hours of audio/video and 1B+ rows of financial and phone records reviewed, ~200,000 investigator-hours by its estimate
Compliance: says it meets FBI CJIS Security Policy requirements; agencies keep ownership of their data; patents filed on its citation engine
Use of funds: expand adoption across local, state and federal justice agencies and grow its Ambassador Team (led by former San Ramon police chief Denton Carlson)
Also: grant program gives eligible public defender offices and defense nonprofits free access
Reported by: Pulse 2.0
