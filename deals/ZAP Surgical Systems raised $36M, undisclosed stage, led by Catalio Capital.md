# ZAP Surgical Systems raised $36M, undisclosed stage, led by Catalio Capital Management. The San Carlos, Calif. company makes ZAP-X, a vault-free gyroscopic radiosurgery system for non-invasive robotic treatment of brain tumors and other neurological conditions; it recently shipped its 50th system and says nearly 10,000 patients have been treated.

Other investors: continued support from Baheal, ZAP's strategic partner in China
Board: Nicholas von Guionneau, M.D., Ph.D. (Catalio) joins as board observer
HQ: San Carlos, CA
Founded: 2014 by neurosurgeon John R. Adler, M.D. (CEO; Stanford emeritus professor of neurosurgery and radiation oncology)
Use of funds: broaden US and international commercial adoption, expand manufacturing and deployment capacity, continue development across neuroscience applications
Traction: 50th ZAP-X system shipped; nearly 10,000 patients treated worldwide
Pipeline: studying "radiomodulation" (targeted low-dose radiation to influence neurological activity); preliminary first-in-human pilot in treatment-resistant depression, multicenter studies planned for depression and addiction (investigational)
Reported by: Pulse 2.0, FinSMEs, PitchBook News
