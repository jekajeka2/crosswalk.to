# Familiar Therapeutics raised $5M seed led by Pioneer Fund, Icehouse Ventures and BreakTHRU. The Redwood City, Calif. biotech licenses veterinary rights to drugs already researched for human disease and takes them through FDA approval for dogs and cats; its lead, FAM-010, targets canine hemangiosarcoma, which has no approved treatment.

Other investors: angel investors; BreakTHRU is a syndicate of animal health leaders and veterinarians
HQ: Redwood City, CA
Founders: Elizabeth Iorns (CEO) and Dan Knox (COO), co-founders of Science Exchange (YC, acquired 2024); founded May 2026
Pipeline: 13 programs (4 lead, 9 early) across oncology, neurology, cardiology, respiratory and immunology; two INAD files opened with FDA CVM; a provisional patent filed
FAM-010: pilot study at University of Illinois College of Veterinary Medicine in fall 2026, led by Timothy M. Fan
Other leads: intervertebral disc disease, airway disorders in flat-faced breeds, immune-mediated disease
Model: biopharma partners keep human rights and share in veterinary sales
Use of funds: clinical development of lead programs and the wider pipeline
Reported by: Pulse 2.0
