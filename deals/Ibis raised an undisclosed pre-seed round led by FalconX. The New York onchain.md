# Ibis raised an undisclosed pre-seed round led by FalconX. The New York onchain credit platform, out of stealth, originates, tokenizes and services structured credit facilities, starting with crypto trading firms, and launched with a $250M credit facility from FalconX.

Other investors: Anchorage Digital, Innovating Capital, and executives from Erebor, Revolut, Injective Foundation, Agora, Selini, Superstate, Flowdesk, Blockchain.com, DV Chain, Pyth and GenCap Management
Debt: separate $250M credit facility from FalconX providing credit capacity for eligible crypto trading firms
HQ: New York, NY
Founder and CEO: Cooper Emmons
Partners: Anchorage Digital Bank as custody, banking and design partner; Innovating Capital on credit and transaction structuring
Plans: expand beyond crypto into GPUs, robotics and venture capital asset managers
Reported by: PR Newswire, FinSMEs
