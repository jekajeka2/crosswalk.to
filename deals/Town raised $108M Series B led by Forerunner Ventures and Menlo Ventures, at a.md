# Town raised $108M Series B led by Forerunner Ventures and Menlo Ventures, at a $1B post-money valuation. The two-year-old San Francisco startup, founded by former Plaid CTO Jean-Denis Greze, gives each employee an AI assistant ("Townie") that connects to email, calendar and work systems, and Townies can share approved information with each other and work with customers.

Other investors: a16z, First Round Capital, Better Tomorrow Ventures, Precedent.vc, Scribble Ventures, Serena Ventures, Sparked Ventures
Valuation: $1B post-money
Total raised: at least $160M, including a $55M Series A announced in June (TechCrunch)
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founder: Jean-Denis Greze (former Plaid CTO); founded about two years ago
Traction: launched publicly four months ago and says it reached millions of dollars in ARR within weeks
Product news: a shared workflow that automatically updates every employee's Townie with new business information
Reported by: TechCrunch, Axios Pro Rata, DealBook (The New York Times)
