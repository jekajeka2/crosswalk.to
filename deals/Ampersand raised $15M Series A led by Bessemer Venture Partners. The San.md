# Ampersand raised $15M Series A led by Bessemer Venture Partners. The San Francisco startup builds integration infrastructure that lets AI agents read and write inside customers' CRMs, ERPs and other heavily customized systems of record, handling custom fields, objects, permissions and workflows.

Other investors: Matrix and Flex Capital (existing); Yelp, Tenacity Capital, CTO Fund, Mana Ventures and angels (new)
Board: Bessemer partner Lauri Moore joins
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Ayan Barua (CEO; co-founder and CTO of Siftery, acquired by G2, then VP Engineering at G2) and Lauren Long (built Firebase Extensions at Google)
Product: also released in beta Andi, an AI integration agent that does per-customer implementation work
Customers: Hatch, the AI communications platform Yelp acquired in 2026, says Ampersand let it serve customers with complex integration requirements
Reported by: PR Newswire
