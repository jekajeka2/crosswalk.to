# Arceus Legal raised $17M undisclosed round led by Greycroft. Arceus is an AI-driven law firm that does legal work for fast-growing companies at flat prices quoted upfront, with licensed attorneys approving all work produced with its in-house AI system, CounselOS.

- Other investors: Craft Ventures, South Park Commons, others
- HQ: San Francisco, CA
- Founder and CEO: Mac Liu
- Product: CounselOS keeps each client's preferences, past agreements and negotiation history; integrates with Salesforce, HubSpot and Gmail; progress updates via Slack and email
- Pricing: standard contract review listed at $500; free if it takes more than eight hours (most finish in three to five)
- Work so far: mostly commercial contracts (MSAs, NDAs, vendor deals), now adding employment and privacy matters
- Traction: customer base quadrupled in September
- Use of funds: hire attorneys, engineers and AI researchers at the SF headquarters and expand into more practice areas
- Reported by: SiliconANGLE
