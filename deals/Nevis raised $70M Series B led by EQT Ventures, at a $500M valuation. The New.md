# Nevis raised $70M Series B led by EQT Ventures, at a $500M valuation. The New York startup sells an AI operating system for wealth managers whose agents automate advisors' paperwork across CRM, meeting notes and financial-planning workflows.

Other investors: Charles Schwab, QuantumLight (new); Sequoia Capital, ICONIQ, Ribbit Capital (returning)
Total raised: $110M
Valuation: $500M
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: former Revolut executives Mark Swan, Philipp Burda and Ivan Chalov; founded 2024
Use of funds: expand its AI agents, deepen integrations with custodians and wealth-tech systems, scale product and go-to-market teams
Reported by: Fortune, StrictlyVC, The SaaS News, Business Wire
