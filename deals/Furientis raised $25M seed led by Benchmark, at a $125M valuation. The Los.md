# Furientis raised $25M seed led by Benchmark, at a $125M valuation. The Los Angeles defense startup is building a low-cost, 100-mile-range interceptor for homeland defense, designed for mass production (1,000 vehicles a year per factory, under an hour of final assembly each), and has won its first funded Department of War prototyping contract.

Valuation: $125M (per TechCrunch, via Axios Pro Rata)
Board: Benchmark general partner Chetan Puttagunta joins
Government contract: a prototyping Other Transaction Agreement with the Department of War to keep developing the interceptor and demonstrate it against Department requirements; value not disclosed
HQ: Los Angeles, CA
Founders: Brody Franzen (CEO) and Aris Simsarian
Approach: uses commercial off-the-shelf parts where it can, builds its own solid rocket motors and seekers, and flight-tests prototypes about every two weeks
Use of funds: vertically integrated manufacturing, more flight testing, hiring in engineering and manufacturing in Los Angeles
Reported by: Pulse 2.0, TechCrunch, Axios Pro Rata
