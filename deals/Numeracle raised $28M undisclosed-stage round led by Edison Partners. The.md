# Numeracle raised $28M undisclosed-stage round led by Edison Partners. The McLean, Va. company verifies businesses and links their identities to the phone numbers they call from, so carrier call-screening systems stop labeling or blocking legitimate business calls as spam.

Investors: Edison Partners (sole investor in the round)
Total raised: $34.8M since 2018, including a $6.8M Series A in December 2023
Valuation: not disclosed
HQ: McLean, VA
Founder: Rebekah Johnson (founder and CEO), founded 2018
Business model: annual subscription to monitor and remove incorrect spam labels; per-call fee to show business name, logo and reason for calling
Use of funds: hiring, international expansion (starting with Europe), and extending verification beyond calls to messaging, chat apps, marketplaces and AI agents
Traction: about 4,000 business customers, about 85 employees, profitable in 2026; about 90% of sales inquiries arrive through unpaid inbound channels
Reported by: Crunchbase News
