# Minerva Humanoids raised $10M pre-seed led by General Catalyst. The San Francisco company builds Roger, a semi-autonomous humanoid robot that skilled specialists teleoperate via VR headset for dangerous work in onshore and offshore oil and gas and in bomb disposal (EOD) and hazmat response.

Co-leads: Long Journey Ventures, Credo Ventures
Other investors: MVP Ventures, Kaya, RunwayVC, Compound, Founders Embassy, Hugging Face, Codecademy, YG, Walter Ventures, Tiny Ventures, Chaotica, Babylon
Amount: approximately $10M; the round brings the company out of stealth
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: CEO Sandor Felber (previously MIT CSAIL humanoid research, Tesla Optimus) and CTO Maurice Rahme (six years at Boston Dynamics, a technical lead on Stretch)
Product: Minerva Intelligence AI control system handles balance, fall recovery and navigation while translating the remote operator's input; went from idea to a walking humanoid in five months
Manufacturing: software and AI developed mainly in the US; first Roger prototypes built in Germany; plans production in Germany and the US without single-source Chinese components
Traction: first paid pilots launching this fall; showcase and keynote at the NATO EOD Centre of Excellence (Slovakia); on-site testing at one of the world's 10 busiest airports; research partnerships with MBZUAI and the University of Waterloo
Use of funds: advance hardware and AI for energy and EOD applications ahead of deployments later this year
Reported by: GlobeNewswire (company press release)
