# Northstar Security raised $7M seed led by Wing Venture Capital. The San Francisco startup, out of stealth, builds a real-time Security Context Graph across a company's security and IT tools so analysts and AI agents can find toxic combinations of risks (an unprotected laptop owned by a privileged user with payroll access) and remediate them.

Other investors: MVP Ventures, Outliers Venture Capital; angels Gerhard Eschelbeck (former Google CISO), Renaud Deraison, Justin Somaini
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Amer Deeba (CEO; co-founded Normalyze, acquired by Proofpoint in 2024; senior roles at Qualys) and Ed Rowe (CTO; former CTO of Farmers Business Network, former SVP engineering at AppDynamics)
Product: 70+ integrations including AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Defender, Microsoft Entra, Okta, GitHub, Slack, Workday and Zendesk; the graph is exposed to outside AI agents through MCP
Customers: Cequence Security uses its agentic remediation workflows
Use of funds: product development, team expansion, go-to-market
Reported by: PR Newswire, SiliconANGLE
