# Infisical raised $40M Series B led by Elad Gil, at a $600M valuation. The San Francisco company runs open-source security infrastructure for secrets, certificates, encryption keys and privileged access; its Agent Vault lets AI agents reach critical systems without ever holding the underlying credentials.

Other investors: Gradient, Y Combinator, Gallery Ventures, Pace Capital, Dynamic Capital
Valuation: $600M
Total raised: nearly $60M (StrictlyVC)
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Vlad Matsiiako (CEO), Maidul and Tony (co-founders); founded about 2022 (StrictlyVC: four years old)
Use of funds: product development, expanding engineering and go-to-market teams, building out security infrastructure for AI agents
Traction: 1,300+ customers, 300,000 developers, 10B secrets secured daily, 30,000+ GitHub stars; customers include Airbus, Volkswagen, Hugging Face, OpenRouter, Lucid, First American Financial, Wise, Clay
Elad Gil also invested in the Series A
Reported by: PR Newswire, StrictlyVC, Tech Startups
