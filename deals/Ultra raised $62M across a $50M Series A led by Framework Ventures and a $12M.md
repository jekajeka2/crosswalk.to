# Ultra raised $62M across a $50M Series A led by Framework Ventures and a $12M seed led by Y Combinator and NextView. The New York startup builds and deploys warehouse robots that pack e-commerce goods for shipping.

Other investors: M13, Wing Venture Capital
Total raised: $62M
HQ: New York, NY
Founded 2024 by Chetan Parthiban, Jonathan Schwartz, Max Friefeld and Oliver Ortlieb
Reported by: PitchBook, AlleyWatch
