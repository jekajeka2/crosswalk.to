# AIsa raised an undisclosed seed extension from strategic investors Tether and HIVEN, no lead named, bringing total funding to more than $9M. The San Francisco startup builds a resource and transaction network that lets enterprise AI agents reach trusted external resources and execute authorized transactions within defined controls.

Amount: undisclosed (The SaaS News headlines it as $9M, but its own text and FinSMEs give $9M+ as total funding)
New investors: Tether, HIVEN (strategic)
Existing investors: Alibaba, Tribe Capital, Sumitomo Corporation, Draper Associates, Saison Capital
Total raised: more than $9M
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founder and CEO: Jordan Liu
Product: one programmable interface combining resource discovery and gateway services with transaction infrastructure (usage metering, billing, settlement) for enterprise agents
Use of funds: grow its global resource network, strengthen enterprise governance, scale transaction infrastructure
Reported by: FinSMEs, The SaaS News

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/aisa-raises-9m-seed-extension
