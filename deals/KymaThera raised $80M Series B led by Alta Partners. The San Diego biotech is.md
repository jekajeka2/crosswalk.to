# KymaThera raised $80M Series B led by Alta Partners. The San Diego biotech is developing K-1728, an oral pan-mutant selective PI3Kα inhibitor for HR+/HER2- breast cancer and PI3Kα-driven vascular malformations, with Phase 1 dosing expected in Q4 2026.

Other investors: Venrock, Foresite Capital (Series A co-leads), J. Wood Capital (new) and others
Total raised: more than $100M
HQ: San Diego, CA
CEO: Rob Kania, Ph.D.
Prior round: Series A co-led by Foresite Capital and Venrock in 2024
Use of funds: advance K-1728 through initial clinical proof-of-concept
Pipeline: K-1728 targets both kinase- and helical-domain PI3Kα mutations while sparing wild-type PI3Kα; preclinical data showed tumor regressions at low once-daily doses and a wide window before hyperglycemia
Reported by: PR Newswire (company release)
