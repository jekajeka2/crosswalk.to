# Sharp Performance raised $18M Series A led by Hidden Variable. The New York startup matches police officers, firefighters and paramedics with confidential one-on-one coaches who come from public safety or special operations and work independently of the member's department.

Other investors: Andreessen Horowitz (existing), Commonweal Ventures, Wolfson Group, FJ Labs, Ford Foundation
Total raised: $30M
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: CEO Ben Curley and former Green Beret Andrew Sakmar; founded about three years ago
Traction: 110+ police, fire and EMS departments in 18 states offer Sharp (more than doubled in the past year); 16,000+ coaching sessions booked; 60+ vetted coaches including former officers, detectives, firefighters, paramedics, Navy SEALs and Green Berets
Product: matching, shift-aware scheduling, anonymized department reporting, and a mobile app with breathing and physical reset techniques
Use of funds: expand into more departments and states, improve the app and coaching platform, build more first-responder health and performance products
Reported by: Business Wire, Pulse 2.0, StrictlyVC, Axios Pro Rata
