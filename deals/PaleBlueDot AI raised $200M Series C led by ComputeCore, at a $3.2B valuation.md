# PaleBlueDot AI raised $200M Series C led by ComputeCore, at a $3.2B valuation. PaleBlueDot runs AI compute infrastructure: its own GPU clusters customized for large or specialized workloads, a GPU marketplace drawing on global supply partners, and serverless inference.

- Other investors: B Capital (existing shareholder) and other global investors
- Prior round: $150M Series B, announced January 2026
- Valuation: $3.2B
- HQ: Palo Alto, CA
- Founded in 2024; CEO Stephen Watts
- Traction: over $5B in signed customer contracts as of end of September 2026; U.S. and Japanese customers make up more than half of monthly revenue
- Its B300 cluster in Japan earned NVIDIA Exemplar Cloud status
- Use of funds: more compute capacity across locations and hardware; grow full-stack and go-to-market teams, focused on frontier labs, neolabs and U.S. enterprises
- Reported by: PR Newswire
