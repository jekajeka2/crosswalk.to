# Forgepoint raised $9.8M seed co-led by Primary Venture Partners and a16z speedrun. The New York startup sells Frank, an AI agent that works as a manufacturer's inside sales rep, reading RFQs and attached files and pricing custom parts from the shop's own cost models and approval rules.

Other investors: angels from OpenAI, Brex, Vercel and SoFi
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Jack Strauss (co-founder and CEO; ex-Northrop Grumman, Bain, Xwing) and Alex Pareto
Use of funds: bring Frank to more shop floors and extend into other manufacturing back-office work
Traction: customers report 30% higher monthly quote throughput and 55% less time per quote
Reported by: SiliconANGLE, Axios Pro Rata
