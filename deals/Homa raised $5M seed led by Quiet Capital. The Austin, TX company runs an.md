# Homa raised $5M seed led by Quiet Capital. The Austin, TX company runs an AI-powered homebuying platform with its own licensed brokerage that gives buyers commission rebates; it operates in Florida, Texas and California.

Other investors: Restive Ventures, Cambrian Ventures
HQ: Austin, TX
Founders: CEO Arman Javaherian (former senior director at Zillow) and Federico Campeotto (Ph.D. in artificial intelligence)
Use of funds: scale operations in California, build and launch in-house mortgage lending, and release a mobile app later this year
Traction: operates in Florida, Texas and California
Stage: seed per the FinSMEs newsletter and The SaaS News; FinSMEs' article URL calls it pre-seed
Reported by: FinSMEs, The SaaS News, HousingWire

Source: https://www.finsmes.com/2026/10/homa-raises-5m-in-pre-seed-funding.html
