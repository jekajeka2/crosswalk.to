# Broccoli raised $52M Series B led by Khosla Ventures, at a $300M valuation. The San Francisco startup uses AI agents to handle calls, scheduling, follow-ups, membership renewals and customer outreach for plumbing, HVAC, electrical and other home-service businesses.

Other investors: Y Combinator, Operator Partners
Valuation: $300M (The SaaS News)
Round: StrictlyVC reports a $52M Series B; The SaaS News says the $52M spans the company's Series A and Series B
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: AJ Jain and Aditya Ketkar; founded 2024
Use of funds: scale engineering and sales teams and speed up product development
Reported by: StrictlyVC, Fortune, The SaaS News
