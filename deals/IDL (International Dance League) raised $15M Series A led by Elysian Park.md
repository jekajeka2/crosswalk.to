# IDL (International Dance League) raised $15M Series A led by Elysian Park Ventures. The Los Angeles company runs a season-long professional league for competitive dance, with six teams competing in head-to-head matchups and season standings.

Other investors: Sony Group Corporation, Sony Corporation, KB Partners, APEX, Yamaha Music Innovations Fund, October Ventures; Elysian Park also invested in an earlier round
HQ: Los Angeles, CA
CEO, commissioner and co-founder: Connor Lim
Traction: its first season launched in May 2026, and all six events sold out (New York, Vancouver, Sydney, Seoul, Los Angeles). It reports 2 billion social views and 6 million livestream views, a first media rights deal with ESPN2, and partnerships with Honda and e.l.f. Cosmetics
Teams: Brotherhood (Vancouver), Quick Style (Oslo), Jam Republic (Singapore), 1MILLION (Seoul), Royal Family (Auckland), GRV (Los Angeles; first season champion). All six return for the 2027 season
Use of funds: expanded tour operations and larger venues, broadcast and digital production, dancer and team operations, fan engagement
Reported by: Pulse 2.0, FinSMEs
