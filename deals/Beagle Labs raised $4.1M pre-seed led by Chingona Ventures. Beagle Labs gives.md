# Beagle Labs raised $4.1M pre-seed led by Chingona Ventures. Beagle Labs gives commercial property insurance underwriters (carriers and MGAs) interior property and liability data by combining AI analysis with more than 7K field inspectors, and it returns inspections in 14 days or less.

- Other investors: Sovereign's Capital, Remarkable Ventures, C2 Ventures, South Loop Ventures, Red Bike Capital
- Based in New York (covered by AlleyWatch's NYC startup report); founded in 2023 by CEO Dwight Neptune, Luis Guzman, Keelan Gayle and Josie Madingo
- Business model: pay-per-building inspections and a pay-per-use property intelligence platform, with commercial property and E&S lines as the focus
- Reported by: AlleyWatch
