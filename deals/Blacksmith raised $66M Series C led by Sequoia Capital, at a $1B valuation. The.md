# Blacksmith raised $66M Series C led by Sequoia Capital, at a $1B valuation. The San Francisco company runs purpose-built infrastructure for continuous integration (building and testing code), which customers switch to with a one-line workflow change; it says CI jobs finish twice as fast at 60% lower cost on average.

Other investors: Premji Invest; existing investors Peak XV Partners, GV, Y Combinator
Total raised: $125M
Valuation: $1B
HQ: San Francisco, CA (hiring in New York and San Francisco)
Founders: Aditya "JP" Jayaprakash (CEO), Aayush Shah, Aditya Maru (University of Waterloo; Shah and Maru ex-Cockroach Labs, Jayaprakash ex-Faire); founded 2024
Use of funds: grow the team and expand its compute footprint (nearly 10 MW across US and European data centers, on track to grow at least 5x in 2027)
Traction: ARR up 17x since January; weekly CI jobs up from 5M to 90M+; 10,000+ companies including Ashby, Granola, Handshake and LangChain; LangChain says runner costs fell ~40%
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
