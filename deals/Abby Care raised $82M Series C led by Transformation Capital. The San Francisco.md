# Abby Care raised $82M Series C led by Transformation Capital. The San Francisco startup, founded in 2021, trains and pays family members to provide Medicaid-covered home care for their relatives.

Other investors: CVS Health Ventures, Frist Cressey Ventures, Pivotal Ventures; previous investors Khosla Ventures, Thrive Capital, Sequoia Capital
Total raised: $117M in equity (AlleyWatch)
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founder: Havi Nguyen; founded 2021
Reported by: Fortune, StrictlyVC, Axios Pro Rata, AlleyWatch

Source: https://fortune.com/2026/10/15/startup-abby-care-raises-82-million-to-turn-family-members-into-paid-caregivers
