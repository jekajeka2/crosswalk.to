# Multiliquid raised an undisclosed seed round led by Polychain, bringing total funding to $8M. Built by Los Angeles-based Uniform Labs, it is a compliance-first liquidity protocol that lets institutions swap tokenized funds (Treasuries, money market funds, private credit) and stablecoins such as USDC in a single atomic transaction, 24/7.

Other investors: Strobe Ventures, CMT Digital, Metalayer Ventures, Generative Ventures, Circle Ventures
Total raised: $8M
HQ: Los Angeles, CA (developer Uniform Labs)
Founder and CEO: Will Beeson; founding team are veterans of Standard Chartered and UniCredit
Product: supports tokenized funds from Wellington Management, VanEck, Janus Henderson and Fasanara, plus major stablecoins including USDC
Pitch: GENIUS Act bars payment stablecoin issuers from paying yield, so institutions need rails to rotate between yield-bearing tokenized funds and stablecoins without waiting days for subscription and redemption cycles
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
