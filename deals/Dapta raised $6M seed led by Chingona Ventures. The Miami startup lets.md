# Dapta raised $6M seed led by Chingona Ventures. The Miami startup lets nontechnical small-business owners build AI agents in minutes that answer calls, qualify leads and book appointments, mainly for law firms, insurance brokers, mortgage lenders and home services.

Other investors: Crew Capital, Broom Ventures, Cometa VC; existing investors 500 Global and Fen Ventures
Total raised: $7.4M
HQ: founded in Miami, also operating out of Scottsdale, AZ
Founder: Nicolás Rojas (CEO), originally from Colombia; pivoted from no-code APIs to SMB AI agents
Traction: AI agent Dapti has 200,000+ users, adding ~4,000 a week; revenue doubled in six months, 2026 revenue expected above $4M; ~90 South Florida customers; customers include Miami Tech & Arts University, Nona Cleaning, Aether Mechanical, Mercado Libre and Rappi
Pricing: subscriptions from $99/month, free tier
Use of funds: product development (Calendly-like scheduling tool, internet phone system) and expansion across the US and Latin America
Team: ~50 employees and contractors
Reported by: Refresh Miami
