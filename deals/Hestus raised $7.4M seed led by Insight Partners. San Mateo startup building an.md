# Hestus raised $7.4M seed led by Insight Partners. San Mateo startup building an autocomplete-style AI assistant inside CAD tools like Autodesk Fusion that proposes geometry changes and turns mechanical engineers' design intent into manufacturable models.

Other investors: Chalfen Ventures, Rock Yard Ventures, Garage Capital
Total raised: $8.9M
HQ: San Mateo, CA
Founders: Sohrab Haghighat and Kevin Chu (both ex-Cruise Automation)
Use of funds: hire ML engineers and software developers, advance its AI model, speed up the product roadmap
Reported by: The SaaS News, Unite.AI

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/hestus-raises-7-4m-seed
