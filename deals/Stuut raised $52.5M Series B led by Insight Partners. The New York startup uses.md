# Stuut raised $52.5M Series B led by Insight Partners. The New York startup uses AI agents to run enterprises' whole order-to-cash process (collections, cash application, payments, disputes and deductions) on top of their existing ERP, CRM and payment systems.

Other investors: Andreessen Horowitz, M12 (Microsoft's venture fund), Activant
Total raised: $93M; the Series B comes ten months after its Series A
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Tarek Alaruri (CEO), Adam Chaarwari, Ben Winter
Traction: 150+ customers, including Fortune 50 and Fortune 500 companies; customer base up 5x since last year; $3B+ moved through the platform; 81.7% of outbound collections and 95% of incoming payment matching run without humans; customers report up to 40% more cash flow and a 47% cut in DSO
Customers: Verifone, Honeywell (on legacy SAP), ZoomInfo ($21.2M collected, DSO from 51 to 40 days), Bishop Lifting (45 branches, overdue receivables down 35%)
Partners: Fiserv, EY, Altamont, HIG
Use of funds: expand into credit scoring, lending and automated fund movement, plus credit and order management
Amount note: Axios Pro Rata reported $52.2M; the company's release says $52.5M
Reported by: GlobeNewswire (company release), SiliconANGLE, Axios Pro Rata
