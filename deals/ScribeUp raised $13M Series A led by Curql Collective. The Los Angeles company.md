# ScribeUp raised $13M Series A led by Curql Collective. The Los Angeles company sells recurring-bill management infrastructure that plugs into digital banking apps, letting credit unions, fintechs and neobanks help customers find, manage and cancel subscriptions with agentic AI.

Round terms: $10M equity plus a $3M venture debt facility
Other investors: Mucker Capital, Service Ventures
HQ: Los Angeles, CA (per FinSMEs)
Founders: Jordan Mackler and Yohei Oka; founded 2020
Use of funds: hiring, technology development (its agentic AI tracks 200,000+ unique subscription billers), go-to-market
Reported by: The SaaS News, Banking Dive, FinSMEs

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/scribeup-raises-13m-series-a
