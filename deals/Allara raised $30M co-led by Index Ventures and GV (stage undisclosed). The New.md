# Allara raised $30M co-led by Index Ventures and GV (stage undisclosed). The New York virtual, insurance-covered women's health practice pairs women's health clinicians with registered dietitians to treat hormonal, metabolic and reproductive conditions (PCOS/PMOS, thyroid, fertility, perimenopause) in one care team.

Other investors: Camino Partners, Foreground, Family Fund (new investors); Index Ventures and GV are existing investors who increased their commitments
Amount: $30M per the company, AlleyWatch and StrictlyVC; Axios Pro Rata reported $39M
Total raised: $75M
HQ: New York, NY
Founder: Rachel Blank (founder and CEO); company founded about six years ago
Use of funds: extend AI tools across the patient journey, from intake to ongoing treatment management, and build one of the largest datasets in women's health
Traction: more than 120,000 women served; in-network with major insurers in most states; most patients seen same day or within a week; 69% of members with irregular cycles reported improved regularity
Services also include GLP-1 medications, hormone replacement therapy, prescription skincare and hair loss treatment, and virtual urgent care
Reported by: AlleyWatch, StrictlyVC, Fierce Healthcare, Axios Pro Rata, Tech Startups
