# Marklo raised $5M seed led by Forerunner. Founded in 2025 by Stella & Dot founder Jessica Herrin, the Burlingame, Calif. startup pulls Shopify, Klaviyo, Attentive, Meta and Google data together so consumer brands can see what drove past sales, and uses AI to recommend what to promote next and draft campaign plans.

Other investors: Freestyle Capital; angels including former Klaviyo and current Shopify leaders
HQ: Burlingame, CA
Founder and CEO: Jessica Herrin (founded Stella & Dot and WeddingChannel.com); founded 2025
Use of funds: hire engineers, develop the product, expand customer support
Traction: pre-revenue; 100+ brands in pilot, including Away, Clare V., Monica + Andy, Maya Brenner and Latico Leathers; opening to the public ahead of the holiday season
Business model: planned monthly subscription plus usage-based charges; free to try for now
Reported by: Crunchbase News (exclusive)
