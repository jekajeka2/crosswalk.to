# Biotia raised $9M undisclosed-stage round (oversubscribed), lead not named. Biotia is a clinical metagenomics company. Its BIOTIA-ID Urine Test finds the cause of recurrent and complicated UTIs with 97% sensitivity and 99% specificity, and is available in all 50 states.

- Investors: VillageMD co-founders Tim Barry and Dr. Clive Fields, Convergent Ventures, DigitalDx Ventures, Cloquet Capital Partners, Continuum Health Ventures, I-Lab Angels, EGB Capital, Leawood Venture Capital, Red Bear Angels, Red Bear Ventures
- Total raised: $19.4M in reported equity funding (AlleyWatch)
- The round exceeds Biotia's previously announced Series A
- HQ: New York, NY; it runs a CLIA-certified high-complexity lab there and is a spinout of the Jacobs Technion-Cornell Institute at Cornell Tech
- Founded in 2016 by Niamh O'Hara, PhD (CEO), Chris Mason, PhD (global director) and Rachid Ounit
- Use of funds: scale the NYC lab, launch more women's health diagnostics, expand into orthopedic diagnostics, pursue reimbursement
- Partners: Mayo Clinic, Hospital for Special Surgery, NASA
- Board expanded alongside the round
- Reported by: PR Newswire, AlleyWatch
