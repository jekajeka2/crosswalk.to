# Switched Source raised $17M+ undisclosed-stage round led by Cubit Capital. The Chicago company makes Phase-EQ, an automated power-flow control device that balances load across the three phases of utility distribution circuits, freeing capacity for roughly 100-200 more homes per unit without new lines.

Other investors: ABB, Emerald Technology Ventures, Red Cedar Ventures, Southern States, Southwire Company and additional strategic investors
HQ: Chicago, IL
Co-founder and CEO: Charles Murray; developing grid power-flow control technology since 2016
Use of funds: more Phase-EQ deployments across North America; manufacturing, testing, quality and supply chain expansion, with new roles at its Chicago HQ; more utility distribution products
Traction: 150,000+ operating hours across 12 utility service areas; field data shows 10-25% more load-serving capacity; 14-20 weeks from purchase order to site activation
Follows a five-year, $99M project under the DOE's Grid Resilience and Innovation Partnerships Program to deploy Phase-EQ in the Southeast for storm restoration
Reported by: Pulse 2.0
