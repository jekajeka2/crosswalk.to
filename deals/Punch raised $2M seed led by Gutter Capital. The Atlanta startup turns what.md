# Punch raised $2M seed led by Gutter Capital. The Atlanta startup turns what construction superintendents say into its app, photos and forwarded emails into daily reports, follow-up tasks, delay alerts and purchase orders, applying each contractor's own rules alongside its existing estimating and accounting software.

Stage and lead: The SaaS News reports the round as a seed led by Gutter Capital; Pulse 2.0 described Gutter Capital as a returning investor
Other investors: several existing customers and technology-focused angel investors
HQ: Atlanta, GA (Punch Software Inc.)
Founder: Taylor Sandusky, a former construction project manager; founded 2025
Use of funds: expand engineering and customer success teams, keep building the platform to support a broader set of contractor workflows
Traction: Piedmont Drywall's general superintendent says daily reports fell from about 30 minutes to three, saving him about 2.5 hours a day across reporting, material orders and payroll
Product: customers start with one workflow (daily reporting, purchasing or invoice review) and expand; the platform also captures approval rules and processes as a company playbook for newer workers
Reported by: Pulse 2.0, The SaaS News
