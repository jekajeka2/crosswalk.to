# Tolan raised $30M, undisclosed stage, from Union Square Ventures, Khosla Ventures and Homebrew (no lead named). The San Francisco startup (formerly Portola) makes a voice-first AI companion app with customizable characters, used by about 250,000 people a month. It will use the money to add its first free tier and widen what the companion can do.

Investors: Union Square Ventures, Khosla Ventures, Homebrew
Total raised: the round doubles its total funding to date
Valuation: not disclosed; the company calls it a significant step up from its previous valuation, which PitchBook puts at $120M
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: CEO Quinten Farmer, CTO Evan Goldschmidt and president Ajay Mehta, in 2023. Farmer and Goldschmidt previously built Even, the employer-linked neobank sold to Walmart in 2022
Traction: ~250,000 monthly users; ARR more than doubled year over year to double-digit millions; 30-day net user retention up 115% year over year
Users: about 70% women, 40% parents, one in six caring for an aging parent, mostly aged 30-45
Pricing: users have paid $10-$200 a month; a free tier is coming
Use of funds: free tier and new use cases
Reported by: Upstarts Media
