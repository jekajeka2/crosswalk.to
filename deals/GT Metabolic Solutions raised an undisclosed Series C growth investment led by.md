# GT Metabolic Solutions raised an undisclosed Series C growth investment led by J.P. Morgan Life Sciences Private Capital. The Minneapolis medical device company makes a linear magnetic anastomosis system that creates gastrointestinal connections by magnetic compression, without incisions, sutures or staples, usable endoscopically, robotically or laparoscopically.

First institutional financing for the company; the fifth transaction by J.P. Morgan Life Sciences Private Capital
HQ: Minneapolis, MN (also locations in San Jose, CA and Montréal)
CEO: Thierry Thaure (co-founder)
Regulatory: first company to receive both an FDA De Novo grant and 510(k) clearance for magnetic compression anastomosis
Use of funds: global commercial launch, product development, surgeon training, and new indications including endoscopic and colorectal procedures
Reported by: Pulse 2.0, FinSMEs
