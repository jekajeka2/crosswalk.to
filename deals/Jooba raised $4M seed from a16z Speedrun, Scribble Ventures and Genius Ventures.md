# Jooba raised $4M seed from a16z Speedrun, Scribble Ventures and Genius Ventures (no lead named). The San Francisco AI-native recruiting firm works as an embedded hiring partner for startups, pairing custom AI sourcing agents with human talent engineers and delivering candidate pipelines into clients' Slack.

Investors: a16z Speedrun, Scribble Ventures, Genius Ventures
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founder and CEO: Mark Benliyan
Use of funds: general availability and further development of its recruiting platform
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata, The SaaS News
