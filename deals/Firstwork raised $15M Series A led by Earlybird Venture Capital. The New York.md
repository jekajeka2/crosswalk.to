# Firstwork raised $15M Series A led by Earlybird Venture Capital. The New York applied-AI startup runs agents that handle frontline workforce operations, plugging into existing HR, payroll and screening systems to automate document verification, background checks, training coordination and compliance across the employment lifecycle.

Other investors: Legion VC, Deel Ventures, Gradient, Y Combinator
Total raised: $20M since its 2024 launch
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Vardhan Kapoor and Shubham Choudhary; founded 2024
Use of funds: hire across applied AI, AI research, engineering and go-to-market; expand in the US and Europe; extend its agents into more frontline workforce operations
Reported by: The SaaS News, FinSMEs
