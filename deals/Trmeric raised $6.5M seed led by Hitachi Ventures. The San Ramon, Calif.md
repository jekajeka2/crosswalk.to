# Trmeric raised $6.5M seed led by Hitachi Ventures. The San Ramon, Calif. startup sells an agentic platform for enterprise transformation programs: a repository of a company's strategy, initiatives and outcomes with agents (led by one called Tango) for planning, resourcing, governance, reporting and ROI measurement.

Other investors: Schema Ventures, CerraCap Impact Venture Capital, FalconX Ventures, Executive Venture Fund; angels Gokul Rajaram, Yatish Mishra, Shailesh Lakhani
Round: oversubscribed
HQ: San Ramon, CA
Founders: Siddharth Bohra (CEO), Debottam Datta
Traction: powers $1B in enterprise transformation; customers include Veolia, Seagate and ServiceTitan
Use of funds: product development, customer adoption, scaling the business
Reported by: PR Newswire (company release)
