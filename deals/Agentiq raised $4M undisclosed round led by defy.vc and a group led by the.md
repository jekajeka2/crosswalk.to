# Agentiq raised $4M undisclosed round led by defy.vc and a group led by the owner of two major European football clubs. Agentiq Sports runs SEC-qualified Reg A+ offerings that let fans buy units tied to a share of a pro athlete's future on-field brand income.

Leads: defy.vc; a group led by the owner of two major European football clubs (not named)
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Zach Kurtz (CEO, former D1 baseball player at Richmond) and Reuben Abraham (CTO, employee 10 at Pave, ex-NerdWallet)
Structure: each athlete offering is a series of a Delaware Series LLC; the athlete gets non-debt capital and brand advisory in exchange for a percentage of covered future on-field brand income
Use of funds: platform launch, team and pipeline growth
Traction: first offerings feature Diamondbacks closer Justin Martinez and Pirates outfielder Esmerlyn Valdez; also signed Hunter Dobbins, Ronny Cruz, Carlos Virahonda; pipeline of 200+ athletes, targeting 50+ on the roster within 12 months
Reported by: PR Newswire

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/agentiq-raises-4-million-to-launch-fan-to-athlete-investment-platform-302898371.html
