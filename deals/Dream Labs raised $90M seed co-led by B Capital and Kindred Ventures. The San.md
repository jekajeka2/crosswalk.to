# Dream Labs raised $90M seed co-led by B Capital and Kindred Ventures. The San Francisco startup came out of stealth with Dream-1, a robot foundation model trained on human egocentric video that controls two-armed robots with parallel grippers from a video feed and natural-language instructions, now sold through a public API at $3/hour ($0.75/hour for a lightweight version).

Other investors: A.Capital, Unusual Ventures, Wing VC, SV Angel, Scribble VC, Adrian Aoun
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founded: 2026; co-founder and CEO Joel Jang; team did robot-learning work at Nvidia
Product: Dream-1 treats robot skills as video prediction; Dream API live for all developers; Fine-Tuning-as-a-Service turns customer task videos into a custom model within hours; browser-based Robot Playground
Traction: working with 20+ robot hardware makers whose machines cost $2,000-$10,000; strong at pick-and-place and folding clothes
Use of funds: compute to extend Dream-1 to wheeled mobile robots and dexterous, humanlike hands
Reported by: SiliconANGLE, StrictlyVC, Pulse 2.0
