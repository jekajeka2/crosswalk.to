# Urologic Design raised $1.2M pre-seed led by Angel Investor Forum. The Branford, CT startup is developing a patented urethral occlusion device for men with stress urinary incontinence, a temporary option between absorbent products and surgery.

Other investors: HBS Alumni Angels of Boston, Haven Founders Fund, Connecticut Innovations, Yale Angels, individual investors; round oversubscribed
HQ: Branford, CT
Founded 2022 by Dr. David Hesse (CSO; Associate Professor of Urology, Yale School of Medicine)
Use of funds: develop first product, R&D, regulatory work, preparation for a first-in-human study in a small cohort of men with stress urinary incontinence
Traction: working prototype, IP secured, extensive bench testing
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/urologic-design-raises-1-2m-pre-seed-round-to-develop-novel-medical-device-for-urinary-incontinence-302923536.html
