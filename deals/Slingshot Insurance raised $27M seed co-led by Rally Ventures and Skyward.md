# Slingshot Insurance raised $27M seed co-led by Rally Ventures and Skyward Group. The Minneapolis startup, emerging from stealth, uses AI to automate the administrative work of connecting software companies with insurers, making small embedded policies sold at checkout (flights, youth sports registration, equipment rental, some as cheap as $25) economical.

Leads: Rally Ventures, Skyward Group (co-leads)
HQ: Minneapolis, MN
Status: emerging from stealth with this round
Plans: also pursuing an acquisition
Form D (filed 2026-10-23): $27M sold of a $27M equity offering; first sale 2026-08-21; 13 investors; incorporated 2026 (Delaware)
Related persons as filed: Ethan Genteman (Executive Officer, Director); Nicholas L. Malz (Executive Officer); Brock Noland, Justin Kaufenberg, Jeffrey Hinck (Directors)
Reported by: Crunchbase News (exclusive), Axios Pro Rata, FinSMEs, Tech Startups, SEC Form D
