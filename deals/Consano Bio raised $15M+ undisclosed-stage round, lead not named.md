# Consano Bio raised $15M+ undisclosed-stage round, lead not named (oversubscribed, from accredited private investors). Clinical-stage Burlington, Mass. biotech developing C-1101, a platelet-derived multi-protein biologic given by epidural injection to repair nerve-root injury in chronic sciatica (lumbosacral radiculopathy), which has no FDA-approved drug.

- Investors: a syndicate of accredited private investors; no lead named
- Total raised: more than $45M in the three years since inception
- HQ: Burlington, MA (Greater Boston); CEO Andrew Hall
- Use of funds: complete the randomized, double-blind Phase 1 trial of C-1101 (NCT07264270) in the US and Australia, and build out IP for C-1101 and other assets
- Milestones: FDA Fast Track designation for C-1101 in August 2026; Phase 1 enrollment expected to complete early 2027, data in 1H 2027
- Reported by: PR Newswire (company release)
