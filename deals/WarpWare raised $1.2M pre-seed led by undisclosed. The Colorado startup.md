# WarpWare raised $1.2M pre-seed led by undisclosed. The Colorado startup, founded by Sierra Space alumni, builds an AI-enabled engineering platform that unifies spacecraft flight software, guidance, navigation and control, and simulation/testing, replacing ground-up flight software development with agentic workflows.

Structure: SAFEs, primarily angel-funded
Investors: Rockies Venture Club, Cowtown Angels, Aggie Angel Network, Vesper Alpha, Marlboro Lane Ventures, individual angels
HQ: Colorado (city not stated)
Founders: CEO Alex Reynolds and COO Mickey Mathew, both formerly at Sierra Space
Use of funds: bring its agentic engineering tools to more commercial and government space markets; build out sales and marketing
Reported by: The SaaS News, Payload

Source: https://payloadspace.com/warpware-closes-pre-seed-to-bring-agentic-tools-to-flight-software
