# Ledgebrook raised $200M undisclosed-stage round co-led by Allianz X and Rockefeller Capital Management. The Boston AI-native specialty insurer writes general, professional and specialty liability cover for mid-sized businesses in the E&S market through wholesale brokers, using its Blackbird underwriting platform to quote in hours.

Round: primary equity financing; existing and new investors also participated
Reinsurance: Allianz Re agreed to a separate multi-year reinsurance agreement with Ledgebrook
HQ: Boston, MA
Founder: Gage Caligaris (CEO), a Harvard mathematician and actuary; founded 2022
Use of funds: scale its technology and extend Blackbird, its proprietary AI underwriting platform
Traction: on track to pass $1B in cumulative premium written since it started writing business in 2023; carrier Ledgebrook Specialty Insurance Company rated A- (Excellent) by AM Best in August 2026; about 300 employees, including some 80 underwriters and 50 engineers
Reported by: PR Newswire (company release)
