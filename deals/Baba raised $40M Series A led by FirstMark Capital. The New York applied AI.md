# Baba raised $40M Series A led by FirstMark Capital. The New York applied AI company pairs dedicated care coordinators and licensed therapists with AI to help Medicare and Medicaid patients and families connect medical, financial, social and behavioral health needs, working with health plans under value-based care arrangements.

Other investors: CVS Health Ventures, Valtruis; returning backers General Catalyst, Genius Ventures, Ground Up Ventures
HQ: New York, NY
Use of funds: expand clinical care teams, advance AI research, grow health plan partnerships to accelerate value-based care arrangements
Note: Axios Pro Rata describes Baba as a patient advocacy startup for Medicare and Medicare Advantage
Reported by: GlobeNewswire, The SaaS News, Axios Pro Rata

Source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/20/3384598/0/en/applied-ai-company-baba-raises-40-million-series-a-to-improve-the-medicare-experience.html
