# Pascal raised $8.5M seed (a follow-on to its seed round) co-led by Sazze Partners and LB Investment, plus $839K in grants for about $9.3M in new funding. The Watertown, Mass. Harvard spinout builds refrigeration and cooling systems that replace gaseous refrigerants with nonflammable, PFAS-free solid refrigerants; first commercial deployment is with a multinational beverage maker.

Other investors: KDB Silicon Valley; existing investors Engine Ventures, Khosla Ventures, Blindspot Ventures
Grants: $839K total ($500K ARPA-E, $239K InnovateMass, $100K Massachusetts Clean Energy Center Climatetech Tax Incentives Program)
Total raised: $17.3M
HQ: Watertown, MA (new 21,000 sq ft HQ opened Sept 2026; TechCrunch gives Cambridge, MA)
Founders: Adam Slavney (CEO), Jinyoung Seo (CTO); spun out of Prof. Jarad Mason's lab at Harvard in 2023
Use of funds: initial commercial deployment with a multinational beverage manufacturer, validating energy and cost savings; next development phase early 2027
Traction: working pilot refrigeration prototype; efficiency comparable to vapor-compression systems doing the same job, per the company
Reported by: StrictlyVC, TechCrunch, Pulse 2.0, Axios Pro Rata, FinSMEs
