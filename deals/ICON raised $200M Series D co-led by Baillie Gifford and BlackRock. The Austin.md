# ICON raised $200M Series D co-led by Baillie Gifford and BlackRock. The Austin construction-tech company builds robotic 3D-printing construction systems, including Titan, a multi-story system it says cuts six weeks and 40% of the cost from an average home build.

Other investors: Capital Factory, CAZ Investments, Moderne Ventures, Moonshots Capital, Norwest, Overmatch, The Syndicate, Tiger Global
HQ: Austin, TX
Co-founder and CEO: Jason Ballard
Use of funds: manufacture and deploy more robotic construction systems, commercial production of Titan, a large R&D facility in Austin, next-gen tech (Titan, Olympus), expansion across Texas, Florida, Washington, D.C. and other U.S. markets
Traction: on pace for 4x revenue growth in 2026; 270+ homes and structures built across five states and two countries, including Wolf Ranch (Georgetown, TX), Community First! Village (Austin) and U.S. Army military infrastructure
Business units: Build, Tech, and Prime (defense and space), which has received $370M+ in government contracts and works with NASA on Project Olympus for lunar construction
Reported by: Pulse 2.0
