# Torch Systems raised $9.5M undisclosed-stage round in SAFE notes, no lead named, plus $2M in venture debt ($11.5M total financing). The San Francisco startup makes TerraNode, a solar-powered sensor box with optical, thermal, acoustic and gas sensors and edge AI, that monitors grids, solar farms and other remote infrastructure for fire, overheating, intrusion and vegetation.

Investors: Khosla Ventures, Abstract Ventures, Construct Capital, Starship Ventures
Instrument: SAFE notes
Venture debt: $2M from First Citizens Innovation Banking (formerly SVB)
Total financing: $11.5M
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founder: CEO Vasya Tremsin, who began the wildfire-detection project in 2018 as a high school student
Use of funds: manufacturing and deploying TerraNodes, go-to-market and field teams, the Torch Intelligence Platform
Traction: hundreds of TerraNodes deployed in CA, OR, NM, VA, PA and RI; Dominion Energy uses it at solar sites; early fire detections in CA, NM and OR
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata, Pulse 2.0
