# Mach9 raised $34M Series A co-led by First Round Capital and Bessemer Venture Partners. The San Francisco company makes AI CAD software for civil engineering that turns LiDAR and other 3D scans of roads, utilities and sites into engineering drawings, auto-drafting curbs, signs, poles, lane markings and wires with centimeter-level accuracy, up to 10x faster at one-fifth the cost.

Other investors: Prudence; Quiet Capital (existing)
Total raised: $49M
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Alex Baikovitz (CEO), Haowen Shi, Michael Mong and Zach Sussman, who met at Carnegie Mellon's Robotics Institute
Use of funds: expand its AI software for surveying and civil engineering
Traction: used on 2,500+ projects in all 50 states, surveying 100,000+ miles of infrastructure corridors; customers include WSP, HNTB, NV5, Caltrans, Minnesota DOT and the Maryland State Highway Administration; Pennoni says it does 50% more work with the same team
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
