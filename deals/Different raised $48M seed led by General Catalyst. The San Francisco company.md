# Different raised $48M seed led by General Catalyst. The San Francisco company, out of stealth and co-founded by ThoughtSpot and Nutanix co-founder Ajeet Singh, sells an AI marketing system that plans and launches campaigns (ads, emails, landing pages, answer engine optimization) across channels for lean B2B marketing teams, learning from a shared memory of strategy, brand and results.

Other investors: Lightspeed Venture Partners, Together Fund, Haystack Ventures, MXV Capital, Firebolt Ventures
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Ajeet Singh (CEO; co-founded ThoughtSpot and Nutanix), Abhishek Pani (led ML behind Adobe's marketing products), Abhinav Yajurvedi (marketing infrastructure at Flipkart and Myntra)
Marketers review and approve work before it publishes
Traction: Motif's three-person team went from one campaign at a time to a dozen-plus across four channels; Descope redirected 60% of paid ad spend lost to poor targeting
Use of funds: expand engineering and go-to-market teams
Reported by: Pulse 2.0, FinSMEs

Source: https://pulse2.com/different-raises-48-million-seed-to-automate-b2b-demand-generation-with-ai
