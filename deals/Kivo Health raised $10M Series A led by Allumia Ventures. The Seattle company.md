# Kivo Health raised $10M Series A led by Allumia Ventures. The Seattle company runs a nine-week, therapist-led pulmonary rehab program over live video for older adults with COPD, shipping the equipment to patients' homes, and is covered by Medicare, Medicaid and major private insurers.

Other investors: Y Combinator, Corewell Health Ventures, Heuristic Capital, MassMutual Ventures; existing investors SNR Ventures, Liquid 2 Ventures, Samsung NEXT, Magic Fund
Round: oversubscribed
Total raised: more than $16M
HQ: Seattle, WA (per the company's announcement and GeekWire; Axios, StrictlyVC and FinSMEs report San Francisco)
Founders: Dr. Victor Sadauskas (CEO, former Kaiser Permanente emergency physician) and Vaughn Koch (CTO, sold Movity to Trulia in 2010); founded 2023
Use of funds: expand health plan partnerships and clinical operations nationally, advance its AI platform (an AI health coach blending AI-led and human-led care); later expansion into other high-cost chronic diseases of seniors
Traction: about 50 employees; covered by Medicare, Medicaid, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Blue Cross, Aetna and Priority Health; a peer-reviewed study of its program found 24% better symptoms, 34% better functional capacity and 83% median attendance; a company-cited actuarial analysis estimates $8,245 in annual savings per patient
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata (https://axios.link/3W4Y6UC), GeekWire, StrictlyVC, FinSMEs, Pulse 2.0
