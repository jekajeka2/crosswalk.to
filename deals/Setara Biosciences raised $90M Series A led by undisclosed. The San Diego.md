# Setara Biosciences raised $90M Series A led by undisclosed. The San Diego biotech, spun out of Jazz Pharmaceuticals' acquisition of Actio Biosciences, develops oral small-molecule drugs against the genetic causes of rare neurological and neuromuscular diseases; its lead TRPV4 inhibitor STAR-0871 targets Charcot-Marie-Tooth type 2C and overactive bladder.

Investors: Jazz Pharmaceuticals (new); previous investors Deerfield Management, Canaan, Droia Ventures, EcoR1, Euclidean Capital, Regeneron Ventures
No lead named
HQ: San Diego, CA
Founded: 2026, formed after Jazz bought Actio Biosciences for its lead program ABS-1230 (KCNT1-related epilepsy); Setara carries forward the rest of Actio's pipeline
Leadership: Actio co-founder David Goldstein, president and executive chair
Lead program: STAR-0871 (formerly ABS-0871), oral TRPV4 inhibitor; healthy-volunteer Phase 1 complete; Phase 1b THERON in CMT2C patients planned for 2027; Phase 2a in overactive bladder planned
FDA designations: Rare Pediatric Disease, Orphan Drug, Fast Track
Other programs: early-stage genetic epilepsy and migraine
Reported by: Fierce Biotech, StrictlyVC, Pulse 2.0, Axios Pro Rata
