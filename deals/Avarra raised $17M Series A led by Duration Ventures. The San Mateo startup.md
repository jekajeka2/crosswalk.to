# Avarra raised $17M Series A led by Duration Ventures. The San Mateo startup deploys AI avatars that coach sales reps before calls, join live deals to answer questions, and meet buyers on the website, all running one playbook captured from a company's top sellers.

Other investors: Lightspeed Venture Partners, Draper Associates, GTMfund, Alumni Ventures; strategic investment from Zoom
HQ: San Mateo, CA
CEO: David Knight
Use of funds: engineering and go-to-market hiring, expanding customer-facing avatars
Traction: ARR up more than 400% year over year; contracted mid-market and enterprise ARR up 698%; more than 10,000 AI coaching and role-play sessions a week
Customers: Elastic, Masimo, Proofpoint, Netskope, Owner.com; customers report rep ramp time cut roughly in half
Platform runs natively on Zoom
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
