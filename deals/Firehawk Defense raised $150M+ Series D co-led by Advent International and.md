# Firehawk Defense raised $150M+ Series D co-led by Advent International and Rochefort Asset Management, at a $1.4B+ valuation. The Dallas company makes solid rocket motor propellant and Hydra-70 rocket motors, and is developing 155mm artillery base bleed motors, using a continuous thermoplastic process that forms propellant in hours rather than weeks.

Amount: more than $150M (Series D), described by the company as oversubscribed
Valuation: more than $1.4B
Leads: Advent International (Boston); Rochefort Asset Management (West Palm Beach, FL), which also gave Firehawk a senior secured loan in April 2026
Other investors: new and existing investors and strategic partners (not named)
HQ: Dallas, TX, per the Oct. 29 announcement (Dallas Innovates reported Addison, TX)
CEO: Will Edwards
Use of funds: build out propulsion manufacturing at its Lawton, Okla. plant and its Crawford, Miss. missile integration facility; speed up production of artillery motors, solid rocket motors, energetics and propellant for U.S. and allied customers
Facilities: the Crawford site is running and integrating Hydra-70 rockets now; the Great Plains Arsenal propellant plant in Lawton is expected to start up in early 2027 at 740,000 lb/year, scalable to more than 3.1M lb
Traction: U.S. Air Force solid rocket motor development contract; a six-for-six Hydra-70 live-fire test in August 2026 after a U.S. Army OTA award; a teaming deal with General Dynamics Ordnance and Tactical Systems to supply base bleed motors
Reported by: PR Newswire (Rochefort Asset Management announcement), Dallas Innovates, PitchBook News
