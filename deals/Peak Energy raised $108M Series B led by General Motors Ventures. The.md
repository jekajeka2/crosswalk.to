# Peak Energy raised $108M Series B led by General Motors Ventures. The Burlingame, Calif. company makes passively cooled sodium-ion energy storage systems for the grid, and is now taking them into data center power infrastructure.

Other investors: Maverick Silicon, Infinite Capital, Eclipse, Xora Innovation, LG Technology Ventures
Round: oversubscribed
HQ: Burlingame, CA
Founded: 2023, by veterans of Tesla, Enovix and Fluence; co-founder and CEO Landon Mossburg
Use of funds: scale domestic manufacturing, fill more than $1B in existing customer orders, launch its product for data centers
Traction: more than $1B in commercial agreements, including Jupiter Power, RWE and Energy Vault; partnership with GM (announced June 2026) to co-develop sodium-ion storage cells
Manufacturing: 4 GWh factory in Sacramento, CA, starting production in Q1 2027
Product claims: up to 20% lower cost of energy than conventional storage; no cooling systems, zero planned maintenance, zero water use
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
