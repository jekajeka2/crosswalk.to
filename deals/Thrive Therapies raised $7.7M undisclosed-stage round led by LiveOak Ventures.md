# Thrive Therapies raised $7.7M undisclosed-stage round led by LiveOak Ventures. The Dallas company places speech, occupational, physical and behavioral therapists inside schools for children with disabilities, and its Thrive Clinical platform logs session-level outcomes that also feed Medicaid billing.

Other investors: Activate Venture Partners, Charter School Growth Fund
HQ: Dallas, TX
Founder and CEO: Dr. Kristin McGraner (previously founder and CEO of STEM Preparatory Academy for 11 years)
Use of funds: expansion into more markets, hiring clinicians, further development of the Thrive Clinical platform
Traction: serves students in 200+ schools and districts across six states (FL, NC, OH, TN, TX, WA) under multiyear district agreements; 18,000+ therapy sessions recorded on the platform
Outcomes (company-reported): 85%+ of students met annual IEP goals; 46% met goals and no longer needed services, versus a ~8% national average the company cites
Reported by: Pulse 2.0, FinSMEs

Source: https://pulse2.com/thrive-therapies-raises-7-7-million-to-expand-school-based-healthcare-services-for-children-with-disabilities
