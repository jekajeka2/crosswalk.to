# EstateMin raised €1M pre-seed led by undisclosed. The New York legal-tech startup, with Irish roots, sells an AI platform for trust and estate law firms that digitizes client intake, extracts data and automates document preparation, integrating with existing case management systems.

Investors: NDRC, First Port Syndicate, Enterprise Ireland (High Potential Start-Up programme), angels Mike Hanrahan, Moira McKenna and Conor Stanley, among others
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Isabella Hughes, Oisin Dolphin and John Noonan; founded 2024
Use of funds: US customer acquisition, go-to-market, engineering and product hiring
Reported by: The SaaS News (citing Silicon Republic)
