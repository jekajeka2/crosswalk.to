# Aatmunn raised up to $8M growth round led by Memento Growth Partners. The Livonia, Mich. company makes SPANR, a data layer that connects industrial frontline devices from different makers, normalizes their data and applies AI for worker safety, gas detection, asset tracking and inspections.

Other investors: existing investors participated
HQ: Livonia, MI
Founded: 2014 as Guardhat; CEO and co-founder Praveer Kumar
Use of funds: expand sales and marketing, accelerate SPANR R&D including AI integration
Traction: used by 300+ enterprises across the US, Canada and Europe; safety teams protecting 80,000+ workers; customers include Fortune 500 firms in energy, aerospace, logistics, construction and manufacturing; SOC 2 Type II audited
Also sold white-label to OEMs: one industrial hardware OEM launched two product lines on SPANR in five and a half months
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
