# Asymptote Labs raised $5.5M seed led by Tower Research Ventures. The New York startup builds runtime infrastructure for enterprise AI agents: Beacon, an open-source telemetry layer that normalizes agent activity into a common data model so companies can monitor, secure and control agents at runtime.

Other investors: Lytical Ventures, 1984 Ventures, South Park Commons
Round: oversubscribed
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Justin D'Souza and Shukan Shah (early engineers at Doppel), founded 2026
Use of funds: grow engineering, speed product development, scale go-to-market
Compliance: SOC 2 Type II
Reported by: The SaaS News

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/asymptote-labs-raises-5-5m-seed
