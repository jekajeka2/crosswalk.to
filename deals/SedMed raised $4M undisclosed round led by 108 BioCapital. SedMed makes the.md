# SedMed raised $4M undisclosed round led by 108 BioCapital. SedMed makes the SedMed Toilet Lift, a non-electric sit-to-stand device that attaches to standard toilets to help older adults and people with limited mobility; it is used in more than 40 hospital systems.

- Lead: 108 BioCapital ($4M investment; U.S. growth-stage biotech/medtech PE firm)
- HQ: Milford, CT
- CEO: Jeremy Bronen
- Use of funds: commercial expansion, distribution and channel partnerships, IP portfolio, additional hospital-based studies
- Traction: adopted across more than 40 hospital systems
- Investor sees international potential, particularly India

Reported by: PR Newswire (108 BioCapital release)
