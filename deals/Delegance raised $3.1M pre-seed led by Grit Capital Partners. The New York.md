# Delegance raised $3.1M pre-seed led by Grit Capital Partners. The New York AI-native commercial insurance brokerage replaces percentage commissions with a flat 5% disclosed fee, quotes 4,000+ business classifications instantly, and runs servicing (certificates, policy changes, claims, renewals) through its AI agent Orin, with licensed brokers handling complex placements.

Other investors: Core VC, Gilgamesh Ventures, Outside VC
HQ: New York, NY
Founder: Alexander Ledbetter (CEO), formerly at AXA XL
Licensed in 51 jurisdictions; direct appointments with 25 carriers including Chubb, Travelers and The Hartford
Traction: $26M+ in premium commitments for 2026
Covers 36+ lines including general liability, workers' comp, property, commercial auto, cyber, professional liability and D&O
Claims its 5% fee cuts total insurance cost about 10-20% on a typical placement vs. commission structures
Use of funds: hire licensed brokers and engineers; deepen partnerships with banks, private equity firms and software platforms
Reported by: Pulse 2.0
