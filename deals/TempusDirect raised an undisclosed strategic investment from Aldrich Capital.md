# TempusDirect raised an undisclosed strategic investment from Aldrich Capital Partners. The Tempe, Arizona company sells third-party claims cost containment to P&C insurers: medical bill validation and benchmarking, direct provider negotiation, and demand package review, built on a proprietary multi-carrier claims database.

Investor: Aldrich Capital Partners (private equity, tech-driven business services)
Amount and terms: not disclosed
HQ: Tempe, AZ
Founders: CEO Greg Gaughan and COO Josh Dickerson; founded 2016
Use of funds: product development, client services, business development, automation; Aldrich's AI team to deploy more advanced capabilities for clients
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
