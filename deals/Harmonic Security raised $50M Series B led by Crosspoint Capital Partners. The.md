# Harmonic Security raised $50M Series B led by Crosspoint Capital Partners. The San Francisco startup inspects everything employees send to AI tools (browser, desktop AI apps and agentic workflows) with custom small language models that return a verdict in milliseconds, blocking leaks like pasted live credentials, and adds a Usage Intelligence layer showing which AI use cases pay off.

Other investors: existing investors participated (not named)
Total raised: $76M; previous round a $17.5M Series A led by Next47 in October 2024
HQ: San Francisco, CA; staff of 90+ across the US and UK
CEO and co-founder: Alastair Paterson
Integrations: Cisco Cloud Control Agent Builder (over MCP); Anthropic's Claude Compliance API
Traction: 100+ enterprise customers covering millions of employees; distribution partners Climb Global Solutions and Distology
Use of funds: engineering and go-to-market hires in the US and UK
Reported by: SiliconANGLE, StrictlyVC
