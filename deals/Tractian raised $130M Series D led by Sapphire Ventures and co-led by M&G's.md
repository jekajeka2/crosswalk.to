# Tractian raised $130M Series D led by Sapphire Ventures and co-led by M&G's Catalyst, at a $1.4B valuation. The Atlanta company sells sensors plus an agentic "physical AI" platform that monitors vibration, ultrasound and other signals on industrial machines, diagnoses root causes and recommends fixes; 2,000+ facilities use it.

Other investors: N47, General Catalyst, Bosch Ventures, 53 Stations, Monashees, Endeavor Catalyst, Norte Ventures, Y Combinator
Valuation: $1.4B
Total raised: $313.7M in equity (AlleyWatch)
HQ: Atlanta, GA (offices in Monterrey, São Paulo and Poland; 700+ employees)
Founders: Igor Marinelli (CEO), Gabriel Lima and Leonardo Vieira; founded 2019 (AlleyWatch)
Use of funds: scale the platform from failure prediction to full asset-health protection for manufacturers, data center operators, defense, utilities
Customers: Eli Lilly, McKesson, Whirlpool, Coca-Cola bottlers, Cummins, Embraer; 2,000+ critical facilities
Sapphire also led Tractian's Series C
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement), AlleyWatch, FinSMEs, Crunchbase News
