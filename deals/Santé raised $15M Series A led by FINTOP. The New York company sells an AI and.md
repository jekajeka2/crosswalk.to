# Santé raised $15M Series A led by FINTOP. The New York company sells an AI and embedded-payments operating system for independent wine and liquor stores (in-store POS, e-commerce, delivery apps, marketing, receiving), now serving nearly 1,000 retailers processing $2B in annual GMV.

Other investors: returning backers Bonfire Ventures, Operator Collective, Y Combinator, Veridical Ventures
Total raised: $22.6M in reported equity funding (AlleyWatch); the Series A comes 6 months after a $7.6M seed
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Darren Fike (CEO) and Antoine Balaresque; founded 2023
Use of funds: autonomous AI sales tools that re-engage lapsed customers and drive store revenue
Traction: business grew 500% over the period; nearly 1,000 retailers, $2B annual GMV
Reported by: PR Newswire (company release), AlleyWatch, Axios Pro Rata
