# Casap raised $56M Series B led by Base10 Partners. The New York company runs an AI agent platform for financial institutions: specialized agents investigate, decide and act across existing banking systems on high-stakes operations, applying each institution's policies under human oversight.

Other investors: strategic investors Experian and Synchrony; existing investors Emergence Capital and Lightspeed Venture Partners
Total raised: $90M, including a $25M Series A about a year earlier
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Shanthi Shanmugam and Saisi Peters
Use of funds: advance the AI platform and expand services so financial institutions can automate operations previously too expensive, complex or slow to scale
Reported by: The SaaS News, PR Newswire (company announcement)
