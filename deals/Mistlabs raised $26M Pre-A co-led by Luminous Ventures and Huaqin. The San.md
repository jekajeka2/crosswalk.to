# Mistlabs raised $26M Pre-A co-led by Luminous Ventures and Huaqin. The San Francisco startup just took Ambi out of beta. Ambi is an ambient AI system on iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac that listens through the day, keeps memory of its user, and starts tasks unprompted. Next it plans agent-to-agent negotiation between users and its own AI wearable.

Round: described as Pre-A by the company; StrictlyVC says the $26M came across multiple rounds
Huaqin Technology is a Shanghai-based smart-device ODM
HQ: San Francisco, CA
CEO: Peter Mo (TechCrunch: a former Plaud executive); company founded this year
Use of funds: develop a standalone AI wearable, due later this year with a waitlist open
Product: available on Macs and iOS 18 devices; audio not stored by default
Reported by: PR Newswire (company), SiliconANGLE, TechCrunch, StrictlyVC
