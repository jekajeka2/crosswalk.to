# MDIA raised a $3M investment from the Knight Foundation. The Miami company, parent of Miami-Dade County's Miami-Dade Innovation Authority, runs a "Public Innovation Challenge" model that helps local governments test and procure solutions from startups, and will expand it to other US cities.

Investor: John S. and James L. Knight Foundation
HQ: Miami, FL
Founded: launched 2023 as Miami-Dade Innovation Authority; MDIA becomes the parent brand for a network of communities
Leadership: Leigh-Ann Buchanan (president and CEO)
Use of funds: launch the model in an initial cohort of Knight Foundation communities, including Akron, Ohio and Macon, Ga., with more cities to come
Traction: eight public challenges run in Miami-Dade; solicitation-to-pilot time cut by up to 67% (18-24 months down to as little as seven); 70+ applications per challenge vs. 5-15 for a standard RFP
Reported by: Refresh Miami, FinSMEs

Source: https://refreshmiami.com/news/mdia-lands-3m-knight-foundation-investment-to-expand-its-innovation-model-nationwide
