New investors: NVentures, Sapphire Ventures
Existing investors: Lightspeed Venture Partners, WndrCo, Amplify Partners, Sky9 Capital, FPV Ventures
Amount: not disclosed; Tech Startups infers about $15M from total funding rising from $59M (at its May stealth exit) to $74M
Total raised: $74M
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Traction: projects across media, advertising, streaming and robotics; claims 60+ fps rendering at under 40ms latency (company figures)
