# Ramp raised $1.85B undisclosed-stage primary round led by Dragoneer Investment Group, at a $60B valuation. The New York fintech runs corporate cards, expense management, bill pay, accounting and AR automation for 85,000+ businesses, and now sells AI token spend management and an inference router (Router.com).

Co-leads: Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Sands Capital, Avenir Growth
Other investors: Capital International, Fidelity, T. Rowe Price, D1 Capital Partners, Greenoaks, ICONIQ, GIC, 8VC, 1789 Capital, Alpha Wave Global, Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan, Coatue, D.E. Shaw, Definition Capital, FirstMark, General Catalyst, Generation Investment Management, Glade Brook, Khosla Ventures, Lightspeed, Pinegrove Opportunity Partners, Soma Capital, Stripes
Total raised: about $5B in equity (earlier reports said $4.85B)
Valuation: $60B
HQ: New York, NY
Founded 2019 by Eric Glyman, Karim Atiyeh and Gene Lee
Traction: $2B+ annualized revenue, cash-flow positive; revenue growing 25% faster than a year ago; $275B+ annualized purchase volume (up 175% YoY); customers generating $100K+ in revenue up 113% YoY; median customer saves 7.5%
Earlier reports (Bloomberg, Oct 12) named Dragoneer and Thrive as leads; the company names Dragoneer as lead with four co-leads
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement), Pulse 2.0, AlleyWatch, PitchBook (citing Bloomberg), FinTech Futures
