# Wabi raised $55M led by IVP, at a $1.25B valuation (stage undisclosed). The San Francisco startup, founded by Replika creator Eugenia Kuyda, makes a shared personal AI assistant that combines an AI agent, personalized mini-apps and group spaces for couples, families and friends.

Other investors: a16z, Forerunner, Amazon Alexa Fund; angels including Tobi Lütke, Marc Benioff, Marissa Mayer, Dick Costolo and Alex Pall
Valuation: $1.25B
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founder: Eugenia Kuyda (CEO), previously founded Replika
Use of funds: product development and expansion
Traction: raise follows its recent public launch; on iOS, Android and Mac, Windows coming
Reported by: PR Newswire
