# DashFuel raised an undisclosed growth round led by Five Elms Capital. The San Francisco company sells one cloud platform for petroleum wholesalers, fuel carriers and retailers covering quoting, tank monitoring, dispatch, delivery, billing reconciliation and back-office integration.

Amount: undisclosed
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founder: Miles Moen (Founder & CEO); founded 2022
Use of funds: expand the platform and AI-powered automation, build out go-to-market, grow the team and customer success
Traction: customers across the US; company cites strong gross and net revenue retention
Investor: Five Elms Capital (B2B vertical software growth investor, $3B+ AUM); partner Thomas Kershisnik
Reported by: PR Newswire

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/dashfuel-announces-growth-investment-from-five-elms-capital-302915849.html
