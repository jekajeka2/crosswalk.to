# Electric Plant Company raised $11.2M seed co-led by Onto Ventures and Climactic. The three-year-old San Francisco startup builds sensors and an AI foundation model for plant electrophysiology, reading plants' electrical signals to help growers manage water, nutrients and light.

Other investors: Ponderosa, Endeavor Catalyst, Ludlow, Michigan Rise, The Fund, Schox VC, Dug Song
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founded: about 2023 (three years old, per StrictlyVC)
Reported by: FinSMEs, TechCrunch, StrictlyVC, Axios Pro Rata
