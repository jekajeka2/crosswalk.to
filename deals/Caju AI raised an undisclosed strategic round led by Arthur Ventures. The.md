# Caju AI raised an undisclosed strategic round led by Arthur Ventures. The Charlottesville, Va. company sells a platform that lets enterprises and government agencies govern and compliantly capture business conversations on WhatsApp, iMessage, SMS and other mobile and private messaging channels, with an AI layer that categorizes messages, extracts themes and prioritizes what compliance teams review.

Amount: undisclosed (described as growth capital)
Lead: Arthur Ventures (Andrew Heim, General Partner); Arthur manages ~$2B and invests in B2B software companies outside Silicon Valley
HQ: Charlottesville, VA
Co-founder and CEO: Otavio Freire
Use of funds: product development including AI capabilities, global go-to-market and partner channel expansion, customer success
Customers: global enterprises and government agencies, including at the U.S. federal level, across financial services, life sciences, regulated industries and the public sector; customers in North America, Europe, Latin America and Asia, direct and through archiving and compliance partners; SOC 2 compliant
Advisers: Williams Mullen (Caju), Gunderson Dettmer (Arthur Ventures)
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/caju-ai-secures-strategic-investment-from-arthur-ventures-302918669.html
