# Powerlattice raised $86M (stage undisclosed, no lead named) of a $111M round, per an SEC Form D filed 2026-10-30. The Vancouver, WA startup makes a power delivery chiplet that sits inside the processor package and says it can cut AI chip energy use by more than half.

Offering size: $111M; $25M remaining; 18 investors so far; equity; first sale 2026-10-15
Investors in the round: not named in the filing; company declined to comment
Prior funding: $31M raised when it emerged from stealth in Nov 2025, including a $25M Series A led by Playground Global and Celesta Capital
Board/related persons as filed: Peng Zou (CEO, director), Sujith Dermal (executive officer, director), Gang Ren (executive officer, director), Pat Gelsinger (director; GP at Playground Global), Steve Fu (director; Celesta Capital), Wen Hsieh (director)
HQ: Vancouver, WA; offices in Chandler, AZ, San Jose, CA and Hsinchu, Taiwan
Founders: Peng Zou, Gang Ren, Sujith Dermal, founded 2023 (veterans of Qualcomm, Intel, NUVIA)
Product: power delivery chiplet; this week demonstrated what it calls the first monolithic integrated voltage regulator chiplet (controller, power transistors and inductors on one die)
Traction: providing engineering samples for GPUs, CPUs and custom accelerators (as of 2025)
Entity: Delaware corporation; exemption 06b
Reported by: GeekWire, SEC Form D
