# Sharp Biomedical raised an undisclosed pre-seed led by gener8tor 1889. The North Dakota medical device company is developing toolsets and implants for simple, manual spine procedures that outpatient pain clinics can perform for early-to-mid-stage chronic pain patients.

Other investors: several physician angel investors
HQ: Fargo, ND per the company's release (FinSMEs lists Grand Forks, ND)
Founder and CEO: Dan Newman
Use of funds: fully fund development and testing of its first toolset and implant system, toward regulatory milestones and commercial launch
Reported by: PR Newswire, FinSMEs
