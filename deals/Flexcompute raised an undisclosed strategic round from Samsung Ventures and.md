# Flexcompute raised an undisclosed strategic round from Samsung Ventures and Applied Ventures (Applied Materials). The Watertown, Mass. company builds a "Physics World Model" that combines neural networks with first-principles physics and GPU-native solvers to predict how hardware designs will behave before they are built.

Other investors: additional, unnamed investors joined
HQ: Watertown, MA
Founded by researchers from Stanford and MIT; co-founder and president Vera Yang
Team: 70+ PhDs; solvers cover fluid dynamics, electromagnetics, structural mechanics and thermal analysis
Traction: engineering teams at 250+ companies in aerospace, automotive, semiconductors, photonics and advanced manufacturing; says it solved a 5-billion-degree-of-freedom chip-package warpage model within 2% of commercial finite-element results in minutes
Use of funds: grow the research team, add GPU compute, extend the model across fluids, electromagnetics, structures, thermal and multiphysics
Reported by: Pulse 2.0, FinSMEs
