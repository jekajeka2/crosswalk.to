# Rosie raised $7M across two rounds led by Bling Capital and Reach Capital, stage undisclosed. The Seattle startup sells monthly memberships ($250 for 8 hours up to $750 for 24) for vetted caregivers ("Pros") who do a mix of childcare and household work, with an AI system that tracks each family's routines and handles matching, scheduling and payments.

Other investors: Graham & Walker, Marketplace Capital, Ingeborg Investments, Precursor Ventures, Mother Ventures, Yonder Ventures; angels including DoorDash co-founder Evan Moore and Aviel Ginzburg (Founders' Co-op)
HQ: Seattle, WA
Founders: Mitra Raman (CEO, former Amazon engineer) and Karthika Shankar (COO, 12+ years at Amazon); launched January 2026
Use of funds: first expansion beyond Seattle, to Austin (early 2027, per GeekWire) and then San Francisco
Traction: seven-figure ARR within eight months of launch; 17,000+ hours of care delivered to 250+ member families (GeekWire says nearly 300); 100+ Pros
Reported by: Rosie (PR Newswire), GeekWire, Axios Pro Rata
