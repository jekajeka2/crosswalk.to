# Relay raised $36M Series B1 led by International Paper. Relay sells AI-backed push-to-talk radios and a cloud platform for frontline workers in industrial and hospitality settings, with translation, location awareness and automated workflows; it works with about 10% of the Fortune 500.

- Stage: Series B1 (per PitchBook)
- Other investors: Cerity Partners clients, Harmonic Growth Partners, Thayer Investment Partners; earlier leads G2 Venture Partners and Wind River Ventures added to their stakes
- Total raised: more than $90M
- HQ: Raleigh, N.C.
- Use of funds: its software and AI platform, including an agentic layer for querying shift data, worker-safety features, enterprise integrations, next-gen hardware, and expansion in industrial and hospitality markets
- Reported by: PR Newswire, SiliconANGLE, PitchBook, FinSMEs
