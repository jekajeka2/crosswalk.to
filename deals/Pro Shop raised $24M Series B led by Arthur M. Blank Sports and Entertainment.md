# Pro Shop raised $24M Series B led by Arthur M. Blank Sports and Entertainment. The Ponte Vedra Beach golf media, entertainment and commerce company owns Pro Shop Studios, GolfWRX, Sugarloaf Social Club and Paige Co., and produces golf content for Netflix and Prime Video.

Other investors: Causeway Media Partners, Ares Sports, Media and Entertainment funds, PGA Tour, Powerhouse Capital, Phoenix Capital Ventures
HQ: Ponte Vedra Beach, FL
Founders: Chad Mumm, Mark Olsen, Joe Purzycki, David Miller; founded 2023
Use of funds: digital media and original programming (talent-led franchises, premium live events, competition formats), expanding owned media brands and commerce businesses, future acquisitions and partnerships
Reported by: PR Newswire, The SaaS News
