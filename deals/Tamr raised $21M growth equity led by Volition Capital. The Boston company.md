# Tamr raised $21M growth equity led by Volition Capital. The Boston company sells AI-native master data management: machine learning and AI agents, with human review, that unify, clean and enrich fragmented records across CRM, ERP and data warehouses so enterprise AI runs on trusted data.

Other investors: existing investors (not named)
HQ: Boston, MA
Founders: spun out of MIT CSAIL in 2013; co-founders include Turing Award winner Michael Stonebraker, Andy Palmer (now chairman), CTO Alex Pagan, Ihab Ilyas, Daniel Bruckner and George Beskales; CEO Anthony Deighton
Use of funds: advance agentic capabilities around data curation and context delivery; scale its AI and operational infrastructure
Traction: more than doubled SaaS revenue in its prior fiscal year; 19 patents; customers include Old Mutual
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
