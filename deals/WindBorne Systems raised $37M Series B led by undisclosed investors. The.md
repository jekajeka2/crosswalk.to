# WindBorne Systems raised $37M Series B led by undisclosed investors. The Redwood City startup flies a fleet of about 700 autonomous weather balloons that collect atmospheric data, which it sells and also feeds into its own AI weather forecasting model; it just won a US Air Force data contract worth up to $49M.

Backers include Khosla Ventures
HQ: Redwood City, CA
Founded 2015; CEO John Dean, co-founder and CTO Andrey Sushko
Round disclosed by CEO John Dean to TechCrunch in August 2026, to continue scaling the business
Traction: ~700 balloons aloft worldwide; customers include the National Weather Service, US Air Force and Navy
New US Air Force contract (awarded without competitive bidding) worth up to $49M, paid per data order; $1.7M ordered so far, its largest contract to date
Reported by: TechCrunch, StrictlyVC

Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/05/ai-makes-weather-prediction-better-can-windborne-make-it-lucrative
