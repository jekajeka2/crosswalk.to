# Subeca raised ~$3M Series A-2 led by Burnt Island Ventures. The Cary, N.C. company sells smart water meters bundled with software to small and mid-sized utilities, using a communication module on Amazon's free Sidewalk network to give real-time meter data and catch leaks early.

Other investors: Cycle H2O (new), SUEZ and several additional investors
Prior round: 2024 Series A of $6M led by SUEZ, with Burnt Island Ventures and Amazon's Climate Pledge Fund
HQ: Cary, NC (modules produced there)
CEO: Anne Mushow
Traction: live in 23 communities; targets the 51,000+ US utilities and businesses priced out of advanced metering
Use of funds: growth and bringing real-time meter data to small and medium-sized utilities and hyperscalers
Reported by: PR Newswire, FinSMEs
