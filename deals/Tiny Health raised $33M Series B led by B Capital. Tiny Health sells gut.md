# Tiny Health raised $33M Series B led by B Capital. Tiny Health sells gut microbiome tests, which started as at-home tests for babies and now cover all ages, with adults its largest testing segment. It also sells through healthcare providers and other companies.

- HQ: Austin
- Founder: Cheryl Sew Hoy
- Use of funds: expand the microbiome testing business and research, including whether gut microbes can flag disease risk early. The data comes from nearly 200,000 microbiome samples.
- Also studying whether interventions guided by test results improve outcomes
