# Birch Hill raised $4M seed led by Multicoin Capital. The New York firm assesses, structures and distributes US private credit onchain: it curates lending vaults on Morpho, is building onchain credit strategies, and has applied to FINRA for broker-dealer membership to sell private credit to accredited investors.

Other investors: ParaFi Capital, Castle Island Ventures, Nascent
Total raised: $6.5M
HQ: New York, NY
Divisions: Vaults (official curator on Morpho, setting risk parameters), Strategies (Flagship Vaults, Birch Hill Absolute Value onchain credit strategy), Enterprise (FINRA broker-dealer application pending)
Plan: securitize pools of US loans onchain, starting with multifamily, agriculture and energy credit
Team: eight people, including executives hired from Yearn and Gauntlet
Regulatory: met the SEC Crypto Task Force in August on tokenized credit issuance
Reported by: Pulse 2.0

Source: https://pulse2.com/birch-hill-raises-4-million-seed-round-led-by-multicoin-capital
