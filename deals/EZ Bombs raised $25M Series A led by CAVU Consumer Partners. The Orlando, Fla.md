# EZ Bombs raised $25M Series A led by CAVU Consumer Partners. The Orlando, Fla. company makes shelf-stable, pre-portioned "Seasoning Bombs" that dissolve during cooking to replace the multi-step flavor bases of Mexican and Hispanic dishes like birria and pozole.

HQ: Orlando, FL
Founded late 2023 by Brandon Castaneda (CEO), his mother Tina Castaneda and sister Nichole Munoz
Board: CAVU Principal Brandon Neumann joins; CAVU's in-house value creation team UNCOMMON will work with the company
Use of funds: wider retail distribution, more manufacturing capacity, new products, executive hiring
Traction: from zero retail locations at the start of 2025 to 8,000+ today (Walmart, Target, Costco, Kroger, Albertsons, Publix, Amazon, TikTok Shop); No. 1 food and beverage product on TikTok Shop in 2025 with 1M+ units sold; profitable while scaling
Products: Birria Bomb first, now Pozole, Tinga, Chicken Chipotle and Chile Colorado
SEC Form D (EZ Bombs Inc., filed 2026-10-22): $15M of equity sold of a $15M offering, 1 investor, first sale 2026-08-21; the filing covers less than the announced $25M; related persons Brandon Castaneda (Executive Officer, Director) and Mike Jalonen (Executive Officer, Director); Delaware corporation, incorporated 2026: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2153061/000215306126000001/0002153061-26-000001-index.htm
Reported by: FinSMEs, Pulse 2.0, Axios Pro Rata, SEC Form D
