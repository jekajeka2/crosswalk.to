# Satlyt raised $8M seed led by Non Sibi Ventures; its SEC Form D (filed 2026-10-19) reports $7.8M of equity sold of a $9M offering to 39 investors. Satlyt builds software that lets satellites run AI inference and share computing workloads in orbit, downlinking only the outputs instead of raw data, as a virtual orbital-data-center network.

Other investors: TLCOM, Antler, Slauson & Co., Launch Africa Ventures, Enza Capital, Demos Capital, BAG Collective, Gaingels, Askya, AXIAN Investment
Total raised: over $10M
Form D: $7.8M equity sold of a $9M offering ($1.2M remaining), 39 investors, first sale 2026-09-25, filed 2026-10-19
Related persons (Form D): Rama Ochieng Afullo (Executive Officer, Director); Kent Lucas (Director)
HQ: Sunnyvale, CA and Nairobi, Kenya (joint headquarters)
Founder: Rama Afullo (ex-SpaceX, Google); founded 2024 (Delaware corporation, incorporated 2024 per Form D)
Use of funds: grow the team, expand in-orbit capabilities, and R&D for its virtual orbital-data-center network
Reported by: The SaaS News, StrictlyVC, Payload, TechCrunch, SEC Form D

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/satlyt-raises-8m-seed
