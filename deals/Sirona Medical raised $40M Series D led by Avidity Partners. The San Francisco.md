# Sirona Medical raised $40M Series D led by Avidity Partners. The San Francisco company sells RadOS, a cloud-native radiology operating system that unifies PACS, reporting, diagnostic viewing, worklist and workflow AI on one platform; usage, ARR and contracted ARR each grew more than 2.5x year over year.

Other investors: 8VC, GreatPoint Ventures, KLT Holdings, and other new and existing investors
Total raised: roughly $150M in venture capital
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founder: Cameron Andrews (Founder and President), founded 2018; CEO Ken Kaufman
Use of funds: the company says the round funds its operating plan indefinitely; deploying RadOS to more practices, hospitals, health systems and international markets in 2027
Traction: dozens of contracted customers across hundreds of integrated sites, more than 12 million primary imaging exams a year under contract since its November 2023 public launch; platform usage, ARR and contracted ARR all up more than 2.5x year over year
Reported by: PR Newswire
