# Broccoli raised $52M across Series A and Series B, both led by Khosla Ventures, at a reported ~$300M valuation: a new $30M Series B plus a previously undisclosed $22M Series A. The San Francisco startup uses AI agents to handle calls, scheduling, follow-ups, membership renewals and customer outreach for plumbing, HVAC, electrical and other home-service businesses.

Round breakdown: $30M Series B (new) plus a $22M Series A closed about nine months earlier and first disclosed now (Tech Startups, Axios, FinSMEs, The SaaS News); StrictlyVC reported the full $52M as a Series B
Other investors: Y Combinator, Operator Partners
Valuation: ~$300M post-money per Fortune, citing a person familiar; the company declined to confirm
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: AJ Jain and Aditya Ketkar; founded 2024
Use of funds: scale engineering and sales teams and speed up product development
Reported by: Fortune, StrictlyVC, The SaaS News, Tech Startups, Axios Pro Rata, FinSMEs
