# Rocker raised $12.85M undisclosed-stage round led by Greycroft. The San Francisco startup, from Stripe alumni, launched an enterprise AI agent that connects to Google Docs, Slack, email and other work tools to track a user's top priorities, catch slipping decisions and execute tasks.

Other investors: Anthropic, Anthology Fund (Menlo Ventures), Matchstick, Stripe, plus angels from Stripe, Vercel and Meta
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Alex Vogenthaler (CEO; ex-Stripe head of product for Connect and enterprise, ex-Google Drive/Workspace) and Peter Terrill (ex-Stripe Connect engineering lead; co-founded Payable, acquired by Stripe); company about one year old
Traction: waitlist opened at launch, prioritizing larger teams; in discussions and test use with large organizations including public companies (unnamed)
Reported by: Upstarts Media, StrictlyVC, PitchBook, FinSMEs

Source: https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/stripe-alums-launch-rocker-ai-assistant
