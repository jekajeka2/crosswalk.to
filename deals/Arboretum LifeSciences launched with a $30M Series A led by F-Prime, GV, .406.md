# Arboretum LifeSciences launched with a $30M Series A led by F-Prime, GV, .406 Ventures, Hims & Hers and Amgen. The Cambridge, MA company runs clinical genetic testing for health systems in common diseases (cardiovascular, metabolic, autoimmune) and builds molecular datasets for genomically stratified drug trials.

HQ: Cambridge, MA
Founders: leaders from the Broad Institute's Data Sciences Platform; CEO Anthony Philippakis (also a GP at GV), President Clare Bernard
Health-system partners: Advocate Health, Geisinger (MyCode-Connect), Providence, Cardiovascular Associates of America
Business: genetic testing plus a research network for health systems; patient matching, trial design and target-discovery datasets for biopharma
Reported by: Pulse 2.0, FinSMEs, PitchBook News
