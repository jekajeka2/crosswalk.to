# Rendezvous Robotics raised $20M seed co-led by MaC Venture Capital and Toyota Ventures. The Golden, Colo. company builds modular spacecraft tiles that launch separately and autonomously assemble, expand and reconfigure in orbit, based on MIT's TESSERAE research.

Other investors: University of Michigan Endowment, Astera (co-founded by Jed McCaleb), Industrious Ventures, and existing investors Aurelia Foundry, ATX Venture Partners, Mana Ventures
First priced round; oversubscribed
HQ: Golden, CO
Founders: Joe Landon (CEO) and Dr. Ariel Ekblaw (board chair; invented TESSERAE at MIT and founded the Aurelia Institute)
Use of funds: next-generation spacecraft development, flight demonstrations, team expansion
Milestones: parabolic flight campaign November 2026; final ISS TESSERAE demo of 32 tiles December 2026; low-earth-orbit demonstration of seven tiles no earlier than April 2028
Reported by: PR Newswire
