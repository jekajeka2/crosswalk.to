# Volantis raised $88M Series A co-led by Lachy Groom and Abstract Ventures. Volantis is building A-1, an AI inference system that uses a photonic chip-to-memory interconnect to pool off-chip memory. The company says this will let it serve 20T+ parameter models at up to 10,000 tokens/sec per user.

- Other investors: John Doerr, VXI Capital, Triatomic, Susa Ventures; angels Dwarkesh Patel, Naveen Rao, Sholto Douglas
- HQ: San Francisco, CA
- CEO and co-founder: Tapa Ghosh; founding team from NVIDIA, AMD, Broadcom and Ayar Labs (first CoWoS product, first high-volume tunable VCSELs, early co-packaged optics)
- Tech: custom micro-VCSELs on the gallium arsenide supply chain, which avoids indium phosphide constraints, with links under 1 pJ/bit. It aims to raise memory capacity and bandwidth together by nearly two orders of magnitude
- Use of funds: develop and commercialize A-1, grow the engineering team, move toward customer deployments
- Traction: plans first integrated inference engines to customers in 2027
- Reported by: PR Newswire
