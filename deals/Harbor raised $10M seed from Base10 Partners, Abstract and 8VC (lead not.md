# Harbor raised $10M seed from Base10 Partners, Abstract and 8VC (lead not named). The San Francisco company assesses the risk of enterprise AI agents (mapping agent activity against regulatory frameworks, monitoring operational and security risk in real time) and insures companies against agent-caused losses, starting with regulated industries such as banking and insurance.

Investors: Base10 Partners, Abstract, 8VC, NFX, Restive, Innovation Endeavors
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Jesús Santos, Daniel Elechiguerra, Cristian Bartolomé, Luis Botín
Use of funds: build out its risk platform and insurance products for AI agent deployments in regulated industries
Reported by: The SaaS News

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/harbor-raises-10m-seed
