# Offe Market raised $3M seed led by Slauson & Co. The Los Angeles startup runs an off-price online marketplace selling brands' excess fashion, beauty, home and lifestyle inventory at 30-80% off, with software that syncs directly to brands' inventory systems.

Other investors: InvestTN, Baukunst (led the pre-seed); angels Jill Fisher Bowen, Stephanie Kleinjan, Ruffin Mitchener
Total raised: $4.3M
HQ: Los Angeles, CA
Founder: Rachel Gannon (CEO; previously at Rue La La, Macy's and ban.do)
Use of funds: AI-powered pricing, personalized discovery and live commerce
Traction: revenue, shopper count and order volume up 2.5x year over year; 200+ brands including Harper Wilde, OBEY, Wrangler and Dolce Vita; an Echo Park store doubles as a content studio
Reported by: PR Newswire
