# Truck Parking Club raised an undisclosed growth investment led by Argentum Capital Partners. The Ringgold, Ga. company runs a marketplace of 7,200+ instantly reservable truck and trailer parking and storage locations, turning unused space at warehouses, trucking yards, storage sites and hotels into bookable parking.

Investor: Argentum Capital Partners, a New York growth equity firm; managing partner Daniel Raynor joins the board
HQ: Ringgold, GA
Founders: Evan Shelley, co-founder and CEO
Use of funds: add locations through dedicated supply teams, invest in technology, and expand direct work with fleets
Traction: 7,200+ locations in three and a half years; hundreds of thousands of trucks and trailers parked; drivers at 94 of the top 100 trucking companies have booked through the platform
Reported by: Truck Parking Club (PR Newswire)

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/truck-parking-club-secures-growth-investment-from-argentum-capital-partners-302913531.html
