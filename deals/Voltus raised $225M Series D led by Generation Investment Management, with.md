# Voltus raised $225M Series D led by Generation Investment Management, with Activate Capital and Vitol co-leading. The San Francisco company runs a distributed energy resource platform and virtual power plant that aggregates batteries, building cooling, distributed generation, EVs and flexible industrial load and dispatches them into US and Canadian power markets.

Round: oversubscribed Series D; Generation Investment Management led, Activate Capital and Vitol co-led
Other investors: Broadscale Group, Climate Investment, NGP, Ajax Strategies, All Aboard Fund (CTVC lists All Aboard Coalition)
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Use of funds: grow the platform to 20 GW of flexible capacity by 2030; expand its Bring Your Own Capacity program in the PJM and MISO markets; add more connected batteries and other distributed energy resources
Pitch: uses capacity already connected to the grid to meet demand from data centers, AI and electrification instead of relying only on new generation
Context: NERC expects North American peak demand to rise 24% over the next decade
CTVC listed it as the largest climate round of the week of 10/4-10/11
Reported by: Pulse 2.0, CTVC
