# Optivolt raised $32M Series A led by VXI Capital. The San Francisco company embeds active power electronics (its Power Balancer) inside solar panels to shift energy among cell groups in real time, recovering output lost to shade, dirt and uneven degradation and building in rapid shutdown and monitoring; 20,000+ panels with it have shipped.

Other investors: RA Capital, Valo Ventures, Atlas Innovate; angels include Enphase founder and former CEO Paul Nahi, former SunPower CEO Tom Werner, and former New York energy official Richard Kauffman
Total raised: $42M
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founder and CEO: Rohit Kalyanpur (nine years of development, per the company)
Use of funds: scale manufacturing and deploy into residential, commercial and utility-scale solar, including projects powering AI data centers
Traction: 20,000+ panels with Power Balancer shipped; 99.4% conversion efficiency under typical conditions; up to 6x the power of panels with external optimizers in internal shading tests (company figure)
IP: 19 issued patents, 27 pending; UL certifications for its junction-box enclosure, panel electronics and rapid-shutdown system
Reported by: Pulse 2.0, FinSMEs, PitchBook News
