# Aventra Defense Systems raised $17.5M undisclosed-stage round led by Konvoy. The Herndon, Va. company builds Piranha, a low-cost, balloon-deployed autonomous precision payload that reenters from the stratosphere and glides long range to deliver ISR or kinetic effects; it has flown 400 flights including in the Army's Arcane Thunder 26 exercise.

Other investors: Lavrock Ventures (existing), Booz Allen Ventures, Sands Capital, Langford Industries; the total includes a funding facility from Western Alliance Bank
HQ: Herndon, VA
Founders: Michael Weigand (CEO), Brian Retherford and Jessup Meng, former Army officers; founded 2025
Use of funds: scale Piranha production, speed up flight testing, add long-range strike, one-way attack and ISR platforms
Traction: first series of delivery contracts; fielded overseas within a year; tested with U.S., allied and partner military units
Reported by: PR Newswire
