# Ultra Security raised $10M seed, lead not named, as it emerged from stealth. The Birmingham, Mich. startup sits between autonomous AI agents and the systems they access, giving real-time visibility, policy enforcement, governance and anomaly detection over agents' tool calls and data access.

Investors: Ludlow Ventures, Hidden Capital, Entrée Capital, Detroit Venture Partners, firedrop, FDVC, plus angels from the security and SaaS communities
HQ: Birmingham, MI
Founded 2025 by Chase Lee, former founder and CEO of Trustpage
Use of funds: scale development of its agentic security platform for monitoring and controlling AI agents in real time
Reported by: The SaaS News, Business Wire

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/ultra-security-raises-10m-seed
