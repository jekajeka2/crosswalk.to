# DrizzleX raised $6.8M Series A-2 led by Israel Investment Fund Group. The Santa Fe Springs, Calif. company sells fixture-level water monitoring for multifamily and commercial real estate: patented micrometers track usage minute by minute so owners can find leaks, rank repairs by cost and bill tenants for water.

Other investors: a consortium of DrizzleX customers (property owners and operators using the platform)
HQ: Santa Fe Springs, CA
Founder: CEO Esther Altura (co-founder); founding year given as 2026 by The SaaS News
Use of funds: scale sales and go-to-market, streamline hardware manufacturing, key hires, expand partner network (property management platform integrations, plumbing and billing partners), new data products from its water metrics
Reported by: The SaaS News (citing a National Law Review press release)

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/drizzlex-raises-6-8m-series-a-2
