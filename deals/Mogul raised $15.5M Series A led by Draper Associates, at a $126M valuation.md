# Mogul raised $15.5M Series A led by Draper Associates, at a $126M valuation. The AI-powered real estate investing platform lets everyday investors buy into individual vetted residential properties and receive monthly distributions; it uses AI across sourcing, underwriting support and asset monitoring.

Other investors: Stephen Sullivan (BlueChew co-founder, largest check) and Dr. Alex Jovanovich, Draper Dragon, Avalanche Blizzard Fund, Jason Calacanis' LAUNCH, Venture Togethers, Gaingels, angels
Round was oversubscribed: 55% above its original $10M target
Total raised: over $23.6M
Valuation: $126M
HQ: Washington, DC (per release dateline and SEC filing)
Founders: Alex Blackwood (CEO) and Joey Gumataotao, ex-Goldman Sachs Real Estate Investment Group; founded 2022
Use of funds: scale AI infrastructure, expand property portfolio, new revenue streams; hiring in engineering, data science and supply
Traction: cash-flow positive; 150+ assets managed with four supply staff; 14.5x 2024-2025 revenue growth, 5.6x YoY revenue, 4x YoY customers; 11 employees; on pace for $9M revenue run rate by year end, targeting $60M within 18 months
SEC Form D (mogul Technologies Inc., filed 2026-10-22): $15.5M of equity sold of a $15.5M offering; 20 investors; minimum investment $82K; first sale 2026-08-06; related persons Alexander Blackwood (Executive Officer, Director), Joseph Gumataotao (Executive Officer, Director), Eitan Prince (Executive Officer); Delaware corporation, incorporated 2022: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2151460/000215146026000002/0002151460-26-000002-index.htm
Reported by: PR Newswire, SEC Form D
