# GMI Cloud raised $223M Series B led by ARCHIV, alongside a separate $445M credit facility led by CTBC ($668M in total). The Mountain View, Calif.-based company provides GPU infrastructure and inference services for AI workloads.

- Other equity investors: Nvidia, DSC Investment, Trend Micro, KB Investment, Kyobo Life, KT Corp
- Debt: $445M credit facility led by CTBC, separate from the equity round
- HQ: Mountain View, CA
- Founder: Alex Yeh
- Product: on-demand GPU compute, model inference and AI agent runtime services

Reported by: Axios Pro Rata, FinSMEs, Crunchbase Daily
