# Treso raised $3.75M pre-seed led by Redesign Health. The Kansas City startup is building a financial operating system for independent physician practices that combines banking, accounting, receivables, expense management and procurement, for practices it says have been left to piece their finances together with tools that are too expensive or disconnected.

Other investors: Locke Health, KCRise Fund
HQ: Kansas City, MO
Founders: Ryan Greenhaw and Zvika Badalov; founded 2026
Use of funds: expand the team and build out the platform ahead of a national rollout to independent medical practices
Reported by: Startland News, The SaaS News

Source: https://startlandnews.com/2026/10/treso-pre-seed-round
