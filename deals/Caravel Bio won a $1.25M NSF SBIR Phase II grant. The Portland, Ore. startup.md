# Caravel Bio won a $1.25M NSF SBIR Phase II grant. The Portland, Ore. startup tests millions of protein designs in parallel at a fraction of a cent each, using cell-free synthesis, microfluidic droplets and spore display, to generate training data for AI protein engineering, including proteins with non-canonical amino acids.

Award: $1,246,984 NSF SBIR Phase II grant (non-dilutive)
HQ: Portland, OR
Founder: Trevor Nicks, PhD (CEO); CSO Emily Bowman, PhD
New CTO: Michał Jastrzębski, formerly of Ginkgo Bioworks, GitHub and Intel
Use of funds: extend the platform to more enzyme classes for existing customers and scale products toward pilot deployment in DNA writing, carbon capture and rare earth separations
Completed its Phase I grant in 2024
Reported by: PR Newswire
