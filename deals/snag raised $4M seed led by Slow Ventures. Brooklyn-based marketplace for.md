# snag raised $4M seed led by Slow Ventures. Brooklyn-based marketplace for rooms, sublets and lease takeovers aimed at Gen Z renters in NYC, with ID-verified listers (3D face scan matched to government ID), escrowed rent and deposits, and AI-generated listings.

- Other investors: Precursor Ventures, Long Journey Ventures, Chamaeleon; angels include Brian Kelly (The Points Guy) and Sloane Stephens
- Round closed in ten weeks, oversubscribed
- HQ: Brooklyn, NY
- Founders: Selin Sonmez (CEO) and Niko Georgantas, who previously built a social network for apartment-building residents across 10,000 US buildings
- Traction: launched ten months ago; 30% month-over-month growth to 4,500+ NYC listings; average listing rented in three days; ~80% of listings are rooms; 90M organic TikTok views
- Business model: 10% platform fee on marketplace transactions
- Reported by: AlleyWatch
