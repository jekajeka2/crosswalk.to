# StimCardio raised $30M Series A led by Metis MedTech (John Slump's family office). The Los Angeles clinical-stage company is developing Rhyvive, a wrist-worn closed-loop median nerve stimulation wearable meant to cut atrial fibrillation recurrence after catheter ablation.

Other investors: Catalyst Health Ventures, multiple corporate strategic investors, other new and existing investors
Round described as oversubscribed
HQ: Los Angeles, CA
CEO: Amir Soltanianzadeh
Use of funds: the NeuroPulse 2 multicenter pivotal trial and preparation of an FDA De Novo submission
Traction: NeuroPulse 1 randomized, double-blind, sham-controlled pilot showed a statistically significant reduction in recurrent atrial arrhythmias post-ablation; presented as a late-breaker at HRX Live 2026 and published in Heart Rhythm
Reported by: PR Newswire

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/stimcardio-closes-oversubscribed-30-million-series-a-financing-to-advance-wearable-therapeutic-for-atrial-fibrillation-302922985.html
