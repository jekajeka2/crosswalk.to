# AuxoAI raised $20.7M Series A led by NTTVC. The San Francisco company embeds "Forward Deployed Experts" (AI strategists and engineers) with Global 2000 enterprises to build production AI systems, speeding deployments with its proprietary FDE Workbench, Damia.

Other investors: George Still Jr. (Partner Emeritus, Norwest Venture Partners), Amit Zavery (President, CPO and COO, ServiceNow), Giacomo Sonnino (Managing Director, Charlesbank Capital Partners), all joining as strategic investors and advisors
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Amaresh Tripathy, Arvinder Pal Singh and Kabir Singh; founded 2023
Use of funds: expand its global forward-deployed team, invest in proprietary technology and AI-native delivery, and grow its presence and partner ecosystem
Traction: its FDE team has worked with 40+ clients across manufacturing, consumer goods, healthcare and telecom; customers quoted include Athenahealth and Fogo de Chao; named one of Google's AI Pureplay Partners for the Gemini Enterprise Transformation program
Reported by: AuxoAI (PR Newswire), SiliconANGLE
