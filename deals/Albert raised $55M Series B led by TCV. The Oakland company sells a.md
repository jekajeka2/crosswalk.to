# Albert raised $55M Series B led by TCV. The Oakland company sells a chemistry-native AI operating system for enterprise R&D that ties scientific ontologies to formulation data, experiment records and regulatory compliance, so chemistry and materials teams can design experiments faster.

Other investors: Delta-v Capital, Socium Ventures, HighSage Ventures, Coral Capital, Legion VC, Coatue, J.P. Morgan Growth Equity Partners, Index Ventures, Applied Ventures
Total raised: $105M in equity (AlleyWatch)
HQ: Oakland, CA
Founders: Nick Talken (CEO), Ken Kisner, Neelesh Vaikhary, Zack Kisner; founded 2022 (AlleyWatch, which calls the company Albert Invent)
Use of funds: expand its chemistry-native AI capabilities to shorten development cycles and time to market for consumer products
Reported by: The SaaS News, Business Wire, AlleyWatch
