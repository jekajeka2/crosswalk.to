# Armadin raised $255.5M Series B co-led by Andreessen Horowitz and Accel, at a $2.5B+ post-money valuation. Founded by Mandiant founder Kevin Mandia, Armadin deploys AI agents that simulate cyberattacks to find exploitable attack paths for enterprises and government agencies.

- Other investors: Bain Capital Ventures, Redpoint; previous investors 8VC, Ballistic Ventures, Google Ventures, In-Q-Tel, Kleiner Perkins, Menlo Ventures
- Valuation: $2.5B+ post-money
- Total raised: $445M
- HQ: Palo Alto, CA
- Founder: Kevin Mandia (founder of Mandiant); company is about one year old
- Reported by: StrictlyVC, SecurityWeek

Source: https://www.securityweek.com/kevin-mandias-armadin-raises-255-million-at-2-5-billion-valuation
