# Armadin raised $255.5M Series B co-led by Andreessen Horowitz and Accel, at a $2.5B+ post-money valuation; its SEC Form D (filed 2026-10-15) reports $265M of equity sold to 34 investors. Founded by Mandiant founder Kevin Mandia, Armadin deploys AI agents that simulate cyberattacks to find exploitable attack paths for enterprises and government agencies.

- Other investors: Bain Capital Ventures, Redpoint; previous investors 8VC, Ballistic Ventures, Google Ventures, In-Q-Tel, Kleiner Perkins, Menlo Ventures
- Valuation: $2.5B+ post-money
- Total raised: $445M
- SEC Form D filed 2026-10-15: $265M equity sold of a $265M offering ($0 remaining), 34 investors, first sale 2026-09-30 (the announcement said $255.5M)
- Related persons per the filing: Kevin Mandia (executive officer, director); Travis Lanham (executive officer, director); Evan Pena, David Slater, Frank Verdecanna, Barbara Massa (executive officers); directors Jacob Seid, David George, Ping Li, George Kurtz, Paul Nakasone
- HQ: Palo Alto, CA (the Form D lists Menlo Park, CA); Delaware corporation, incorporated 2025
- Founder: Kevin Mandia (founder of Mandiant); company is about one year old
- Reported by: StrictlyVC, SecurityWeek, SEC Form D
