# SpendRule raised $4M seed led by Mark Cuban's Radical Investments. The Dallas startup uses AI to read contract terms, audit vendor invoices against them and automate recovery of overpayments, starting with health systems.

Other investors: Zeal Capital Partners
Total raised: $6M
HQ: Dallas, TX
Founders: co-CEOs Joseph Akintolayo and Chris Heckler; founded 2026
Use of funds: deepen work with healthcare systems, product development and engineering, expand contract enforcement to more businesses and industries
Reported by: GlobeNewswire, The SaaS News
