# Graylark raised $10.7M seed led by General Catalyst. The Boston company makes Raven, a visual intelligence platform that helps law enforcement and government investigators geolocate where images were captured, identify vehicles, and detect manipulated or AI-generated visual content.

Other investors: AI Grant, Alexandr Wang, Recorded Future
HQ: Boston, MA
Founder: Daniel Heinen
Use of funds: expand the research team, build purpose-built vision models, strengthen data infrastructure, bring Raven to more law-enforcement and government organizations
Funding date per the company: August 29, 2026
Reported by: The SaaS News (citing Graylark)

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/graylark-raises-10-7m-seed
