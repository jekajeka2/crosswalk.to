# Qualitate raised $25M Series A led by Insight Partners. The New York company's AI-native continuous intelligence system runs automated interviews with B2B buyers and industry experts and turns the conversations into searchable, evidence-grounded research, replacing project-based primary market research.

Other investors: IA Ventures, Crew Capital (existing)
HQ: New York, NY
Founder: CEO Sagar Kadakia
Use of funds: scale the AI-native platform and integrate it further into enterprise business workflows, making primary research faster and cheaper
Reported by: Business Wire, The SaaS News
