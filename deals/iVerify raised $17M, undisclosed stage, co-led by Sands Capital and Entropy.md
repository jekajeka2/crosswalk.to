Other investors: NAventures (National Bank of Canada), Morgan Stanley, United Airlines Ventures
HQ: New York, NY and Belfast, Northern Ireland
Founded: about three years ago
Reported by: StrictlyVC, Axios Pro Rata, National Law Review (press release)
