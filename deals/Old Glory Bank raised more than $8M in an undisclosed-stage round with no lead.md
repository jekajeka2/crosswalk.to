# Old Glory Bank raised more than $8M in an undisclosed-stage round with no lead named, from over 150 existing and new accredited investors under Reg D Rule 506. The Oklahoma digital bank raised it after the Federal Reserve declined to approve the holding company in its planned deSPAC, and says the money returned it to adequately capitalized status.

Investors: more than 150 existing and new accredited investors
Total raised: more than $67M from private investors (Old Glory Holding Company)
HQ: Elmore City, OK (its physical branch); online accounts since April 2023
Background: the deSPAC that would have taken new Texas holding company Old Glory Financial Company public on Nasdaq ended when the Fed did not approve bank holding company status; management now plans a direct listing of the existing holding company
Traction: more than 85,000 personal and business accounts across all 50 states; deposits grew from about $10M to $270M as of June 30, 2026
Products: accounts, lending, cards and payments, Old Glory Pay, Old Glory Cash-IN, crowdfunding platform Old Glory Alliance, and crypto-integrated Next Gen Banking
Leadership: co-founder, president and CEO Mike Ring
Reported by: Pulse 2.0
