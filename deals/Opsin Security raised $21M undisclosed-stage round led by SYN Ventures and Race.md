# Opsin Security raised $21M undisclosed-stage round led by SYN Ventures and Race Capital. The San Jose startup gives security teams one view of the AI agents employees build across Microsoft Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise and Gemini, tying each agent's purpose, identity, permissions, data and tools to its actual behavior to catch excess access and off-mission actions.

Leads: SYN Ventures and Race Capital (StrictlyVC lists them as investors without naming a lead)
HQ: San Jose, CA
Founders: CEO James Pham and Oz Wasserman (both ex-Abnormal Security) and engineering leader Jeremy Mailen; founded about two years ago
Data point: across its customers, 93% of misaligned tools connected to agents had full, unrestricted access; only 1 in 200 required human approval before acting
Customers: nearly 20 enterprises in healthcare, financial services and manufacturing, including Culligan, Barry-Wehmiller, Wellstar Health System and UiPath
Use of funds: expand the platform as agents spread across enterprise apps and workflows
Reported by: citybiz, Pulse 2.0, StrictlyVC
