# Coherence Neuro raised $45M Series A co-led by Founders Fund and the OpenAI Startup Fund. The San Francisco startup develops implanted devices and AI models that read electrical biomarkers of cancer to track tumor growth, aiming at closed-loop systems that also treat tumors with electrical stimulation.

Stage: Pulse 2.0 and Axios Pro Rata report a Series A; StrictlyVC called it a seed round
Other investors: new investors Lux Capital, 8VC, Timeless, Olive Capital, Ahren; angels including Neuralink head neurosurgeon Dr. Matthew MacDougall and Neuralink principal investigator Dr. Rory Murphy, Romina Nejad, Chad Byers, Maor Shlomo; existing investors Blackbird, Possible, Topology, Artesian, XEIA, Xtal, Divergent
HQ: San Francisco, CA (also expanding an office in Cambridge, UK)
Founded: about four years ago
Traction: validated in preclinical mouse brain-tumor models, where recordings near growing tumors trained models that predict growth trajectories; has begun testing devices in human trials
Use of funds: first chronic human trial in glioblastoma, FDA Breakthrough Device designation, preclinical expansion into pancreatic cancer and other indications
Reported by: Pulse 2.0, StrictlyVC, Axios Pro Rata, Dealroom
