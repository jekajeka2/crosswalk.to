# Avante raised $30M Series A led by Frist Cressey Ventures. The Seattle company's AI agents help employer benefits teams compare vendor contracts against actual claims, catch plan-document errors and prepare renewals, and let employees ask about their own coverage.

Other investors: Sorenson Capital, Hearst Ventures, Morgan Stanley Next Level Fund, Fuse (existing)
Total raised: $40M, after a $10M seed led by Fuse in 2024
HQ: Seattle, WA
Founded 2023 by Rohan D'Souza (CEO), Carly Eckert (head of innovation and impact) and Kabir Shahani (executive chairman, ex-CEO of Amperity)
Use of funds: international expansion and more AI agents, building on its 'Ava Agents' beta
Traction: 70+ customers including Samsung Semiconductor, Real Chemistry, Zscaler and News Corp; bookings up nearly 5x in a year; platform reaches 385,000+ employees and family members
Reported by: GeekWire, Axios Pro Rata

Source: https://www.geekwire.com/2026/seattle-startup-avante-raises-30m-to-bring-ai-agents-to-corporate-benefits-teams
