# Ascerta (formerly Pay-i) raised $18M Series A led by Dell Technologies Capital. Ascerta tracks enterprise AI spending, adoption and ROI by person, team and tool, across Microsoft Copilot, Bedrock AgentCore, Agentforce, GitHub Copilot and Claude Code. It started out in AI cost management and has since expanded into measuring business outcomes.

- Other investors: Hitachi Ventures, BGV, Wipro Ventures, existing backers
- Total raised: $22.9M, including a $4.9M seed in May 2025
- HQ: Bellevue, Wash.; founded in 2024 by Microsoft veterans David Tepper (CEO), Doron Holan and Erik Winters
- Use of funds: product expansion, go-to-market and engineering hires (15 people now, about 40 targeted by end of 2027), integrations with every major enterprise AI tool
- Customers include Atos and Wipro
- Source: GeekWire
