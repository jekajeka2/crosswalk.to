# Strella raised $4.25M Series A extension led by SilverBlue. The Seattle ag-tech startup uses IoT gas sensors (measuring ethylene released by ripening fruit) and machine vision to track produce ripeness from orchard to grocery store, so growers, packers, shippers and retailers can spot spoilage early and waste less.

Other investors: LeVert Ventures, Beta Milouot, PeakBridge
Total raised: $26.25M
HQ: Seattle, WA (founded in Philadelphia in 2019; moved to Seattle four years ago to be near Washington's orchards)
Founder: CEO and co-founder Katherine Sizov
Traction: 19-person team; customers in 26 countries; covers apples, pears, avocados and citrus, recently adding kiwifruit
Plan: become the "system of record for quality" across the food supply chain
Reported by: GeekWire
