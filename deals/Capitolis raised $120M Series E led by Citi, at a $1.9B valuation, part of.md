# Capitolis raised $120M Series E led by Citi, at a $1.9B valuation, part of $220M in total financing with debt. Capitolis runs a financial resource optimization platform for banks and institutional investors; the money funds its acquisition of securities lender eSecLending.

Total financing: $220M = $120M Series E equity + debt from First Citizens Innovation Banking, Hercules Capital and Pinegrove Venture Partners (Axios reports $100M of new debt)
New strategic investors: Bank of America, Nomura, Tradeweb Markets
Existing investors participating: Barclays, BNP Paribas, J.P. Morgan, State Street, UBS
Earlier VC backers: 9Yards Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, Canapi Ventures, Index Ventures, S Capital, Sequoia Capital, Spark Capital
Total raised: $457.8M in reported equity funding (AlleyWatch)
Valuation: $1.9B
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Gil Mandelzis (CEO), Igor Teleshevsky and Thomas Glocer; founded 2017
Use of funds: the acquisition of eSecLending (announced Sept. 29 for $200M cash), adding securities lending and a network of institutional asset owners
Businesses: Capital Marketplace and Portfolio Optimization
Reported by: GlobeNewswire (company release), AlleyWatch, Axios Pro Rata
