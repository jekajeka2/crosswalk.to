# CScale raised $145M Series C led by Atreides Management, Valor Equity Partners and Premji Invest. CScale builds optical interconnects that link thousands of AI accelerators across dozens of racks into large scale-up clusters, and says its design contains optical-link failures to keep clusters up.

- Other investors: Nvidia, Intel Capital, existing backers Sutter Hill Ventures and Maverick Silicon
- Total raised: $188M; the company exited stealth with the announcement
- SEC Form D (filed 2026-10-01): $145M sold of a $195M equity offering, $50M remaining, 37 investors, first sale 2026-07-17
- Directors per the filing: Martin Lund and Sanjai Kohli (executive officers), Stefan A. Dyckerhoff, Gavin S. Baker, Sandesh Patnam
- HQ: Palo Alto, CA
- Founded in 2023 by Sanjai Kohli (CTO, founder of SiRF); CEO Martin Lund, formerly head of Cisco's chip engineering group and Broadcom's switch business (the Form D gives 2022 as the incorporation year)
- Product: an "integrated light engine" planned as a system-on-chip with an onboard CPU
- Use of funds: technology development and commercialization
- Reported by: StrictlyVC, Business Wire, SiliconANGLE, Axios Pro Rata, FinSMEs, SEC Form D
