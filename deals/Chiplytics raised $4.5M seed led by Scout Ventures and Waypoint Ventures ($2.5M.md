# Chiplytics raised $4.5M seed led by Scout Ventures and Waypoint Ventures ($2.5M seed plus a $2M Air Force TACFI award). The Seattle startup uses AI, automation and robotics to screen every semiconductor chip and electronic component for counterfeits and defects, rather than spot-checking samples.

Other investors: QKSS, Industrious Ventures, Interwoven Ventures, Seattle Alliance of Angels, Cornell's Red Bear Angels
Round breakdown: $2.5M seed financing + $2M Air Force Tactical Funding Increase award
HQ: Seattle, WA (R&D); new inspection lab in Bellport, NY inside a Quality King distribution center
Founders: Steven Dourmashkin (CEO), Stephen DiBartolomeo (president, ex-NSA cryptologic warfare officer); CTO Connor Dempsey
Founded: 2022, via a Department of Energy lab-to-market program with Sandia National Laboratories; validated with the U.S. Army, Navy and Air Force
Use of funds: deploying its verification technology and the new East Coast inspection facility
Team: six full-time employees
Reported by: GeekWire
