# Otter Health raised $8M seed co-led by Fika Ventures and Evidenced. The Washington, DC healthcare technology company launched an AI-powered, EHR-agnostic platform that lets payers and providers measure care decisions against clinical evidence in real time, as an alternative to traditional prior authorization.

Other investors: Slow Ventures, Metrodora, CoFound Partners, Layout Ventures, Patient Capital, Semper Virens
HQ: Washington, DC (per announcement dateline)
Co-founder and CEO: Keith Florance
What it does: continuous comparison of referrals, outpatient procedures, pathway adherence and care transitions to the evidence standard, with peer benchmarking and targeted clinician engagement
Traction: working with multiple payer and provider organizations; says it has kept non-concordant decisions under 5% after removing prior authorization
Reported by: PR Newswire
