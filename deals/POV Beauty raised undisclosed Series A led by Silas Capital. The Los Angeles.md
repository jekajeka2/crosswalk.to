# POV Beauty raised undisclosed Series A led by Silas Capital. The Los Angeles skin-prep cosmetics brand, co-founded in 2025 by beauty creator Mikayla Nogueira and CEO Ani Hadjinian, sells skincare-makeup hybrid prep products and launched in all Sephora stores nationwide this year.

Other investors: RÉDO Capital; existing investor Imaginary Ventures (led the pre-launch institutional round)
Amount: undisclosed
HQ: Los Angeles, CA
Founders: Mikayla Nogueira (co-founder), Ani Hadjinian (co-founder, CEO); brand launched 2025
Use of funds: accelerate growth in Sephora, expand the team, grow on TikTok Shop, and build a pipeline of skincare-makeup hybrid products
Traction: nationwide launch in all Sephora stores and online; recently rolled out a community app
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
