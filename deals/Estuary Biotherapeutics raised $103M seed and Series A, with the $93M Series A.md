# Estuary Biotherapeutics raised $103M seed and Series A, with the $93M Series A led by RA Capital Management. The Menlo Park, CA biotech came out of stealth to take a pair of gene therapies for an inherited kidney disorder into clinical trials.

Round: $93M Series A led by RA Capital Management plus $10M in previously undisclosed seed capital, $103M total
Other investors: 4BIO Capital, GV, Deep Track Capital, Biogen New Ventures, UPMC Enterprises
SEC Form D filed 2026-10-16: $40.7M sold of a $103.7M equity offering ($63M remaining), 25 investors so far, first sale 2026-07-01
Related persons as filed: Wayne E. Sackett (Executive Officer, Director); Jacob Bauer, Owen Smith, Emily Minkow (Directors)
HQ: Menlo Park, CA (Delaware corporation, incorporated 2022)
Use of funds: advance two gene therapy programs for an inherited kidney disorder into the clinic
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata, Fierce Biotech, Crunchbase News, SEC Form D
