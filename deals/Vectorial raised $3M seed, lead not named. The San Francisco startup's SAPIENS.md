# Vectorial raised $3M seed, lead not named. The San Francisco startup's SAPIENS model builds behavior models of a company's target audience from real data and simulates how people react to product journeys, marketing campaigns and AI agents, so product, design and marketing teams can test before building.

Investors: Smilegate, SenseAI Ventures, Berkeley SkyDeck, Berkeley AI Research Lab, Stanford GSB fund, Dent Capital, plus product leaders from Meta, Shopify and Mastercard
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Rahul Garg, Jasmine Kaur, Taranveer Singh
Use of funds: expand behavioral datasets through new data partnerships; develop SAPIENS 2.0 as generative simulation infrastructure for product development and launch
Reported by: GlobeNewswire, The SaaS News, SiliconANGLE

Source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/20/3384464/0/en/human-behavior-simulation-platform-vectorial-raises-3m-to-help-companies-launch-successful-products-the-first-time.html
