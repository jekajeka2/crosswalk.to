# Re:Build raised $2.1B undisclosed-stage financing led by General Catalyst and JPMorgan Chase's Strategic Investment group. The Massachusetts company designs integrated factory environments and provides manufacturing services that take startups' hardware from concept to full-scale US production, and incubates industrial startups.

Leads: General Catalyst; JPMorgan Chase's Strategic Investment advisory group
Total raised: $4B+ (per StrictlyVC)
HQ: Cambridge, MA per The New York Times DealBook and Axios Pro Rata; StrictlyVC lists Framingham, MA
Founded 2020 by Jeff Wilke (former head of Amazon's global retail business), William Barker and Miles Arnone (CEO)
About 15 factory sites in the U.S.; expects to triple its footprint in coming years
Revenue up 25-30% a year over the past several years, per the CEO
Customers span aerospace, defense, robotics and automation; incubated EV maker Slate Auto
General Catalyst CEO Hemant Taneja: the goal is a "domestic Shenzhen"
Axios Pro Rata: https://axios.link/4in3D0e
Reported by: Re:Build (GlobeNewswire), The New York Times DealBook (first reported by Niko Gallogly), Axios Pro Rata, StrictlyVC
