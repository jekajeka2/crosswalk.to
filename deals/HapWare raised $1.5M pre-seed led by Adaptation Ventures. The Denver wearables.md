# HapWare raised $1.5M pre-seed led by Adaptation Ventures. The Denver wearables startup makes ALEYE, a haptic wristband that pairs with Meta smart glasses or a phone camera to turn facial expressions, gestures and body language into vibration patterns for blind, low-vision and DeafBlind users; TIME named it a 2026 Best Invention.

Other investors: Sidecut Ventures, Mines Venture Fund, Avesta Fund, The Pitch Fund and others; round oversubscribed
Total raised: $1.8M in equity (AlleyWatch)
HQ: Denver, CO (AlleyWatch says Golden, CO)
Founders: Jack Walters (CEO, co-founder), Dr. Bryan Duarte (CTO, co-founder); founded 2025 (AlleyWatch)
Use of funds: launch ALEYE, shipping in 2027 at a $359 preorder price
Traction: US distribution lined up with organizations including Braille Institute of America and Society for the Blind plus assistive-tech distributors; plans a haptic SDK for developers
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement), AlleyWatch
