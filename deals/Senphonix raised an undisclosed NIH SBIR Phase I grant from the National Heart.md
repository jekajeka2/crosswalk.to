# Senphonix raised an undisclosed NIH SBIR Phase I grant from the National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute. The Minneapolis medtech startup is developing SleeveSense, an upper-arm sleeve worn for days to months that continuously monitors vital signs in the hospital and at home.

Grant: NIH SBIR Phase I via NHLBI; amount not disclosed
Prior funding: NSF STTR Phase I award announced in September; other non-dilutive support from Microsoft, the Arizona Commerce Authority (WearTech Applied Research Center) and the University of Arizona
HQ: Minneapolis, MN
Founders: Mike Haldane and John Grant, PhD (co-founders and co-CEOs)
Use of funds: clinical-environment testing of the software stack, cloud and database scaling, and cybersecurity work toward SOC 2 Type II
Plans: clinical study in late 2026 or early 2027; FDA 510(k) submission early to mid-2027
Business model: no upfront hospital cost; billed only when a sleeve is assigned to a patient
Reported by: PR Newswire
