# Bedrock Robotics raised $600M Series C co-led by CapitalG and Emergence Capital, at a $4.1B valuation. The San Francisco company retrofits existing heavy construction equipment, starting with large excavators, to run fully autonomously on live commercial job sites.

Other investors: new investors US Innovative Technology Fund (USIT), Bessemer Venture Partners, Temasek; existing investors 8VC, Georgian, Valor Equity Partners, Xora, Eclipse, Perry Creek Capital, NVIDIA and others
Total raised: nearly $1B
Valuation: $4.1B
HQ: San Francisco, CA
CEO and co-founder: Boris Sofman; co-founder and VP engineering: Ajay Gummalla
Use of funds: expand deployments of Bedrock-equipped machines across US commercial construction sites, strengthen field operations, scale hardware and supply chain
Traction: expanded from the West and Central South into the Midwest and South Atlantic; anchor partnership with Southwest contractor Haydon to automate earthwork in Texas; regional partners in Minnesota, Florida, Georgia, Missouri and Virginia; machines working on data center, healthcare and heavy civil sites; 2027 target of triple-digit autonomous machines
Next: autonomous trench work, then bulldozers, ADTs and wheel loaders; longer term mining and agriculture
New hires: Amy Jones Satrom (VP operations, ex-Amazon, Nuro, Uber); Lando Juarez (general counsel, ex-Uber Autonomous Mobility & Delivery)
Reported by: The Robot Report
