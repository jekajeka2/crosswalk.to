# Quotr raised $4M seed led by Llama Ventures. Quotr is an AI-native construction estimation and procurement platform: contractors upload plan sets and it runs quantity takeoff, cost estimating, bidding and proposal generation in one workflow, while tracking material prices and supplier availability.

- Other investors: strategic angel investors
- HQ: San Francisco, CA
- CEO: Hanyang Liu
- Customers: enterprise contractors and developers
- Use of funds: expand enterprise deployments, grow its supplier network, scale the platform, and enter heavy industrial and mission-critical construction
- Reported by: The SaaS News, FinSMEs

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/quotr-raises-4m-seed
