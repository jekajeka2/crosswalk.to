# Formic raised $40M Series B led by Mercato Partners, plus $10M in asset-backed financing from S2G Investments ($50M total). The six-year-old Bolingbrook, Ill. company deploys and operates robots for manufacturers on a subscription (robotics-as-a-service) model.

Round terms: $40M Series B equity plus $10M asset-backed debt from S2G Investments, $50M total (split per Axios Pro)
Other investors: Light Ray Ventures, Lightscape Partners, Citi
HQ: Bolingbrook, IL (Tech Startups and FinSMEs say Chicago)
Founded: 2020 (six years old)
Traction: hundreds of thousands of hours of real production experience (Tech Startups)
Reported by: Business Wire, StrictlyVC, Axios Pro Rata, FinSMEs, Tech Startups
