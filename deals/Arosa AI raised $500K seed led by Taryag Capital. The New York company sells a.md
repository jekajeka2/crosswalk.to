The New York company sells a conversational AI workspace for real estate developers, owners and investors that goes from a property address to a development strategy, combining zoning research, entitlement analysis, 3D development concepts and project financial modeling.

Round terms: Taryag Capital put in $100K in cash and $400K in AI credits
HQ: New York, NY
Use of funds: product development and research roadmap
Reported by: The SaaS News, FinSMEs
