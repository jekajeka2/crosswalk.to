# Foundry Robotics raised $100M Series A led by Marlinspike Partners. The Emeryville, Calif. startup builds and operates autonomous factories for complex assembly, designed around AI-powered robots organized into skill-based work cells (cabling, fastening, precise placement) rather than product lines.

Other investors: Khosla Ventures (led the seed), Red Glass Ventures, SE Ventures, Wing VC, Axiom Ventures, Hanabi, Zero Shot Fund
HQ: Emeryville, CA
Founded: less than a year ago
Reported by: The Information, StrictlyVC

Source: https://www.theinformation.com/articles/foundry-raises-100-million-autonomous-factories
