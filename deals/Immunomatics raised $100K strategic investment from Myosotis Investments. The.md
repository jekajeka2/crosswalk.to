# Immunomatics raised $100K strategic investment from Myosotis Investments. The San Marcos, CA healthtech builds AI clinical decision support that scores donor-recipient risk for blood stem cell and solid organ transplants, using machine learning on HLA typing and outcomes data.

Investor: Myosotis Investments B.V., the life sciences investment firm of GenDx co-founder Wietse Mulder, who also joins as strategic advisor
HQ: San Marcos, CA
Founder: Lidio Meireles
Use of funds: validation and commercialization of its AI donor risk assessment, expanding clinical data, advancing regulatory strategy
Reported by: The SaaS News, PR Newswire
