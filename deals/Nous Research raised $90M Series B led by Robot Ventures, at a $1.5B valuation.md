Valuation: $1.5B

Context: TechCrunch reported in July that Nous was finalizing a round of at least $75M at the same valuation; the completed round came in at $90M
Reported by: Tech Startups (Startup Funding News Today, October 7, 2026), citing The Wall Street Journal
