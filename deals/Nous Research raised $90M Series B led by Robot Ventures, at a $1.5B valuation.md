# Nous Research raised $90M Series B led by Robot Ventures, at a $1.5B valuation. The company builds open-source AI models and AI agents.

Other investors: Nvidia, Microsoft's M12, Samsung, USV, 1789 Capital
Total raised: about $160M (Tech Startups' estimate: roughly $70M raised before this round)
Valuation: $1.5B
HQ: New York, NY per Tech Startups; Axios Pro Rata lists it as Austin-based
Context: TechCrunch reported in July that Nous was finalizing a round of at least $75M at the same valuation; the completed round came in at $90M
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata, Tech Startups (citing The Wall Street Journal)
