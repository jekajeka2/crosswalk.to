# Beltic raised $7.3M seed led by Norwest. Beltic builds "know your agent" (KYA) infrastructure: it verifies the identity, authority and legitimacy of AI agents' transactions in real time, so merchants, payment providers and card networks can accept agent traffic. With a $1.5M pre-seed, it has raised $8.8M in total.

- Other seed investors: Restive Ventures, Oxford Seed Fund, Collide Capital (which led the $1.5M pre-seed; Latitud Ventures, Positive Ventures and angels joined that round)
- Total raised: $8.8M; the company emerged from stealth with this announcement
- HQ: San Francisco; founded in 2025; CEO Isha Bhatnagar was previously at Coinbase
- Use of funds: agent verification infrastructure, including verifying agent-to-agent transactions; now onboarding design partners
- Reported by: PR Newswire, Axios Pro Rata
