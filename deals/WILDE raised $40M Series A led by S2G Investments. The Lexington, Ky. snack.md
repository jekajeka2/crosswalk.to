# WILDE raised $40M Series A led by S2G Investments. The Lexington, Ky. snack brand makes protein chips and crackers from chicken breast, egg whites and bone broth (10+ grams of protein per serving), sold in 35,000+ US retail locations, and has passed $100M in sales.

Stage: Series A per Axios Pro; the company's release calls it a $40M investment from S2G Investments
HQ: Lexington, KY
Founded 2015 by Jason Wright (founder and CEO)
Use of funds: scale production, extend beyond its core Protein Chips into new snack formats after the recent Protein Crackers launch, and update brand identity, packaging and marketing
Traction: more than $100M in sales; 35,000+ retail locations nationwide; recently opened a 130,000 sq ft production facility, which it calls the only USDA-regulated salty snack plant in the world, raising capacity sixfold
Reported by: PR Newswire, Axios Pro Rata
