# Notey raised $3M seed led by Tivoli Ventures, co-led by Malta Venture Capital. New York music-education company that pairs game design with an adaptive curriculum to make instrument practice engaging, and gives teachers visibility into students' practice between lessons.

Other support: a $1M non-dilutive development grant from Malta Enterprise
HQ: New York, NY (also operates in Columbia, SC and Valletta, Malta)
Founders: Dr. Mak Grgić, Adithya Bellathur and Yishai Pinchover; founded 2023
Use of funds: deeper platform engagement, new instruments and partnerships, opening its European operation in Malta, hiring in the US and Malta, and rolling out its AI-native WorldBuilder platform
Partnerships: distribution partnership with Telemach
Reported by: The SaaS News, EIN Presswire

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/notey-raises-3m-seed
