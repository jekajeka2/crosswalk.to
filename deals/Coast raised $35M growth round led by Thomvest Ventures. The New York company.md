# Coast raised $35M growth round led by Thomvest Ventures. The New York company is a financial platform for the physical economy (construction, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, transportation, utilities and other field businesses): it started with a fuel card and now sells corporate cards, expense management, Bill Pay and Accounts Payable with vehicle- and driver-level controls and job costing.

Other investors: new investors Intuit Ventures, Small World Capital, Alpha Square Group; existing investors ICONIQ Growth, Accel, Insight Partners, Better Tomorrow Ventures, Avid Ventures, Vintage Investment Partners, Colle Capital, Synchrony, RXR, The Fintech Fund
HQ: New York, NY
Founder and CEO: Daniel Simon
Use of funds: reach more customers; invest in AI-powered expense automation, new Bill Pay and Accounts Payable products, and embedded partnerships with fleet and field service platforms
Product: telematics-enabled card controls, fuel grade and gallon-level detail, job costing, accounting integrations; partners include Samsara and Fleetio
Thomvest first backed Coast in 2024
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
