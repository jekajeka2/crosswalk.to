# Sensydia raised $6M+ in an undisclosed-stage round led by Colle Capital Partners. The clinical-stage Los Angeles medtech makes the Cardiac Performance System (CPS), which uses AI on acoustic and electrical heart signals to estimate pulmonary pressures without a catheter, for heart failure and pulmonary hypertension care in the office.

Other investors: Orlando Health Ventures (substantial support)
Terms: oversubscribed initial closing of more than $6M in its latest financing
HQ: Los Angeles, CA
CEO: Anthony Arnold; advisor Bill Hawkins (retired Medtronic chairman and CEO)
Use of funds: complete the ACOUSTIC-1 study through FDA authorization and prepare commercial launch
Regulatory: CPS has FDA Breakthrough Device designation but is not yet FDA-authorized; Sensydia got 510(k) clearance in 2018 for an ejection-fraction algorithm
Reimbursement: a new Category III CPT code, 1036T, for non-invasive hemodynamic assessment took effect in July 2026
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
