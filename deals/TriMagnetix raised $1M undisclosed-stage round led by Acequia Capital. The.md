# TriMagnetix raised $1M undisclosed-stage round led by Acequia Capital. The two-person Seattle startup builds nanomagnetic, radiation-resistant chips that run on electrical pulses and, in initial lab tests, use 800x less energy than a traditional chip for the same data; it is targeting satellites first.

Other investors: Athenaeum Ventures; Actuate Ventures has committed to the round (GeekWire says "up to $1M" combined across the three firms)
Prior funding: $200K from climate fund SNØCAP last year
HQ: Seattle, WA
Founders: Madison Hanberry (founded 2023; moving from chief of R&D to CEO), Lucas Hahn (co-founder, president and chief strategic officer), Aspen White (co-founder)
Traction: first paid pilot with WaveWorks Technologies; in the Plug and Play Semiconductor Accelerator and Nvidia Inception
Use of funds: continued chip development, fabricated at the University of Washington's Washington Nanofabrication Facility; aims for a commercial product within the next year
Reported by: GeekWire
