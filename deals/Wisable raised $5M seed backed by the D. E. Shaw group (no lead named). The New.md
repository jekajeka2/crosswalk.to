# Wisable raised $5M seed backed by the D. E. Shaw group (no lead named). The New York firm runs technology-enabled M&A advisory for owners of small and midsize businesses with at least $1M in annual revenue, using AI for financial analysis, buyer outreach and due diligence.

Investors: the D. E. Shaw group
HQ: New York, NY
Founded: advising small business owners since 2024
CEO: Bryan O'Connell, previously co-founder and CEO of Huckleberry (acquired by Paychex in 2023), later GM of a Paychex subsidiary, ex-McKinsey and Morgan Stanley
Use of funds: expand its technology-enabled M&A advisory model as more small business owners approach retirement, succession or exits
Traction: team with 80+ years of combined experience across about $300M in transaction value, in home services, automotive services and light manufacturing
Reported by: Pulse 2.0

Source: https://pulse2.com/wisable-raises-5-million-seed-funding-backed-by-the-d-e-shaw-group
