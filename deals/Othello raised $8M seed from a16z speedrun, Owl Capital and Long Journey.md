# Othello raised $8M seed from a16z speedrun, Owl Capital and Long Journey Ventures (lead not named). The New York applied AI lab trains its own model, Hamlet, to coach sales reps live during calls, popping up suggestions in under 500 milliseconds in any online meeting without joining as a participant.

Investors: a16z speedrun, Owl Capital, Long Journey Ventures
Angels: Andy Fang (DoorDash), Chris Larsen (Ripple), Thomas Wolf (Hugging Face), David Cramer (Sentry), Waseem AlShikh (Writer), Dean Sysman (Axonius), Alex and Philippe Bouaziz (Deel), Kevin Stein (TransDigm CEO)
HQ: New York, NY
Co-founder and CEO: Jared Zelman
Traction: deployed at 50+ large enterprises including several Fortune 500 companies; customers include Microsoft, Capital One, Lyft, a top-10 accounting firm and a top-10 asset manager; reps use it on 95%+ of calls
Product: desktop app that starts with the call and shows live pop-ups; serves insurance, accounting, financial services, asset management, software, cybersecurity, healthcare and manufacturing sales teams
Reported by: PR Newswire

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/othello-raises-8-million-for-real-time-ai-sales-coaching-during-live-calls-302908716.html
