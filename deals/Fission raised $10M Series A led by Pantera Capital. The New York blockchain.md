# Fission raised $10M Series A led by Pantera Capital. The New York blockchain fintech builds a liquidity layer for tokenized investment funds, letting eligible holders of tokenized private credit and alternative funds get capital without waiting for redemption windows, with transfer and compliance controls built in.

Other investors: Circle Ventures, Borderless Capital, Ari Rubenstein (CEO of GTS), plus existing backers New Form, gumi Cryptos Capital, Anthony Scaramucci, Manhattan Venture Partners, Reflexive Capital, Lattice Capital
HQ: New York, NY
Founder and CEO: Jonathan Shaffer; team from Point72 and Securitize
Use of funds: expand asset coverage, liquidity capacity and institutional integrations
Traction: has processed millions of dollars in transactions
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
