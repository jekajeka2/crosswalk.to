# Soufflé Therapeutics raised $135M Series B led by Juvia Group. The Boston biotech develops RNA therapies that target specific cells outside the liver to treat muscle diseases, heart failure and metabolic disorders; the oversubscribed round funds its lead clinical programs.

New investors: TCG Crossover Management, BB Biotech, Fidelity Management & Research Company, Balyasny Asset Management
Existing investors: Amgen Ventures, ARCH Venture Partners, Bessemer Venture Partners, Breyer Capital, The Invus Group, Janus Henderson, Leaps by Bayer, Polaris Partners, Safar Partners, TAS Partners, Temasek, Vida Ventures
HQ: Boston, MA
Founded: about five years ago (per StrictlyVC)
Use of funds: advance lead clinical programs and continued pipeline progress
Reported by: Business Wire, StrictlyVC, Axios Pro Rata, FinSMEs, PitchBook News, Fierce Biotech
