# Stand raised $70M Series C led by Saga Ventures, at a $1B valuation. The San Francisco home insurer simulates how each home would fare in wildfire, wind and other disasters, helps owners make it resilient, then insures it with discounts of up to 70%.

Other investors: Lowercarbon, Eclipse, Equal Ventures, Inspired Capital, Neotribe, Jackson Square Ventures, among others
Total raised: $130M
Valuation: $1B
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Co-founder and CEO: Dan Preston
Traction: insures more than $10B of property, up from $1B a year ago
Product: the Stand World Model builds a digital model of each home and simulates its performance under fire, wind and other disasters; Stand recommends and verifies improvements
Carriers: underwrites and services policies from Concert Specialty Insurance Company (California) and Stand Insurance Exchange (Florida), reinsured by Arch, RenaissanceRe, Hannover, Nephila and Hiscox
New executives: Drew Saad (ex-Chief Underwriting Officer, PURE Insurance) as President of P&C; Tony Landi (ex-Nephila, Openly, Everest Re) as CFO
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
