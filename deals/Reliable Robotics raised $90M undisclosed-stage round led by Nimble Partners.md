# Reliable Robotics raised $90M undisclosed-stage round led by Nimble Partners, at a valuation above $1B (plus a $25M SVB term loan, $115M total). The Mountain View, Calif. company builds systems that automate existing aircraft (engine start, taxi, takeoff, landing), aiming for remotely supervised pilotless flight, and is pursuing FAA certification.

Debt alongside: $25M term loan from Silicon Valley Bank (total financing announced $115M)
Total raised: $380M
Valuation: above $1B
HQ: Mountain View, CA; hangars in California; subsidiary Reliable Airlines operates from Albuquerque, NM
Founded: 2017 by former SpaceX engineers
Team: about 250 people
Use of funds: expand aircraft fleet, production facilities and engineering team toward FAA certification
Expansion: new Radar Center of Excellence in Bothell, WA (about 25 staff at open, led by VP of programs Chris Schulenberg and radar fellow Marc Pos, ex-Honeywell) developing AESA air-to-air radar for detect-and-avoid; growing San Diego engineering for military aviation autonomy
