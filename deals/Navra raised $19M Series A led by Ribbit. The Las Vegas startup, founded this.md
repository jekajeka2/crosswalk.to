# Navra raised $19M Series A led by Ribbit. The Las Vegas startup, founded this year by SoFi and Figure co-founder Mike Cagney, is building a desktop and mobile platform giving retail and institutional investors access to blockchain-based lending, trading and yield products through one interface.

Other investors: Baseline, DCM (founding Figure investors); strategics Jump Crypto, Figure Technology Solutions
Round: oversubscribed
HQ: Las Vegas, NV
Founder: Mike Cagney (co-founder and CEO; co-founded SoFi and Figure), founded 2026
Product: keyless self-custody designed to meet qualified custody requirements, onchain access to yield protocols and cash rails, an embedded AI agent; team management, audit trails and fund-accounting integrations for institutions; direct and white-label
Partners: Figure is its first blockchain partner; integrated Figure's Democratized Prime yield protocol and YLDS stablecoin
Use of funds: AI infrastructure, platform expansion, customer and partner acquisition
Launch: limited rollout to retail and institutional customers in late October 2026
Reported by: Business Wire (company announcement), Pulse 2.0, StrictlyVC
