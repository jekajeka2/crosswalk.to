# RapidCanvas raised $29M Series B led by Vertex Ventures SEA & India. The Austin, TX enterprise AI company pairs its platform with dedicated field AI engineers to take enterprises from AI pilots to production applications built around specific business processes.

Other investors: Hitachi Ventures; existing investors Accel, Peak XV Partners, Valley Capital Partners, Titanium Ventures
Total raised: $52.5M
HQ: Austin, TX (per SEC Form D)
Incorporated: 2021
Use of funds: expand go-to-market teams, strengthen partnerships with private equity firms and advisers, grow AI engineering teams in India, the US and Brazil, platform development and governance
SEC Form D (filed 2026-10-14): $29M sold of a $29M equity offering, first sale 2026-09-21, 6 investors; related persons Rahul Pangam (Executive Officer, Director), Uttam Phalnikar (Executive Officer, Director), Dinesh Katiyar (Director), Piyush Kharbanda (Director); no placement agents
Reported by: The SaaS News, ETEntrepreneur (Economic Times), SEC Form D
