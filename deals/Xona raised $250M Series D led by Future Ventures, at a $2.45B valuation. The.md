# Xona raised $250M Series D led by Future Ventures, at a $2.45B valuation. The Burlingame, Calif. company is building Pulsar, a commercial low-Earth-orbit satellite navigation network it says gives centimeter-level positioning with signals up to 100x stronger than GPS, more resistant to jamming and spoofing.

Total raised: more than $570M since 2019
Valuation: $2.45B
HQ: Burlingame, CA (satellite manufacturing in-house there)
Co-founder and CEO: Brian Manning
Use of funds: expand the Pulsar constellation, manufacturing capacity and commercial infrastructure; launch initial services in 2027, then a broader global network
Traction: first FCC authorization for a private navigation constellation alongside government GNSS; October launch put six more Pulsar satellites in orbit, four to support beta navigation service in multiple countries from early 2027; customers integrating Pulsar-compatible firmware into devices
Target uses: autonomous vehicles, robotics, industrial systems, communications, financial infrastructure
Reported by: Pulse 2.0
