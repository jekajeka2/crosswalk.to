# Group14 Technologies raised €150M (~$169M) Series D extension from a new, undisclosed investor. The Woodinville, Wash. company makes silicon-carbon battery material SCC55, and will use the money to build its fourth Battery Active Materials site (BAM-4, 10 GWh initial) in Sagunto, Spain.

Total Series D: now over $665M including the extension
HQ: Woodinville, WA
CEO and co-founder: Rick Luebbe
Use of funds: BAM-4 in Sagunto, Spain (initial 10 GWh); broader global manufacturing scale-up
Capacity: BAM-3 in Sangju, Korea (10 GWh) operational and scaling; BAM-2 in Moses Lake, Wash. (two modules, 20 GWh) expected to produce in 2027; total near-term footprint 40 GWh
Traction: SCC55 delivered to 150+ customers from its Woodinville pilot plant (BAM-1, operating since 2021); 200+ patents; used in smartphones, drones, hypercars and eVTOLs
Reported by: PR Newswire
