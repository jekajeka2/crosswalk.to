# Mostest raised $4M seed led by Capital Q Ventures, NEXUS Luxury Collection and T-Squared Social, at a $16M post-money valuation. The New York startup runs an AI-driven event booking platform that connects event customers with hospitality operators, aiming to make booking an event as simple as a dinner reservation.

Other investors: Wildwood Ventures, Locker Room Capital, Newbond, Todd Deutsch, Charlie Walk, Jeffrey Davis, Neal Luthra
Round: oversubscribed seed
Valuation: $16M post-money
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: CEO Sarah Klingman and CTO Aaron Kennedy; founded 2026
Use of funds: develop its AI technology, deepen integrations with hospitality partners, expand enterprise relationships, enter new markets
Reported by: The SaaS News (citing the company's EIN Presswire release)

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/mostest-raises-4m-seed
