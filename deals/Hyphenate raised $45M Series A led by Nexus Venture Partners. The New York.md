# Hyphenate raised $45M Series A led by Nexus Venture Partners. The New York startup, formerly Maximor, builds AI agents that automate business finance work such as bookkeeping, order management, invoicing and refunds, with human oversight.

Other investors: B Capital; existing investors Foundation Capital, BoldCap, Gaia Ventures
Amount: $45M per the company and The SaaS News; Axios Pro Rata reported $40M
Total raised: $54M
HQ: New York, NY
Founded 2024 by Ramnandan Krishnamurthy and Ajay Krishna Amudan; formerly known as Maximor
Use of funds: expand into accounting and strategic finance services
Reported by: The SaaS News, StrictlyVC, Tech Funding News, SiliconANGLE, Axios Pro Rata

Source: https://techfundingnews.com/hyphenate-45m-series-a-nexus-b-capital-ai-finance-agents
