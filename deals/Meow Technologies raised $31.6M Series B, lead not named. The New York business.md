# Meow Technologies raised $31.6M Series B, lead not named. The New York business banking fintech makes white-label treasury management software. Stifel Bank will offer the platform to its commercial clients, starting with venture and fund banking, after Stifel Financial's strategic investment.

Amount and stage: $31.6M Series B, per FinTech Futures (Oct 19); the Stifel announcement (Oct 15) called it a strategic minority investment and gave no amount
Investor: Stifel Financial Corp. (strategic minority investment); sources don't say whether Stifel led the Series B
HQ: New York, NY
Founder: Brandon Arvanaghi, founded 2020
Product: business banking, expense management and treasury software (invoice management, card issuance, agentic banking infrastructure)
Use of funds: develop and distribute its white-label digital banking platform; Stifel Bank will offer Meow's cash management, payments and multi-entity fund operations tools to its innovation-economy clients
Reported by: The SaaS News, GlobeNewswire (Stifel announcement), FinTech Futures

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/meow-secures-strategic-investment
