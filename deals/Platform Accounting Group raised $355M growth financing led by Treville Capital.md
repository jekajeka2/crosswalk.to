# Platform Accounting Group raised $355M growth financing led by Treville Capital Group. The Salt Lake City company operates and acquires boutique accounting and advisory firms across the US (65+ acquisitions, 50,000+ clients), giving local firms centralized technology, compliance, recruiting and operations while they keep their own brands.

Other investors: Vista Credit Partners, Siguler Guff, Aquiline, Brightwood Capital Advisors; existing investor The Cynosure Group (first invested early 2024)
Structure: "strategic growth financing" from Treville Capital Solutions, which provides hybrid capital as a less dilutive alternative to traditional debt or equity
HQ: Salt Lake City, UT (also New York)
Founded: 2015 by CEO Reyes Florez; President and COO Tyson Chapman
Use of funds: accelerate acquisitions, expand services, invest in technology and talent
Services: tax, client accounting, audit and assurance, advisory, technology consulting, wealth management, family office
Board: Treville partner Dan Bailey and former Deloitte global CEO Barry Salzberg join
Advisors: Perella Weinberg Partners (financial)
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
