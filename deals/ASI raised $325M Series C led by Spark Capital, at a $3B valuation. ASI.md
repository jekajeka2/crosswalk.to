# ASI raised $325M Series C led by Spark Capital, at a $3B valuation. ASI (formerly Air Space Intelligence) builds frontier AI models that simulate physical systems such as air traffic and power grids, and sells command-and-control software to the FAA, airlines, the US military and grid operators.

Other investors: Citadel Securities, Craft Ventures, Meritech Capital, Energy Impact Partners, Andreessen Horowitz
Valuation: $3B
HQ: California (no city given in the sources)
Founder and CEO: Phillip Buckendorf
Use of funds: expand in aviation, defense and energy, move into new industries such as logistics, and grow its research and engineering teams
Traction: $875M, 12-year FAA contract signed in June to manage future US air traffic (won against bids from Palantir and Thales, per Bloomberg), live at Dulles since last month; five-year, $99M US Army command-and-control deal earlier this month; customers include United, Delta, US Air Force and National Grid
Reported by: SiliconANGLE, The SaaS News
