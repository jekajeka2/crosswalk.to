# Zenflow raised $52M Series D led by Cook Medical. Zenflow makes the Zenflow Spring System, an FDA PMA-approved (Dec 2025), reversible in-office implant for benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH); Cook Medical also takes exclusive U.S. distribution rights.

- Other investors: ClavystBio (new); existing investors Invus Opportunities, F-Prime, Medtech Venture Partners and others
- Also venture debt from HSBC Bank USA and Trinity Capital
- HQ: South San Francisco, CA
- CEO: Shreya Mehta; chairman Andrew Cleeland
- Use of funds: commercial launch of the Spring System following FDA PMA approval in December 2025
- Exclusive U.S. distribution agreement with lead investor Cook Medical, whose MedSurg division covers urology
- Product: in-office BPH treatment with multiple implant sizes and full reversibility; no post-procedure catheter, preserves sexual function; pitched as "first-line interventional therapy" in place of drugs
- Round described as oversubscribed

Reported by: GlobeNewswire (company release)
