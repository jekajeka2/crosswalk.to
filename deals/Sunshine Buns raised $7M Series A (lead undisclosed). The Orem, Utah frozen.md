# Sunshine Buns raised $7M Series A (lead undisclosed). The Orem, Utah frozen cinnamon roll brand sells fully baked, individually wrapped buns that go from frozen to warm in 45 seconds, and is launching in Target stores nationwide.

Other investors: not named
HQ: Orem, UT
Founders: Chrisi and Matt Hammer, Kass and Brian Martin; started with home-baked orders in Utah in 2022, then a storefront
Use of funds: increased production and inventory for new retailers, marketing, new products, team growth
Traction: nationwide Target launch; Southern California Costco warehouses; regional grocers including Hy-Vee, United Supermarkets, Harmons, New Seasons Market, Gelson's and Bristol Farms; another national grocery retailer due later this fall
Products: Original with Cream Cheese Frosting, Cookies & Cream, Strawberries & Cream; 8g protein per bun
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/sunshine-buns-on-a-roll-with-major-national-retail-expansion-302920517.html
