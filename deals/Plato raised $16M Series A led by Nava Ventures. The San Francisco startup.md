# Plato raised $16M Series A led by Nava Ventures. The San Francisco startup builds resettable simulated workplaces (customer support, people ops, finance, accounting, healthcare admin) where AI labs train and evaluate models with reinforcement learning before deploying them on live systems.

Other investors: Gradient Ventures (led the seed), Transpose Platform, Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman
Nava Ventures founder Freddie Martignetti joins the board
Total raised: $19M
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Robert Farlow (CEO, previously built an annotation platform at Yutori) and Pranav Putta (CTO, previously trained computer-use models at MultiOn); founded 2025
Use of funds: expand its library of real-world work simulations, paying professionals to recreate their jobs and deploying agents on real tasks to collect experience
Evaluations grade both outcome and process, and check whether models exploit weaknesses in a simulation
Reported by: PR Newswire, Axios Pro Rata

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/plato-raises-16-million-to-build-the-training-grounds-for-ais-next-leap-into-real-work-302921832.html
