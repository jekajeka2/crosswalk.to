# TXSE Group raised an undisclosed third funding round (stage undisclosed, no lead named), bringing its total capital raised to $430M, the most ever raised by a new exchange. TXSE Group is the Dallas-based parent of the Texas Stock Exchange, a national securities exchange the SEC approved a year ago, competing with NYSE and Nasdaq for primary listings.

Total raised: $430M across three rounds
Investors: existing owners supplied over 75% of the third round; major holders include BlackRock, Charles Schwab, Citadel Securities, J.P. Morgan, Goldman Sachs and Bank of America
HQ: Dallas, TX
Use of funds: build capital reserves, scale primary listings and continuous trading, strategic initiatives
Traction: companies with a combined $115B market value announced moves to TXSE from NYSE and Nasdaq in the 20 days before the announcement
Reported by: PR Newswire, FinTech Futures
