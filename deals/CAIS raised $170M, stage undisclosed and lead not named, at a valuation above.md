# CAIS raised $170M, stage undisclosed and lead not named, at a valuation above $2B. The New York company runs a marketplace that gives independent wealth advisors access to private-market alternative investments; it is No. 2 to iCapital ($7.5B valuation) and plans to use the money for acquisitions after growing organically since 2009.

Valuation: more than $2B
Investors: Apollo Global Management and Hamilton Lane are CAIS investors (both also back iCapital)
HQ: New York, NY
Founded: 2009
Use of funds: acquisitions, to win more independent RIAs
Traction: serves 2,500+ wealth management firms supporting 65,000+ financial advisors (iCapital: 3,300+ firms)
Reported by: PitchBook News

Source: https://pitchbook.com/news
