# AvalonX raised up to $10M undisclosed-stage round led by Ridown Capital. The Alpharetta, Ga. AI security startup, out of 18 months in stealth, sells continuous penetration testing to small and midsize businesses by subscription, pairing autonomous attack agents with human testers.

Investor: Ridown Capital, the investment arm of Onecom co-founders Aaron Brown and Darren Ridge (invested "up to $10 million")
HQ: Alpharetta, GA
Founders/leaders: CEO and co-founder Craig Goodwin (former CISO at Fujitsu, Monster and CDK Global), Ricardo Valdes, Ben O'Nion
Product: Artemis platform, powered by Aether, a proprietary 400B-parameter security model trained on nine years of offensive-security data and 60B dark-web records; findings map to SOC 2, ISO/IEC 27001, PCI DSS v4.0 and HIPAA
Launched publicly: October 2026
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
