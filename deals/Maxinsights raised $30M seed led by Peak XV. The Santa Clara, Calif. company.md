# Maxinsights raised $30M seed led by Peak XV. The Santa Clara, Calif. company sells first-person video of people doing real-world tasks (cooking, cleaning, workplace work) as training data for robotics foundation models, with 2M+ hours collected by 5,000+ paid contractors in six countries; it also launched MaxLoop (automated QA/annotation) and MaxGlove (a tactile force-sensing glove).

Other investors: Tola Capital, South Park Commons, INCE Capital, Alumni Ventures
HQ: Santa Clara, CA
Founders: CEO Yuguang (YG) Yuan (co-founded Mega Gear Robotics, acquired; ex-Eaton) and CTO Kun Wang (ex-Google X, Waymo; co-founded QCraft)
Traction: 30+ customers including frontier AI labs, 8 on recurring contracts; ~1.5M hours of model-ready data; Genesis AI trained GENE-26.5 on 200,000 hours of its video; largest data provider for Dyna Robotics' Dyna-2
MaxLoop: cut manual review ~90% and review cost ~80%; capacity ~450,000 hours of footage a month
MaxGlove: built with Genesis AI; aims for 1M hours of force data in 2027
Use of funds: expand collection network, automate data processing, add sensing capabilities
Reported by: Pulse 2.0
