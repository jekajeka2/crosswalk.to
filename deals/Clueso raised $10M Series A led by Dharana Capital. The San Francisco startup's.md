# Clueso raised $10M Series A led by Dharana Capital. The San Francisco startup's AI agents navigate real software interfaces to capture footage, then script, voice, edit and auto-update business videos such as product tutorials and training content.

Other investors: Founders Future, Neotribe Ventures, Y Combinator, f7 Ventures (SiliconANGLE names Founders Fund rather than Founders Future)
Total raised: over $12M
HQ: San Francisco, CA (also operates in Bengaluru, India)
Founders: Akash Anand (CEO), Prajwal Prakash, Neel Balar; founded 2023
Use of funds: expand go-to-market, grow team from 35 to 50 by year-end, extend into interactive product demos and documentation
Traction: Zendesk reports 5x+ productivity gains and 80% lower video production costs
Reported by: SiliconANGLE, StrictlyVC, Tech Startups
