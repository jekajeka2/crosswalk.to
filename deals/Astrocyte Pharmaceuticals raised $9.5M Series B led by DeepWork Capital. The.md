# Astrocyte Pharmaceuticals raised $9.5M Series B led by DeepWork Capital. The clinical-stage biotech develops AST-004, an astrocyte-targeting cerebroprotective drug for concussion, traumatic brain injury and stroke, now in Phase 2.

- Other investors: existing investors plus new investors MintPharma Capital, Waterstar Capital, Dynagrow Capital, Angel Star Ventures
- Total raised: $40M including financing and grants
- HQ: Groton, Conn.
- Use of funds: Phase 2 development of AST-004, including completing the STARFAST sports-concussion trial (enrollment complete, topline results expected this winter)
- Reported by: PR Newswire
