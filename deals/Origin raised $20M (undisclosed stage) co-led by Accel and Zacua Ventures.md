# Origin raised $20M (undisclosed stage) co-led by Accel and Zacua Ventures, emerging from stealth. The San Francisco physical AI company builds OG-1, a robot that autonomously finishes interior walls (compound, sanding, priming) on live construction sites, run by its own Construction Action Model.

Other investors: Unilazer Ventures, Schema Ventures, Z21, and angels from Anduril, Skild AI, 1X and Tesla
Total raised: $20M
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Yogesh Ghaturle (CEO; previously founded Perpule, acquired by Amazon in 2021), Ashish Daga, Tushar Makkar; founded early 2025
Use of funds: deploy and expand the OG-1 fleet, large customer pilots in the US and Europe, expand into more interior trades (taping, drywall install, drilling, conduit)
Traction: working with two of the largest US drywall finishing contractors; first robots on New York job sites within 10 months of founding; claims 3x human speed; pilots planned in Germany, Sweden, France and Saudi Arabia
Goal: 150 robots deployed by end of 2027
Reported by: Pulse 2.0

Source: https://pulse2.com/origin-20-million-funding
