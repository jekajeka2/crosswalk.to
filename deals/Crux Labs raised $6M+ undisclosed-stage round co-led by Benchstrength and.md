# Crux Labs raised $6M+ undisclosed-stage round co-led by Benchstrength and Misneach. The New York startup, out of stealth, captures expert judgment inside enterprise workflows, calibrates custom evaluations to it, monitors 100% of AI agent traffic and ships continuous agent improvements to production.

Other investors: Analog Ventures, Pareto Holdings
Round described as oversubscribed
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Julie Kheyfets (CEO; previously led North America for Tractable AI and was CEO of Block Renovation) and Krishna Kulkarni (CTO; previously led brand engineering at Meta, built AI products at Checkr, scaled Healthify); they met at Princeton
Use of funds: expand research and engineering, accelerate enterprise deployments
Traction: customers include Fortune 500s and high-growth companies in healthcare, cybersecurity and financial services
Reported by: PR Newswire, SiliconANGLE

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/crux-labs-raises-over-6m-to-align-ai-agents-with-human-judgment-inside-the-enterprise-302923216.html
