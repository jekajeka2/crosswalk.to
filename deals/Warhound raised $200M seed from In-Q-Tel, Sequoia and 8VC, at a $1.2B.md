# Warhound raised $200M seed from In-Q-Tel, Sequoia and 8VC, at a $1.2B valuation, emerging from stealth. Founded by Colossal Biosciences' Ben Lamm with Anduril's Palmer Luckey as a partner, the Dallas company is gene-editing and cloning working dogs (from Belgian Malinois and German Shepherd cell lines) for the US military, law enforcement and first responders, with Anduril supplying sensor vests and goggles.

Investors: In-Q-Tel, Sequoia, 8VC (no lead named)
Valuation: $1.2B
HQ: Dallas, TX (per FinSMEs)
Founders: Ben Lamm (Colossal Biosciences CEO); Palmer Luckey is a partner and holds no equity; Colossal owns equity and licenses its technology
Rationale: the company says 80%+ of US military working dogs come from Europe and fewer than half are fit for service
Plan: first cloned dogs expected to be born early next year, entering service within about two years; Colossal's Viagen handles cloning; training program certified under American Humane Society guidance
Customers targeted: Department of Defense, military service groups, police forces, first responders; pricing not set
Reported by: TechCrunch, StrictlyVC, FinSMEs
