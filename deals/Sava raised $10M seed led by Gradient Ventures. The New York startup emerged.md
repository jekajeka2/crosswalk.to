# Sava raised $10M seed led by Gradient Ventures. The New York startup emerged from stealth with Sava Trust Company, a Nevada-chartered administrative trustee whose AI agents, supervised by human trust officers, handle trust onboarding, KYC, custodian syncing, accounting and distributions for a flat annual fee per trust.

Other investors: Y Combinator, SV Angel, Instacart co-founder Max Mullen, Orange Collective, SurgePoint Capital; angels Tomer London (Gusto co-founder) and Eleonora Sharef
HQ: New York, NY (per the company; Axios Pro Rata described it as SF-based)
Founders: Nimit Maru (CEO; founded Fullstack Academy, acquired 2019, and Bloomspot, acquired by JPMorgan Chase 2012) and Rush Sadiwala
Head of Trusts Jordan Wolff was previously head of trust & estate administration at Hightower Advisors
Use of funds: technology and infrastructure; building APIs so fintechs can embed trust capabilities
Target: onboarding in days rather than weeks or months
Reported by: PR Newswire, Axios Pro Rata
