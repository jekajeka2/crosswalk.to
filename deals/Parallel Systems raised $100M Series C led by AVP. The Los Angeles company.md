# Parallel Systems raised $100M Series C led by AVP. The Los Angeles company builds battery-electric autonomous rail vehicles (Panther) that let railroads run short-haul freight lanes under 500 miles that today go by truck.

Other investors: new backers Hillspire, Agility Global, Cobalt Capital; returning Anthos Capital, Congruent Ventures, Riot Capital, Collaborative Fund
Total raised: more than $200M
HQ: Los Angeles, CA
Founders: Matt Soule (CEO, 13 years at SpaceX); founded 2020; Arun Gupta is executive chairman
Use of funds: production scale-up of the third-generation Panther rail vehicle, full commercialization, expansion into international markets
Traction: several major railroads under contract to deploy; FRA-supervised pilot in Georgia with short-line operator Genesee & Wyoming; moved from prototyping to live commercial deployment under regulatory test approval since its 2025 Series B
Reported by: PR Newswire (company release)
