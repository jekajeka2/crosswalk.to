# Basalt Health raised $20M Series A led by NEA. The Nashville startup's AI platform reads post-acute care referrals arriving by fax, portal or EMR and checks them against clinical and payer criteria so facilities can decide on admissions faster.

Other investors: existing investors Frist Cressey Ventures and 25m Health (NEA is also a returning investor)
Total raised: about $24.5M
HQ: Nashville, TN
Founded: 2024 by CEO Ben Hackett
Use of funds: expand the product into discharge and payer workflows; grow engineering, sales, marketing and implementation teams
Reported by: Business Wire (company announcement, Sept. 24, 2026), The SaaS News
