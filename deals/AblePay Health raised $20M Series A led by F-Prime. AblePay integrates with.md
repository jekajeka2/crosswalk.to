# AblePay Health raised $20M Series A led by F-Prime. AblePay integrates with health systems to guarantee providers non-recourse payment of patient out-of-pocket balances on an accelerated timeline, while giving enrolled patients flexible payment options, savings and billing advocacy.

Other investors: A1 Health Ventures, .406 Ventures
HQ: Allentown, PA
Founder and CEO: John Fistner; founded 2016
Use of funds: reach more health systems and patients, deepen the technology platform, expand services for patients and providers
Reported by: PR Newswire
