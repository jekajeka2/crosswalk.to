# Matchpoint Therapeutics raised $150M Series B co-led by Nextech Invest and Norwest. The Watertown, MA biotech designs covalent small molecules against hard-to-drug targets; its lead asset MPT-062, an oral first-in-class T cell modulator for autoimmune and inflammatory disease, enters the clinic in 2027.

New investors: Invus, BB Biotech, T1D Fund, BOLD Longevity Growth
Existing investors: Access Biotechnology and Atlas Venture (founding), Sanofi Ventures (Series A lead), Digitalis Ventures, all other existing investors
Board: Kanishka Pothula (Nextech Invest) and Brian Matesic (Norwest) join
HQ: Watertown, MA
CEO: Andre Turenne
Use of funds: take MPT-062 to clinical proof-of-concept (clinical development starts 2027) and advance the broader pipeline
Partnerships: a discovery collaboration with Novartis (July 2025 agreement) funds an additional program
Reported by: GlobeNewswire (company release)
