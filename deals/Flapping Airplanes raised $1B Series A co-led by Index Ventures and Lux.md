# Flapping Airplanes raised $1B Series A co-led by Index Ventures and Lux Capital, at a $6B valuation (per Bloomberg). The year-old San Francisco AI research lab is building models that learn from far less data than frontier systems, on top of its own GPU megakernel framework that runs training, inference and other workloads inside a single kernel; it has no commercial product yet.

Other investors: Sequoia and GV (returning from the seed); Kleiner Perkins, Valor Equity Partners, Atreides Management, Jump Trading, XTX Ventures and Nvidia among listed backers
Total raised: $1.18B, including a $180M seed in January led by Index with Sequoia and GV
Valuation: $6B, per Bloomberg, roughly 4x the seed valuation nine months ago
HQ: San Francisco, CA; a couple dozen people working in person
Founders: CEO Ben Spector (Stanford CS PhD student, lead author of ThunderKittens, started MIT's Prod accelerator), Asher Spector (Stanford statistics PhD) and Aidan Smith (Thiel Fellow, ex-Neuralink); founded about a year ago
Board: Mark Xu of Index joins
Thesis: Index cites robotics, trading and life sciences as markets that need more data- and compute-efficient AI
Reported by: SiliconANGLE (citing Bloomberg, the company's X announcement and Index)
