# Scuba raised $34M seed led by Accel and IVP (pre-seed led by Accel plus seed co-led by Accel and IVP). The New York startup, from former Slack CPO Noah Desai Weiss and engineering leader Dolapo Falola, builds a "context engine": shared team memory of captured articles, pages and docs that people and AI agents can reach via MCP in ChatGPT, Claude or Slack.

Rounds: $34M total across a pre-seed led by Accel and a seed co-led by Accel and IVP
Angels: Stewart Butterfield and Cal Henderson (Slack co-founders), Patrick Collison (Stripe), Mike Krieger, Reid Hoffman, Jeff Weiner, Drew Houston, Parker Harris, Christina Cacioppo (Vanta), Winston Weinberg (Harvey), Shiv Rao (Abridge)
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Noah Desai Weiss (CEO, former Slack chief product officer) and Dolapo Falola (former Slack engineering leader, led engineering at The Browser Company); began building in 2025, formerly known as Flashback
Traction: hundreds of small teams have used it over six months before today's public launch
Reported by: Upstarts Media

Source: https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/exclusive-slack-alums-launch-scuba
