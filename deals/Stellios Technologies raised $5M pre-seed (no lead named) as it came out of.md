# Stellios Technologies raised $5M pre-seed (no lead named) as it came out of stealth. The Austin company builds MW-class high-power microwave systems (klystrons, gyrotrons and their high-voltage power systems) for counter-drone defense and industrial uses, and runs a compact aneutronic fusion research program for energy and propulsion.

Investors: Sofreh Capital (Shervin Pishevar), Wave Function Ventures, Cubit Capital, Mana Ventures, Atoms & Bits, Output Capital, Erik Bruckner and other strategic investors
HQ: Austin, TX
Founders: Dr. Richard Starson (CEO, Caltech Ph.D., previously co-founded microwave-powered launch company Escape Dynamics); founded 2026
Traction: $2M in signed customer purchase orders before raising outside capital; the round was oversubscribed
Reported by: Stellios Technologies (PR Newswire)
