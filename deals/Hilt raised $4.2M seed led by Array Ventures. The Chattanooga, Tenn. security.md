# Hilt raised $4.2M seed led by Array Ventures. The Chattanooga, Tenn. security startup monitors how data moves across cloud, endpoints and networks at the kernel level, using graph neural networks to flag or quarantine suspicious activity in real time, including actions taken with valid credentials or by AI agents.

Other investors: Verdict Capital, Base10 Partners, Brickyard, Liquid 2 Ventures, Sequel, the Sarah Smith Fund, Alumni Ventures
Total raised: $4.7M, including a $500K pre-seed led by Pear VC in October 2025
HQ: Chattanooga, TN (moved from San Francisco with investor Brickyard)
Founders: William Cielen (CEO, 19), Alex Genest (CTO); founding engineer Zin Bitar
Traction: a large hedge fund paid six figures upfront before the product was built, against a potential $1M-$2M contract; a second high-frequency trading firm since added; customer count doubled in the month after its go-to-market push (total not disclosed)
Model: runs inside customers' own infrastructure; annual fee per data collector
Team: 11 employees
Use of funds: hiring and building market presence
Reported by: Crunchbase News (exclusive)
