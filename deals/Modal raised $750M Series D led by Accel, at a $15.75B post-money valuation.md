# Modal raised $750M Series D led by Accel, at a $15.75B post-money valuation; its SEC Form D (filed 2026-10-20) reports $601.9M of equity sold so far of an $800M offering. The New York company, founded in 2021, provides serverless cloud infrastructure for running and scaling AI workloads, and says it recently passed a $1B annual revenue run rate.

Other investors: Kleiner Perkins; previous investors Menlo Ventures, General Catalyst, Redpoint, Lux Capital, Amplify Partners, Bain Capital Ventures
Valuation: $15.75B post-money
Total raised: $1.2B in equity (AlleyWatch)

Related persons as filed: Erik Bernhardsson (Executive Officer, Director), Akshat Bubna (Executive Officer), Seth Zelnick (Executive Officer), Sarah Catanzaro (Director), Patrick Chase (Director)
