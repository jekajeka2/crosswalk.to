# Serval raised $150M Series C led by Sequoia Capital, at a $3B valuation. The San Francisco company sells an AI service management platform where staff describe a workflow in plain language and AI agents write the automation and resolve employee requests, starting in IT and spreading to HR, finance and legal.

Other investors: Conviction and Headline (new); Redpoint, General Catalyst, First Round, Evantic Capital and Elad Gil (returning)
Total raised: $277M, including a $75M Series B (Sequoia, $1B valuation, Dec 2025) and a $47M Series A (Redpoint, Oct 2025)
Valuation: $3B, up from $1B at the Series B
HQ: San Francisco, CA; opening offices in New York, London and Austin
Founded 2024; co-founders Jake Stauch (CEO) and Alex McLeod (CTO)
Use of funds: its Catalyst agent, which scans tickets and drafts automations for admin review; a new ProServal unit hiring 100+ on-site automation engineers in 12 months; 500+ hires overall
Traction: headcount 10x in a year; ARR doubling every quarter since December (absolute figure undisclosed); two Fortune 20 customers signed in recent months, plus Fox, WPP and Live Nation; competes with ServiceNow, one automaker migrated off it in 45 days
Reported by: SiliconANGLE, Axios Pro Rata
