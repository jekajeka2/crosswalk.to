# Suki raised $90M Series E led by Healthier Capital, bringing total funding to $255M. The Redwood City, Calif. company makes an AI ambient clinical intelligence platform that automates physicians' medical documentation, revenue cycle and administrative workflows, and now serves 450+ healthcare organizations.

Other investors: Crosslink Capital, Hercules Capital, Hedosophia, Venrock, Flare Capital Partners, Philips (Philips Ventures), Zoom Ventures
Total raised: $255M in equity (AlleyWatch)
HQ: Redwood City, CA
Founders: Punit Soni, Anshu Sharma, Karthik Rajan; founded 2017
Use of funds: scale ambient clinical intelligence and build agentic AI features that automate post-encounter tasks such as patient referrals and scheduling
Traction: 450+ healthcare organizations
Note: The SaaS News named no lead; Axios Pro Rata (per Axios Pro) names Healthier Capital as lead
Reported by: The SaaS News (citing Inc.), Axios Pro Rata, Crunchbase News, AlleyWatch

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/suki-raises-90m-series-e
