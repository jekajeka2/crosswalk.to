# American Advanced Materials raised $12.5M seed led by Amity Ventures. The Mountain View, Calif. startup is building an AI-native plant to make titanium sponge in the US, which has produced none since 2020, followed by zirconium oxychloride; it has signed offtake letters of intent worth over $1B.

Other investors: Human Capital, Anti Fund, Powerset
HQ: Mountain View, CA (also New York, NY)
Founders: Alex Liegl (CEO; founded Tenet Energy and Layer1) and Diego Represas (President/CTO; founded Able AI, acquired by Moody's); founded 2025
Use of funds: grow the team and advance front-end engineering and design of its manufacturing projects; plans to raise project financing for the commercial facility
Traction: offtake LOIs worth over $1B with domestic melters and alloy makers
Context: US titanium sponge imports hit a record 44,000 metric tons in 2025 (USGS); the first facility uses the Kroll process
Reported by: PR Newswire
