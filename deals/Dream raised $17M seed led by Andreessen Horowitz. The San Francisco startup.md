# Dream raised $17M seed led by Andreessen Horowitz. The San Francisco startup, founded by ex-Palantir execs, builds "built world models" trained on 100M+ sq ft of US building-permit data to automate code-compliant building design and engineering, aiming to replace AEC tools like Autodesk's AutoCAD and Revit.

Other investors: SV Angel, XYZ, Zigg Capital
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Andy Elder (led product design for an 800-person Palantir team) and Arjun Mathur (SVP in Palantir's commercial practice); Stanford roommates who started the company in early 2025
Training data: more than 100 million square feet of building data from US permit archives
Use of funds: further develop its built world models for building design and engineering
Emerged from stealth with this round
Reported by: Business Wire (company announcement), TechCrunch, StrictlyVC, The SaaS News
