# AdaptClose raised an undisclosed pre-seed round led by Sturges Ventures. AdaptClose is an AI sales coaching and call recording platform for home services companies (roofing, HVAC, solar), transcribing in-home and phone sales conversations, scoring them against the sales process and giving managers coaching tools.

- HQ: New York, NY
- Founded in 2026; CEO Pat Brien
- Target customers: home services contractors with 5 to 30 sales reps that lack a dedicated sales operations team
- The round coincided with the product's public launch
- Use of funds: public launch and scaling the platform
- Reported by: The SaaS News (via EIN Presswire)

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/adaptclose-raises-pre-seed
