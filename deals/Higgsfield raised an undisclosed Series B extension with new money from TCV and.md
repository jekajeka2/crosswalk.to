# Higgsfield raised an undisclosed Series B extension with new money from TCV and NVIDIA. The San Francisco AI video and image platform for brands, agencies and studios says it crossed $1B in annualized revenue in September, 18 months after launch.

New investors in the extension: TCV, NVIDIA
Original Series B (August): led by DST Global, with Goldman Sachs Alternatives, Tribe Capital, AI Capital Partners, Smash Capital, GFT Ventures and Valor Capital
HQ: San Francisco, CA, with roots in Kazakhstan through the Astana Hub
Founders: Alex Mashrabov (CEO, previously co-founded AI Factory, behind Snapchat Cameos), Yerzat Dulat, Mahi de Silva
Use of funds: product development, global infrastructure, hiring and go-to-market expansion
Traction: $1B+ annualized revenue (Sept); 40M+ users in 238 countries and territories, US the largest market; enterprise growing 100%+ month over month; contracted business revenue up 10x since June; 12T tokens across 150M generations a month
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
