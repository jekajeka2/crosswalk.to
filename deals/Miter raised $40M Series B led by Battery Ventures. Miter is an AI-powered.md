# Miter raised $40M Series B led by Battery Ventures. Miter is an AI-powered workforce management platform for construction contractors, covering payroll, HR, field operations, job costing and expenses; it says about 2 of every 100 US construction workers are paid through it.

- Other investors: existing backers Bessemer Venture Partners and Coatue
- Total raised: $78M
- HQ: San Francisco (engineering in SF and New York)
- Use of funds: AI products for back offices and jobsites, a larger engineering team, US go-to-market hiring
- More than 2,000 contractors use it; customer count has tripled since the May 2025 Series A
- Source: PR Newswire
