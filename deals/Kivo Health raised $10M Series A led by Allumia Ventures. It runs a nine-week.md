# Kivo Health raised $10M Series A led by Allumia Ventures. It runs a nine-week, therapist-led pulmonary rehab program over live video for older adults with COPD, shipping the equipment to patients' homes, and is covered by Medicare, Medicaid and major private insurers.

Other investors: Y Combinator, Corewell Health Ventures, Heuristic Capital, MassMutual Ventures
Total raised: more than $16M
HQ: San Francisco, CA (per Axios, StrictlyVC and FinSMEs; GeekWire reports it as Seattle-based)
Founders: Dr. Victor Sadauskas (CEO, former Kaiser Permanente emergency physician) and Vaughn Koch (CTO, sold Movity to Trulia in 2010); founded 2023
Use of funds: building an AI health coach that blends AI-led and human-led care; later expansion into other chronic conditions of older adults
Traction: about 50 employees; covered by Medicare, Medicaid, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Blue Cross, Aetna and Priority Health; a company-funded analysis estimates $8,245 in annual savings per member
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata (https://axios.link/3W4Y6UC), GeekWire, StrictlyVC, FinSMEs
