Other investors: NTT Docomo, Connecticut Innovations, Alumni Ventures, Monday Capital
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Ari Nazir (CEO) and İlker Selim Zorluoğlu; about one year old
Traction: 70+ online retailers; Turkish supermarket chain A101 conversion up 83%; Tchibo average order value up 13.6%; Mavi runs its AI concierge on it
Use of funds: scale the platform globally; longer-term expansion into financial services and energy
Reported by: SiliconANGLE, StrictlyVC
