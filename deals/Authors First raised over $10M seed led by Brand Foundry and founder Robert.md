# Authors First raised over $10M seed led by Brand Foundry and founder Robert Hamwee. The New York AI studio helps science-fiction and fantasy authors adapt their books into film and visual media, combining proprietary and third-party AI generation tools with visual effects platforms.

Other investors: Bolt Ventures (The Family Office of David Blitzer), Andy Mills, Mike Duggal, John Kline
HQ: New York, NY (per FinSMEs)
Founder: Robert Hamwee, a private equity veteran
Use of funds: develop its AI-assisted production tools and adapt authors' written works for the screen
Reported by: The SaaS News, Variety, Axios Pro Rata, FinSMEs

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/authors-first-raises-10m-seed
