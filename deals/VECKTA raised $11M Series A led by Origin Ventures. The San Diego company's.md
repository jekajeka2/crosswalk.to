# VECKTA raised $11M Series A led by Origin Ventures. The San Diego company's platform returns a business case and recommended solar-and-storage system for a site from just its address, then runs standardized RFPs through a marketplace of 3,000+ prequalified suppliers so businesses can buy onsite energy at a competitive price.

Other investors: Tech Square Ventures, VoLo Earth Ventures, Cove Fund, Catalyst by Wellstar, Engage, Emerald Bay Ventures, She's Independent Investments, Mobilize AI x Energy Ventures; builds on a 2023 seed round
HQ: San Diego, CA
Founders: Gareth Evans, CEO and co-founder
Use of funds: expand its U.S. customer team, product, partnerships and supplier network
Traction: profitable; customers include Enterprise Mobility, Lithia & Driveway, Phillips Edison & Co. and Regency Centers; this year customers contracted onsite systems at 128 sites and paid 20-45% less than bids they sourced themselves; its analysis of ~34,000 sites found 37% could support solar or storage at positive returns ($3.1B of project value)
Reported by: VECKTA (PR Newswire)
