# Ultra raised $50M Series A led by Framework Ventures. The Brooklyn startup leases its OP1 packing robot to e-commerce warehouses as a monthly service; it ships in a crate, plugs into a standard outlet and Ethernet, and is working in under a day.

Other investors: M13, Wing Venture Capital
Prior round: $12M seed led by Y Combinator and NextView Ventures
Total raised: $62M
HQ: Brooklyn, NY (Industry City)
Founded 2024 by Chetan Parthiban, Jonathan Schwartz, Max Friefeld and Oliver Ortlieb
Use of funds: deploying hundreds more robots, expected in 2027; hiring; moving to a larger Industry City space
Traction: robots have packed 500,000+ orders at sites in New York, Georgia, New Jersey and Texas; customers include 3PL Highline Commerce
Correction: the earlier post gave $62M as the round size; the Series A is $50M and $62M is the total raised
Reported by: AlleyWatch, PitchBook
