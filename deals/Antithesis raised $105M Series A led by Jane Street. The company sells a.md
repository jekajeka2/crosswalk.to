# Antithesis raised $105M Series A led by Jane Street. The company sells a deterministic simulation testing platform: a deterministic hypervisor replicates production so engineers can replay runs, inject realistic faults and get automatic root-cause analysis of bugs.

Lead Jane Street is also a major customer
Total raised: over $180M
HQ: Tysons Corner, VA
Founded 2018 by Will Wilson (previously co-founder of FoundationDB)
Use of funds: grow engineering, expand the platform's autonomy features, scale sales and marketing across North America, Europe and Asia
Round date per The SaaS News: December 3, 2025 (republished by The SaaS News Oct 19, 2026)
Reported by: The SaaS News
