# Dan-O's Seasoning raised $25M growth investment led by The Newcastle Network. The Louisville, Ky. seasoning brand, started at a farmers market in 2015, sells flavor-first blends in 22,000+ retailers and online to a community of 15M+ home cooks.

Investor: The Newcastle Network, a consumer-focused private equity firm (portfolio includes Kizik, Super7, Pet Life Unlimited); its first food investment
HQ: Louisville, KY
Founder: Dan Oliver, founded 2015; CEO Phillip Crosby; both continue to lead
Use of funds: new flavors and formats, wider retail and online availability
Traction: 22,000+ retailers nationwide; community of 15M+ home cooks
Reported by: PR Newswire
