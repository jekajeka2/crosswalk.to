# Factory raised $200M undisclosed-stage round, at a $5B valuation. Factory is an agentic coding startup whose AI agents carry out programming tasks largely on their own; customers include Nvidia, Blackstone, Royal Bank of Canada, Palo Alto Networks, Adobe and T-Mobile.

- Investors: Khosla Ventures, Blackstone, Sequoia Capital, Insight Partners and others (no lead named in the sources)
- Round raised in September 2026; sources say "this month" without an exact announcement date
- HQ: San Francisco, CA; founded about three years ago; co-founder and CEO Matan Grinberg
- Customers: Nvidia, Blackstone, Royal Bank of Canada, Palo Alto Networks, Adobe, T-Mobile
- Grinberg names Cognition (maker of Devin) as Factory's biggest competitor
- Reported by: StrictlyVC, TechCrunch

Source: https://factory.com/news/5-billion-valuation
