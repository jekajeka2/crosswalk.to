# Clockwork.io raised $31M undisclosed-stage round co-led by Seligman Ventures, Wing Ventures and Premji Invest. Palo Alto-based fault-tolerance software that keeps AI training, RL and inference jobs running through GPU, link and network failures instead of restarting from checkpoints.

- Other investors: returning backers NEA and e& Capital
- Total raised: $73M
- HQ: Palo Alto, CA (legal name Clockwork Systems Inc.)
- Products: LinkPass (reroutes traffic around failed links), TorchPass (migrates work off failing GPUs); new multi-node platform snapshots (TorchSnap) and async application checkpoints for RL
- Traction: LinkedIn runs LinkPass fleet-wide and says it prevents tens of thousands of GPU-hours of downtime a month; Together AI is offering TorchPass as a service on its GPU clusters; WhiteFiber (NASDAQ: WYFI) is expanding use
- CEO: Suresh Vasudevan

Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement), SiliconANGLE
