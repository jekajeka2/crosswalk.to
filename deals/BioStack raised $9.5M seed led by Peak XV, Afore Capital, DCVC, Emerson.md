# BioStack raised $9.5M seed led by Peak XV, Afore Capital, DCVC, Emerson Collective, SV Angel, Verdict Capital, Streamlined Ventures and Y Combinator. The San Francisco YC startup links fragmented hospital records (notes, labs, imaging, medications, outcomes) into longitudinal, de-identified patient datasets for training and evaluating medical AI, covering 100M+ records across the US, India, Brazil and Argentina.

Investors: Peak XV, Afore Capital, DCVC, Emerson Collective, SV Angel, Verdict Capital, Streamlined Ventures, Y Combinator (the company says the round was "led by" all eight; Axios Pro Rata lists them as participants with no lead)
Total raised: $10M in equity (AlleyWatch)
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Parth Patwa and Sanat Mishra (co-founder, CEO); founded 2025 (AlleyWatch)
Use of funds: expansion across the US and international markets, platform development, growing engineering and research teams
Traction: works with hospitals in the US, Brazil, India and Argentina; 100M+ de-identified patient records; several hundred thousand X-rays, MRIs and CT scans combined with pathology and clinical records; in India, connects cancer patients with an investigational cancer vaccine program
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement), Axios Pro Rata, AlleyWatch

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/biostack-raises-9-5m-to-turn-global-hospital-data-into-multimodal-training-datasets-for-medical-ai-302916123.html
