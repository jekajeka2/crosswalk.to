# ClarityCare raised $16.5M Series A led by Headline. The San Francisco healthcare AI startup automates prior-authorization paperwork for health plans and providers as payers face CMS-0057-F deadlines on January 1, 2027.

Other investors: Maverick, Transpose, Firstminute Capital, Top Harvest, Arthur Ventures, FJ Labs (per Axios Pro Rata)
Amount: $16.5M per MedCity News; Axios Pro Rata reported $16.3M
Round: oversubscribed Series A
Total raised: $19.8M
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Use of funds: expand sales and engineering teams, develop new products, grow presence in the healthcare market, particularly the government sector
Context: CMS-0057-F sets prior-authorization decision deadlines of 72 hours for expedited and seven days for standard requests; payers must comply by January 1, 2027
Reported by: MedCity News, Axios Pro Rata (https://axios.link/4z6AQ7a)

Source: https://medcitynews.com/2026/10/claritycare-raises-16-5m-to-speed-up-prior-authorizations
