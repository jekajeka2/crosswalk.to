# Siena raised $17M Series A led by York IE. The New York company sells an AI "Agent of Record" platform for consumer brands: support, shopping, social and voice agents plus a shared inbox, all running on one customer-intelligence layer (orders, subscriptions, reviews, loyalty data).

Other investors: Joyance Ventures; existing investors Aglaé Ventures and Sierra Ventures
Total raised: $29.7M
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Andrei Negrau (CEO) and Lisa Popovici (President), founded 2023
Use of funds: build additional autonomous agents and expand the infrastructure brands use to serve customers and drive revenue
Traction: hundreds of consumer brands including FIGS, SPANX, Comfrt and HexClad; says it automates up to 80% of customer interactions
Reported by: The SaaS News, Pulse 2.0

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/siena-raises-17m-series-a
