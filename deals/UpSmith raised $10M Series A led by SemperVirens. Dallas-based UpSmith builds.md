# UpSmith raised $10M Series A led by SemperVirens. Dallas-based UpSmith builds an AI profit engine for home-services contractors (HVAC, plumbing, electrical) that uses agents to find and follow up on missed sales opportunities, convert leads and manage estimates, bookings and recurring revenue.

Other investors: Hannah Grey Ventures, Asymmetric Capital Partners, CIV, Synetro Group, WaterStone Impact Fund, Colin M. Evans (Sandwith Ventures), Deason Capital Services, Zachary Bookman
HQ: Dallas, TX
Founded: 2022 by CEO Wyatt Smith
Use of funds: grow the engineering team and scale distribution across the home-services industry
Product: rolling out an AI voice agent for inbound customer calls and a unified customer timeline of job activity and interactions
Reported by: StrictlyVC, Dealroom, The SaaS News, ACCESS Newswire
