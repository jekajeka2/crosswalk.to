# Type One Energy raised $200M Series B co-led by Breakthrough Energy Ventures and Clutterbuck Capital. The Knoxville company is developing stellarator fusion power plants, starting with Project Infinity at TVA's Bull Run site in East Tennessee.

Other investors: Lowercarbon Capital, Siemens Energy Ventures, SiteGround Capital (new)
HQ: Knoxville, TN
Founded: about seven years ago
Use of funds: optimized stellarator development, FusionDirect program, Project Infinity: the Infinity One engineering prototype and a planned 400 MWe Infinity Two commercial plant
Milestone: round follows the State of Tennessee's initial fusion operating license for Project Infinity
Also: pursuing a UK project via the Infinity Fusion Consortium with Tokamak Energy, AECOM, Sheffield Forgemasters and Barclays
Model: partner-intensive, using existing industry manufacturing, engineering, financing and operating capabilities
CEO: Christofer Mowry
Reported by: TechCrunch, Pulse 2.0, StrictlyVC
