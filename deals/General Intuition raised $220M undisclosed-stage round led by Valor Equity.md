# General Intuition raised $220M undisclosed-stage round led by Valor Equity Partners and Atreides Management, at a $6.2B valuation (up from $2.3B in June). The one-year-old lab trains AI action and world models on gameplay video and player actions, to operate across virtual and physical environments.

- Leads: Valor Equity Partners and Atreides Management (per Crunchbase News)
- Other investors: 776, Point72, Khosla Ventures, General Catalyst
- Valuation: $6.2B (was $2.3B in June)
- Total raised: $650M+
- HQ: New York, NY; founded about a year ago
- Reported by: GamesBeat, StrictlyVC, AlleyWatch, Crunchbase News

Source: https://gamesbeat.com/general-intuition-raises-another-220m-at-6-2b-valuation-for-training-models-with-game-data
