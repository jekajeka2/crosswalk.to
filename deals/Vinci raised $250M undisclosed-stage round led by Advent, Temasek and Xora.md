# Vinci raised $250M undisclosed-stage round led by Advent, Temasek and Xora Innovation, at a $1.5B valuation. Vinci builds AI-assisted physics simulation software for semiconductor and hardware engineers, starting with thermal modeling for dense AI chips.

Other investors: Eclipse, Khosla Ventures, Madrona
Total raised: at least $296M (Tech Startups' estimate from prior disclosed financing plus this round)
Valuation: $1.5B
HQ: Palo Alto, CA
CEO: Hardik Kabaria
Use of funds: expand from thermal modeling into full-system simulation, including vibration and electromagnetics
Traction: moving from a handful of pilot deployments toward broader production use (CEO to Reuters)
Reported by: Tech Startups (citing Reuters)
