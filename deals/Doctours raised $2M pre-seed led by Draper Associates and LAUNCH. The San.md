# Doctours raised $2M pre-seed led by Draper Associates and LAUNCH. The San Francisco company runs a medical-tourism platform that lets Americans compare, book and coordinate care abroad with upfront all-inclusive prices, vetted clinics and U.S.-based coordinators, starting with hair restoration.

Other investors: Halogen Ventures, StoryHouse Ventures, HMC INQ
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Model: free for patients; partner clinics pay Doctours for coordination
Offer: published packages from $2,200 to $8,690; each clinic personally visited and credential-checked; five Istanbul partner clinics hold Turkey's International Health Tourism Authorization
Use of funds: build out its AI-assisted patient coordination platform and clinic network; expand to other planned-care categories, including fertility
Reported by: Pulse 2.0

Source: https://pulse2.com/doctours-raises-2-million-pre-seed
