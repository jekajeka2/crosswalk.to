# Wally raised $25M Series A led by Maveron. The New York company runs a $249-a-year dental membership with no insurance: unlimited AIRFLOW cleanings, exams, x-rays, 3D scans and AI-driven diagnostics, delivered from clinics built inside co-working spaces.

Other investors: Bling Capital; angels including Hims co-founder Jack Abraham
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Tyler Burnett (CEO), Stipe Latkovic, Chelsea Patel
Strategic partnership: EMS Dental (maker of the AIRFLOW cleaning technology), announced with the round
Traction: more than 50,000 members, visiting about 3x a year; 15 locations in New York City, Philadelphia, Jersey City and Chicago; 97% hygienist retention
Use of funds: expand from 15 to 100 locations by end of 2027, entering Washington DC, Atlanta, Austin and Miami (waitlists open); hiring clinicians and operators
Reported by: PR Newswire (company release), Axios Pro Rata
