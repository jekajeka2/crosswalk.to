# SignSplit raised $400M strategic seed led by W Group, at a $1B valuation. SignSplit came out of stealth with a platform where people and institutions license their data, work and likeness ("signed data") to AI and robotics companies and researchers for pay, plus a verification layer that lets platforms detect signed content and its consent terms.

- Investor: W Group, a fintech and technology group (11 businesses, 40M+ clients); the round is a financing commitment that combines capital with a multi-year package of strategic resources
- HQ: Wilmington, DE (dateline); incorporated as a Delaware public benefit corporation (SignSplit PBC)
- Founded 2024 by Glib Denisov (co-founder, executive chairman, CPO); co-founder and CEO Alessandro Monterosso
- Use of funds: global rollout
- Product: data and research pools built to a buyer's needs; contributors supply real-world data, knowledge and skills and are paid for it

Reported by: PR Newswire (company release)
