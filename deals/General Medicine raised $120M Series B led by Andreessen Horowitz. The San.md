# General Medicine raised $120M Series B led by Andreessen Horowitz. The San Francisco company runs a nationwide online "healthcare store" where customers search, compare and buy care (medications, labs, telehealth, specialist e-consults, select in-person visits) and see cash and insurance prices before deciding.

Other investors: Matrix, VXI Capital, Eli Lilly and Company, Mercy Health (through Granger Management), BoxGroup
Total raised: $152M
a16z partner: Vineeta Agarwala, MD, PhD, led the investment
HQ: San Francisco, CA, with offices in Boston
Founders: TJ Parker (CEO), Elliot Cohen (President), Ashwin Muralidharan (CPO), the PillPack founding team; founded 2023
Launched nationwide May 2025
Catalog: more than 2,900 products and services; licensed clinicians usually available same day, plus an AI chat for exploring symptoms
Use of funds: expand the store into more categories of care
Also building an enterprise offering for health systems, payers and pharmacies (per a16z)
Reported by: PR Newswire, a16z
