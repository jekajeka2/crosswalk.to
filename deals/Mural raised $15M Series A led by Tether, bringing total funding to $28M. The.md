# Mural raised $15M Series A led by Tether, bringing total funding to $28M. The New York company, founded by former Palantir engineers, is a compliant system of record that fintechs and platforms (Deel, Opera, Axexo) use to run accounts, payments, identity and compliance for their customers and the AI agents acting for them, across dollars, stablecoins and local currencies.

Other investors: North Island Ventures, existing investors
Total raised: $28M
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Sinclair Toffa (CEO) and Chris Fernandes, both ex-Palantir engineers; founded 2022
Use of funds: global expansion and regulatory and payments infrastructure for agentic finance
Traction: $2.1B in payment volume for end users in 128 countries since its API launched in 2025; 2026 volume to date nearly 6x all of 2025
Model: forward-deployed engineers embed with customers in each market to launch integrations
Licensing: money transmission through Mural Networks (FinCEN-registered MSB, NMLS 2744530) and partner FDIC-insured US banks
Reported by: PR Newswire
