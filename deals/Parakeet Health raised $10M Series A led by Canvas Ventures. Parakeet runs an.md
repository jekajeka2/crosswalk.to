# Parakeet Health raised $10M Series A led by Canvas Ventures. Parakeet runs an AI patient-access platform (inbound calls, outreach, fax referrals, web scheduling across voice, SMS, email and fax) for specialty medical groups, and now serves six of the 10 largest US dermatology groups.

- Other investors: Blank Space Ventures, StoryHouse Ventures, HMC INQ (round oversubscribed)
- Total raised: $13M
- HQ: San Francisco, CA
- CEO and co-founder: Jung Park
- Traction: 10x ARR growth over the past year; 2,800+ providers across 1,100+ locations; national collaboration with Qualderm Partners (~160 practices in 17 states)
- Performance claims: inbound phone agent hit 2.3x the resolution rate of an established AI voice vendor in a head-to-head pilot; rebooks no-shows at up to 4.9x and cancellations at up to 4.2x the rate of leading patient-engagement vendors
- Business model: performance-based pricing tied to appointments booked, interactions resolved and revenue generated; enterprise deployments in 4-6 weeks
- Use of funds: expand the unified platform and move into primary care, behavioral health, women's health, eyecare, PT, GI and radiology
- Reported by: PR Newswire
