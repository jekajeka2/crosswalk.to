# Reach Security raised $50M+ Series B led by Top Tier Capital Partners, bringing total funding to $80M. The San Francisco startup runs a deterministic AI platform that discovers, validates and tunes the security controls an enterprise already owns, autonomously closing exploitable attack paths at machine speed.

Other investors: Ballistic Ventures, In-Q-Tel, M12 (Microsoft's venture fund), existing investors
Amount: "more than $50M" per the company announcement
Total raised: $80M in equity (AlleyWatch)
HQ: San Francisco, CA (AlleyWatch says Palo Alto)
Founders: Garrett Hamilton (CEO), Colt Blackmore (CTO); founded 2021
Use of funds: product innovation, more integration partnerships, international expansion, growth in US enterprise and federal markets
Reported by: The SaaS News, Pulse 2.0, AlleyWatch
