# FSH Technologies raised $20M Series A led by undisclosed investors, with Lachy Groom, General Catalyst and Ramp participating. The Philadelphia startup builds AI software for unglamorous government work such as administering school food services and managing public records.

Other investors: Lachy Groom, General Catalyst, Ramp, Scribble Ventures, Operator Partners, Basis Set Ventures (no lead named)
Total raised: $25M
Round came together in about a week, per the CEO
Seed: led by Contrary, which first backed CEO Lilly Chen in 2021 before the company took its current shape in 2024
HQ: Philadelphia, PA
Founders: Lilly Chen (co-founder and CEO, former Facebook AI infrastructure engineer); Jason Chen, the Contrary investor who first backed her, is now COO
Approach: rework the underlying processes across departments rather than putting a chatbot on top of existing systems
Reported by: Crunchbase News
