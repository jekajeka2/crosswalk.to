# Outer Spaces raised $8M pre-seed led by Upfront Ventures. The El Segundo company builds OS1, a modular backyard pavilion with solar generation in the roof (2.4-5.3 kW) and 15-60 kWh of battery storage under the deck that powers the home and provides backup; first systems ship in 2027 in Southern California.

Other investors: Unlock Venture Partners, Mucker Capital, Genius Ventures
HQ: El Segundo, CA
Founders: Jiake Liu (CEO), Terry Lin (chief design officer), Robin Liao (CTO), creators of the outdoor furniture brand Outer; founded 2025
Product: 12x12 ft and 12x24 ft sizes; minimal excavation, no concrete pour; sold online and through installers, builders, landscapers and developers; pricing due early 2027
Use of funds: build the team and pursue channel partnerships
Reported by: PR Newswire, Axios Pro Rata
