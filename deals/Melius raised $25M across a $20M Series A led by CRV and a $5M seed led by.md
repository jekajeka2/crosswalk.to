# Melius raised $25M across a $20M Series A led by CRV and a $5M seed led by General Catalyst. The New York startup runs an "agents lab" for creative work: teams of AI agents that produce images, video and audio from idea to finished asset, so creative teams skip prompt-engineering across a dozen tools.

Round: $20M Series A led by CRV; $5M seed led by General Catalyst (total funding $25M)
Other investors: Genius Ventures, Vine Ventures, Anti Fund
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: CEO Joowon Kim and CTO Young Kim (both ex-Ramp; Young co-founded Venue, which Ramp acquired in 2023), COO Arnav Ramu (ex-Hg, Guggenheim)
Traction: came out of stealth in July 2026 and crossed a seven-figure run rate in under three months; thousands of teams use it daily (company figures)
Use of funds: hiring, building out go-to-market and sales, expanding the agents lab
Reported by: GlobeNewswire (company release)
