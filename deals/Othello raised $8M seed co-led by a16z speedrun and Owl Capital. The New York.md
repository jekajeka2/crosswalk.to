# Othello raised $8M seed co-led by a16z speedrun and Owl Capital. The New York applied AI lab trains its own model, Hamlet, to coach sales reps live during calls, popping up suggestions in under 500 milliseconds in any online meeting without joining as a participant.

Leads: a16z speedrun and Owl Capital (per AlleyWatch interview with the CEO; the company's press release listed investors without naming a lead)
Other investors: Long Journey Ventures, Stratus Ventures
Angels: Andy Fang (DoorDash), Chris Larsen (Ripple), Thomas Wolf (Hugging Face), David Cramer (Sentry), Waseem AlShikh (Writer), Dean Sysman (Axonius), Alex and Philippe Bouaziz (Deel), Ryan King (Chime), Kevin Stein (TransDigm CEO)
HQ: New York, NY
Founders: Jared Zelman (co-founder and CEO), Raphael Assor (co-founder)
Traction: deployed at 50+ large enterprises including several Fortune 500 companies; customers include Microsoft, Capital One, Lyft, a top-10 accounting firm and a top-10 asset manager; reps use it on 95%+ of calls
Product: desktop app that starts with the call and shows live pop-ups; can run without storing audio or transcripts for regulated customers; serves insurance, accounting, financial services, asset management, software, cybersecurity, healthcare and manufacturing sales teams
Business model: enterprise pricing, selling mainly to large companies
Reported by: PR Newswire, AlleyWatch, Pulse 2.0
