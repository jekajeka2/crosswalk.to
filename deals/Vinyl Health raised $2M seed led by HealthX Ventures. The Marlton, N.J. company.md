# Vinyl Health raised $2M seed led by HealthX Ventures. The Marlton, N.J. company makes an AI personal health assistant (iOS and Android) that records medical appointments with ambient AI, pulls records from connected providers, and helps patients and families understand treatment plans, follow-ups and share information with caregivers.

HQ: Marlton, NJ
Founder: Dr. Kaitlan Baston, former New Jersey health commissioner and hospital CEO
Use of funds: broader public launch and continued product development
Product: now available on Apple App Store and Google Play; records appointments and voice notes, retrieves records from connected providers, organizes care context across specialists, secure sharing with providers, caregivers and family
Business model: says it does not depend on selling patients' health data
Reported by: Pulse 2.0
