# Star Therapeutics raised $200M undisclosed-stage round co-led by OrbiMed and Janus Henderson Investors. The eight-year-old South San Francisco biotech develops antibody therapies for hematology, immunology and other diseases with limited treatment options.

Other investors: Agent Capital, Catalio Capital Management, Cormorant Asset Management, New Leaf Venture Partners, NYBC Ventures, RA Capital Management, Redmile, Sofinnova Investments, Soleus Capital
HQ: South San Francisco, CA (Axios Pro Rata had said San Francisco)
Founded: about 2018 (eight years old, per StrictlyVC)
Context: follows Incyte's July acquisition of Star subsidiary Vega Therapeutics in a deal valued at up to $2B (Crunchbase News)
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata, Crunchbase News, StrictlyVC
