# EMode Photonix raised $1M pre-seed led by Ground State Ventures. The Boulder, Colo. startup sells simulation software for nonlinear integrated photonics, used to design devices for frequency conversion, quantum light generation and other light transformations in quantum computing and sensing.

Other investors: Harlow Capital Management
HQ: Boulder, CO
Founders: Eric and Maiya Stanton
Use of funds: product development and sales, including an enterprise offering for complete photonic integrated circuit design
Reported by: The SaaS News (via The Quantum Insider)
