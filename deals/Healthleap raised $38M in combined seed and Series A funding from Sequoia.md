# Healthleap raised $38M in combined seed and Series A funding from Sequoia Capital, First Round Capital and Hummingbird Ventures (no lead named). The San Francisco clinical AI platform works inside the EHR to screen hospitalized patients for conditions that often go undiagnosed and untreated, such as malnutrition.

Round: $38M combined seed and Series A
Investors: Sequoia Capital, First Round Capital, Hummingbird Ventures (no lead named)
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Use of funds: extend screening to more conditions, including congestive heart failure, aspiration pneumonia, delirium, pressure ulcers and certain hospital-acquired infections
Announced: last week, per MedCity News (Oct 14, 2026)
Reported by: MedCity News

Source: https://medcitynews.com/2026/10/healthleap-raises-38m-to-expand-ai-screening-for-overlooked-conditions-in-hospitalized-patients
