# Mochatrade raised $6M seed led by CoinFund. The New York startup, founded this year, lets Indian retail traders fund accounts in rupees via UPI and take long or short positions on tokenised U.S. stocks with up to 50x leverage, around the clock.

Other investors: Coinbase Ventures, Pioneer Fund, Y Combinator, Nicky Montana
Amount: $6M per The SaaS News and FinTech Global; The Block earlier reported $5.56M total raised
HQ: New York, NY
Founded: 2026
Registered as a Virtual Digital Asset Service Provider with FIU-IND; integrates TradingView charting
Use of funds: expanding Indian retail traders' access to U.S. markets
Reported by: The SaaS News, FinTech Global, StrictlyVC, The Block
