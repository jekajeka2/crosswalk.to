# Harbor raised $10M seed co-led by Base10 Partners, Abstract and 8VC. The San Francisco company, founded this year, assesses the risk of enterprise AI agents (mapping agent activity against regulatory frameworks, monitoring operational and security risk in real time) and insures companies against agent-caused losses, starting with regulated industries such as banking and insurance.

Other investors: Restive, NFX, Innovation Endeavors, Kibo Ventures, Masia, MVP Ventures
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Founders: Jesús Santos, Daniel Elechiguerra, Cristian Bartolomé, Luis Botín; founded 2026
Use of funds: build out its risk platform and insurance products for AI agent deployments in regulated industries
Reported by: The SaaS News, StrictlyVC, Coverager

Source: https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/harbor-raises-10m-seed
