# Zaria raised $5.2M seed led by Lightspeed Venture Partners. The Jackson, Wyo. company runs real-time loan servicing, backup servicing, collateral management and agency infrastructure for structured finance and digital asset-backed lenders, and has applied to the OCC to charter Zaria National Trust Bank.

Other investors: Refract Ventures, Epoch VC
HQ: Jackson, WY
Founder: Emily Barron (co-founder and CEO)
Use of funds: grow the team, extend the technology and scale existing servicing, collateral management and agency services; supports its pending OCC application for Zaria National Trust Bank, a special-purpose national trust bank
Traction: live with partners including Ledn, Maple, Galaxy, Kraken and Figure across warehouse facilities, forward-flow loan sale programs and securitizations
Reported by: PR Newswire, Axios Pro Rata
