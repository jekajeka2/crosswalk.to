# Devin, by Cognition: The autonomous software engineer that works in its own cloud machine and opens pull requests. Launched: General availability December 2024; Devin 2.2 February 2026; the current plans since April 2026. Way in: joins itself over MCP: a custom MCP in Customize and a key or sign-in.

Price: Free: the Devin Desktop editor with a light quota. Pro $20 a month for Devin Cloud with daily and weekly quotas; Max $200; Teams from $80 a month plus $40 a seat.
Where: Web at app.devin.ai, Slack, Teams, a CLI, Devin Desktop (the former Windsurf), JetBrains and Zed through the CLI, an API.
Model: Several frontier models; the default is undocumented.
Schedules: Automations with a Schedule trigger: every hour, day, or week, or a cron expression; also Schedule Devin from a session's menu.
How to get it: Sign up at app.devin.ai; Pro, Max, and Teams are self-serve.
The catch: The autonomous agent needs a paid plan from $20 a month with daily and weekly quotas, and custom MCP servers need an org admin.
Guide: https://crosswalk.to/agents/devin
