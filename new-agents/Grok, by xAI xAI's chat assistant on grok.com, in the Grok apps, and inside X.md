# Grok, by xAI: xAI's chat assistant on grok.com, in the Grok apps, and inside X, with connectors and automations. Launched: iOS app January 2025; Android April 2025; connectors May 2026; automations July 2026. Way in: joins itself over MCP: a custom connector at grok.com and a sign-in.

Price: Free with daily Grok. Since October 12, 2026 new customers buy The X Subscription: Plus $8 a month, Premium $30 (Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor and X Premium in one weekly pool), Super $100, Ultra $200. Existing SuperGrok plans continue.
Where: Web at grok.com, iOS, Android, and inside the X app.
Model: Grok 4.7 in Expert mode; the Fast model is unnamed.
Schedules: Automations at grok.com/automations (and in the apps): describe the job once and pick daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly, or yearly; each run reports back by email or notification.
How to get it: Sign in at grok.com or install the Grok app; free to start.
The catch: The custom connector form is OAuth only as documented (a plain bearer key is refused), and Grok Bot is a separate xAI product with its own page here.
