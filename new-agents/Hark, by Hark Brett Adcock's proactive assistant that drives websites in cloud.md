# Hark, by Hark: Brett Adcock's proactive assistant that drives websites in cloud browsers. Launched: Hark Pro, October 6, 2026, in the US. Way in: reach by email; it drives websites, no MCP.

Price: Free. $20 a month doubles the usage, $100 gives ten times. The first 100,000 sign-ups get a paid plan free.
Where: Web, iOS, and Android. Dedicated devices are promised for 2027.
Model: Not documented. It works through cloud browsers that click through real websites the way a person would, several at once, with a small window showing what it is doing.
How to get it: Sign up at hark.ai, connect the accounts you want it to run (email, calendar, cards), and tap the Action Buttons it suggests.
Connect: Connect your email account in Hark, the one it already sends from.
Guide: https://crosswalk.to/agents/hark
