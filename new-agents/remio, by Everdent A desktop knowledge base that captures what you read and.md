# remio, by Everdent: A desktop knowledge base that captures what you read and record, with an agent that runs tasks on your Mac or PC. Launched: Version 3.0 (the agent) April 2026; Automations August 2026. Way in: joins itself over MCP: a connector in its MCP settings.

Price: Free with 500 AI credits a month. Pro $13.90 a month (10,000 credits); Plus $42; Max $139; or $14.90 a month to bring your own keys.
Where: Desktop on Windows (x64) and Apple-silicon Macs; iOS and Android companions that need the desktop app; a Chrome extension.
Model: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi through its cloud; bring your own key; or a local model.
Schedules: Automations in the sidebar run on a schedule or on events (mail, Slack, a meeting), while the desktop app is open.
How to get it: Download the installer from remio.ai.
The catch: It runs on your desktop: automations and the phone apps need the desktop app running, and the MCP setup form's fields are not documented.
Guide: https://crosswalk.to/agents/remio
