# Instinct, by Instinct: A personal assistant you text or call, with its own phone, computer, and email address. Launched: August 2026, invite only. Past 100,000 users by early October. Way in: reach by email, its own address; MCP connections exist on some accounts but are undocumented.

Price: Free during the beta; no price announced. The founder has said he would rather take a cut of transactions than charge a subscription.
Where: You text it (iMessage, WhatsApp) or call it. No app to open: it works from its own phone number, email address, and cloud computer.
How to get it: Join the waitlist at app.instinct.com or get an invite link from a member. Each member gets five invites; some have been resold online.
Connect: Sign in at crosswalk.to/keys. It shows your crosswalk address and the exact text to send Instinct, address filled in.
