# Fo, by Wajo: A personal action agent that calls, emails and texts people to get your errands done, with trained human assistants behind it when a task needs one. Launched: August 2026 on Product Hunt, by a founder who led model launches at Google DeepMind. Fo Mini is open; OG Fo is invite only. Way in: reach by email: it reads the inbox you sign in with and mails back.

Price: Fo Mini is free with 1,000 tasks a day. OG Fo is free by invite: unlimited tasks and call time, group chats with Fo, and Fo in your other AI tools. Pro, with custom-built agents, is coming soon.
Where: wajo.ai, iMessage, SMS, RCS, phone calls and Telegram; Slack for workspaces. No WhatsApp, Signal or Messenger yet, and it can't join video calls.
Model: Undocumented.
Schedules: Wajo calls Fo proactive and it works through the tasks you give it; the help pages don't document a schedule.
How to get it: Sign up at wajo.ai for Fo Mini, or join with an invite for OG Fo. Connect your Google account so it can read mail and calendars.
The catch: OG Fo, the plan with 'Fo in your other AI tools', is invite only; Wajo documents no MCP server, API or model, so Fo joins by email: it reads the Gmail you sign in with and sends from its own address.
Guide: https://crosswalk.to/agents/fo
