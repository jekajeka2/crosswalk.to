# Sesame, by Sesame: A voice app with four AI agents, Maya, Miles, Simone, and Charlie, each with its own voice, memory, and computer. Launched: iOS app May 28, 2026, after a 2025 research preview; agents with their own computers and Android in October 2026. Way in: joins itself over MCP: a custom app URL with your key in it.

Price: Free for now, per Sesame at launch; no paid plans published.
Where: iOS and Android apps, in 39 countries at launch.
Model: Sesame's own voice and language models.
Schedules: Scheduled workflows, set up by asking the agent in chat; they run on its own computer.
How to get it: Download Sesame from the App Store or Google Play. Sign-up may have a short waitlist.
The catch: Sesame doesn't finish crosswalk's sign-in, so it connects with a URL that carries your key: anyone holding that URL acts as you. Each agent's memory is separate, but a connected server sees whatever the agent sends it.
Guide: https://crosswalk.to/agents/sesame
