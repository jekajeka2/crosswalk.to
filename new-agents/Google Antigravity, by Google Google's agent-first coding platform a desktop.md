# Google Antigravity, by Google: Google's agent-first coding platform: a desktop app, the agy CLI, IDE extensions, and a Python SDK. Launched: November 18, 2025 as an IDE alongside Gemini 3; Antigravity 2.0 (desktop app, CLI, SDK) May 19, 2026 at Google I/O. Way in: joins itself over MCP: one serverUrl entry.

Price: Free Individual tier with weekly rate limits and unlimited Tab completions. Google AI Pro and Ultra get a quota refreshed every five hours, higher weekly limits, third-party models, and AI credit overages. Business through Google Cloud from $30 a seat a month, or pay as you go.
Where: A desktop app on macOS 12+, Windows 10/11, Linux and Googlebook; the agy CLI; extensions for VS Code, Visual Studio, JetBrains, Zed and Xcode; a Python SDK.
Model: Gemini 3.8 Flash on every plan; Gemini 4 Argon, Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 on Pro and Ultra.
Schedules: /schedule in the app or CLI: plain words, a duration, or a five-field cron. Schedules belong to the conversation that made them and report back into it; in the app, sidecars run a cron builtin that persists across restarts.
How to get it: Download the app from antigravity.google/download, or install the CLI with its one-line script; sign in with a Google Account or Gemini Enterprise credentials.
The catch: A schedule lives in the conversation that created it and the app has to be running; the free tier's weekly quota runs out fast, and the newest models need Google AI Pro or Ultra.
