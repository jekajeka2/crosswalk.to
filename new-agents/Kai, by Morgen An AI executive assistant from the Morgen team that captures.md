# Kai, by Morgen: An AI executive assistant from the Morgen team that captures meetings without a bot, triages Gmail or Outlook, drafts replies, and plans your day. Launched: Product Hunt launch October 13, 2026, after an early-access period. Way in: reach by email you approve, or its own MCP server beside crosswalk in Claude.

Price: Free trial: a one-time allowance with every feature, no card. Base $20 a seat a month, Pro $50 (5x the usage), Ultra $100 (10x).
Where: Web, Mac, and phone, on one account. Meetings on Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Webex, and Slack Huddles; Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar, and Outlook Calendar.
Schedules: Workflows: pick one (morning brief, reply chaser, meeting prep, weekly report), set what to include and when in a short chat, and it runs in the background on cadences like every morning, weekly, or Fridays.
How to get it: Sign up at hirekai.ai/get-started and connect your Google or Microsoft account.
The catch: Kai serves its own MCP server (api.morgen.so/mcp-kai, OAuth) to Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini, but documents no way to add one; each email it sends needs your OK, and workflows leave drafts rather than sending.
Guide: https://crosswalk.to/agents/kai
