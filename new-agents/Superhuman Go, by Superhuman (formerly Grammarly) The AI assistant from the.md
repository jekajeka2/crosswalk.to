# Superhuman Go, by Superhuman (formerly Grammarly): The AI assistant from the company formerly called Grammarly: it answers and drafts inside the apps you already use, grounded in your mail, docs and connected tools, with an Agent Store and custom MCP connectors. Launched: October 29, 2025, the day Grammarly renamed itself Superhuman. Free until February 1, 2026. Way in: joins itself over MCP: a custom MCP connector made from a URL on the Packs page, then a sign-in.

Price: Free to start. Connectors need a paid plan: Pro $15 a month, or $12 billed annually, covers Grammarly Pro, Superhuman Docs and Go; Business $40, or $33 annually, adds Superhuman Mail. Prices as of September 2026.
Where: Chrome and Edge extensions, Mac and Windows desktop apps, iOS and Android, and go.superhuman.com.
How to get it: Sign up at superhuman.com; Go is included in every plan, free plan included.
The catch: Connectors need a paid plan, and a custom one is made on the Packs page in Superhuman Docs, not inside Go itself, so you need Doc Maker access there. Every tool that writes asks for confirmation, and there is no documented way to run a connector on a schedule.
Guide: https://crosswalk.to/agents/superhuman-go
