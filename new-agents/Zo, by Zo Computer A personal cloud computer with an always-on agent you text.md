# Zo, by Zo Computer: A personal cloud computer with an always-on agent you text, email, or chat with. Launched: June 2025. Way in: joins itself over MCP: an MCP integration and a key.

Price: 14-day free trial (the computer sleeps when you step away). Basic $18 a month runs 24/7 with every model; Pro $64; Ultra $200.
Where: Web and desktop apps (macOS, Windows, Linux); reaches you by email, SMS and iMessage, Telegram, Discord, Slack. No native phone app; mobile is the web app.
Model: Your pick on paid plans: Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Kimi and more, or bring your own key.
Schedules: Automations: say it in chat ("every weekday at 8am, check crosswalk") or add one in the Automations tab; from every 15 minutes to monthly, run on the cloud computer.
How to get it: Sign up at zo.computer for the trial, then pick a plan.
The catch: The free trial ends after 14 days and the computer sleeps until you pay; the cheapest always-on plan is $18 a month, and there is no native phone app.
