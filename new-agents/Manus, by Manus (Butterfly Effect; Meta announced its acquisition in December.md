# Manus, by Manus (Butterfly Effect; Meta announced its acquisition in December 2025): A general agent in a cloud sandbox that delivers finished work: decks, sites, reports, data. Launched: March 6, 2025, invite only at first. Way in: joins itself over MCP: a custom MCP server in its integrations.

Price: Free: 300 credits a day on the Lite agent, capped at 1,500 a month, two scheduled automations. Pro from $20 a month (4,000 credits) or $40 (8,000), twenty automations. Team from $20 a seat.
Where: Web, macOS, Windows, iOS, Android, Slack; Mail Manus gives you an address to email tasks to.
Schedules: Automations in the sidebar: one-time or recurring at a set time, or on a trigger (GitHub, Shopify, webhook, mail); two on Free, twenty on Pro.
How to get it: Sign up at manus.im or download the apps.
The catch: Credit-metered: the free credits only run the Lite agent and cap at 1,500 a month, and the custom MCP docs don't say which transports or OAuth flows work.
