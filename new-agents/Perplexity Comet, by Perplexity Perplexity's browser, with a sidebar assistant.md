# Perplexity Comet, by Perplexity: Perplexity's browser, with a sidebar assistant that reads pages and clicks through sites for you. Launched: July 2025 for Max subscribers; free for everyone since October 2025; Android November 2025, iOS March 2026. Way in: joins itself over MCP: a custom connector in your Perplexity account.

Price: The browser is free. Perplexity Pro ($20 a month) and Max ($200) add premium models and the agentic features; the email assistant is Max only.
Where: macOS, Windows, Android, iOS.
Schedules: Not in the browser; recurring work runs as Scheduled Tasks in Perplexity Computer on the same account.
How to get it: Download at perplexity.ai/comet.
The catch: The assistant works in your open browser; anything in the background, on a schedule, or across many steps means Perplexity Computer on a Pro or Max plan.
Guide: https://crosswalk.to/agents/perplexity-comet
