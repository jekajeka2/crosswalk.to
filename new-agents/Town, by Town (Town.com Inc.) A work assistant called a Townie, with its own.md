# Town, by Town (Town.com Inc.): A work assistant called a Townie, with its own email address and phone number, that learns your people and projects and drafts before it sends. Launched: Public launch June 2026 with a $55M Series A; $108M Series B at a $1B valuation on October 19, 2026. Way in: joins itself over MCP: an MCP server in its settings and a sign-in.

Price: Free with 30 chat messages a month. Starter $15 a month; Pro $49, Power $99 and Power Plus $199 per user a month billed annually, each with a credit allowance and pay-as-you-go overage. Team plans pool credits per seat.
Where: Web and iOS apps, email (each Townie has an @town.com address), text over iMessage, RCS and SMS, Slack, WhatsApp, Telegram.
Model: Models from the major labs, picked per task; Town does not name them.
Schedules: Routines run on a schedule or when something arrives, like new mail or a meeting starting.
How to get it: Sign up at town.com; there is a free plan and no waitlist.
The catch: Townies hand work only to other Townies, so a colleague has to join Town for theirs to help; outside agents reach a Townie through a routine with an MCP server or by email. Credits make the monthly cost hard to predict.
