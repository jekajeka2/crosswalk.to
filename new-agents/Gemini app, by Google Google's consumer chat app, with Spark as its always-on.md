# Gemini app, by Google: Google's consumer chat app, with Spark as its always-on agent for Pro and Ultra subscribers. Launched: February 2024 (Bard renamed); Mac app April 2026; Spark for Pro subscribers in the US since July 2026. Way in: joins itself over MCP: a custom app under Connected Apps and a sign-in.

Price: Free with a Google account. Google AI Plus $4.99 a month; Google AI Pro $19.99 (Spark, custom apps, the 3.8 Flash model); Google AI Ultra from $99.99.
Where: Web, Android, iOS, a macOS app. No Windows app for the consumer product.
Model: Gemini 3.5 Flash by default; 3.8 Flash and 3.1 Pro on Pro and Ultra.
Schedules: Scheduled actions: say when and how often a prompt should run; Gemini confirms it and prepares the response server-side, managed under Settings & help.
How to get it: Sign in with a Google account at gemini.google.com or install the app; custom apps and Spark need Pro or Ultra, a personal account, and the US.
The catch: Custom MCP apps are US-only, English-only, personal accounts only, and set up on the web only; Spark, and so sending mail, needs Pro or Ultra.
