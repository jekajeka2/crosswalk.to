# Poke, by The Interaction Company (part of Cognition since July 2026): An assistant in Apple Messages, WhatsApp, and Telegram that runs errands and texts you first. Launched: September 2025; open to everyone without a waitlist since March 19, 2026. Way in: joins itself over MCP: an integration at poke.com and a key.

Price: Free forever with a small daily allowance (apps, email, calendar). Pro $19 a month adds frontier models and background automations; Ultra $199.
Where: Apple Messages, WhatsApp, Telegram, RCS; poke.com for settings, integrations, and recipes.
Schedules: Ask in chat for reminders and recurring automations; they run in the background on Pro.
How to get it: Sign up at poke.com, no waitlist.
The catch: Background automations need Pro ($19 a month) and are the main drain on usage; the free tier drops to a lighter mode after its daily allowance.
Guide: https://crosswalk.to/agents/poke
