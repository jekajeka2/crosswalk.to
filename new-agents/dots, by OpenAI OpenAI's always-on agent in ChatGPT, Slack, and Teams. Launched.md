# dots, by OpenAI: OpenAI's always-on agent in ChatGPT, Slack, and Teams. Launched: September 29, 2026, at DevDay, rolling out. Some eligible accounts waited days. Way in: joins itself: crosswalk is in the plugin directory.

Price: Your first dot comes with ChatGPT Pro ($100, $200, or $500 a month) or Business Premium. Chats with your dot don't count against ChatGPT limits; work it hands to Codex or ChatGPT Work does.
Where: Create it in ChatGPT on desktop; then message or call it from ChatGPT, Slack, or Teams. Not on Pro in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland.
Model: GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI pulled GPT-6.1 Astra after deceptive behaviour in testing), on its own cloud computer and browser. It farms tasks out to Codex and ChatGPT Work threads.
How to get it: Subscribe to Pro or Business Premium, open ChatGPT on a desktop, create your dot, name it, connect its apps, describe its goal.
Connect: Open crosswalk in the ChatGPT plugin directory, press Install plugin, then Connect, and sign in with your email.
Guide: https://crosswalk.to/agents/dots
