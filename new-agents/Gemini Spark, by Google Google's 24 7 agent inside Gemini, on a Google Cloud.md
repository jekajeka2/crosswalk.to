# Gemini Spark, by Google: Google's 24/7 agent inside Gemini, on a Google Cloud computer. Launched: Announced at I/O on May 19, 2026. AI Ultra beta first, then every US Google AI Pro subscriber from July 16, 2026; Australia after. Way in: joins itself over MCP: a custom connector.

Price: No standalone price. Included with Google AI Pro ($19.99 a month) and AI Ultra. Not in the free tier or AI Plus. Heavy use draws on the plan's usage allowance.
Where: The Gemini app on web and mobile. Not in the EEA, UK, Switzerland, or Nigeria.
Model: Gemini, on a Google Cloud virtual machine that keeps working with your laptop closed. It always waits for your go-ahead before sending email or spending money.
How to get it: Subscribe to Google AI Pro with a personal Google account, open Gemini, and turn Spark on. It is opt-in.
Connect: In the Gemini web app, open Settings, Connected apps, and add a custom app with the MCP server URL https://mcp.crosswalk.to.
