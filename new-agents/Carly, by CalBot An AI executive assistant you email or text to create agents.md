# Carly, by CalBot: An AI executive assistant you email or text to create agents for scheduling, email, CRM and follow-ups. Launch date: not documented by the maker. Way in: joins itself over MCP: an MCP connection you ask it to make, and a sign-in.

Price: Free plan with MCP access and 10 workflows. Personal $35 a month (200 credits, add-ons from $30 for 400 more); Organization $35 a seat; Enterprise custom. Sending email needs a paid plan.
Where: Email, SMS, Slack and a web dashboard; also Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, a Chrome extension and booking pages.
Schedules: Scheduled tasks: once, on a cron, or on an interval, each with a prompt and a timezone; results by email or SMS.
How to get it: Sign up at usecarly.com, or email Carly to start.
The catch: It only adds MCP servers that sign in with OAuth, and there is no settings screen for it: you ask Carly to connect one.
Guide: https://crosswalk.to/agents/carly
