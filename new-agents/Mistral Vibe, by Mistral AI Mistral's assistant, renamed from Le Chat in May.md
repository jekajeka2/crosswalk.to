# Mistral Vibe, by Mistral AI: Mistral's assistant, renamed from Le Chat in May 2026; its Work mode runs tasks on a schedule. Launched: Le Chat February 2024; apps February 2025; renamed Vibe May 28, 2026. Way in: joins itself over MCP: a custom MCP connector and a key or sign-in.

Price: Free with limited access and up to five scheduled tasks. Pro $14.99 a month; Team $24.99 a user; Enterprise custom.
Where: Web at chat.mistral.ai, iOS, Android, plus Vibe Code in a CLI and in VS Code, JetBrains, and Zed.
Model: Mistral Medium 3.5 by default on paid plans.
Schedules: Work mode: Scheduled in the sidebar, New Task, pick once, daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly and a time; or ask Work in chat to schedule it.
How to get it: Sign up free at chat.mistral.ai or install the Le Chat app; upgrade in-app.
The catch: The rename is recent and the old chat mode is being phased out; the Gmail connector drafts but never sends, and custom MCP connectors are admin-only and marked beta on paid tiers.
