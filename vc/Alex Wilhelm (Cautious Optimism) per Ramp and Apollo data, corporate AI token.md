Separately, DHS banned DeepSeek from civilian agency procurement and IT systems over the risk of data transfer to the PRC.

DeepSeek: DHS ordered DeepSeek products, and third-party products built on DeepSeek, out of civilian agency procurement and IT systems, citing possible data transfer to the PRC and security vulnerabilities in the iOS app; intelligence agencies and Commerce had already limited use
