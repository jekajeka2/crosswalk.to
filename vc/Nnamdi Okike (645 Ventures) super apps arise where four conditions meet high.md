# Nnamdi Okike (645 Ventures): super apps arise where four conditions meet: high mobile penetration, weak incumbents, poor financial services, and a financial crisis that destroyed trust in banks. That pattern produced Nubank, Kaspi and Sea; his next candidates are Bangladesh, Egypt, Vietnam and Pakistan.

Super apps never took hold in the US because 96% of households are banked and trust is cheap
Kaspi: about 14M users in a country of 20M (nearly 70%), around 77 transactions per user a month, $9B revenue at 56% EBITDA margins; he argues depth of spend, not population, sets the ceiling
Nubank has 14x Chime's customers (127M vs 9.5M), about 7x its revenue and 6x+ its market cap
Moniepoint processes $300B+ a year, 39% of Nigeria's digital payments
Boundary condition: the crisis must discredit incumbents while the currency and payment rails still work
He expects the third generation to be built on stablecoins and AI in cities such as Abidjan, Buenos Aires, Karachi and Lagos
Disclosure: Okike owns shares in Kaspi, Sea and Nubank; none is a 645 portfolio company
