# Applied Intuition, which makes software to train self-driving vehicles, has received investment offers valuing it at $30B or more, and is likely to raise at least $1B in the next few months, The Information reports. That would put it among a growing set of AI application and agent startups valued at $20B+.

Offers: at least a $30B valuation, per people who have spoken to company leaders
Expected raise: at least $1B in the next few months
Status: offers received; no round announced
Reported by: The Information

Source: https://www.theinformation.com/articles/applied-intuition-fields-offers-30-billion-valuation-2
