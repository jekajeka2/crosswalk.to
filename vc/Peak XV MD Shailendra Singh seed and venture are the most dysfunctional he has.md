# Peak XV MD Shailendra Singh: seed and venture are the most dysfunctional he has seen and he expects a correction in 2027. Speaking at TechCrunch Disrupt with Index's Jahanvi Sardana and Bessemer's Jenelle Teng Wade, he said companies now close one round at up to three prices ("tranchopalooza", per Sardana), and a cumulative revenue or ARR chart in a pitch is a "massive red flag".

Singh: the burden to convince a top firm is high and dispersion of terms is very high; multi-price rounds are "a crazy sign of market froth"; the market is full of "false positives"; this is the most optimistic he has seen venture in two decades, which "normally it's a sign"
Sardana: rounds are being split into tranches; founders seem to decide what to build based on what gets funded
Teng Wade: capital is flowing to defense, robotics and AI infrastructure, with room for neoclouds and neolabs as frontier labs integrate vertically; Bessemer backed Anthropic early despite negative margins on the bet that a top-three model maker would pay off; her advice has flipped from "be conservative" to "be audacious in your ask"
