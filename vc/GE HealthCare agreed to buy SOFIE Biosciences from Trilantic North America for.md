# GE HealthCare agreed to buy SOFIE Biosciences from Trilantic North America for $945M in cash. Dulles, Va.-based SOFIE runs a US network of 15 contract manufacturing sites with 21 cyclotrons making PET radiopharmaceuticals.

- Acquirer: GE HealthCare (Nasdaq: GEHC)
- Price: $945M cash
- Seller/backer: Trilantic North America
- SOFIE HQ: Dulles, VA
- Footprint: 15 contract manufacturing sites, 21 cyclotrons, producing PET radiopharmaceuticals (cancer scan chemicals)

Reported by: PE Hub, Axios Pro Rata

Source: https://www.pehub.com/trilantic-north-america-inks-945m-sale-of-sofie-biosciences-to-ge-healthcare
