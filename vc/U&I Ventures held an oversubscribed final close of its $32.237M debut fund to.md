# U&I Ventures held an oversubscribed final close of its $32.237M debut fund to back pre-seed and seed founders building AI-native infrastructure in security, engineering and IT. The San Francisco firm, founded in 2025 by Kim Moore (formerly at Glynn Capital and Innovation Endeavors), runs its sourcing and diligence through a network of 1,800 CIOs, CTOs and CISOs.

Fund I: $32.237M, final close, oversubscribed
Strategy: 40-45 pre-seed and seed companies, initial checks from $250K to more than $800K
Portfolio so far: nine investments across AI security, agent reliability, compliance and enterprise infrastructure, including Pluto Security and ZeroDrift; seven are still in stealth
Network: about 650 CISOs and security executives, 850 CTOs and 300+ CIOs, who meet weekly in closed-door sessions in San Francisco, New York, Tel Aviv and London
Team: founder and GP Kim Moore (backed Chronosphere and Drata at Glynn and Innovation Endeavors; employee No. 23 at Asana); founding operating partner Kim Cooper
Reported by: PR Newswire
