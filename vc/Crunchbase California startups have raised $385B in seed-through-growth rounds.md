# Crunchbase: California startups have raised $385B in seed-through-growth rounds so far in 2026, more than all US startups raised in any prior full year and more than double California's 2025 total. That is over three-fourths of US startup funding, an apparent record share. OpenAI and Anthropic account for more than half of it.

California's share of US startup funding has risen for three straight years. The gains extend beyond SF AI unicorns to the broader Silicon Valley region and Southern California.
The next-largest California rounds this year include Waymo ($16B, Feb), Prometheus, Jeff Bezos's physical AI startup ($12B, spring), Databricks ($5B, Aug) and Safe Superintelligence ($5B, Nvidia-led).
Seed and early stage: California companies have raised nearly $62B so far in 2026, roughly 57% of all US funding at those stages and an apparent record.
xAI's $20B raise counted as California earlier this year. xAI is now part of SpaceX, which is headquartered in Texas.
