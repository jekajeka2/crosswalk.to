# Graduate Ventures, the Rotterdam university-linked VC, launched its second seed fund at €125M with €100M already committed; LPs include Frank Slootman (Snowflake), Michiel Muller (Picnic), Jonas Ruyter (DataSnipper) and Peter Wennink (ASML), plus Invest-NL and Oost NL.

Fund: second seed fund, €125M target, €100M committed
Focus: AI, deep tech, healthcare and climate startups from eight Dutch universities and three university medical centres
Track record: 90+ startups backed since 2021; runs a pre-seed fund and two seed funds
Founders: Frans van Houten (ex-Philips CEO), Sandro van Hellenberg Hubar, Gert Jan van der Hoeven, Menno Antal
Led by managing partner Auke van den Hout, team of 10
Reported by: VC Wire
