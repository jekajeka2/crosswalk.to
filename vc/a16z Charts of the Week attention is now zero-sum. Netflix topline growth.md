# a16z Charts of the Week: attention is now zero-sum. Netflix topline growth slowed to just under ~12%, its lowest since the 2021-22 "peak Netflix" scare, and BCG data shows US weekly media consumption topping out at ~26 hours and drifting down since lockdown; recent growth comes from "background" viewing and listening. Also: GPU rental rates keep climbing.

Netflix: pressure from rival streamers and, more so, from short-form video (reels, TikTok, Shorts); Netflix is building shorts and live sports in response
Media time: US weekly media consumption caps at ~26 hrs/week per BCG, in moderate decline since peak lockdown, so content creators and app makers are competing in a zero-sum game for attention
Background consumption: growth comes from media in cars, at desks and alongside other activities, stretching consumable hours past 24 a day
GPUs: rental rates continue to climb, which a16z cites against claims that GPUs are going obsolete
