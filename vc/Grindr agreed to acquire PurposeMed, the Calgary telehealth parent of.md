# Grindr agreed to acquire PurposeMed, the Calgary telehealth parent of HIV-prevention provider Freddie, for $250M in cash and stock, plus up to $70M more tied to 2027 targets. It's Grindr's first major acquisition as it moves from dating into healthcare; PurposeMed had raised only $19.7M.

- Price: $250M in cash and stock; up to $70M earnout tied to 2027 performance targets
- PurposeMed: seven years old, Calgary; total funding $19.7M
- Investors: True North Fund, BDC Capital, Panache Ventures, Azure Capital Partners, Prairies Economic Development Canada, The C100, University of Calgary
- Reported by: StrictlyVC, The Wall Street Journal
