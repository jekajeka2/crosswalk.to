# a16z, leading doxxnet's Series A: AI makes mass surveillance cheap to analyze and agents widen the attack surface, so privacy needs its own infrastructure. doxxnet, from Prolexic founder Barrett Lyon, runs a parallel private network on its own backbone, IP space, naming system and certificate authority, with agents configuring it ('Agentic Defined Networking').

- Thesis: "security through obscurity" died once AI could process everything collected; agents that read documents, call APIs and browse with user credentials make who observes their connections a mainstream concern
- Product: private browsing, direct peer-to-peer calls, messages and file transfer with no central server, encrypted device/agent meshes, non-Internet domains resolving inside the network, DNS-level phishing/malware blocking
- Infrastructure: own global backbone with dedicated hardware, own IP space, private naming and certificate authority; sign-up needs no email or phone number
- Founder: Barrett Lyon (Prolexic, acquired by Akamai; BitGravity, acquired by Tata Communications; Defense.net, acquired by F5)
- Round: Series A led by a16z (Axios Pro reported it as $38M; company listed as Doxx, Miami)
