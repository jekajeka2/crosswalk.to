# Crunchbase: nuclear startups have raised more than $6B so far in 2026, a record ahead of any comparable period, led by Commonwealth Fusion Systems' $1B July round and Valar Atomics' $1B across two Series B tranches. But the public side has soured: X-energy has shed about half its $12B debut valuation, and Oklo is down about two-thirds from its high.

- Funding: more than $6B into fission and fusion startups year to date, beating 2025, the prior record year; round counts also at historically high levels
- Largest rounds: Commonwealth Fusion Systems $1B equity (July); Valar Atomics $1B across two Series B tranches
- IPOs: at least three nuclear companies listed above $1B in the past six months. X-energy (Rockville, MD) hit $12B in April trading and has since lost about half; Standard Nuclear (Oak Ridge, TN) and Deep Fission (Berkeley, CA) trade well below their peaks
- Oklo shares are down about two-thirds from a high about a year ago
- Headwinds cited: public backlash against data center buildouts, high capital costs and long licensing timelines
- Reported by: Crunchbase News

Source: https://news.crunchbase.com/clean-tech-and-energy/nuclear-startup-funding-up-public-markets-bearish
