# BAG Ventures, founded by ex-Google VP Bonita Stewart and ex-CapitalG partner Jackson Georges Jr., closed an $11.3M Fund I for early-stage AI. Checks are $100K-$500K. The thesis: enterprises are leaving the AI 'experimental sandbox' and will pay for outcomes and completed jobs, not per-seat tools, so thin model wrappers get wiped out.

- 10 companies backed so far, including SXD, BizTrip (AI travel agent) and Nomadic (agentic reasoning)
- Focus: AI infra, compute, physical/edge AI, security, governance, vertical SaaS
- 150+ LPs, including Google and operators from Nvidia, Amazon and Snowflake. The edge they pitch is warm customer introductions.
- Looking for: teams that have worked together before, an MVP, at least one partner, workflow depth and proprietary data; also IAM / zero-trust for AI agents
- Plans to deploy the rest of the fund over the next two years
- Reported by: TechCrunch
