# Formation Bio, the New York AI-native pharma company, launched a $600M debut fund (Drug Development Accelerator Fund) to acquire 10-15 clinical-stage drug programs over 3-5 years, each housed in a standalone entity run on Formation's development platform.

Commitments: $600M from research foundations, institutional endowments, hospitals and philanthropic organizations
GP: a wholly owned Formation Bio subsidiary; Formation Bio also committed its own capital
Strategy: acquire or in-license Phase 1/2 programs from pharma and biotechs, target clinical milestones within 2-4 years, then partner programs for commercialization
Rationale (CEO Benjamine Liu): gives LPs access to individual drug assets, creates liquidity paths without selling the parent, lowers cost of capital over time
HQ: New York, NY, with a second office in Boston
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/formation-bio-announces-600-million-debut-fund-to-expand-drug-development-capacity-302921852.html
