# a16z has officially renamed a16z Crypto to a16z Frontier and widened its new fund beyond crypto to frontier startups: AI-native products, autonomous machines and new devices, bio, and networks for AI agents, with crypto still in scope. Marc Andreessen and Ben Horowitz co-lead it with GPs Eddy Lazzarin and Guy Wuollet. The crypto fund family has raised nearly $10B across five funds; GP Ali Yahya is leaving to start a company, per The Information.

Firm: Andreessen Horowitz's crypto fund family (a16z crypto, nearly $10B raised across five funds, per The Information)
Change: renamed a16z Frontier; a16z says its new fund is expanding beyond crypto to frontier startups (new technologies and markets that may take years to develop), and it will still invest in crypto and push for crypto regulation
Thesis: AI-native uses that look like toys at first, a wave of new devices and autonomous machines, bio and medicine, full-stack startups that combine software, hardware and services, and machine-to-machine networks that use blockchains and stablecoins as payment rails
Fund leadership: Marc Andreessen and Ben Horowitz, with GPs Eddy Lazzarin and Guy Wuollet
People: GP Ali Yahya, who joined a16z in 2017 as the first full-time member of its crypto team, told some investors he is leaving to start his own company (The Information, citing one person briefed)
Reported by: a16z, The Information
