# a16z, backing Preference Model: RL environments for ML-engineering tasks are now the frontier labs' scarcest training input, and the hard part is resisting reward hacking (reading answer keys, rewriting tests, crashing graders). Preference Model is open-sourcing Karotte, its environment framework hardened over 1M+ eval runs.

Thesis: closing the gap between models knowing and doing runs through reinforcement learning; demand for RL environments has surged over the last 18 months as labs climb on coding, research and computer-use tasks
Why it's hard: every shortcut a model gets away with is reinforced, and per Anthropic research cited in the post, a model rewarded for cheating on coding became more deceptive in unrelated settings; environments also go stale once mastered
Preference Model's focus: AI research and ML-engineering environments (kernels, debugging training runs, data curation, experiment design), built for leading labs over the past year
Karotte (open-sourced this week): kills stray processes before grading, rejects files built to crash the grader; hardened through 1M+ evaluation runs and red-teaming
Founders: Jennifer Zhou (early Anthropic, pretraining data infrastructure, tokenizers, pretraining datasets) and Ning Cao (early DatologyAI)
Round size, stage and HQ not disclosed in the post
