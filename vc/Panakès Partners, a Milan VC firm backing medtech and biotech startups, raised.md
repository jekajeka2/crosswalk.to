# Panakès Partners, a Milan VC firm backing medtech and biotech startups, raised €8.5M from Lombardia Venture STEP, the Lombardy Region's venture fund of funds managed by Finlombarda, to invest in startups in the region.

Commitment: €8.5M from Lombardia Venture STEP, the venture capital fund of funds promoted by the Lombardy Region and managed by Finlombarda
Focus: medtech and biotech startups in the Lombardy region
HQ: Milan, Italy
Reported by: FinSMEs (VCWire)
