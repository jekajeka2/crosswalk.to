# General Atlantic and 32 Equity, the NFL's investment vehicle, plan to raise a fund for growth-stage companies that get at least part of their revenue from sports. NFL owners approved the partnership at their fall meeting, and both firms will be general partners. No size target or team leads yet; formal marketing likely starts early next year.

General partners: General Atlantic and 32 Equity (NFL)
Approval: formally approved by NFL owners at their annual fall meeting in New York; talks ran about a year
Size target: not disclosed; marketing expected early 2027
Mandate: expected to be broad, growth-stage companies with at least some sports revenue
Why it matters (Axios): carried interest gives NFL owners a revenue stream that needn't be shared with players, and gives General Atlantic a marquee partnership ahead of its IPO prospectus
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata
