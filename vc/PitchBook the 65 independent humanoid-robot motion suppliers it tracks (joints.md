# PitchBook: the 65 independent humanoid-robot motion suppliers it tracks (joints, motors, transmissions, sensors) raised $409.4M in 2026 through Sept. 5, nearly half their cumulative funding since 2012. Actuators are 40-60% of a humanoid's hardware bill, but all 11 humanoid developers it compared build proprietary joints, actuators or motors, which caps the independent-supplier market.

- Actuators account for 40-60% of a humanoid's hardware bill of materials (McKinsey, cited by PitchBook)
- At a 50% share of the parts budget, a 20% cut in actuator cost lowers the total hardware bill by 10%
- 65 independent motion suppliers raised $409.4M this year through Sept. 5, nearly half their cumulative funding since 2012
- Caveat: all 11 humanoid developers in PitchBook's comparison group develop proprietary joints, actuators or motors; they may buy components and outsource production while keeping design control, which splits the opportunity among specialized manufacturers
- Price cuts must not cost reliability: joints have to withstand repeated use, resist overheating and perform consistently
- Reported by: PitchBook (The Daily Pitch, Oct. 5, 2026; analyst note by Ali Javaheri)
