# AlleyWatch: US startups raised $27.89B in September 2026, up 54% from August; four $1B+ rounds (Crusoe, The Boring Company, Cognition, Stoke Space) took $9.9B of it, and late-stage capital doubled to $19.84B as AI infrastructure, space and defense absorbed the money.

Total US venture funding, September 2026: $27.89B, up 54% month over month
$1B+ rounds: Crusoe, The Boring Company, Cognition, Stoke Space; together $9.9B (about 35% of the month)
Late-stage: $19.84B, double the prior month
Sectors drawing the capital: AI infrastructure, space, defense
Reported by: AlleyWatch (The September 2026 US Venture Capital Funding Report, in The Pulse, Oct 30)
