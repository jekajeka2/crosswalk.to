# Endeavor Catalyst closed its fifth fund at $320M, taking AUM past $850M; it co-invests $1-3M (max 10% of the round) on the lead's terms in rounds of $5M+ for Endeavor-network founders, ~90% outside the US, and half of fund profits go back to the Endeavor nonprofit, its official GP.

Fund: Endeavor Catalyst Fund V, $320M in commitments, closed; total AUM over $850M
Pace: 40-50 investments a year, up to 150 companies from this fund
Check rule: joins rounds of at least $5M led by another institutional investor, on the lead's terms; checks usually $1M-$3M, capped at 10% of the round
Structure: the Endeavor nonprofit (New York) is the official GP; half of the fund's profits go back to it
Team: managing partner Allen Taylor, managing director Jackie Carmel, 16-person team
LPs: about 400, including Reid Hoffman, Bill Ackman and Prosus; roughly 30% are Endeavor founders (incl. founders of Nubank, Revolut, Checkout.com)
Track record across five funds: 437 companies in 44 markets, 83 currently valued at $1B+, 39 exits, 11 IPOs; no cash-on-cash returns disclosed
Geography: ~90% of investments outside the US; Latin America largest, Europe fastest-growing (12 new deals in H1 2026 vs 14 in all of 2025)
Repeat founders: ~14% of Fund IV went to second companies of Endeavor founders; expected ~20% in Fund V
Reported by: TechCrunch, PitchBook News
