# Vintage Investment Partners raised $920M in new capital, bringing AUM to over $4.8B. The venture-only fund-of-funds, secondaries and growth investor (Tel Aviv, London, New York; founded 2003) is betting on demand for liquidity as companies stay private longer. It cites PitchBook-NVCA data that three firms took nearly half of all US VC raised in H1 2026.

Strategies: fund of funds, secondaries, growth; invests in venture funds and venture-backed companies on a primary and secondary basis
More than $1.1B raised over the past three years across primary, secondary and co-investment strategies
Offices: Tel Aviv, London, New York
Co-managing partners: Asaf Horesh, Abe Finkelstein
People moves: Tzlil Kovetz promoted to Partner; Chief Value Officer Ilan Leiferman (leads Value+) promoted to Partner; Brachie Sprung appointed Chief Investor Relations Officer
Value+ platform connects portfolio companies with 650+ enterprises
Reported by: PR Newswire (firm announcement)
