# Advent International raised $30B in a fundraising drought: nearly $27B for its 11th flagship buyout fund, GPE XI (above its $26B target; second-largest buyout fund ever outside state-run vehicles), plus $3B for a debut midmarket fund. GPE XI took 20 months to raise, versus six months for the $25B Fund X in 2021.

Flagship: Advent International GPE XI, nearly $27B, beat its $26B target (PitchBook calls it the 11th flagship; Axios reported it as the ninth)
New midmarket fund: over $3B, Advent's first
Time to raise: 20 months, vs six months for 2021-vintage Fund X ($25B)
Performance: Funds IV and V were top quartile by IRR; none are now, per PitchBook data, but all flagships match or beat benchmarks on distributions
Realizations: $13B returned to LPs in 2026 from full or partial exits of 27 businesses
Strategy: complex deals such as corporate carveouts and take-privates, mainly in North America and Europe
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata, PitchBook News
