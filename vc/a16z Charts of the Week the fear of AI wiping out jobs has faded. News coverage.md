# a16z Charts of the Week: the fear of AI wiping out jobs has faded. News coverage of AI and job loss is back to about where it was a year ago, explicit AI-linked layoff announcements in August and September were the lowest of 2026, and Indeed postings for software, manufacturing and especially engineering roles keep rising.

Context: Microsoft offered buyouts to ~60,000 employees (about a quarter of staff), worth ~$10B if all accepted; its CFO framed it as "agency," not AI, and speculation tied it to capex
News reports on AI and job loss have reverted to ~1-year-ago levels after a May peak
Indeed: SWE, manufacturing and engineering postings continue an upward inflection from last year; HR postings, a leading hiring signal, are back at their pre-pandemic baseline
a16z argues claims of widespread AI substitution lacked evidence then and still do, while AI may be lifting demand for technical roles
