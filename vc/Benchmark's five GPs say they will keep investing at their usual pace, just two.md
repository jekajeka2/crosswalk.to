# Benchmark's five GPs say they will keep investing at their usual pace, just two or three new bets per partner per year, even after this summer's $2B raise across two funds. Peter Fenton expects the new $1.25B growth fund to make only four to eight concentrated bets, and says holding the core fund at $500M for 20 years was "probably a tactical error."

Funds: $2B across two funds announced this summer, only the second time in 31 years Benchmark has gone beyond a much smaller vehicle; includes a $1.25B growth fund
Growth fund thesis (Fenton): before it, Benchmark's early-stage mandate kept it out of deals that would have returned 10x to 1,000x; he expects one or two investments to generate 90% of returns
Pace: two or three new investments per partner per year; Monday partner meetings have no agenda
Partners: Peter Fenton, Eric Vishria, Chethan Puttagunta, Ev Randall (ex-Founders Fund, Bond, Kleiner Perkins), Jack Altman (joined in February after raising a $275M second fund for his own firm); three of the four partners TechCrunch interviewed three years ago have left
Market view: the partners call many recession warning signs X-fueled hysteria
Reported by: TechCrunch (Disrupt 2026 onstage interview)
