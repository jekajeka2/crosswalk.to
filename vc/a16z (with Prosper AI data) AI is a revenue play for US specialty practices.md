# a16z (with Prosper AI data): AI is a revenue play for US specialty practices, not just cost cutting: four levers worth ~$125K (about 10%) per physician a year, $108B in total. 1 in 6 daytime calls go unanswered (81% after hours), only 54% of patients return for follow-ups on schedule, and front-end errors drive nearly half of claim denials.

Capture demand: AI call answering and schedule-filling worth ~$44,800 per physician a year; 18% daytime missed-call rate across ~2,000 practices in 29 states; 19% of provider schedules go unfilled
Recapture demand: AI outreach to bring overdue follow-up patients back worth up to $37,400 per physician (40% return late, 6% never return)
Protect reimbursements: AI benefits checks before visits to avoid front-end denials worth ~$28,500 per physician
Recover revenue: outbound AI calls to collect patient balances worth ~$14,100 per physician; patients now pay 36% of their care cost and ~8% of it goes unpaid
Context: 84% of medical groups report higher operating costs than a year ago (avg +11%); CMS won't renew the temporary 2.5% Medicare boost expiring at the end of 2026; inflation-adjusted Medicare physician pay is down 33% since 2001
Data: Prosper AI interactions covering $3B+ of care plus MGMA specialty data and its CallIQ phone-access study (dermatology, ophthalmology, GI, OB-GYN, primary care, orthopedics)
Caveat: the study comes from Prosper AI, which sells AI patient-access tools
