# Anurag Gurtu (Airrived CEO) in Crunchbase News: enterprise AI startups leaning on armies of forward deployed engineers are booking labor-intensive services as software ARR. His test for investors: if you pulled the embedded engineer out tomorrow, would the customer still get what it paid for? Two companies at the same $20M ARR can need 10 engineers or 100, and the gap shows up in gross margin and onboarding cost.

FDE job postings are up 800-1,000%+ year over year (Lightcast via Fortune: 1,000%+ Jan-Aug 2026; Indeed: 643 postings in Apr 2025 to 5,330 in Apr 2026)
Pay: Anthropic lists $280K-$320K base, OpenAI $185K-$300K base plus equity; some Anthropic roles reach $400K total comp (Fortune)
OpenAI pledged up to $1.5B to a deployment JV (DeployCo) valued at $10B, with $4B more from TPG, Bain Capital, Advent, Brookfield and Goanna, and guaranteed its PE backers a 17.5% annual return over five years
Proposed checks for buyers and investors: fully loaded cost to onboard the next customer, whether it falls with scale, and gross margin once hypergrowth slows
"Software ARR should get cheaper to deliver as it scales. Human-powered ARR does not."
