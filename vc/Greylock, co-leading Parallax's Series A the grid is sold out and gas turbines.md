# Greylock, co-leading Parallax's Series A: the grid is sold out and gas turbines are booked until 2030+, so the scalable off-grid power for AI data centers over the next 5 years is natural gas from a turbine built for data centers, not jets or utilities. Parallax 3D-prints its turbines to control its own supply chain; first product is a 10MW unit.

- Thesis: existing turbines are engineered for jet-engine and large-utility reliability demands, with complex supply chains booked for years; data centers need power now, easy to install and produce at scale
- Market sizing per Greylock: ~100 GW U.S. power shortfall over the next 5 years, worth over $100B
- Approach: additive manufacturing (3D printing) for supply-chain control; first product a 10MW gas turbine purpose-built for data centers
- Founder: Carl (Stanford mechanical engineering; surname not given in the post)
- Round: Series A, co-led by Greylock; co-investors Founders Fund, Eclipse, Lux, Diffusion and General Catalyst; amount not disclosed
- Company works out of a 30,000 sq ft warehouse in Brisbane, California

Reported by: Greylock

Source: https://greylock.com/blog/introducing-parallax-the-ai-energy-company
