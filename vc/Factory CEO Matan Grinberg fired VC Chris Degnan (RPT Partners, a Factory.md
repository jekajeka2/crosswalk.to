# Factory CEO Matan Grinberg fired VC Chris Degnan (RPT Partners, a Factory investor) as board advisor, alleging he shared confidential info with rival Cognition; two hours later Degnan announced he's Cognition's CRO, and said RPT and managing partner Chad Peets will work closely with Cognition.

- Degnan: Snowflake's first sales hire, then its CRO for 11 years; partner at Newport Beach-based RPT Partners for the last five months; also lists a go-to-market advisor role with Iconiq.
- Grinberg says Degnan admitted a "casual" conversation with a Cognition exec, then disclosed Monday he'd been in ongoing talks; fired from the board Tuesday.
- Factory raised $200M at a $5B valuation this month; Cognition raised $2B at a $48B valuation earlier this month.
- Context: a16z is reportedly the subject of a DOJ probe into partners serving on competitors' boards.
- Degnan, Peets and Factory did not respond to comment requests.
