# Amulya Vishwanath (Techable VC; seven years leading product marketing for Nvidia's robotics platforms): robotics is in its "Lego era", with actuators, robotic hands, software and safety standards still being rebuilt, so no single foundation model is about to give it a "ChatGPT moment". She writes first checks in physical AI across robotics, materials science and life sciences.

Counterview cited: General Intuition CEO Pim de Witte has argued a single general foundation model will make much specialized robotics work redundant
Vishwanath is founder and managing partner of Techable VC, one of the first women executives to spin out of Nvidia and launch an early-stage firm
Format: TechCrunch Equity podcast interview (substance is in the episode)
Reported by: TechCrunch
