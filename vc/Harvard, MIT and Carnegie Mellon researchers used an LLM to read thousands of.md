# Harvard, MIT and Carnegie Mellon researchers used an LLM to read thousands of PE fund reports (2009-2018 vintages): how GPs described portfolio companies predicted performance better than reported valuations in the first three years; negative macro commentary was the strongest predictor of poor returns.

Method: an LLM scored the sentiment of GP commentary on each company in thousands of PE reports for 2009-2018 funds, then compared it with the valuations in the same reports against later performance
Finding: written commentary beat valuations as a predictor of performance within the first three years of an investment
Topics: a focus on cash flow and business models was associated with significantly better exit performance; talk of capital structure pointed to worse outcomes
Strongest negative signal: negative macroeconomic commentary
Josh Lerner (Harvard Business School, co-author): GPs find it easier to blame the macro economy than admit backing the wrong entrepreneur
Reported by: PitchBook News (Jessica Hamlin, The Daily Pitch, Oct. 7, 2026)
