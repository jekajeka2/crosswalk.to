# Disruptive, the Dallas growth equity firm, has raised $10B for its first flagship fund (up from $7.5B secured toward a $10B target in early October), after investing through SPVs since its 2012 founding; founder Alex Davis says the move responds to fears SPV holders will be burned as SpaceX goes public.

Fund: first flagship fund, $10B raised
Earlier: $7.5B secured toward a $10B target, per The Wall Street Journal (Oct 7, https://axios.link/4hzA5gN)
Active investments include Databricks, Groq, ElevenLabs, Helsing, Reflection AI and Shield AI
Why: Axios frames it as startup-ecosystem pushback against SPVs; Davis: "All of a sudden it's going to be a shitshow for SPVs and we don't want to get lumped into it."
Docs were readied as summer ended, with a quick LP response
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata, citing The Wall Street Journal for the earlier figure
