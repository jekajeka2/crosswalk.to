# Oren Zeev (Zeev Ventures, a one-man firm managing nearly $3B): FOMO and huge fund sizes push big platforms to chase the same handful of AI companies, widening the gap between private and public valuations; he sits that game out for high-conviction early-stage bets with smaller funds, and sees the AI application layer as underappreciated next to infra, models and chips.

Format: Venture Unlocked podcast with Samir Kaji
Zeev's view on vertical AI: real moats exist beyond thin wrappers; he pushes back on the "SaaS apocalypse" narrative
Also covers separating durable growth from momentum and creative accounting, and holding conviction in Navan through extreme uncertainty
Zeev was an early investor in Audible, Chegg, Houzz, Tipalti and Navan; previously a GP at Apax Partners
Reported by: Venture Unlocked
