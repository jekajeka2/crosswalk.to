# Crunchbase: global venture funding hit $159B in Q3 2026, down 25% QoQ from $212B but up 53% YoY. A record 27 companies raised $1B+ rounds (up from 16 in Q2) and took about a third of all capital. AI got $102B (64%). U.S. startups took $91B (57%), and the Bay Area alone took 24% of the global total.

- YTD Q1-Q3 2026: $679B, the most ever raised in the first three quarters of a year; Q3 was the lowest quarter of 2026 but beat every quarter since Q2 2022
- Largest rounds: Databricks and Safe Superintelligence, $5B each; Crusoe, Moonshot AI, Mistral AI, Nscale, The Boring Co. and Kling AI each raised more than $3B. Five of the eight $3B+ raisers were founded in the past four years
- Late stage: $105B (-23% QoQ, +73% YoY); rounds of $100M+ were about 90% of late-stage dollars
- Early stage: $40.6B (+25% YoY); $100M+ rounds were half of early-stage financings
- Seed: $13B, of which $2.6B came in $100M+ seed rounds
- Aerospace, robotics, data centers, semiconductors and energy each raised $10B+ in the quarter
- Half of Q3 capital went to companies founded since 2022
- Exits: biggest IPOs were ChangXin Memory ($8.6B raised at $85.5B), Shein ($26.3B valuation, vs. its $100B 2022 peak), Enflame, Bending Spoons ($18.4B) and Unitree ($9B). Biggest M&A were Nvidia–Hugging Face ($12.9B), AMD–World Labs ($8.2B) and Stripe–OpenRouter ($7.5B)
- Data as of Oct. 2; seed totals usually rise as late reports come in
