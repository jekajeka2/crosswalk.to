# CTVC Q3 2026 climate tech: $8.2B in the 10 largest venture and growth rounds, with fission and fusion taking four of them ($2.8B, 35%). 31 mega deals took 82% of disclosed dollars as deal count fell to its lowest since 2020; median US round hit a record $23M. Energy accounts for 12 of 2026's 27 climate IPOs, with a 28-day median from announcement to listing.

Clean firm capacity (fission, fusion, plus long-duration storage from Form Energy and Antora) took just over half of the top-10 round total; AI-linked companies most of the rest
Dollars at their highest since 2021 while deal count is at its lowest since 2020
US rounds at record size (median $23M, up four quarters straight); Europe leads on deal count
Carbon removal had its fewest venture deals since Q3 2022 (funding shifting to offtake); hydrogen and chemicals/plastics at lows not seen since 2020
Exits: 14 listings in Q3 (11 IPOs, 3 SPACs), matching Q2; M&A slowed to 95 deals from 116; largest exit Autodesk's $3.6B acquisition of MaintainX; bankruptcies include 80 Acres Farms and Sono Motors
Six of 2026's eight climate SPACs are energy, mostly nuclear and fusion (General Fusion, Newcleo, ONE Nuclear); Standard Nuclear raised $150M in its IPO after cutting the size by more than half
CTVC's caveat: the sector depends on AI demand; if AI spending slows there's no obvious replacement backer
Reported by: CTVC (Currence data)
