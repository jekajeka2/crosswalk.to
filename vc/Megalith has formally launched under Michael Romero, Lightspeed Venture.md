# Megalith has formally launched under Michael Romero, Lightspeed Venture Partners' IR and ops lead for the past 13 years, and former Coatue investor Preston Moore. It focuses on co-investing in early-stage tech startups by buying seed funds' pro rata rights and other follow-on allocations, paying the seed funds for sourcing instead of relying on LP-in-seed-fund handshake deals.

Founders: Michael Romero (13 years in IR and operations at Lightspeed Venture Partners; the "top fundraiser" in The Information's headline) and Preston Moore (formerly at Coatue)
Model: takes a seed fund's pro rata allocation or other follow-on investments in its portfolio companies and compensates the seed fund for sourcing
Focus: co-investments in early-stage tech startups (Axios)
Contrast: large firms usually get later-round access by investing in seed funds as LPs, with an informal agreement to be told when a hot startup is about to raise again
Reported by: The Information (paywalled; teaser only), Axios Pro Rata (Oct. 23)

Source: https://www.theinformation.com/articles/lightspeeds-top-fundraiser-joins-firm-takes-seed-funds-pro-rata
