# CTVC: Fervo's Cape Station (Utah) is the first utility-scale enhanced geothermal plant selling power. Its first 33MW GeoBlock declared commercial operation on Sept 30. Per COO Sarah Jewett, PPAs now run $100-130/MWh, with $130 "no longer the cap." Phase I cost about $7,000/kW against a $3,000/kW target.

- First GeoBlock synced to the grid Sept 24 and hit 33MW net (the PPA threshold) on Sept 30. It is one-third of the ~100MW Phase I; the other two blocks are due by Jan 1, 2027 (power to Southern California Edison; the first block sells to Shell Energy)
- 400MW Phase II under construction for 2028. Wells drilled so far are more productive than Phase I's
- Google PPA (Sept 1): 396MW, the largest enhanced-geothermal PPA to date, with an option for ~600MW more
- Cost: Phase I ~$7,000/kW and Phase II ~$5,500/kW (Canary Media). Target is $3,000/kW overnight capex ("stretch but achievable") and a 50MW GeoBlock built in 18 months. Fervo's S-1 models firm power at $115/MWh
- Drilling times down ~75% since 2022. Project Red temperatures held steady 500+ days and recovered ~70% of circulated water (single injector/producer). Cape water-loss data is coming
- An independent engineering report puts the Cape resource at up to 4GW
- CTVC's investor read: capital waits for Cape's water-loss data, blocks two and three, thermal-decline data and Phase II at ~$5,500/kW. Then "a big rush of capital could come in"
