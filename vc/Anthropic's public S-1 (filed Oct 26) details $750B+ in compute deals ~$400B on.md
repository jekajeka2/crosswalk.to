# Anthropic's public S-1 (filed Oct 26) details $750B+ in compute deals: ~$400B on Google TPUs (incl. $161.2B Broadcom leases), up to $120.5B with SpaceX's AI unit, $110B+ with AWS, $44.9B Azure, ~$44.6B Nscale, ~$41.3B Lambda. Q2 revenue $11.6B with a first operating profit of $974M; ~$132B cash at June 30; Spark Capital (~$777M invested) is the only VC named. Earlier, per The Information, Anthropic told pre-IPO investors Q3 revenue was $16.8B (+~45% q/q), with valuation talk around $1.5T, down from ~$2T (Reuters).

S-1 compute: Google $114.5B direct through July 2033, Broadcom $161.2B five-year TPU leases, est. $125-150B more TPU capacity 2028-2031; SpaceX (ex-xAI) up to $120.5B through 2029, ~$3.1B/month at full capacity expected by December, cancellable on 90 days' notice after early 2027; AWS at least $110B through April 2036; Microsoft Azure at least $44.9B Nov 2026-May 2033 (up from $30B)
Most commitments are non-cancellable; Microsoft, Google and Amazon deals are payable regardless of usage; Q2 compute and infrastructure spend was under $2B/month
S-1 financials: Q2 revenue $11.6B (vs $787M a year earlier), first operating profit $974M, net loss $66.3B mostly from valuation-linked accounting charges; Q3 left blank, with space for Q3 results and an Oct 31 run rate (Cautious Optimism)
Cash: ~$132B cash and short-term investments at June 30, 95%+ of what it raised privately; $65B of venture funding came a month before quarter-end (Axios)
Investors: Amazon valued its stake at $190.4B at June 30 (incl. $6.2B-principal convertible notes valued at $97.9B); Spark Capital is the only VC firm named, due to related-party rules (partner Yasmin Razavi is the only investor on the board), not a 5% stake
Founders: seven co-founders hold ~230-245M shares each (~$14B apiece at the last private price); Public Benefit Fund got ~847M shares (5% fully diluted, ~$50B)
International: 27.8% of H1 2026 revenue (29.5% in H1 2025) (Cautious Optimism)
Timing: marketing could start the week of Nov 9 (Bloomberg, via GeekWire); Nasdaq ticker ANT
Earlier (Oct 14 private pre-IPO investor meeting, per The Information, people briefed, not company-confirmed): Q3 2026 revenue $16.8B, ~45% above Q2 and more than 3.6x full-year 2025; Q2 had grown 140%+ over Q1 on Claude Code demand; some investors discussing ~$1.5T valuation vs ~$2T previously expected (Reuters, via StrictlyVC)
Reported by: GeekWire, Axios Pro Rata, Cautious Optimism, The Information, Reuters, StrictlyVC
