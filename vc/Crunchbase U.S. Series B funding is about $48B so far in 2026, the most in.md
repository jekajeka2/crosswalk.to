# Crunchbase: U.S. Series B funding is about $48B so far in 2026, the most in years, but round counts are on track for a multiyear low. Less than a third of Series B deals were under $30M (about half in 2021-2025), at least 110 rounds were $100M+, and about two-thirds of the dollars went to AI startups (about half in 2025).

Total: about $48B into U.S. rounds at or around Series B so far in 2026, the highest in years; deal count is below last year's
Small rounds are getting rare: less than a third of deals were under $30M, against about half in 2021-2025; only 26 rounds were under $10M
Big rounds: at least 110 companies raised $100M+ at this stage, about a quarter of all deals
Largest: Prometheus (Jeff Bezos' physical AI startup) at $12B, described as Series B in reports but not announced that way; World Labs and Valar Atomics at $1B each
AI's share: about two-thirds of Series B dollars and more than half of deals, against about half of dollars in 2025
Method: the sample counts U.S. rounds of $1M+ and includes rounds that look like Series B but weren't announced as one
Reported by: Crunchbase News

Source: https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/seriesb-startup-funding-up-deals-down-ai-prometheus
