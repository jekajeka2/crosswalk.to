# Notable Capital (the former GGV Capital US) is raising its 10th fund without managing partner Jeff Richards on the Form D; Glenn Solomon and Hans Tung are listed. Richards says he stays active but won't make new investments from the next fund.

- Fund: Notable Capital's 10th fund, in market per its Form D
- Listed managing partners: Glenn Solomon, Hans Tung
- Jeff Richards (via text to Axios): "I remain 100% active and engaged; just decided not to make new investments from the next fund. It gives our younger investors like Dan [Cahana] and Chelcie [Taylor] room to run..." He sits on 14 boards, five of them over $100M ARR.
- Background: Notable is the US venture group formerly known as GGV Capital, which split from its China arm over geopolitical tensions

Reported by: Axios Pro Rata
