# Fifth Wall's Brendan Wallace: proptech funding totals undercount the sector because the best bets no longer look like proptech. Horizontal AI tools such as Higgsfield, and full-stack AI or robotics companies that own real estate (Fifth Wall just put $135M into Atoms), are where the money goes, while thin point-solution apps are becoming unfinanceable.

Fifth Wall: Los Angeles, founded 2016, about $3B in commitments and capital under management; portfolio includes Procore, Opendoor, Blend and Hippo; LPs include CBRE, Hines, Hilton and Marriott
Made a large growth-fund investment in Higgsfield, a visualization tool real estate agents and leasing brokers use to render spaces, which he says beat industry-specific tools
His view: a tenant engagement app a landlord once bought can now be rebuilt in a weekend, so only software with a hard-to-copy IP moat justifies a purchase
He guesses real estate firms' spending on foundation models is at or near the point of passing their spending on proptech apps
A new model: bespoke AI development labs, such as portfolio company Antares, which build AI tools for a client on a fee-for-development basis rather than licensing them
Context: Crunchbase's analysis has proptech funding on pace to match or slightly exceed last year, across fewer deals
Reported by: Crunchbase News (interview)
