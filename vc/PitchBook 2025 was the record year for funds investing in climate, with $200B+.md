# PitchBook: 2025 was the record year for funds investing in climate, with $200B+ in commitments counting generalist vehicles, mostly from record infrastructure fundraising. Climate-specialist fundraising peaked at $77.9B in 2023; specialist AUM hit $469.9B in 2025 and is projected to top $700B by 2030.

Driver: a shift toward energy security has kept money flowing as climate-specialist fundraising cooled
2026: pace has slowed on private-market fundraising challenges, infrastructure fundraising cycles and capital absorbed by AI
Performance: 1- and 3-year IRRs for climate PE and energy-transition infrastructure specialist funds beat PitchBook's broad PE and infrastructure benchmarks through 2025 (directional, small samples)
Source report: PitchBook, Climate Investing in the Private Markets (Anikka Villegas)
Reported by: PitchBook (The Daily Pitch, Oct. 13, 2026)
