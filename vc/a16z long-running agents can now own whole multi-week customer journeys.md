# a16z: long-running agents can now own whole multi-week customer journeys (insurance intake to payout, mortgage pre-approval to closing, plaintiff-law intake to demand letter), plugging the value leaks at each human handoff, so AI vendors can be paid on the revenue they create rather than just on cost savings: an "AI-native Red Ventures."

Thesis: until recently agents handled bounded tasks (send an email, summarize a call) and short support interactions; new capabilities let them pursue goals across dozens of steps, modalities and weeks.
The "relay race": long journeys with drop-off or value leakage at each handoff. Plaintiff-law example: prospective clients sign with whichever firm calls back first, firms give up on the fifth witness call, routine claims get the first offer because nobody reads the doctor's note closely enough to spot a brain injury, and bills or treatment gaps slip out of demand letters.
The agent version: one case agent from intake on, waking on events or deadlines, spinning up sub-agents to call witnesses or update the client, keeping the complete case record and learning across cases.
Business model point: vendors who own the full journey can price on outcomes (revenue expansion), not seats or cost savings.
