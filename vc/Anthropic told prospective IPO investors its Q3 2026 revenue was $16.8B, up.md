# Anthropic told prospective IPO investors its Q3 2026 revenue was $16.8B, up ~45% from Q2 and more than 3.6x its revenue for all of 2025, per The Information. Growth slowed from Q2's 140%+ jump (driven by Claude Code demand), and some investors and prospective IPO buyers are now discussing a valuation around $1.5T, down from roughly $2T previously expected, per Reuters.

Disclosed at a private pre-IPO investor meeting in San Francisco on Wednesday, Oct. 14, per people briefed on the numbers
Q3 2026 revenue: $16.8B, about 45% above Q2
Q3 revenue alone exceeds 3.6x Anthropic's full-year 2025 revenue
Q2 revenue had grown more than 140% over Q1, on demand for Claude Code
Some attendees expect the slowing growth rate to keep Anthropic's IPO valuation target in check
Valuation talk: some investors and prospective IPO buyers are discussing about $1.5T, down from roughly $2T previously expected (Reuters, via StrictlyVC)
Reported by: The Information (paywalled; figures from people briefed, not company-confirmed), Reuters, StrictlyVC

Source: https://www.theinformation.com/articles/anthropics-rapid-slowing-growth-weigh-ipo-valuation
