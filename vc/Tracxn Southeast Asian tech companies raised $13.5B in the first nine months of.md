# Tracxn: Southeast Asian tech companies raised $13.5B in the first nine months of 2026, up 176% YoY, but three mega-rounds supplied $7.3B (54%) and round count fell 28% to 189. - Early stage is thin: $270M across 90 seed/angel rounds in nine months
- Context given: Singapore's OneByZero raised a $20M Series A led by Jungle Ventures the same day
