# Carta and NewView Capital partnered to make employee tender offers easier to run: NewView structures the secondaries and Carta handles the equity platform. Their joint study projects tender offers at Series C+ startups will triple over three years, though only 44 were executed in H1 2026.

The program combines NewView's secondary deal-structuring work with Carta's equity platform to help startups launch and manage liquidity programs.
NewView/Carta study: tender offers at Series C and later startups are projected to increase threefold over the past three years; only 44 were executed in the first half of 2026.
NewView led a HighTouch tender at a $1.3B valuation last year, when the company was seven years old.
Recent tenders cited: Decagon, Wayve, and ElevenLabs (two in under a year).
Ravi Viswanathan (founder and managing partner, NewView): companies stay private 12 to 15 years, and tenders are a retention tool now that unicorn status no longer draws talent.
Reported by: TechCrunch
