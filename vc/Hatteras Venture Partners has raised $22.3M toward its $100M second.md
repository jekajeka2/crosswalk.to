# Hatteras Venture Partners has raised $22.3M toward its $100M second opportunities fund for follow-ons in select portfolio companies, per a new SEC filing. Earlier this month the Durham, NC firm named former GSK and UnitedHealth Group CEO Andrew Witty chairman and hired former National Cancer Institute director Ned Sharpless as head of creation strategies.

- Fund: second opportunities fund, $100M target; $22.3M raised so far per a new SEC filing (first close announced Oct. 1, amount then undisclosed); makes follow-on investments in select portfolio companies
- Chairman: Andrew Witty (ex-CEO of GSK and UnitedHealth Group)
- Hire: Ned Sharpless, former director of the National Cancer Institute, as head of creation strategies
- Firm: Durham, NC; about 26 years old; backs biotech, medtech and healthtech
