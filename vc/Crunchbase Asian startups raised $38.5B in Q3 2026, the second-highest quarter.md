# Crunchbase: Asian startups raised $38.5B in Q3 2026, the second-highest quarter on record but below Q2 (which had DeepSeek's $7.4B round). About 55% (~$21B) went to AI. China took ~$27B (70%), India $2.9B, Singapore $2.5B. Seed hit a multi-year high of $4.9B (+22% QoQ).

Largest Q3 rounds: Moonshot AI $3.5B Series F at a reported $35B valuation; Kling AI (Kuaishou spinout) ~$3B
Late/growth stage: ~$20B, nearly double a year ago
Series A/B: $13.6B, down 28% QoQ, up 65% YoY
Deal counts roughly flat: capital concentration persists but isn't intensifying
India and Singapore funding fell sharply QoQ; Israel slightly down
Data as of Oct. 5, 2026
Reported by: Crunchbase News
