# Crunchbase: North American startups raised $92B in Q3 2026, down 35% from Q2 but up 50% year over year. The drop mostly reflects no new OpenAI or Anthropic megarounds; deal counts held steady, and about two-thirds of the money ($61B) went to AI companies.

Late and growth stage: $66.45B. Largest rounds: Databricks $5B, Crusoe $3.9B, The Boring Co. $3B, Cognition $2B; more than a dozen $1B+ rounds. For comparison, OpenAI raised $110B in Q1 and Anthropic $65B in Q2
Early stage: $20.6B, down from a multiyear peak in Q2. Largest: River AI $1.1B Series A, Valar Atomics $660M Series B, Fab2 $500M Series A
Seed: at least $5B (preliminary, likely to rise). Largest: Walden Robotics $300M, Veeda AI $90M
M&A: 11 North American startup acquisitions at $1B+, led by Nvidia–Hugging Face ($12.93B), AMD–World Labs (about $8.2B in stock) and Stripe–OpenRouter (reportedly about $7.5B)
IPOs: 17 venture-backed North American IPOs raised just under $4B, led by biotech (Adarx, Braveheart Bio, Electra Therapeutics); Anthropic reportedly eyes a listing as early as November, and OpenAI filed confidentially in June
Reported by: Crunchbase News
