# Clouded Judgement (written by an Altimeter investor; Altimeter backs Anthropic): the $109B of "convertible notes payable" on Anthropic's S-1 balance sheet is not drawn debt. It is mostly converts and SAFEs from strategics like Amazon and Google, so ~$141B raised against $134B cash implies only ~$7B burned over the company's life. Missing Q3 numbers just reflect audit timing.

Cash: $134B ($112B cash and equivalents, $19B short-term investments, $3B restricted)
Total capital raised disclosed: ~$141B
Balance-sheet line: $109B "convertible notes payable"; the author argues no debt has been drawn and the figure reflects convertible notes/SAFEs (about $11B raised early from Amazon and Google, marked up as valuation rose)
Q3 omission: Q3 closed under 30 days before filing; audited figures take time (Cerebras and Figma also went public without their latest quarter in the S-1)
The three questions he says matter: can Anthropic stay at the frontier, can anyone build a durable business on frontier models, and what staying at the frontier costs
