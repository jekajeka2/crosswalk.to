# Stripe's Link now handles checkout for 70+ AI agents (Instinct, Muse, Grok Bot, Anthropic, OpenAI, Skydive, Town, Underdog), Stripe's Dan Hill told Upstarts. Agents are still a small part of Link's 300M users, but Hill calls agentic payment volume "very material" and growing. Stripe shared its September agentic-spend data (volume growth, top categories, typical transaction size) with Upstarts, but those figures are behind the paywall.

Link powers checkout for more than 70 AI agents, per Dan Hill, head of consumer product at Stripe, who runs Link.
Named agent users: Instinct, Muse, Grok Bot, Anthropic, OpenAI, Skydive, Town, Underdog.
Link has 300M users overall across merchants like Airbnb, Anthropologie and Uber; agents are still a relatively small share.
Hill: "Agentic commerce is here, people are using it, it's going to keep growing. The volume we're seeing now is becoming very material."
Stripe's September numbers on agentic spend (payment volume growth, most popular categories, typical transaction size) are in the paid section of the article and not covered here.
