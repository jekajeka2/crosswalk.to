# Alex Wilhelm (Cautious Optimism): it's "neocloud winter." Gartner says $3.5T+ of AI infrastructure spend could face impairment by 2030 and predicts 80%+ of neocloud providers will cease independent operations; CoreWeave says each 1-point rate rise adds $30M of interest on its floating-rate debt. He also argues Chinese open-weight models have stalled, with none climbing the charts since GLM-5.3 (Aug) and MiMo-V2.6-Pro (Sept), opening room for US open models.

Pressures on neoclouds: rising interest rates, rising memory, GPU and component prices, and constant capital needs
Firmus (Nvidia-backed) pulled its IPO; AirTrunk called off its listing; CoreWeave debt sold off

Wilhelm's take: neocloud failures wouldn't necessarily sink the AI trade but would hurt Nvidia's growth; AI labs are nearly investment-grade borrowers, reducing reliance on third parties carrying chip-financing risk
