# Continuation-fund deals are running well below 2025's record as traditional exits recover: PitchBook counts 95 deals worth $47.4B in the year to Sept. 30, vs 161 deals and $97.6B in all of 2025, per its Q3 2026 US PE Breakdown. PE-backed M&A closings rose ~6% QoQ and ~7.9% YoY in Q3.

2026 YTD (to Sept. 30): 95 continuation fund deals, $47.4B aggregate value (includes estimates for undisclosed deals)
2025 (record year): 161 deals, more than $97.6B
Q3 2026 PE-backed M&A closings: up nearly 6% from Q2 and about 7.9% from Q3 2025
Take: PitchBook PE analyst Kyle Walters expects continuation funds to be used more selectively rather than as the "overflow valve" they were in 2025
Reported by: PitchBook News (The Daily Pitch, Oct. 6, 2026), from the Q3 2026 US PE Breakdown
