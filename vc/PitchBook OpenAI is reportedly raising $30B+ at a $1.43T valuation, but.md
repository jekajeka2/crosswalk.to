Estimated Q4 2026 revenue: ~$15B; full-year 2026: ~$37B, if the run rate climbs steadily
Losses: costs are almost 3x revenue, so revenue must grow nearly 3x faster than costs before losses shrink
Headcount: 8,000 in October, up from 4,500 in March (PitchBook data)
Author: Harrison Rolfes, senior research analyst, PitchBook
Reported by: PitchBook (The Daily Pitch, Oct. 13, 2026)
