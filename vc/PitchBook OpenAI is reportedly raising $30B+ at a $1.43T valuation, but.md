# PitchBook: OpenAI is reportedly raising $30B+ at a $1.43T valuation, but PitchBook's own math puts it closer to $1T. OpenAI said its annualized run rate at the end of September was about $50B, not the ~$70B investors had estimated; a day later it was reported to expect $70B by year-end, a target PitchBook says assumes growth slows by about half. $1.43T is 20.4x that $70B; Q2 costs were $2.84 per $1 of revenue.

Run-rate correction: ~$50B annualized at end of September vs ~$70B investors had estimated; OpenAI then reportedly said it expects to reach $70B by year-end
Year-end target needed 40% Q4 run-rate growth after 77% in Q3 (PitchBook, Oct. 13)
Estimated Q4 2026 revenue: ~$15B; full-year 2026: ~$37B, if the run rate climbs steadily
Losses: costs are almost 3x revenue, so revenue must grow nearly 3x faster than costs before losses shrink
Headcount: 8,000 in October, up from 4,500 in March (PitchBook data)
PitchBook also tests the reported valuation against OpenAI's own 2030 plan (analyst note "OpenAI: Priced on a Target It Has Not Hit")
Author: Harrison Rolfes, senior research analyst, PitchBook
Reported by: PitchBook (The Daily Pitch, Oct. 13, 2026; The Research Pitch, Oct. 17, 2026)
