# Megalith, a low-profile venture firm, buys seed funds' pro rata rights and other follow-on allocations in their breakout startups, paying the seed funds for the discovery instead of relying on the usual LP-in-seed-fund handshake deals. Lightspeed's top fundraiser has joined the firm.

Model: offers to take a seed fund's pro rata allocation or other follow-on investments in its portfolio companies, compensating the seed fund for sourcing.
Contrast: large firms usually get later-round access by investing in seed funds as LPs, with an informal agreement to be told when a hot startup is about to raise again.
Hire: Lightspeed's top fundraiser has joined Megalith (per the headline; the teaser doesn't name them).
Reported by: The Information (paywalled; teaser only)
