# PitchBook: Kalshi's reported $1B raise at a $40B valuation sits near PitchBook's $42.1B bull case; its $30.4B 2028 base case already assumes ~150% three-year revenue CAGR and 50%+ EBITDA margins, and state challenges target sports markets, an estimated 80% of Kalshi's YTD revenue.

Deal: Kalshi reportedly seeking $1B at a $40B valuation
PitchBook base case (2028): $30.4B, assuming near-150% three-year revenue CAGR, 50%+ EBITDA margin and a 75th-percentile EV/EBITDA peer multiple; excludes a regulatory change impairing sports markets
PitchBook bull case: $42.1B, assuming 20% higher earnings and a 90th-percentile EV/EBITDA multiple
Risk: legal challenges seek to give US states authority over sports contracts, which PitchBook estimates generated 80% of Kalshi's YTD revenue
Author: Franco Granda, Senior Research Analyst, PitchBook
Reported by: PitchBook News (The Daily Pitch, Oct. 7, 2026)

Source: https://pitchbook.com/news
