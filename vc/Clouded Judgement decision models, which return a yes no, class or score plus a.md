# Clouded Judgement: decision models, which return a yes/no, class or score plus a confidence instead of text, could be the next big drop in token prices after open-weight models. TypeSafe's Jev was used by ~13% of paid Vercel AI Gateway teams within 24 hours of launch, priced at $0.042 per million input tokens with free output.

- Uses: model routing, agent controls (continue/stop/retry/escalate), AI judges for policy compliance, contract review
- Pricing: >100x cheaper than frontier models, but only ~4x cheaper than a small general model like GPT6-Luna; the bet is better accuracy, lower latency and calibrated confidence on classification tasks
- Market moves the same week: OpenAI's Decisions API, Databricks' ai_decide function, and Perplexity's open-weight decision model; The Information reported TypeSafe is talking to investors at a $10B valuation
- Estimated 10-15% of tokens generated today could be served by decision models (a figure the author got by asking Claude)
- Thesis: falling price (P) will speed up token quantity (Q) growth in token-spend TAM
- SaaS multiples this week: overall median 4.2x EV/NTM revenue; top-5 median 38.3x; 10Y at 5.2%
- Reported by: Clouded Judgement
