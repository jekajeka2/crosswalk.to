# Bain Capital Ventures partner Ajay Agarwal argues full-stack robotics companies, not robot "brain" model developers, will win physical AI, because each sector needs specialization. That puts him against VCs putting big money into hardware-agnostic robot models on the view that hardware is where venture dollars go to die.

- Where he said it: an on-stage interview with Axios' Dan Primack last week
- The other camp: investors backing robot-brain developers argue trained models can plug into many kinds of hardware because the physics stays the same
- Agarwal's background: he backed Kiva Systems, which Amazon acquired and made the base of its warehouse robotics

Reported by: Axios Pro Rata
