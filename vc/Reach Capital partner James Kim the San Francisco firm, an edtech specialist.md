# Reach Capital partner James Kim: the San Francisco firm, an edtech specialist since 2015, now invests ~50% learning, 25% health, 25% work, betting that selling to school districts (regulated, multi-stakeholder, board-approved) is close enough to selling to health systems and insurers to give it an edge, as with school-based teletherapy company Cartwheel. Its $265M Fund V (AUM ~$1B) will back ~50 pre-seed to Series A companies.

Firm: Reach Capital, founded 2015 as a spinout of NewSchools Venture Fund; 180+ companies backed (ClassDojo, Newsela, Nearpod, Cartwheel, Stepful, Replit, Brilliant, WorkWhile)
Fund V: $265M, announced August; AUM nearly $1B; ~50 new companies at pre-seed, seed and Series A
Thesis: products must show a concrete mechanism for increasing access to quality education, care or earnings; "makes workers more productive" is not enough
Also discussed: what Reach saw in AI-detection company GPTZero, deals that initially split the partnership, and how ownership stakes and secondaries shape returning capital
