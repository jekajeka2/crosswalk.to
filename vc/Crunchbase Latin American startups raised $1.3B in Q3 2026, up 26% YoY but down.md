# Crunchbase: Latin American startups raised $1.3B in Q3 2026, up 26% YoY but down 9% from Q2. Late-stage/growth took $757M (+74% YoY); seed/angel jumped to $266M (+128% QoQ), led by Decade's $85M, the largest LatAm seed ever. Mexico has out-raised Brazil YTD ($1.52B vs $1.08B), but concentrated in a few winners.

Q3 2026 total: $1.3B across seed through growth, +26% YoY, -9% QoQ
Late-stage and growth: $757M, up 74% from $435M in Q3 2025, down 21.3% from $962M in Q2 2026 (Q2 was the most at that stage since Q2 2022)
Angel and seed: $266M (20.5% of total), up 128% from $117M in Q2 and 62% from $165M a year earlier
Early stage: $288M, down 18.9% QoQ and 34% YoY
Largest rounds: Black Giant (Chile, EnergyX lithium project) $225M at a reported $900M valuation; Erco Energía (Colombia, solar) $129M Series C at ~$600M; Kapital (Mexico, AI financial services) $125M Series C at $1.6B, co-led by Tru Arrow Partners and Fasanara; Kesh Bank (Brazil) $106.9M equity-and-debt seed at $626M; Decade (Brazil, AI wealth advisory) $85M seed backed by Benchmark, Greenoaks and others
Mexico YTD: $1.52B vs Brazil's $1.08B, ~40.7% more; Endeavor says Mexico's money is concentrated in a few winners while Brazil has breadth and leads in realized exits
Endeavor Catalyst: international VCs were more active in LatAm in the first three quarters of 2026 than in 2024 and 2025 combined; ~60 private LatAm tech companies have raised $150M+ each, a potential IPO pipeline
