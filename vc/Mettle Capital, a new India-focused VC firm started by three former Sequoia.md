# Mettle Capital, a new India-focused VC firm started by three former Sequoia India partners after a contentious exit from the spun-off unit, has raised $350M to invest in Indian tech startups, per two sources cited by Newcomer.

Fund size: $350M (per two sources with direct knowledge; not confirmed by the firm)
Founders: three former partners at Sequoia India (now the spun-off unit), after an acrimonious split
Focus: Indian tech startups
Context: Newcomer reads the raise as a sign LPs are not writing off India, even as founders and investors there eye Silicon Valley while US AI startups widen the gap
Reported by: Newcomer (exclusive)

Source: https://www.newcomer.co/p/exclusive-former-sequoia-india-partners
