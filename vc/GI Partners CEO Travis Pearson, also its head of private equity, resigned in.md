# GI Partners CEO Travis Pearson, also its head of private equity, resigned in early October after about a year in the job, per PitchBook. The $49B San Francisco manager pushed the planned launch of its seventh PE fund to 2027 and won't name a new CEO; a seven-member executive committee will run strategy.

Firm: GI Partners, San Francisco, $49B investment manager (PE, real estate, data infrastructure)
Departure: CEO and head of PE Travis Pearson; resignation accepted in early October; he took the top job in September 2025
Timing: weeks before the PE annual general meeting, where the firm had planned to start fundraising for PE Fund VII
Fund VII: launch and timing to be revisited in 2027
Leadership: no new CEO planned; a seven-member executive committee makes strategic decisions; founder Rick Magnuson (CEO until 2025) remains executive chairman
Prior fund: last PE fund closed at $3.9B in June 2021; LPs included Ohio Public Employees Retirement System and Washington State Investment Board
Also departed in the past two years: Brendan Scollans, co-head of data infrastructure (per LinkedIn)
GI Partners declined to comment; the account rests on people familiar with the matter
Reported by: PitchBook News (The Daily Pitch, Oct 19, 2026)
