# October SEC filings show a crop of new venture funds: ex-Benchmark VCs Victor Lazarte and Kris Fredrickson raised $1.252B for debut AI fund Diffusion ONE (target was $800M); Benchmark filed four partner-only Founders' Funds ($220M, $132M, $4.5M, $7.5M) listing Jack Altman; Trust Ventures and Interlagos each filed for $400M; 8VC filed DSS III ($400M) plus three smaller vehicles; Industry Ventures has raised at least $1.7B for its secondaries fund.

Diffusion ONE: $1.252B raised vs. $800M target (per Forbes in May); already co-led Harvey's $550M Series H with Lightspeed and joined Etched's $300M Series C
Benchmark Partners Founders' Fund T1 ($220M), Fund 2 ($132M), Fund 2-A ($4.5M), Fund T1-A ($7.5M); Jack Altman listed on each filing
Trust Ventures (Austin; founded 2017 by Salen Churi; regulated-industry focus) filed Monday for a $400M Fund V; previously sought a $225M Fund IV in 2023, close unclear
Interlagos Capital (deep tech): $400M Fund II
8VC: $400M DSS III, $30.35M 8VC COSC, $20.35M 8VC BRSC, and 8VC AI Fund XI with $13.5M raised so far; follows its $1.5B Fund VII in July
Notable Capital filed for a fifth fund; Greenoaks (co-led Anthropic's $65B Series H) filed for Greenoaks 7
Industry Ventures: at least $1.7B for its secondaries fund, opened late July
Reported by: TechCrunch (Dominic-Madori Davis)
