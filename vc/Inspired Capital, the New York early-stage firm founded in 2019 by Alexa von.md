# Inspired Capital, the New York early-stage firm founded in 2019 by Alexa von Tobel and Penny Pritzker, closed its fourth fund at $450M, up from $330M (Fund III, early 2024), $281M and a $200M debut; AUM nears $1.4B. Von Tobel says 12 companies from Funds I-II became unicorns (~15% of the portfolio) and the firm has 10+ exits, including MosaicML to Databricks for $1.3B.

Strategy unchanged: ~10 companies a year, "napkin" stage to Series A, $1M-$15M checks
Portfolio split ~45% East Coast, ~45% West Coast, ~10% elsewhere; about a third repeat founders
Funds I-II: 10 companies past $100M revenue, 4 past $400M, 1 past $500M (per von Tobel)
Exits cited: MosaicML (Databricks, $1.3B), DeepTune (Mercor, undisclosed), Saturn (Snap); TeamShares went public via SPAC in June
Claims Fund II ranks top 5% of its cohort; declined to share cash-on-cash returns
Secondaries: sells "a few chips" at multi-billion valuations but keeps 90%+ of ownership
Context: Bay Area firms took 55% of new US VC fund commitments last year (PitchBook)
Reported by: TechCrunch, Venture Capital Journal
