# AlleyWatch: New York startups raised $3.14B across 59 rounds in September 2026, up 274% from August and 49% YoY, while US venture funding fell 5.8% YoY. Ten $100M+ rounds (led by Cyera $400M, EliseAI $350M) took 69% of capital; NYC's share of US VC rose to 10.8% from 4.8% in August, and it took 23.2% of all US Series B dollars.

Rounds: 59, down 38% from 95 in Sep 2025; average round $53.2M (vs $24.6M in August)
Late-stage: 11 rounds, $1.87B (59.6%); Cyera $400M Series G (Goldman Sachs Growth Equity), EliseAI $350M, Precision Neuroscience $250M Series D, Profound $180M Series D, Rightway $155M Series E, Forus $150M Series C, Clay $115M Series D, Savvy Wealth $100M Series C
Series B: 9 rounds, $771M (vs $100M across 3 in August); David (Medici Brands) $250M, General Intuition $220M, Sequen $90M (Prysm Capital)
Series A: 13 rounds, $310.1M (vs $51.6M); Ande $52M seed+A (Lightspeed, Redpoint, Duration, Sierra), HIFI $37M, Antioch $32M, Highstock $30M (a16z, Greylock)
Early stage: 26 rounds, $187.1M, average $7.2M
US totals: $29.1B across 762 rounds in September
Repeat backers: a16z in four September rounds (EliseAI, Clay, Highstock, Adaptive Real Estate); Thrive and Khosla each in three of the ten largest
2026 YTD NYC: $22.23B across 614 rounds, 17% above 2025
October preview: about $3.78B across 41 rounds through Oct 22 (preliminary), led by Ramp $1.9B and Modal $750M
Reported by: AlleyWatch
