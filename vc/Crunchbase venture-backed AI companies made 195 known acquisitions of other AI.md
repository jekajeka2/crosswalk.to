# Crunchbase: venture-backed AI companies made 195 known acquisitions of other AI startups through late September 2026, already 14% more than in all of 2025, while the number of buyers rose only 2%. OpenAI leads with 10 deals this year; Anthropic and Legora have five each, and Harvey, Sierra, Cursor and Cohere are repeat buyers.

Deals: 195 AI-startup acquisitions by venture-backed AI companies, through late September 2026 (+14% vs full-year 2025)
Buyers: up just 2%, so a small group of well-funded AI companies is doing more of the buying
Most active: OpenAI (10), Anthropic (5), Legora (5); Harvey, Sierra, Cursor and Cohere also repeat buyers
Reasons given: fill product gaps, enter new markets, bring specialized teams in-house
Reported by: Crunchbase News (Crunchbase Daily, Oct. 6, 2026)

Source: https://news.crunchbase.com
