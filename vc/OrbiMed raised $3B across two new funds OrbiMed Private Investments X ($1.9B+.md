# OrbiMed raised $3B across two new funds: OrbiMed Private Investments X ($1.9B+, US and European life sciences venture) and OrbiMed Asia Partners VI ($1B+, Asian healthcare growth equity and venture).

Focus: biopharma, medical devices, diagnostics and tech-enabled healthcare services
LPs: medical institutions, university endowments, foundations, pension funds, sovereign wealth funds and family offices
Firm: New York-based, $21B+ AUM, 20 partners and 150+ professionals in 12 locations
Managing partner Carl Gordon cites AI, new therapeutic modalities and China's rise in biotech as expanding the opportunity
Reported by: PR Newswire (firm announcement)
