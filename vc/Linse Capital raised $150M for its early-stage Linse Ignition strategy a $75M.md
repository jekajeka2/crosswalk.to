# Linse Capital raised $150M for its early-stage Linse Ignition strategy: a $75M fund plus $75M in co-investment vehicles, a shift from the Puerto Rico deep-tech firm's longtime later-stage focus.

- Fund: Linse Ignition, $75M early-stage fund, alongside $75M in co-investment vehicles ($150M total)
- Focus: deep tech, including space, robotics, energy, defense, semiconductors and AI
- Firm: Puerto Rico-based, about 11 years old; previously focused on later-stage bets
- Leaders: Michael Linse, Bastiaan Janmaat (both ex-Goldman Sachs)

Reported by: Linse Capital (Business Wire), StrictlyVC, Axios Pro Rata

Source: https://linsecapital.com
