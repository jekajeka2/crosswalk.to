# AMD agreed to acquire Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2B in an all-stock deal. World Labs builds world models (simulated, interactive multimodal environments); AMD says the deal helps it understand how AI workloads are evolving. Lisa Su was an early investor in World Labs.

Price: $8.2B, all stock
Buyer: AMD (CEO Lisa Su, an early World Labs investor per Li)
Target: World Labs, founded by Fei-Fei Li; builds world models. Competitors named: Odyssey, Yann LeCun's AMI Labs
Prior tie: the two companies worked together in 2025 on model training and inference optimization on AMD GPUs
AMD's stated rationale: understanding how AI workloads evolve as "demands on compute infrastructure become more diverse"
Reported by: Newcomer

Source: https://www.newcomer.co/p/anthropics-ipo-cant-wait-forever
