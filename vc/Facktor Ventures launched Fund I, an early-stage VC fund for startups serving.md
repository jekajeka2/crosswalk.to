# Facktor Ventures launched Fund I, an early-stage VC fund for startups serving community health centers and the healthcare safety net, investing mainly at seed and Series A. It aims to be majority-owned by health centers and other safety-net organizations. Fund size not disclosed.

- Based in Los Angeles; affiliated with Facktor, a consulting firm founded in 2006 that has worked with 360+ community health centers
- Focus: digital health, healthcare software and services for safety-net care, including workflows, workforce, patient access, revenue cycle, behavioral health and cybersecurity
- People: Jay Boyer (General Partner), Michael Ceballos (Partner)
- Fund size and LPs not disclosed
- Reported by: PR Newswire
