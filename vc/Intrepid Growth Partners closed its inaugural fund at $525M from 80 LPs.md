# Intrepid Growth Partners closed its inaugural fund at $525M from 80 LPs, including Temasek, Abu Dhabi Investment Council, British Business Bank, Export Development Canada and BDC. The firm, co-founded in 2023 by ex-CPP Investments CEO Mark Machin with Mark Shulgan and Ajay Agrawal, invests only in AI companies based outside Silicon Valley.

- Fund: Intrepid Growth Partners' inaugural fund, $525M close, announced Sept. 14, 2026
- LPs: 80 in total, including Temasek, Abu Dhabi Investment Council, British Business Bank, Export Development Canada and Business Development Bank of Canada
- Founders: Mark Machin (CEO of CPP Investments until 2021), Mark Shulgan and Ajay Agrawal; the firm was founded in 2023
- Focus: "AI-pilled" startups based outside Silicon Valley
- Portfolio example: participated in Metaview's $60M Series C led by Insight Partners (Sept. 2026)

Reported by: PitchBook (The Weekend Pitch, Oct. 4; The Daily Pitch, Sept. 30)
