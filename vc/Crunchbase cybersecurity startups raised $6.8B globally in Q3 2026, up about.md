# Crunchbase: cybersecurity startups raised $6.8B globally in Q3 2026, up about 50% from Q2, even as round counts fell at every stage. Investors are writing bigger checks, especially to AI-security companies: at least 18 AI-plus-security startups raised $100M+ rounds, led by Keyfactor's $1B.

Q3 2026 global cybersecurity venture funding: $6.8B, up ~50% QoQ
Round counts declined across all stages; average check size rose
At least 18 startups at the intersection of AI and cybersecurity raised $100M+ rounds; the largest was Keyfactor at $1B
M&A stayed active, including Visa's $2.4B acquisition of fraud-detection company BioCatch
Reported by: Crunchbase News (daily newsletter, Oct 16, 2026)
