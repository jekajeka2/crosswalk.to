Investor: Seraphim, London, UK (SpaceTech specialist)
Program: Seraphim Academy
Check: $150K per company, in two funding stages
Support: up to 12 months, focused on raising capital, securing customers and scaling internationally
Reported by: VCWire (FinSMEs)
