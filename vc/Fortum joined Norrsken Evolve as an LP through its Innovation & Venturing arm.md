# Fortum joined Norrsken Evolve as an LP through its Innovation & Venturing arm; the pre-seed European sustainability fund, led by GP Johan Attby, recently held the final close of its oversubscribed €62M fund and has backed more than 80 companies across Europe.

- Fund: Norrsken Evolve, pre-seed venture fund for early-stage founders working on European sustainability challenges
- General partner: Johan Attby
- Fund size: €62M, final close, described as oversubscribed
- Portfolio: more than 80 companies across Europe
- New LP: Fortum, investing via its Innovation & Venturing (I&V) arm; Fortum also offers portfolio companies expertise in power generation, energy markets, electrification, customer solutions, and heating and cooling
- Part of the Norrsken ecosystem (50,000+ founders, funders and supporters)
- Reported by: VC Wire
