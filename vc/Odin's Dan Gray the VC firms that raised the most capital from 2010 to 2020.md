# Odin's Dan Gray: the VC firms that raised the most capital from 2010 to 2020 also backed the most future unicorns. Accel is the outlier: under $10B raised but close to as many unicorns as a16z and Sequoia, which each raised well over $10B.

Data: compiled by Dan Gray at Odin, fundraising from 2010 to 2020 compared with the number of future unicorns each firm backed
Finding: firms with more capital tended to back more unicorns ("easier to pay up for the best deals")
Outlier: Accel raised under $10B but backed close to as many unicorns as a16z and Sequoia (each well over $10B)
Reported by: Newcomer (One Big Chart)

Source: https://www.newcomer.co/p/anthropics-ipo-cant-wait-forever
