# Salesforce agreed to buy Listen Labs, an SF-based customer research and human simulation platform, for a reported $2B. Listen Labs had raised over $100M from Ribbit Capital, Sequoia Capital, Conviction and Pear VC.

- Acquirer: Salesforce (NYSE: CRM)
- Target: Listen Labs, San Francisco, a customer research and human simulation platform
- Price: about $2B (reported)
- Prior funding: over $100M from Ribbit Capital, Sequoia Capital, Conviction, Pear VC

Reported by: Axios Pro Rata
