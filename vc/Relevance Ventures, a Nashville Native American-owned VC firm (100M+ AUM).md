# Relevance Ventures, a Nashville Native American-owned VC firm (100M+ AUM), launched Relevance Capital for lower-middle-market health growth equity via a funded independent sponsor model, and named ex-H.I.G. Capital investor James Carey its managing partner.

New arm: Relevance Capital, growth equity in established, profitable health companies with roughly $1M-$15M EBITDA, deal by deal, with part of the equity pre-committed from the Relevance LP network
Venture strategy unchanged: Relevance Ventures keeps backing companies with $1M-$10M revenue across personal, community, digital and financial health
James Carey: previously Partner and Head of M&A at Next Sparc Growth Partners (Len Pagon's single-family office), and earlier at H.I.G. Capital; 20+ years in private markets
Expansion: Carey is based in South Florida; the firm plans a Miami office, adding to Nashville, Charlotte and Denver
Founders: Cameron Newton (Managing Partner) and Dean Newton (Chairman and GP), members of the Patawomeck Tribe of Virginia
Reported by: PR Newswire (firm announcement)
