# Nomada Fund launched in Miami as a "distribution for equity" fund. Instead of cash, it pays for pre-seed and seed equity with media credits across 10+ newsletter, podcast, community, event and SEO channels, backed by ILB Media Group (20+ finance outlets in the US and Latin America). The bet: AI makes software cheap to copy, so distribution and trust become the moat.

Model: like media-for-equity funds that trade TV, radio or print ad space for stakes, but on newer media channels and at pre-seed and seed rather than at maturity
Backer: ILB Media Group, a group of 20+ finance and investment media outlets in the US and Latin America
Targets: early-stage startups with proven traction in Latin America and the US
Co-founder and managing partner Jose Kont (partner at Impacta VC, CAFI, Cuantico VP); co-founder and venture partner Israel García Ballesteros (founder and CEO of Startuplinks)
Venture partners: Rodrigo Giacaman (Startups Latam), Ileana Figueroa (Pulso Capital), Jose Manuel Aguirre (TECHLA); legal partner Eugenia di Marco
No fund size or cash commitment disclosed
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
