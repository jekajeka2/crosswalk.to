# Axios: 2026 US IPO proceeds have already hit an all-time record, but SpaceX produced more than half of them. The number of US IPOs is down 34% from last year, on track for the third-weakest year of the past decade, with Anthropic needing to file within a week to list by Thanksgiving.

Proceeds: all-time record so far in 2026, with SpaceX more than half of the total
Count: number of US IPOs down 34% year over year; almost surely the third-weakest year in the past decade
Anthropic: has less than a week to file if it wants to go public by Thanksgiving
Index rules: under new rules adopted for SpaceX, Anthropic would join indexes early, so fiduciaries like NYC Comptroller Mark Levine (who oversees $300B+ of public pension money and asked Anthropic to delay) would have to become major shareholders
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata (Dan Primack, Oct. 23)
