# Crunchbase: travel tech startups raised just $2.2B globally across 302 rounds in Q1-Q3 2026, on pace for the lowest dollars and deal count since at least 2018 (vs $3.7B in 2025, $12B+ in 2021, $10.4B in 2019), despite a wave of AI travel-planning startups.

Deal volume on track to fall for a third straight year: more money into fewer companies.
Pre-pandemic comps: $9.4B across 1,277 deals in 2018; $10.4B across 1,190 in 2019.
Largest 2026 rounds: Mews (Amsterdam) $300M Series D led by EQT Growth at $2.5B valuation; Aerofugia (Chengdu, eVTOL) ~$144M led by CITIC Securities; Stay22 (Montreal) $122M from Summit Partners; Kindred (SF, home swapping) $85M Series C led by Index plus a $40M Series B; Naboo (Paris) $70M Series B led by Lightspeed.
Several of the year's largest deals went to non-US startups.
Exits: Amadeus bought SkyLink (Feb), Expedia bought Layla (Jul) and Tiqets (Feb); Klook withdrew its US IPO in October.
Navan stock up ~58% YTD to $27 (Oct 28), 8% above its $25 IPO price.
