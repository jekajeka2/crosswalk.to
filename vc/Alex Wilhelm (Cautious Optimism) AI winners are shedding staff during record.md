# Alex Wilhelm (Cautious Optimism): AI winners are shedding staff during record investment. Microsoft offered voluntary buyouts to ~60,000 employees (level 70 and below in the US, Canada, India and UK), and data show industries with greater AI adoption have weaker job growth and higher unemployment than 2015-2019 norms. He also notes Meta plans to raise up to $97.5B in equity for AI, and that China's STAR50 index has fallen 40% (~$480B).

Meta: $25B of Class A shares plus $12.5B of Depositary Shares, on top of a planned $60B at-the-market offering, for up to $97.5B to fund its AI buildout
SpaceX is wrapping up a deal with Apollo Global for $40B of leased GPUs; Musk said the debt market is closed to pure-play neoclouds and open only to companies with other revenue lines
A group of financial firms announced a $63B transaction to deploy Broadcom-based AI compute for Anthropic, under Broadcom's existing compute agreement
Microsoft 'Voluntary Exit Offer': ~60,000 eligible staff, per CNBC; Wilhelm points to 2025-26 layoffs at Oracle, Intel, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, Dell and Cisco and argues they are not just cost-cutting (he notes the employment data shows correlation, not causation)
China's STAR50 index is down 40% in recent months, about $480B in market value, per Reuters; the state is buying stocks
Security: former DOGE staffers used AI agents to break into Anthropic's systems during authorized security testing, which gives their new cybersecurity startup Cathedral credibility
