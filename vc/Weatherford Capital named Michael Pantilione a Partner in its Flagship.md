# Weatherford Capital named Michael Pantilione a Partner in its Flagship strategy, joining its investment committee. He spent 20 years at LLR Partners, rising from summer intern to Partner and backing software and tech-enabled companies.

Role: senior leadership of the Flagship strategy alongside co-founders Will Weatherford (Managing Partner) and Sam Weatherford (Partner); investment strategy, team management, sourcing, execution and portfolio management
Flagship strategy: established markets where regulation and governance shape how businesses operate
Prior boards: Dizzion, eLocal, Magaya, PCS Software, Relay Network, SMG, SparkPost; board observer at CollabNet and CoreDial
Firm: Tampa, Fla. private investment firm founded in 2015; invests across technology, business and consumer services, sports and public-sector markets
Reported by: PR Newswire
