2. Zero tolerance for new features and releases: delay launches until vulnerabilities are patched
3. "Double tap the zombies": turn off neglected labs, test environments and zombie CI/CD projects; if it isn't tied to revenue, kill it
4. End the risk acceptance process, which has become a way to justify never fixing issues
5. Scale up agents, not people: automate detection and response to match machine-speed threats
