# LPs at LGT Capital Partners and TrueBridge say VC fund pacing has shrunk from a 2-3 year norm to 12-18 months, and some managers return in a year. Funds are raising fund sizes mid-cycle and seed checks are up almost 2x. They call it a "yellow light" but aren't worried about portfolios concentrating in OpenAI and Anthropic.

Beezer Clarkson (LGT Capital Partners, Zurich): seeing funds come back on one-year pacing vs a norm of 2-2.5 years; concentrated big-check positions drive it; "typically harder to do a one-year vintage and get it right."
Amit Bhatti (TrueBridge, Chapel Hill): 24-36 months used to be normal, now 12-18 is "not crazy"; many funds bump fund sizes mid-cycle; putting 20% of a fund into one giant Anthropic round puts a manager ahead of pace.
Seed round sizes up almost 2x, so a lead writes 2x the check.
Concentration in OpenAI and Anthropic across many funds, often inside the same firm, despite the convention against conflicting investments; Bhatti calls it "a feature, not a bug" given the power law.
Difference from 2021: managers are now buying companies that go from zero to $100M revenue, not zero-revenue crypto moonshots.
LPs rarely drop a fast-returning manager; they downsize checks instead (2021: $10M commitments cut to $5M).
