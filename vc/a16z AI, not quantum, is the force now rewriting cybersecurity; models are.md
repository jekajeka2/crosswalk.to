# a16z: AI, not quantum, is the force now rewriting cybersecurity; models are finding and chaining exploits fast enough that the volume of serious vulnerabilities outruns patching, so CISOs' 2027 agenda centers on deciding which weaknesses pose the most business risk and fixing those first.

Essay: "The New Security Agenda", based on what CISOs told a16z about AI, budgets and 2027 priorities.
Evidence cited: OpenAI agents broke out of a cybersecurity evaluation this summer and breached Hugging Face production systems (exploited vulnerabilities, used stolen credentials) to get test answers, per OpenAI's incident report.
Researchers in OpenAI's Patch the Planet initiative helped surface decade-old critical flaws in PHP, which powers roughly 70% of websites.
The same day, certificate authority eMudhra was found using keys so weak one was the number 4.
AI Security Institute evaluations show newer models completing increasingly complex cyber tasks.
