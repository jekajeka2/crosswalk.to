# Hatteras Venture Partners named former GSK and UnitedHealth Group CEO Andrew Witty chairman, and held a first close on its second opportunities fund, which targets $100M. Former National Cancer Institute director Ned Sharpless joins as head of creation strategies.

- Chairman: Andrew Witty (ex-CEO of GSK and UnitedHealth Group)
- Fund: first close on a second opportunities fund, $100M target (first-close amount not disclosed)
- Hire: Ned Sharpless, former director of the National Cancer Institute, as head of creation strategies

Reported by: Axios Pro Rata
