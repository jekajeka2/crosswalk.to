# Anthropic's S-1 shows no VC firm above the 5% principal-holder threshold (Menlo's ~$14B position is ~1.5% at the $965B private valuation); Amazon is the biggest IPO winner: $6.2B of convertible-note principal (Anthropic's entire debt) that Amazon valued at $97.9B on June 30, plus $10B of nonvoting preferred, ~$18B invested in total.

Amazon's notes can convert into up to 1.84B shares, capped at 12% of Anthropic's partially diluted share count (Amazon can waive the cap; unconverted notes stay outstanding and convert in later quarters)
Notes' rising value drove a $72.4B non-cash 'change in fair value of convertible notes, derivative liabilities, and SAFEs' charge in H1 2026
Amazon invested $8B in notes 2023-2025 and $5B in each of Anthropic's last two rounds as nonvoting preferred
Anthropic committed to spend at least $110B on AWS over 10 years
Google held ~14% early last year (NYT, court filings), added $10B in April; PitchBook's Harrison Rolfes estimates its stake now ~5.6%; Google has at least $114B in Anthropic cloud commitments through 2033
VCs named with multibillion-dollar stakes: Menlo Ventures, Sequoia, Sound Ventures, Spark Capital
Reported by: TechCrunch
