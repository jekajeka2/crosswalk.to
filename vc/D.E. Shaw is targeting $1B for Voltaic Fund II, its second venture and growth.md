# D.E. Shaw is targeting $1B for Voltaic Fund II, its second venture and growth equity fund and more than double Fund I, PitchBook reports citing people familiar. More than half is expected to go to later-stage, pre-IPO tech and data companies, after D.E. Shaw Ventures co-led Anthropic's $30B Series G and OpenAI's $122B round this year.

Target: $1B for Voltaic Fund II (per people familiar with the matter; not confirmed by the firm)
Predecessor: Voltaic Fund I closed in early 2023 with more than $450M, just shy of a reported $500M goal
Strategy: mid- to late-stage, pre-IPO; technology, data and adjacent sectors; more than half to later-stage private companies
Track record cited: co-led Anthropic's $30B Series G in February ($380B valuation) and, a month later, OpenAI's $122B round ($852B valuation)
Reported by: PitchBook News (The Daily Pitch, Oct. 6, 2026)

Source: https://pitchbook.com/news
