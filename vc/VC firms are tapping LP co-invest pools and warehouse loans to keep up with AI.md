# VC firms are tapping LP co-invest pools and warehouse loans to keep up with AI round sizes. B Capital reinvested in Reflection AI's $2.5B raise (valuation up 3x+ in six months) through a pool of LP money outside its regular fund, because the check was too big for its $500M main fund raised in July.

Mechanism: the pool sits apart from the flagship fund and gives LPs (pensions, endowments, wealthy individuals) more direct access to hot startups at reduced fees
Context: Reflection AI is the most high-profile US maker of open-source AI models
