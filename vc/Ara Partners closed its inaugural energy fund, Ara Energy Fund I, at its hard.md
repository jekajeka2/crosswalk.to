# Ara Partners closed its inaugural energy fund, Ara Energy Fund I, at its hard cap: $2.5B for the strategy, including $1.8B in fund commitments and $700M+ in co-investment. Launched with HF Capital (the Haslam family office), it buys and optimizes conventional energy assets in power, biofuels, and delivery and distribution, decarbonizing where economically accretive.

Timeline: launched Q2 2025, first close December 2025, final close 10 months later at hard cap
LPs: existing Ara investors plus new pension funds, insurers, sovereign wealth funds, endowments and foundations in North America and Europe
Team: Shameek Konar (Partner, Head of Ara Energy), James Chiu (Partner), David Sims (MD), Matthew Dunn (Principal)
Firm: Houston-based, founded 2017, ~$10.6B AUM as of June 30, 2026, across private equity, infrastructure and energy strategies
Placement agent: Threadmark; legal counsel: Davis Polk
Reported by: PR Newswire, Axios Pro Rata
