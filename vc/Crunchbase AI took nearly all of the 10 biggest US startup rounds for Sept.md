# Crunchbase: AI took nearly all of the 10 biggest US startup rounds for Sept. 26-Oct. 2. Instinct's $1B Series C at a $10B valuation led, followed by EliseAI ($350M at $4B), Armadin ($255.5M at $2.5B+), Kahua ($250M at $1B+) and GMI Cloud ($223M equity plus $445M debt); the No. 10 round, Homeward's, was $120M.

- 1. Instinct, $1B Series C (Sequoia, Benchmark, Coatue), $10B valuation, AI personal assistant, San Francisco
- 2. EliseAI, $350M (a16z, Bessemer), $4B valuation, AI for home rentals, expanding into healthcare, New York
- 3. Armadin, $255.5M Series B (a16z, Accel), $2.5B+ valuation, autonomous cybersecurity, Palo Alto
- 4. Kahua, $250M growth (Bain Capital Tech Opportunities), $1B+ valuation, capital-project management, Alpharetta, GA
- 5. GMI Cloud, $223M Series B (Archiv, with Nvidia) plus $445M debt, GPU cloud, Mountain View
- 6. General Intuition, $220M (Valor, Atreides), $6.2B valuation, world models, New York
- 7 (tie). SiMa.ai, $150M Series C (Fidelity, Amplify), $1.45B valuation, physical AI, San Jose
- 7 (tie). Supabase, $150M (GIC), plus Turso acquisition, San Francisco
- 9. CScale, $145M Series C (Atreides, Valor, Premji), optical interconnects, Palo Alto
- 10. Homeward, $120M Series D (Saluda Grade) plus $330M debt, real estate, Austin
- Atreides and Valor co-led two of the ten (General Intuition, CScale); a16z co-led two (EliseAI, Armadin)
- Methodology: largest announced rounds by US-based companies in the Crunchbase database
- Reported by: Crunchbase News

Source: https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/biggest-funding-rounds-ai-cyber-real-estate-instinct
