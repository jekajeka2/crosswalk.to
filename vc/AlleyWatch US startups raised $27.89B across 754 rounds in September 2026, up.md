# AlleyWatch: US startups raised $27.89B across 754 rounds in September 2026, up 54% from August ($18.1B) and 9.8% below September 2025. Four $1B+ rounds (Crusoe $3.9B, The Boring Company $3.0B, Cognition $2.0B, Stoke Space $1.0B) took 36%; late-stage rounds drew $19.84B (71%), double August; Q3 totaled about $65.43B.

53 rounds of $100M+ took $20.81B, 75% of September capital; top 10 rounds totaled $13.28B (48%)
By stage: Late-stage 69 rounds/$19.84B; Series B 54/$3.32B (largest Gimlet Labs $300M); Series A 119/$2.97B (largest Solstice Oncology $225M); early-stage 512/$1.75B, just 6.3% of capital (largest Physical Superintelligence $58M)
AI compute and networking was the deepest pool: Crusoe, Positron ($875M), Gimlet Labs, Celero ($275M), GMI Cloud ($223M), Cornelis Networks ($205M) raised $5.78B; AI software (Cognition, Harvey $550M, Snorkel AI $350M) added $2.90B
Space and defense: Stoke Space, Mach Industries ($600M), Impulse Space ($308M) raised $1.91B
Health led by Enveda ($311M), Encoded Therapeutics ($275M), Precision Neuroscience ($250M), Solstice, BrainChild Bio ($116M): $1.18B combined
By state: California $9.58B (34%); Colorado $4.32B (almost all Crusoe); Texas $4.17B; New York 11.2% of US capital, up from 4.8% in August
Method: disclosed round sizes only; excludes corporate rounds, PE and debt
Reported by: AlleyWatch

Source: https://www.alleywatch.com/2026/10/us-venture-capital-september-2026
