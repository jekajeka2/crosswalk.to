# PitchBook's 2026 Sustainable Investment Survey: venture capital overtook private equity as the top ESG strategy for the first time, largely because more VCs answered: 62% of GP and fund-of-funds respondents offer VC strategies (53% in 2025), PE fell to 38% from 53%.

Finding: VC is the top strategy for ESG among respondents, a first in the survey's history
Respondent mix: 62% of GP and funds-of-funds respondents offer VC strategies, up from 53% in 2025; PE representation fell to 38% from 53%
Caveat: the shift largely reflects who answered, not a measured change in ESG practice
Reported by: PitchBook News (Chart of the Day, The Daily Pitch, Oct. 7, 2026)

Source: https://pitchbook.com/newsletters
