# Anthropic created the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund and committed at least 5% of its shares to it, with up to another 2% possible, to support public-benefit causes such as the employment impact of AI.

Initial commitment: at least 5% of Anthropic's shares
Possible additional allocation: 2% of shares on top of the initial 5%
Purpose: public-benefit causes, including addressing AI's impact on employment
Reported by: The Information

Source: https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/anthropic-commits-least-5-shares-new-public-benefit-fund
