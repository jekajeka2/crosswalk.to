# PitchBook: Spark Capital's ~$777M invested in Anthropic since 2023 would be worth about $10.9B (~14x) if Anthropic IPOs at a $1.5T valuation. The IPO is targeted for the week of Nov. 16 (WSJ); some holders could sell on day one, with other lockups expiring on the 27th, 48th and 62nd trading days instead of the usual ~180 days.

Spark Capital: ~$777M invested in Anthropic since 2023, ~$10.9B at a $1.5T IPO valuation (PitchBook estimate)

IPO targeted for the week of Nov. 16, per The Wall Street Journal citing unnamed sources
