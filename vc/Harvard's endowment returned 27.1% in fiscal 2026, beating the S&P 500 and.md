# Harvard's endowment returned 27.1% in fiscal 2026, beating the S&P 500 and rising to nearly $68B, with venture capital playing a key role (Axios). It also disclosed a multibillion-dollar SpaceX position, while legal fees from fights with the Trump administration ran about $210M for the year.

FY2026 return: 27.1%; endowment value nearly $68B (world's largest)
Venture capital was a key driver, which Axios says may revalidate the Yale Model
Recently disclosed a multibillion-dollar investment in SpaceX
Legal fees, ongoing and one-time: ~$210M in the fiscal year (Harvard spokesperson); the university has done layoffs and left roles unfilled
Most endowment money is restricted and can't fund general operations
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata
