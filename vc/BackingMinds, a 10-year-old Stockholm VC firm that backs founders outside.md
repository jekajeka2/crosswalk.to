# BackingMinds, a 10-year-old Stockholm VC firm that backs founders outside traditional venture networks across AI, climate and resilience, held a first close of more than €50M for its third fund. Backers include the European Investment Fund and the co-founders of Mojang (Minecraft) and King.

Fund: BackingMinds Fund III, first close above €50M
LPs named: European Investment Fund; co-founders of Mojang and King
Focus: founders outside traditional venture networks; AI, climate, resilience
HQ: Stockholm, Sweden
Reported by: StrictlyVC, Arctic Today
