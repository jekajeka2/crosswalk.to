# Dorm Room Fund, the 14-year-old program that lets college students invest in their peers' startups, raised a $50M fund, four times the size of its last vehicle.

- Fund size: $50M, 4x its previous fund
- HQ: New York; running since 2012
- Alumni include founders of Cursor and Shield AI, and investors now at Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz and Lightspeed Venture Partners
- Reported by: The New York Times, StrictlyVC, Axios Pro Rata, PitchBook

Source: https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/29/technology/dorm-room-fund-ai-investment.html
