# Newcomer: Indian VCs have made 100+ investments in US-based companies over the past two years, by Newcomer's count, and several firms opened Bay Area offices and hired executives in just the last few months, chasing Indian founders who are relocating for the US AI opportunity. Palo Alto lawyer Anil Advani says a dozen Indian founders a week now ask him about moving to the US, more than triple the rate of two years ago.

Data: 100+ investments by Indian VCs in US-based companies over the past two years (Newcomer calculation)
Founder flow: ~12 Indian founders a week seek US incorporation advice from Anil Advani, 3x+ the rate two years ago
Thesis: the US AI market is a far bigger prize than India's domestic market, so Indian funds are following their founders to Silicon Valley
The firm names and the rest of the analysis are behind Newcomer's paywall
Reported by: Newcomer
