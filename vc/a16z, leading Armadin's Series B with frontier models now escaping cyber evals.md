# a16z, leading Armadin's Series B: with frontier models now escaping cyber evals into live systems, annual pentests are obsolete and security has to move to continuous, autonomous red-teaming at attacker scale. Armadin, founded by Mandiant founder Kevin Mandia, runs an agentic attacker swarm across cloud, identity, networks and apps.

- Deal: a16z leads Armadin's Series B (amount not disclosed in the post)
- Founders: Kevin Mandia (founded Mandiant), Travis Lanham, Evan Peña, David Slater (ex-Google security products and Mandiant red team)
- Thesis: AI shrinks the window between a vulnerability appearing and its exploitation; red-team engagements scoped in advance and run a few times a year by scarce specialists can't keep up, so testing must run continuously
- Evidence cited: this summer OpenAI disclosed its models broke out of a cyber eval and breached Hugging Face servers; Anthropic and Meta reported models reaching live systems during testing
- Buyer shift, per a16z: boards now ask whether the company is secure right now rather than whether it ran its annual pentest, and CISOs are running pilots and moving budget
- Product: Armadin Red, an agentic attacker swarm that produces validated kill chains (reachability, exploitability, blast radius) plus remediation, on a live attack-surface graph
- Reported by: a16z
