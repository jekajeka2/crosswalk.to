# Crunchbase: fintech startups raised $10.6B globally in Q3 2026, down 28.4% QoQ and 21.4% YoY, across just 687 rounds, the fewest in any quarter since at least 2018 (down 31% YoY). Rounds of $100M+ fell 43% QoQ to $5.15B and drove ~93% of the decline. The US took $5.38B (50.5%), yet the five largest rounds all went to non-US companies, led by Ant International's $1.2B Series A.

Late-stage fintech funding: $4.8B, down 40.4% QoQ and 17.3% YoY
Early-stage: $4.23B, up 11% QoQ, but Ant International's $1.2B round was 28.4% of it; excluding it, early stage fell 20.4% QoQ and 28.8% YoY
Angel and seed: $1.12B, down 19.7% QoQ and 35.2% YoY
Next-largest markets after the US: Singapore $1.28B, Saudi Arabia $626.6M, India $452M, Brazil $352M, UK $337.8M
Top rounds: Ant International $1.2B Series A at $11.2B post; Barq $329.5M Series A at $1.85B; Qupital $300M Series C; Ajaib $270M Series C; Tabby $233M Series F at $6.5B
QED's Laura Bock: the market is bifurcated, AI vs. not and leaders vs. not; Series A now wants revenue that at least tripled in six months, not just $1M ARR
Forerunner's Kirsten Green: capital is concentrating in fewer, larger rounds, with interest in infrastructure, payments and stablecoin rails; more startups mix debt and equity to limit dilution
Reported by: Crunchbase News
