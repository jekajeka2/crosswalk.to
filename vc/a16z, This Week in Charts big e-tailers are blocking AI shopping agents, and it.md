# a16z, This Week in Charts: big e-tailers are blocking AI shopping agents, and it shows in the data. Per Cloudflare, the share of 403 "Forbidden" responses served to agents and bots has roughly doubled since the summer. a16z reads it as e-tailers defending customer relationships and ad revenue, and also guarding against returns and fraud: NRF data shows return rates up about 2x and the total cost of returns up about 3x since 2019.

Example: Amazon now blocks agent shoppers ("instablocked"), even as personal agents like Muse push agentic commerce
Cloudflare: the share of 403 responses to agents and bots is about double its earlier level
a16z caveat: it isn't clear how much of the rise is a response to agentic commerce specifically
NRF: return rates about 2x and total cost of returns about 3x since 2019; reported fraud is widespread, and "dispute abuse" is the most common concern
Same issue also covers TikTok Shop concentration, "the cure for high prices is high prices," and Gen Z spending
Reported by: a16z
