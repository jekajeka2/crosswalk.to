# Mark Williams (Datasite) in Crunchbase News: the IPO market is reopening, but only for the biggest issuers. 58 venture-backed $1B+ companies went public globally in H1 2026 (27 a year earlier), raising $110.8B, but SpaceX alone made up $86B, about 78%. Datasite's IPO-related deal rooms rose 33% year over year, which it says leads listings by 6-9 months.

- H1 2026 (Crunchbase): 58 venture-backed companies valued at $1B+ went public globally, vs 27 in H1 2025 and 69 in all of 2025
- Venture-backed IPO proceeds: $110.8B in H1 2026 vs $12.6B a year earlier; SpaceX alone accounted for $86B
- Datasite data: capital-raising projects up 32% globally year over year in H1 2026, and the IPO-related subset up 33%; this activity tends to precede announced outcomes by 6-9 months
- Median transaction preparation time fell from 14 to 12 days; median diligence time held at 181 days
- The author's caveat: project kickoffs aren't completed offerings. The test is whether they turn into filings over the next 6-9 months and whether new issues hold their valuations after listing
- Reported by: Crunchbase News (guest column by Datasite's CRO for enterprise)
