# Salesforce signed a definitive agreement to acquire Listen Labs, a three-year-old SF startup whose AI agents design studies, recruit participants, run interviews and simulate customer responses. Salesforce did not disclose terms; Axios reported about $2B. Listen Labs had raised about $100M from Ribbit, Sequoia, Conviction and others.

- Acquirer: Salesforce (NYSE: CRM), definitive agreement signed
- Target: Listen Labs, San Francisco, about three years old; AI-powered customer research: agents design studies, recruit participants, conduct interviews, and simulate how customers might respond to products or marketing
- Price: not disclosed in the announcement; Axios Pro Rata reported about $2B
- Prior funding: about $100M total from Ribbit Capital, Sequoia Capital, Conviction, Evantic, Pear VC, Florent Venture Partners
- Reported by: Salesforce, StrictlyVC, Axios Pro Rata

Source: https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/salesforce-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-listen-labs
