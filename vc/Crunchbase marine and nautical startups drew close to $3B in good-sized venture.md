# Crunchbase: marine and nautical startups drew close to $3B in good-sized venture rounds over the past year, but most of it went to one company. Saronic took a $1.75B Series D at a $9.25B valuation, led by Kleiner Perkins. Most of the money went to autonomy, AI and robotics, much of it with defense uses.

Next-largest rounds: Seahi Robotics (China), $150M Series A in July; Regent (Rhode Island, Seagliders), $120M Series B in August led by Mare Liberum and AE Industrial Partners, plus $120M debt
Saronic (Austin): US Navy is a key customer; plans $3B+ next-generation shipyard in Brownsville, TX
Repeat generalist backers: Andreessen Horowitz (Saronic, Arc Boats, Ulysses Ecosystem Engineering); Founders Fund (Regent, Fleetzero, Panthalassa)
Specialist investors: Ocean Zero (ship emissions), Mare Liberum (Pentagon-backed)
Top categories: autonomous sea vessels, water robots, electric watercraft, ocean data
Caveat from the source: it's a capital-intensive space to scale
Reported by: Crunchbase News
