# Mesh acquired Bluvo. Bluvo develops exchange connectivity technology for crypto platforms.

Price and terms: not disclosed
Team: co-founders Florian Sabani (now Senior Staff Software Engineer) and Luca Dalla Chiara (now Head of Exchange Partnerships) joined Mesh
Acquirer: Mesh, a San Francisco crypto payments network founded in 2020 that connects 300+ exchanges and wallets through a single integration
Stated reason: bring Bluvo's founders, who worked the exchange side of the same connectivity problem, into Mesh to improve reliability and uptime across its integrations
Reported by: PR Newswire

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mesh-acquires-bluvo-302907075.html
