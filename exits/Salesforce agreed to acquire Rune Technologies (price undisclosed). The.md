# Salesforce agreed to acquire Rune Technologies (price undisclosed). The Arlington, Va. defense software startup, founded by former Anduril leaders, uses AI to help U.S. and allied military units forecast supply needs, allocate resources and run logistics with limited or no connectivity.

Price and terms: not disclosed
Target's investors: Human Capital, Pax VC, Washington Harbour Partners, a16z, Point72 Ventures, XYZ Venture Capital, Forward Deployed VC; total raised $30M
Founders: David Tuttle and Peter Goldsborough, former Anduril leaders
Founded: about two years ago
HQ: Arlington, VA
Acquirer context: Salesforce says its defense business is growing 80% year over year, and earlier this year it entered a 10-year U.S. Army contract worth up to $5.6B
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata, StrictlyVC, TechCrunch

Source: https://www.axios.com/newsletters/axios-pro-rata-7bbd8ab1-dd33-4c25-a861-737b20c15670.html
