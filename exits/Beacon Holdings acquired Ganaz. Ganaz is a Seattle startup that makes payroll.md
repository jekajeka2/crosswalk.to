# Beacon Holdings acquired Ganaz. Ganaz is a Seattle startup that makes payroll and HR software for agricultural employers and farmworkers, including a Mastercard payroll card for unbanked workers.

Price and terms: not disclosed; co-founder and CEO Hannah Freeman is leaving the company
Ganaz has raised more than $10M; its last disclosed round, in June 2021, valued it at $26M (per PitchBook)
Founded: 2017; a public benefit corporation
HQ: Seattle, WA
Traction: served 250,000+ agricultural workers in the US and Mexico and 100 employers as of 2023; about 17 employees
Acquirer: Toronto-based Beacon Holdings buys software businesses serving traditional industries and had 45 in its portfolio as of September
Reported by: GeekWire
