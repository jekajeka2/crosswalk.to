Price and terms: proposed $12.93B acquisition, pending
Regulatory: the DOJ is examining whether the deal would give Nvidia control over a key AI distribution point or leverage to disadvantage rivals, as part of a broader probe of Nvidia's acquisitions, investments and sales practices
