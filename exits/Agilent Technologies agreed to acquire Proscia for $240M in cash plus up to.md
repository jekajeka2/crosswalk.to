# Agilent Technologies agreed to acquire Proscia for $240M in cash plus up to $30M in earnouts. Proscia is a Philadelphia digital pathology software company.

Price and terms: $240M cash upfront, plus up to $30M in earnouts; agreed, not yet closed
Acquirer: Agilent Technologies (NYSE: A)
Target investors: had raised around $130M from firms including Razor's Edge Ventures, Highline Capital, Flybridge, Scale Venture Partners and Insight Partners
HQ: Philadelphia, PA
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata, PitchBook News, Fierce Medtech
