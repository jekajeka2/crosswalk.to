# Prime Intellect acquired Moondream. Moondream, founded in Seattle by AWS veterans Jay Allen and Vik Korrapati, makes small open-source vision models and Photon, an inference engine that compiles a model into a single GPU megakernel.

Price and terms: undisclosed; Moondream has fewer than 10 employees (per The Information, which first reported the deal)
Investors: $4.5M pre-seed in 2024 from Felicis, Microsoft's M12 GitHub Fund and Ascend
HQ: founded in Seattle; said earlier in 2026 it was moving to San Francisco
Acquirer: Prime Intellect, a San Francisco AI cloud valued at $1B; raised a $130M Series A led by Radical Ventures in July and passed $100M annualized revenue
Reason: CEO Vincent Weisser said Prime Intellect is largely buying the team's skill at making models run faster; Allen and Korrapati will lead inference and expand vision and computer-use agent work
Moondream name stays for small multimodal models; Moondream 3.1 is served on Prime Intellect, and Moondream Cloud customers get platform credits
Reported by: GeekWire
