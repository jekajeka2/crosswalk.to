# Deepgram acquired Daily (price undisclosed). Daily makes and maintains Pipecat, one of the most popular open-source voice AI frameworks, and runs hosted voice and video call infrastructure.

Price and terms: undisclosed; Deepgram commits to keeping Pipecat open source and vendor-neutral
Investors and total raised: $62M+ from Tiger Global, Highfield Capital, Nat Friedman, Lachy Groom and Kevin Weil, per PitchBook
Founded: both companies were in the same Y Combinator batch in 2016; co-founder Kwindla Kramer
Acquirer's reason: open up the voice-agent ecosystem with an orchestration framework; Deepgram and the Pipecat team also launched Real-Time Context Standard (RCS), an open protocol for voice agents
Deepgram reached unicorn status earlier this year with a $130M Series C
Reported by: TechCrunch
