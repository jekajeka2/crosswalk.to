# Intel acquired Classiq for $2.2B. Classiq makes hardware-agnostic quantum computing software that lets developers design, build, test and run algorithms on any type of quantum computer.

Price and terms: $2.2B; Intel's second-largest acquisition after Mobileye ($15.3B, 2017); expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to regulatory approvals
Investors and total raised: just over $200M raised, including a $33M Series B (Feb 2022) and a $110M Series C (May 2025); Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan invested in both rounds
HQ: Tel Aviv, Israel
Customers: BMW (aerodynamics simulation), HSBC (fraud detection), Intesa Sanpaolo (options pricing)
Acquirer's stated reason: Classiq becomes Intel's global quantum development center; Intel plans to combine its platform with Intel's spin-qubit manufacturing to build a full-stack quantum computing platform
Reported by: SiliconANGLE
