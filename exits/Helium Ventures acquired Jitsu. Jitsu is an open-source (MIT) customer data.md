# Helium Ventures acquired Jitsu. Jitsu is an open-source (MIT) customer data platform and Segment alternative that collects event data from websites, apps and servers and sends it to data warehouses and business tools in real time.

Price and terms: not disclosed
Investors: $2M seed led by Costanoa Ventures, with Y Combinator (S20), The House Fund and SignalFire
Founded: 2020; co-founder and CEO Vladimir Klimontovich
Acquirer: Helium Ventures, which acquires and operates software businesses; founded by Rohit Mittal and Priyank Singh (Stilt co-founders); portfolio includes InsurGrid, Maroo and That's The One
Stated reason: invest in engineering, support and integrations while keeping Jitsu open source; releases since the deal added a Kubernetes operator, audit logs, reverse ETL and managed ClickHouse
Reported by: PR Newswire

Source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/helium-ventures-announces-jitsu-acquisition-and-product-expansion-302906656.html
