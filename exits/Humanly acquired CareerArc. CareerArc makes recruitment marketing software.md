# Humanly acquired CareerArc. CareerArc makes recruitment marketing software, including HireSocial for automated social media job posting and Job Maps, an interactive career-site map of open roles.

Price and terms: not disclosed; nine of CareerArc's 13 employees join Humanly (now 50 people)
Acquirer: Humanly, a Bellevue, Wash. hiring-tech startup (founded 2018; $52M raised, including a $25M Series B in April); its fifth acquisition since 2025 after Anthill, Sprockets, Qualifi and HourWork
Target founded: 2009
Target HQ: Burbank, CA
Acquirer's stated reason: connect recruitment marketing with Humanly's AI screening and interviewing, reaching candidates earlier for high-volume employers
Reported by: GeekWire
