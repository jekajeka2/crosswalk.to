# pubX acquired Compliant (terms undisclosed). Compliant, founded by former Unilever and P&G executives, is a media quality and governance company behind the Data Integrity and CTV Quality Indexes used by global advertisers.

Price and terms: not disclosed; announced alongside pubX's $5M Series A led by Chicago Ventures
Compliant keeps its name and operates as a pubX company, led by CEO Jamie Barnard, who also becomes pubX's President, Global Demand Growth
Acquirer's stated reason: put Compliant's media quality and governance signals inside pubX's buyer and seller agents' buying decisions, uniting pubX's publisher-side base with Compliant's brand and agency side
Reported by: PR Newswire (company announcement)
