# Uber agreed to acquire ezCater for $2.3B in cash. Boston-based ezCater runs a platform for catering and workplace meals.

Price and terms: $2.3B in cash (agreed)
Investors: Iconiq, Lightspeed, Insight Partners, SoftBank, Breton Capital Ventures, GIC, Spinnaker Trust, Nobska Point Capital, Maine Angels, SideCar Angels
Total raised: over $420M, including at a $1.2B valuation in 2021
HQ: Boston, MA
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata
