# Rivian acquired Third Dimension. The U.K. startup uses AI to turn images and video into lifelike 3D worlds, which Rivian will use to build simulators for its driver-assistance and robotaxi self-driving system.

Price and terms: not disclosed; the 15-person team, mostly U.K.-based, joins Rivian's autonomy and AI team led by James Philbin
Investors and total raised: $7M seed from Abstract Ventures, MVP, Soma Capital and Solari Capital, among others
Founded: 2024 by CEO Tolga Kart and Piotr Sokólski, veterans of gaming and autonomous vehicles
HQ: United Kingdom
Acquirer's reason: simulate complex, rare driving conditions from Rivian's fleet data (400,000+ uploaded sequences a day) instead of waiting for real-world edge cases; also expands Rivian's London AI hub
Reported by: TechCrunch
