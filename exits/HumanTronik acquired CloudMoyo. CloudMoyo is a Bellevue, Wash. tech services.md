# HumanTronik acquired CloudMoyo. CloudMoyo is a Bellevue, Wash. tech services firm (300+ consultants) spun out of Icertis in 2015 that implements Icertis contract software and builds AI tools on Microsoft Fabric for customers like Expeditors and Ferromex.

Price and terms: not disclosed; the family of the late Samir Bodas (CloudMoyo co-founder, longtime Icertis CEO) sold its stake as part of the deal
Acquirer: HumanTronik, a San Francisco AI startup co-founded this year by Icertis co-founder Monish Darda (CEO) and investor Kabir Narang, building "human language models" that learn how specific executives decide and power agents inside Outlook and Teams
Founded: 2015 (spun out of Icertis by Darda and Manish Kedia)
HQ: Bellevue, WA, with an engineering center in Pune, India
Leadership: Kedia stays CEO of CloudMoyo, which keeps its name and contracts and will sell and implement HumanTronik's technology
Traction: CloudMoyo's AI business is growing about 100% a year, faster than its traditional work
Acquirer's stated reason: a "Service as Software" model, software subscriptions with CloudMoyo support built in, moving from per-user to outcome-based pricing
Reported by: GeekWire
