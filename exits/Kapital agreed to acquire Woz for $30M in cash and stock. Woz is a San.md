# Kapital agreed to acquire Woz for $30M in cash and stock. Woz is a San Francisco AI app builder.

Price and terms: $30M in cash and stock
Target's investors: seed backers include Cervin Ventures, Burst Capital, Y Combinator, Untapped Ventures, MGV and the Lacob Family
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Acquirer: Kapital, a Mexican commercial bank
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata
