# Guidewire acquired Hyperexponential for $1.7B in cash. The U.K. startup makes agentic underwriting and pricing software used by nearly 600 insurers in 40 countries.

Price and terms: $1.7B in cash, plus $400M more reserved if revenue targets are hit (about $2B total); expected to close next year
Investors: Andreessen Horowitz, Battery Ventures
Total raised: nearly $90M (per PitchBook)
Founded: 2017
HQ: United Kingdom
Acquirer's reason: bring AI agents to work alongside humans to underwrite, analyze and price decisions
Reported by: TechCrunch

Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/guidewire-buys-hyperexponential-for-1-7b-in-cash
