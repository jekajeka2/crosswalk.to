# Salesforce agreed to acquire Rune Technologies (price undisclosed). Rune, based in Arlington, Va., develops predictive software for the battlefield.

Price and terms: not disclosed
Target's investors: Human Capital, Pax VC, Washington Harbour Partners, a16z, Point72 Ventures, XYZ Venture Capital, Forward Deployed VC; total raised $30M
HQ: Arlington, VA
Acquirer context: Salesforce says its defense business is growing 80% year over year, and earlier this year it entered a 10-year U.S. Army contract worth up to $5.6B
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata

Source: https://www.axios.com/newsletters/axios-pro-rata-7bbd8ab1-dd33-4c25-a861-737b20c15670.html
