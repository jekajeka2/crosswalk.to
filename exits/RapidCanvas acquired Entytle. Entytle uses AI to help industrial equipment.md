# RapidCanvas acquired Entytle. Entytle uses AI to help industrial equipment makers automate prospecting, quoting and customer service across machines already installed in the field.

Terms: not disclosed
Acquirer: RapidCanvas, a five-year-old Austin AI startup; the deal follows its recent $29M Series B
Acquirer's stated aim: bring AI-driven execution to the industrial aftermarket
Reported by: StrictlyVC, GlobeNewswire (company release)
