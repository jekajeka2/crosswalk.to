# OpenAI acquired Orchestra Bio. The San Francisco startup, founded in 2022, builds AI agents that run biotech drug-development operations, from monitoring studies and tracking finances to resolving clinical-trial scheduling issues.

Price and terms: not disclosed; no timeline given for Orchestra's agents to appear in OpenAI products
Investors and total raised: about $20M as of its August 2025 launch, from Andreessen Horowitz, 8VC and Insight Venture Management (TechCrunch also names Atria)
Founded: 2022, by CEO Ashoka Rajendra
HQ: San Francisco, CA
Acquirer's reason: Orchestra's team and technology join OpenAI's Rosalind life-sciences unit, working on the Rosalind model and Rosalind Workbench (planning experiments, analyzing results); OpenAI cites 10-15 years to bring a drug to U.S. approval and names Moderna and Novo Nordisk as Rosalind customers
Reported by: TechCrunch, SiliconANGLE, a16z
