# Arity (Allstate's mobility data subsidiary) acquired Inrix. Inrix, a 2004 Microsoft spinout in Bellevue, Wash., sells traffic, road-condition and transportation data plus software for signal timing, road safety and retail site selection.

Terms: substantially all of Inrix's assets; price not disclosed; expected to close later this year
Investors and total raised: Inrix has raised more than $288M; it prepared IPOs in 2013 and 2022 but never listed
Founded: 2004, spun out of Microsoft
HQ: Bellevue, WA; about 277 employees per LinkedIn
Customers: Amazon, IBM, General Motors, Windermere Real Estate, state DOTs
Acquirer's reason: Arity president Gary Hallgren says pairing Arity's driving-behavior and risk data (3T+ miles) with Inrix's transportation analytics gives fuller insight into how people, vehicles, goods and roads interact
Context: Texas sued Allstate and Arity in January 2025 over collecting and selling driving data without adequate consent
Reported by: GeekWire
