# The Knot Worldwide (Permira-backed) agreed to acquire Zola. Zola is a 13-year-old wedding planning platform that more than 2 million couples have used to book venues, design save-the-dates and set up gift registries.

Price and terms: not disclosed in the report
Acquirer: The Knot Worldwide, a Permira portfolio company
Target: Zola, operating for 13 years; 2M+ couples have used it for venues, save-the-dates and gift registries
Reported by: PE Hub
