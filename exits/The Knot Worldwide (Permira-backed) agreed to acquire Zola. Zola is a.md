# The Knot Worldwide (Permira-backed) agreed to acquire Zola. Zola is a 13-year-old wedding planning and registry platform that more than 2 million couples have used to book venues, design save-the-dates and set up gift registries; it had raised around $195M.

Price and terms: not disclosed in the reports
Acquirer: The Knot Worldwide, a Permira portfolio company
Target: Zola, operating for 13 years; 2M+ couples have used it for venues, save-the-dates and gift registries
Total raised: around $195M
Investors: Thrive Capital, Lightspeed, Comcast Ventures, NBCUniversal, Convivialité Ventures, The Chernin Group, Legendary Ventures, SoGal Ventures, GingerBread Capital, BAM Elevate, Founders Circle Capital, Canvas Ventures, BBG Ventures
Reported by: PE Hub, Axios Pro Rata

Source: https://www.pehub.com/permiras-the-knot-worldwide-strikes-deal-to-acquire-wedding-platform-zola
