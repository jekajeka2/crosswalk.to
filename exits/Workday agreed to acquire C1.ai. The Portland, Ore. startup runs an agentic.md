# Workday agreed to acquire C1.ai. The Portland, Ore. startup runs an agentic identity platform that controls what employees and AI agents can access across enterprise systems.

Price and terms: not disclosed; agreed, not yet closed
Acquirer: Workday (Nasdaq: WDAY)
Target investors: had raised $111M; StrictlyVC names Greycroft, Felicis Ventures, Accel, CrowdStrike Falcon Fund, Oregon Venture Fund, Operator Collective, Fuel Capital, Fathom Capital, Active Capital and Garuda Ventures; Axios Pro Rata also lists Alumni Ventures, Glynn Capital and ASDF Ventures
HQ: Portland, OR
Context: companies are working to secure a fast-growing population of autonomous AI agents
Reported by: Workday, StrictlyVC, Axios Pro Rata

Source: https://investor.workday.com/news-and-events/press-releases/news-details/2026/Workday-to-Acquire-C1-ai-a-Leading-Agentic-Identity-Platform-for-People-and-Agents/default.aspx
