# RapidCanvas acquired Entytle (terms undisclosed). Entytle, an Austin-based OEM software developer, uses AI to help industrial equipment makers automate prospecting, quoting and customer service across machines already installed in the field.

Terms: not disclosed
Target investors and total raised: $10M from Accel, Moment Ventures and Shengjing Network
Target HQ: Austin, TX
Acquirer: RapidCanvas, a five-year-old, VC-backed Austin maker of custom AI apps; the deal follows its recent $29M Series B
Acquirer's stated aim: bring AI-driven execution to the industrial aftermarket
Reported by: StrictlyVC, GlobeNewswire (company release), Axios Pro Rata

Source: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/28/3390266/0/en/rapidcanvas-acquires-entytle-to-bring-ai-driven-execution-to-the-industrial-aftermarket.html
