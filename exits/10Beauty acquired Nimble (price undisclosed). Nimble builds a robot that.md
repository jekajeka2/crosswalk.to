# 10Beauty acquired Nimble (price undisclosed). Nimble builds a robot that applies nail polish; it had raised $221M, most recently at a $1B valuation, and a buyside source calls the deal a fire sale.

Price and terms: not disclosed; a buyside source told Axios it was a fire sale ("We took it off their hands")
Target's investors: FedEx, Cedar Pine, The Pritzker Organization, Breyer Capital, Informed Ventures, Ascolta Ventures, DNS Capital, Google AI Startups Fund
Target's total raised: $221M, most recently at a $1B valuation
Acquirer: 10Beauty, a beauty robotics company that has raised more than $70M from VCs
Reported by: Axios Pro Rata, PitchBook News
