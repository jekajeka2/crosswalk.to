# IonQ acquired Elisity for up to $1.3B. Elisity's software maps every device on a corporate network, flags devices with overly broad access, and automatically generates and enforces safer access policies to stop attacks and ransomware from spreading.

Price and terms: all-stock, up to $1.3B including $150M in performance-based earn-outs; expected to close by year-end
Investors: Two Bear Capital, AllegisCyber Capital, Atlantic Bridge, Insight Partners, Technology Crossover Ventures, Chevron Technology Ventures; $70M+ raised in total
Founded about seven years ago; HQ San Jose, CA
Customers: 100+ in healthcare, financial services and manufacturing
Acquirer's reason: extends IonQ's quantum security portfolio (it bought ID Quantique last year); IonQ will add detection of quantum-related security risks and cross-sell, with a public-sector push
Reported by: SiliconANGLE, The Wall Street Journal, StrictlyVC
