# OpenAI acquired Orchestra Bio. The four-year-old startup builds AI agents that run biotech drug-discovery operations, from tracking finances to resolving clinical-trial scheduling issues.

Price and terms: not disclosed
Investors and total raised: reportedly about $20M as of 2025 from Andreessen Horowitz, Atria and 8VC
Founded: about four years ago
Acquirer's reason: Orchestra's team and technology join work on Rosalind, OpenAI's life-sciences AI model, and Rosalind Workbench, its tool for planning experiments and analyzing results; OpenAI names Moderna and Novo Nordisk as Rosalind customers
Reported by: TechCrunch, a16z
